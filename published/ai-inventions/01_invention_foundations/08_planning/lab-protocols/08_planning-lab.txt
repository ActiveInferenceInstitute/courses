Lab: Building Your Invention Roadmap

Objective

Create an adaptive roadmap for your invention project that uses Active Inference principles: stage-gated development, expected free energy evaluation, explicit decision points, and built-in plan revision mechanisms. By the end of this lab, you will have a practical, evidence-driven project plan that balances learning with progress.

Materials and Prerequisites

All previous lab outputs (system map, agent profile, prediction error hunt, causal model, prototype plans, learning log, communication strategy)
Paper or digital planning tool (a simple spreadsheet or even a hand-drawn timeline works)
No project management background required
