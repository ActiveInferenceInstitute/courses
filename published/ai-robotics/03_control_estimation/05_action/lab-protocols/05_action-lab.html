Lab: Optimal Control and Active Inference

Objective

Derive and compare optimal control laws (LQR, LQG, MPC) with Active Inference control. You will show that under certain conditions these approaches yield equivalent control actions, and identify where Active Inference provides advantages (unified perception-action, precision weighting).

Prerequisites

  • Completion of the corresponding module.md lecture material
  • Familiarity with Active Inference concepts (generative models, free energy, prediction errors)
  • Basic pseudocode and diagram skills
