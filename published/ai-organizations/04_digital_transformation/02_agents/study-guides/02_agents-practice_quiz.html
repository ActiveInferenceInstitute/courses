Part B: Short Analysis

  1. Design a human-AI team for a loan underwriting process. Specify: which decisions are AI-automated, which are human, what escalation triggers exist, and what governance is needed.
  2. Your organization plans to deploy an AI customer service chatbot. Analyze the delegation of inference: which customer inquiries should the bot handle autonomously, which should escalate, and how should the handoff work?
  3. A team of analysts is anxious about a new AI system that automates much of their routine work. Design a change management approach that addresses their concerns while maximizing the value of the human-AI system.
