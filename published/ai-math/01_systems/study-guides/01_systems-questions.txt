Study Questions: Systems

Define a Markov Blanket formally. What conditional independence relation does it encode?
Write the factorized joint distribution p(η, s, a, μ) for a system with Markov Blanket {s, a}. Explain each factor.
What is the Langevin equation? How does it describe the stochastic dynamics of a system?
How do the Markov Blanket conditions constrain the flow field f(x) in the Langevin equation?
Define variational free energy F. Write the mathematical expression and identify each term.
Prove that F = DKL[q(η) ‖ p(η|s)] - ln p(s). Why is this decomposition important?
Why is minimizing F equivalent to maximizing the evidence lower bound (ELBO)?
What is the recognition density q(η)? What role does it play in variational inference?
Why is exact Bayesian inference (computing p(η|s) directly) typically intractable? How does variational inference address this?
What is the KL divergence DKL[q ‖ p]? Prove that it is always non-negative.
Explain the relationship between surprise (-ln p(s)) and entropy. When is surprise high?
How does the free energy decompose into accuracy and complexity terms? Write the expression.
What is a nonequilibrium steady-state density p*(x)? How does it relate to self-organization?
How does the Fokker-Planck equation describe the evolution of the probability density over states?
What is the relationship between the flow field f(x) and the gradient of surprise (solenoidal vs. curl-free flow)?
How does the Helmholtz decomposition separate the flow into dissipative and conservative components?
What happens to variational free energy when q(η) exactly equals the true posterior p(η|s)?
Compare the free energy bound with the Helmholtz free energy in statistical thermodynamics. What is the analogy?
How does the choice of variational family for q(η) (e.g., mean-field, Gaussian) affect the tightness of the free energy bound?
Derive the conditions under which minimizing F with respect to q recovers exact Bayesian inference.
