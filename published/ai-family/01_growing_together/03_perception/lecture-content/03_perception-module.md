# Module 03: Perception — The Family Feelers

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand that we use **Senses** to know what is happening.
2. Learn about **Inner Senses** (Feelings, Hunger, Tiredness).
3. Know that paying attention helps us take care of each other.

## Introduction: The Family Detectives

How do you know it's dinner time?

* You **Smell** pizza (Nose).
* You **Hear** cooking (Ears).
* You **Feel** hungry (Tummy).
This is called **Perception**.
Families need good perception to work well.
If the baby is crying, we need to **Hear** it.
If Mom looks sad, we need to **See** it.

## Key Concepts

### 1. Outside Senses (The 5 Senses)

We use eyes, ears, nose, mouth, and hands to explore the world.

* "I see a messy room." -> Time to clean.
* "I hear rain." -> Time to stay inside.

### 2. Inside Senses (Interoception)

This is a big word for "Body Feelings".

* Is your heart beating fast? (Scared? Excited?).
* Is your tummy grumbling? (Hungry?).
* Are your eyes heavy? (Tired?).
Listening to your body helps you know what you need.

### 3. Emotional Radar

Families have "Moods".
Can you sense when Dad is stressed?
Can you sense when your brother is happy?
This is like a superpower. When we notice feelings, we can help.

## Activities

### Activity 1: The Blindfold Taste Test

Safety First: Only use yummy food!
Blindfold a partner.
Give them a small bite (Apple? Cheese? Cracker?).
Ask: "What do you perceive?"

* Is it crunchy?
* Is it sweet?
* What is it?

### Activity 2: The Emotion Charades

Write feelings on cards (Happy, Sad, Angry, Tired, Silly).
Pick a card.
Act it out using only your **Face** and **Body** (No talking!).
Can your family guess the feeling?
You are training your eyes to read emotions!

## Summary

Perception is how we gather clues about our world and our family. By using our outside senses and our inside feelings, we become better at understanding what everyone needs.
