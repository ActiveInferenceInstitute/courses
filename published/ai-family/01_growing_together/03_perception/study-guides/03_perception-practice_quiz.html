Practice Quiz: The Family Feelers

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. Which sense helps you know if the trash needs to go out? A) Hearing. B) Smell (Yuck!). C) Taste.

  2. What is "Interoception"? A) Feeling what is happening inside your body (Hungry, Tired, Hot). B) Moving fast. C) Watching TV.

  3. True or False: We can communicate without talking.

    • (True. We use facial expressions and body language that others Perceive).

  4. How do you help a "Overwhelmed" sensory system? A) Turn up the music. B) Turn off lights and find a quiet place (Calm Down Corner). C) Scream.

  5. Why is it important to notice when someone looks sad? A) So you can take a picture. B) So you can offer help or a hug (Empathy). C) It doesn't matter.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. What is the loudest sound in our house?
  2. What does "Safe" feel like to you? (Warm blanket? Mom's lap?).
  3. Play "I Spy". Describe something using only 3 clues (Color, Shape, Size).
