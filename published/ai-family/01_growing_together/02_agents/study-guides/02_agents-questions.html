Course — Module 02 — Think About It

  1. What is your biggest goal for today? (Play? Eat? Rest?).
  2. Do you think the dog has a goal? (Yes, usually food or belly rubs).
  3. Why do parents have different rules than kids? (Different goals: Safety vs. Fun).
  4. Can you have two goals at the same time? (Yes, "Watch TV" and "Eat Snack").
  5. What happens when your goal is blocked? (Frustration!).
  6. How does a baby show agency? (Crying to get what they need).
  7. Do you ever do things you don't want to do? (Yes, like chores. Why? To help the system!).
  8. If you were invisible, what would you learn about your family?
  9. Why is it hard to understand someone else's feelings? (Because you can't see inside their head).
  10. What is "Empathy"? (Trying to feel what another agent feels).
