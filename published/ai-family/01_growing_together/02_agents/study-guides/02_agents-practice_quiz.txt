Practice Quiz: Family Agents

Part A: Reflect Together

What is an Agent?
    A) A spy.
    B) Anyone who can act and has goals (You, Me, The Cat).
    C) A robot only.

Why do people fight?
    A) Because they are mean.
    B) Because their Goals act against each other (Collision!).
    C) Because they are bored.

True or False: Everyone sees the world exactly the same way.
     (False. We all have different Perspectives).

What is "Agency"?
    A) A building.
    B) The power to make choices and take action.
    C) Being old.

Who has the goal "Keep the family safe"?
    A) Usually Parents.
    B) The Baby.
    C) The Mailman.

Part B: Discussion Questions

Think of a time you were mad at a family member. What was your goal? What was their* goal?
How can we help the different agents in our house work together?
If you could swap agency with anyone for a day, who would it be?
