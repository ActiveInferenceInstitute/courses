Lab: The Alignment Game

Goal

To see how easily messages can get messed up (Noise) and how to fix them.

What You Need

  • 3 or more family members.
  • A secret phrase.

Steps

1. The Whisper (Telephone)

Sit in a circle. Person 1 whispers a funny sentence to Person 2. "The purple monkey eats bananas in pajamas." Person 2 whispers it to Person 3. The last person says it out loud!

2. The Result

Did the message stay the same? Usually... No! "The purple donkey bears bananas." Where did the noise come from? (Quiet whisper, bad hearing, bad memory).

3. The Fix (Active Loop)

Try again, but this time:

  • The Listener must Repeat Back what they heard.
  • "Did you say 'Purple Monkey'?"
  • "Yes."
  • Then pass it on.

Discussion

  • Asking "Did I hear you right?" fixes the error!
  • This is how we fix misunderstandings in real life.
  • "Mom, did you say clean my room now or later?"

What We Learned

Communication is a loop. Checking the message ensures we are all on the same page.
