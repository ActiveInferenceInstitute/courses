# Course — Module 04 — Think About It

1. Who makes the big decisions in your family? (Parents? Everyone?).
2. Why do we need rules? (To keep us safe and predict what will happen).
3. What happens if you break a rule? (The system gets confused or upset).
4. Do you have a "Family Motto"? (e.g., "We are kind" or "We never give up").
5. How do you decide what to play? (Rock-Paper-Scissors? Talking?).
6. Can you think of a rule that has changed since you were a baby? (Yes! Bedtime is later now).
7. What is a "Bad Choice"? (One that hurts you or someone else).
8. How does your family solve big problems? (Family meeting? Mom decides?).
9. Do you ever wonder *why* parents say "No"? (Usually to protect you).
10. Is it okay to make mistakes? (Yes! That is how the Family Brain learns).
