Practice Quiz: Family Plans

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What does a Calendar help us remember? A) Names. B) Events (When things happen). C) Math.

  2. What comes FIRST? A) Put on shoes. B) Put on socks. C) It doesn't matter.

  3. True or False: Plans never change.

    • (False. We must be flexible if surprise happens).

  4. Why do we have a Bedtime Routine? A) To be boring. B) To help our body prepare for sleep (Reduce Uncertainty). C) To make parents mad.

  5. What helps you plan? A) A Clock. B) A List. C) Both!

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. What are 3 things we have to do tomorrow?
  2. If we wake up late, how does that change the plan?
  3. Let's plan a special meal together. What ingredients do we need?
