Lab: The Skill Swap

Goal

To teach a family member something new.

What You Need

 Time (15 minutes).
 Patience!

Steps

Pick a Skill

Each person picks one small thing they are good at.

 Kid: Making a paper airplane.
 Dad: Whistling using fingers.
 Mom: Drawing a cat.

The Lesson

Take turns being the Teacher.
Explain the steps clearly.
Watch the Student try.

The Struggle

It might be hard for the student!
If they make a mistake, help them Update.
"Oh, fold it this way, not that way."

Discussion

 Was it harder to teach or to learn?
 How did it feel when you finally got it right?
 We are all teachers and students in this house.

What We Learned

Sharing skills makes the whole family system smarter.
