Growing Together — Module 05: Action — Think About It

Part A: Family Discussion Questions

  1. What is your favorite thing to DO? (Run? Draw? Build? Cook?).
  2. What is the difference between "Work" and "Play"? Can work be fun?
  3. What is one chore you do not mind doing? Why?
  4. Why are chores important for the family system? (Everyone contributes!).
  5. What is a "Habit"? Can you name three things you do without thinking?
  6. How does play help you learn? (Testing speed, strength, rules, imagination).
  7. What is the most helpful thing you did for your family this week?
  8. Why does it feel good to help someone? (The system runs better and you feel proud!).
  9. What action would you like to get better at? (Drawing? Cooking? A sport?).
  10. If you could invent one new action (like flying or teleporting), what would it be?

Part B: Hands-On Activities and Reflection

  1. Do a "Kind Action Challenge": Do three helpful things for family members today without being asked. Write down what you did and how they reacted.
  2. Pick a fast song and see how many toys or items you can clean up before the song ends. Time your action! Can you beat your record next time?
  3. Make "Chore Coupons" for your family: write actions you can do to help ("I will set the table," "One free hug"). Give them as gifts.
  4. Try learning a new action together as a family (juggling, a dance move, flipping a pancake). How does practice change the action?
  5. Draw a comic strip showing your morning routine: Wake up -> Brush teeth -> Get dressed -> Eat -> Go. How many actions do you take before leaving the house?
  6. Play "Robot Chef": Pretend to be a robot while helping cook dinner. Someone gives you step-by-step instructions. How precise must the instructions be?
  7. Have a "Habit Hunt": Walk through your day and count how many things you do on autopilot (putting on shoes, brushing teeth, saying please). Habits save brain energy!
  8. Do something kind for each family member today. At dinner, share what each person did. Notice how action changes the mood of the system.
  9. Build something together using only items from the recycling bin. Work as a team. What did you create? How did you decide who does what?
  10. Before bed, make a list of three actions you want to take tomorrow. Put the list next to your bed. In the morning, check it and get started!
