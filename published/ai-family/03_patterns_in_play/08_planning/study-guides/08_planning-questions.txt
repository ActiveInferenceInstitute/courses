Why does Mario start with an easy level? (to teach you the rules).
What if the basketball hoop was 20 feet high? (Too hard, no one would score).
What if the hoop was 2 feet high? (Too easy, boring).
Why do board games have instructions? (The Code of the System).
Can you change the rules of Tag? (Yes, Freeze Tag, TV Tag, Zombie Tag).
How do you know if a game is broken? (Someone always wins, or it never ends).
Why do we take turns rolling dice? (Randomness generator).
What is a "Glitch"? (A mistake in the code/plan).

Why is "Calvinball" (from Calvin and Hobbes) funny? (The rules change every second!).
