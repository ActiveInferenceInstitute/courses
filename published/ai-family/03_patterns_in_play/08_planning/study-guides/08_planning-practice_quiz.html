Practice Quiz: Designing the Game

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. Who creates the rules of a game? A) The clouds. B) The Game Designer. C) The dog.

  2. What happens if a game is too hard? A) People get frustrated and quit. B) People love it. C) It explodes.

  3. True or False: You can change the rules if everyone agrees.

    • (True! That is called a "House Rule").

  4. What is Playtesting? A) Eating lunch. B) Trying the game to see if it works. C) Sleeping.

  5. Why do games have goals? A) To give us something to aim for (Directed Action). B) To make us tired. C) No reason.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. What is your favorite game? Why?
  2. If you could change one rule in baseball/soccer, what would it be?
  3. Designing is like being a god of a small universe. Discuss.
