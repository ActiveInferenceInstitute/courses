Practice Quiz: The Rules of the Game

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What make a Game possible? A) A ball. B) Rules (Constraints). C) A uniform.

  2. Where do the rules apply? A) Everywhere in the world. B) Only inside the "Magic Circle" (The Game Boundary). C) On the moon.

  3. True or False: Cheating ruins the game for everyone.

    • (True. It breaks the system).

  4. Why do we have boundaries in Tag? A) So we don't run into the street (Safety). B) To be mean. C) To stop the fun.

  5. What happens if players follow different rules? A) The game is confusing (System Failure). B) It is more fun. C) Nothing.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. What is a rule that keeps you safe? (Seatbelt?).
  2. What is a rule that makes a game fun? (Three strikes?).
  3. Have you ever played a game that wasn't fair? How did it feel?
