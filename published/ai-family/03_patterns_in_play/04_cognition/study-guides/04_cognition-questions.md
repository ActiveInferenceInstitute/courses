# Course — Module 04 — Think About It

1. Why do we fake a pass in soccer? (To trick the opponent's prediction).
2. How many moves ahead can a Grandmaster think? (Many!).
3. Is luck a strategy? (No, luck is randomness. Strategy is control).
4. Why is Tic-Tac-Toe boring for adults? (They solved the algorithm).
5. What is a "Poker Face"? (Hiding your data).
6. How do you win Hide and Seek? (Predict where the Seeker won't look).
7. Why do teams have secret plays? (Information Asymmetry).
8. Is it better to attack or defend? (Depends on the context!).
9. Can a computer beat a human at Chess? (Yes, Deep Blue).
10. If you could read minds, would games be fun? (Maybe too easy!).
