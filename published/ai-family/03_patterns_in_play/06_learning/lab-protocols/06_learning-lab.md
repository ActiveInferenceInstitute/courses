# Lab: Juggle Struggle

## Goal

To experience the learning curve of a new hard skill.

## What You Need

* 2 or 3 soft balls (socks work great).

## Steps

### 1. The One Ball Toss

Throw ball from Left Hand to Right Hand.
Make an arc (Rainbow).
Easy? Good.

### 2. The Two Ball Exchange

Ball A in Left. Ball B in Right.
Throw A. When A is *at the top*, Throw B underneath it.
Catch A. Catch B.
Do this 10 times. (Many drops will happen!).

### 3. The Three Ball Cascade (Impossible Mode)

Add the third ball.
Just try.
It will be chaos.

## Discussion

* Did you get frustrated?
* Did your brain start to "understand" the pattern after a while?
* If you practiced for 1 year, could you do it? (Yes!).

## What We Learned

Everything is impossible until it isn't. The bridge is Practice.
