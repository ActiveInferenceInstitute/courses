Practice Quiz: Moving Mountains

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What is Gravity? A) A type of gravy. B) An invisible force that pulls things down. C) A bird.

  2. Which uses "Fine Motor Skills"? A) Jumping on a trampoline. B) Threading a needle. C) Running.

  3. True or False: It is okay to fall down when you are learning.

    • (True! Falling teaches you how to balance better next time).

  4. Who controls your muscles? A) Your Brain. B) Your Shoes. C) The TV.

  5. Why do we practice sports? A) To get sweaty. B) To build clear pathways from Brain to Body (Muscle Memory). C) To look cool.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. Show me a "Gross Motor" move (Jump!).
  2. Show me a "Fine Motor" move (Wiggle pinky!).
  3. Let's have a staring contest. (Controlling eyelids is an action too!).
