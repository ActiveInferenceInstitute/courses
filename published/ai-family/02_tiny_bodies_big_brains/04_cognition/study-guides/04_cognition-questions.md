# Course — Module 04 — Think About It

1. Where do thoughts come from? (Neurons firing!).
2. Can you think of two things at once? (It is very hard!).
3. Why do you forget things? (The memory path faded away).
4. Do animals think? (Yes! Dogs solve puzzles too).
5. What is your favorite memory?
6. How do you know that $2+2=4$? (You learned a rule).
7. Why is school tiring? (Thinking uses a LOT of energy/sugar).
8. Can you imagine a purple elephant? (That is Imagination!).
9. What do you do when a puzzle is too hard? (Ask for help? Take a break?).
10. If your brain was a room, what would be in it?
