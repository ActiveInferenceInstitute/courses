Practice Quiz: Your Amazing Body

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What is the "Engine" of your body? A) Your feet. B) Your Heart and Lungs (Pump blood and air). C) Your hair.

  2. Why do we eat? A) To get Energy (Fuel). B) Because we are bored. C) To make a mess.

  3. True or False: Your body stays exactly the same every day.

    • (False. You are growing and changing!).

  4. What happens when you run fast? A) You get colder. B) You breathe faster and heart beats harder (Response to Demand). C) You fall asleep.

  5. Which is a Good Input for your system? A) Dirt. B) Water and Fruit. C) Gasoline.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. What is your favorite body part? (Legs for running? Eyes for seeing?).
  2. How did you take care of your "Machine" today?
  3. Name 3 things your body can do now that it couldn't do as a baby.
