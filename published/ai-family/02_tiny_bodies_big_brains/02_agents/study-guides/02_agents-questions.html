Course — Module 02 — Think About It

  1. Why do babies stare at you? (They are studying your face data).
  2. What is the weirdest thing you ever tasted?
  3. How do you know fire is hot? (Did you test it? Or did someone tell you?).
  4. Why is it good to be wrong? (Because you learn something new!).
  5. What happens if you mix red and blue paint? (Experiment!).
  6. Can a dog be a scientist? (Yes, they sniff/test everything).
  7. Why do we wear helmets? (To protect the Scientist's Computer/Brain).
  8. Do you have to wear a lab coat to be a scientist? (No!).
  9. What is your favorite experiment? (Baking? Building Legos?).
  10. If you could fly, what would you test first?
