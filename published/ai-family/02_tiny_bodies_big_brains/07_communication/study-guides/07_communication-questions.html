Course — Module 07 — Think About It

  1. How do you know if a dog is happy? (Tail wag!).
  2. Can you say "I'm fine" but look sad? (Yes, faces don't lie).
  3. Why is yawning contagious? (Mirror Neurons!).
  4. What does an "Angry" face look like? (Eyebrows down, lips tight).
  5. Why do we hug people? (To share warmth and safety).
  6. Can you talk with your hands? (Sign language!).
  7. How does a baby ask for milk? (Crying signal).
  8. Why do we laugh? (To show we are happy and playful).
  9. If you were an alien, how would you say hello?
  10. Is it okay to be sad? (Yes! Feelings are just data).
