Course — Module 03 — Think About It

  1. Which sense would you miss the most? (Sight? Hearing?).
  2. Why does spinning make you dizzy? (Liquid in your ears sloshes around).
  3. Why does loud noise hurt? (It is too much energy for your ears).
  4. Can you see in the dark? (No, eyes need light).
  5. Why do babies cry when they are hungry? (Interoception hurts!).
  6. How do bats see? (With sound! Echolocation).
  7. Why do smelly socks smell bad? (Bacteria!).
  8. What is your favorite texture? (Soft blanket? Sticky slime?).
  9. Why do glasses help people see? (They focus the light).
  10. If you could have X-Ray vision, what would you look at?
