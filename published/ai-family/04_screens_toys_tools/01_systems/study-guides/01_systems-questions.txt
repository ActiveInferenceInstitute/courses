Course — Module 01 — Think About It

Where does the Internet live? (In servers and cables).
How does a message get from here to China in 1 second? (Light in fiber optics).
Why do we need passwords? (Keys to the digital house).
Is everything on the Internet true? (No! Verify your sources).
What happens if the Wi-Fi breaks? (The system disconnects).
Can a computer get sick? (Yes, a Virus).
Who owns the Internet? (No one person. It is decentralized).
Why do we call it a "Mouse"? (It looked like one).
What is a "Bug"? (A mistake in the code).
If you could send a message to everyone in the world, what would you say?
