Lab: The Planning Maze

Goal

Use your brain to plan a path before you move!

What You Need

 Paper and markers
 Small toys (like a LEGO figure or a coin)

Steps

Build the Maze

Draw a simple maze on a piece of paper.
Or build one on the floor using books and pillows!
Put a "Treasure" (like a cookie) at the end.

The Robot Mode

Pretend to be a robot that CANNOT PLAN.
Walk into every wall. "Bonk!"
Is this faster or slower? (Slower! And it hurts!)

The Planner Mode

Now, stand at the start. Don't move your feet.
Use your eyes and brain to find the path.
"Go straight, turn left, then turn right..."

Execute

Walk the path you planned.
Did you hit any walls? No!
Planning saves time and bumps!
