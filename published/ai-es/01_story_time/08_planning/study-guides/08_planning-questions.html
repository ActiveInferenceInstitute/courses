Course — Module 08 — Think About It

  1. What would have happened if Nutty just played all day?
  2. Do you plan what to wear in the morning? How?
  3. Why is it better to think before you act?
  4. Can you plan for something that happens next year? (Like a birthday?)
  5. What is a "bad surprise" you want to avoid? (Getting wet in rain?)
  6. How does an umbrella help you plan for rain?
  7. Do squirrels have calendars? How do they know winter is coming? (Perception!)
  8. If you want to be an astronaut, what do you need to plan to do now? (Study? Eat veggies?)
  9. Can you change your plan if something surprises you?
  10. Who is the best planner in your family?
