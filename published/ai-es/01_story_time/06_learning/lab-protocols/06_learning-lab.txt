Lab: Target Practice

Goal

See how your brain learns to aim better with practice!

What You Need

 A crumpled paper ball
 A "Target" (a wastebasket or a circle drawn on paper on the floor)

Steps

The First Throw

Stand far back. Close one eye.
Predict: "I will hit the target!"
Action: Throw the ball.
Result: Did you hit it? Or did you miss? (Too far? Too short?)

The Surprise

If you missed, your brain is Surprised. "Oops! Too hard!"
Your brain is Learning and fixing the map of "How hard to throw."

Try Again

Throw it again.
Did you get closer? That's learning!
Keep trying until you hit it 3 times in a row.

The Trick

Now... take a step back.
Throw it. Miss! (Surprise!)
You have to learn a new map for the new distance.
