Course — Module 06 — Think About It

  1. What is the last thing you learned how to do? (Tie shoes? Ride a bike?)
  2. Why did Beep-Boop fall over?
  3. Is it okay to make mistakes? Why?
  4. If you never made a mistake, would you ever learn anything new?
  5. What does your face look like when you are Surprised? (Show me!)
  6. How do babies learn to walk? (Do they fall down? Yes, a lot!)
  7. Can you learn to juggle by just watching? Or do you need to try and fail?
  8. What is "Surprise"? (Is it when something happens you didn't predict?)
  9. If you touch a stove and it's cold, are you surprised? Why?
  10. Who is the best learner you know?
