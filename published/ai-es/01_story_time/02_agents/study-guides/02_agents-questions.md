# Course — Module 02 — Think About It

1. Are you an agent? How do you know?
2. Is your teddy bear an agent? Why or why not?
3. What was Squeaky the Squirrel's goal?
4. Can an agent have two goals at the same time? (Like wanting to play AND wanting to sleep?)
5. If Squeaky ate the nut, what would happen in winter?
6. Do plants make choices? (This is a tricky one!)
7. What is your goal right now?
8. What action can you take to make your friend smile?
9. Can a computer be an agent?
10. If you were a bird, what would be your biggest goal?
