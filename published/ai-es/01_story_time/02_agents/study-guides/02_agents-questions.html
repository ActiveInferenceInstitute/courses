Course — Module 02 — Think About It

  1. Are you an agent? How do you know?
  2. Is your teddy bear an agent? Why or why not?
  3. What was Squeaky the Squirrel's goal when he found the big green nut?
  4. Can an agent have two goals at the same time? (Like wanting to play AND wanting to sleep?)
  5. If Squeaky ate the nut right away, what would happen in winter?
  6. Do plants make choices? (This is a tricky one!)
  7. What is your goal right now?
  8. What action can you take to make your friend smile?
  9. Can a computer be an agent? What about a robot vacuum?
  10. If you were a bird, what would be your biggest goal?
  11. Draw your own agent character! Give it a name, big eyes, and a thought bubble showing its goal.
  12. Imagine you are a squirrel. You found TWO nuts but can only carry one. Which do you pick? Draw a picture showing your choice.
  13. Play "Agent or Object?" with things in your room. Make two lists: Agents (things that choose) and Objects (things that don't).
  14. Tell a story about a dog agent who wants to find a buried bone. What does the dog feel, think, and do?
  15. Draw a comic strip (3 boxes) showing: (1) An agent sees something, (2) The agent thinks, (3) The agent acts!
  16. Act out being a hungry bear. Show your goal (find honey!) and your action (sniff and walk to the beehive!).
  17. Imagine a robot agent in your house. What would its goal be? Draw the robot doing its job.
  18. Make up an agent that lives underwater. What is its name? What is its goal? How does it move?
  19. Draw Squeaky the Squirrel making his big choice. Show two thought bubbles: "Eat now?" and "Save for winter?"
  20. Write a short story about an agent who has to make a hard choice. What did the agent pick? Was it a good choice?
