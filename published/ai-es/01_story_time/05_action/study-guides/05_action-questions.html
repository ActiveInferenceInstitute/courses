Course — Module 05 — Think About It

  1. What is your favorite action? (Running? Drawing? Sleeping?)
  2. If Benny just looked at the river, would it satisfy his goal?
  3. How does action change the world?
  4. Can you change how you feel by taking an action? (Like hugging a teddy bear?)
  5. What action do you take when you are thirsty?
  6. Is "listening" an action? Why or why not?
  7. How is Action different from Perception?
  8. If you want to be a fast runner, what actions do you need to take?
  9. Can a plant take action? (Do sunflowers move?)
  10. What is the smallest action you can do? (Blink!)
