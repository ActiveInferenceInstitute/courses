Practice Quiz: Action

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is Action? A) Thinking quietly B) Doing something to change the world C) Seeing colors D) Being a rock

    Answer: B! Great job! Action is when you actually DO something to make a change in the world around you.

  2. What action did Benny the Beaver take? A) He flew away B) He built a dam C) He ate a pizza D) He slept

    Answer: B! That's right! Benny chomped trees and pushed mud to build a dam and turn the river into a pond.

  3. If you are cold (Perception), what Action should you take? A) Put on a sweater B) Eat ice cream C) Swim D) Sing

    Answer: A! Smart thinking! Putting on a sweater is the action that changes how you feel from cold to warm.

  4. Which one is an Action? A) Wishing for a pony B) Dreaming about flying C) Kicking a soccer ball D) Feeling sad

    Answer: C! Exactly! Kicking a ball is something you DO with your body. Wishing, dreaming, and feeling happen inside your head.

  5. How do we make our predictions come true? A) By waiting B) By taking Action C) By crying D) By guessing

    Answer: B! You got it! Just thinking about something does not make it happen. You have to take action!

  6. Can a tiny action make a big change? A) Yes, like pushing a domino B) No, never C) Only if you are giant D) Actions don't change anything

    Answer: A! That's right! One little push can knock over a whole line of dominoes. Small actions can lead to big changes.

  7. Who takes actions? A) Agents B) Rocks C) Clouds D) Chairs

    Answer: A! Nice work! Agents are the ones who sense, think, and then take action to reach their goals.

  8. What did Benny predict BEFORE he started building? A) That the river would turn into chocolate milk B) That the rushing river would become a quiet pond C) That it would snow D) That the fish would help him

    Answer: B! Good memory! Benny imagined a quiet pond, and then he took action to make his prediction come true.

  9. What is the difference between Thinking and Action? A) Thinking happens inside your head; Action uses your body to change things B) They are exactly the same thing C) Thinking is louder D) Action happens in your sleep

    Answer: A! Exactly! Thinking is what happens in your brain, and action is when you move your body to change the world.

  10. If you want to turn a blank piece of paper into an airplane, what do you need to do? A) Stare at the paper really hard B) Fold the paper with your hands C) Put the paper under your pillow D) Ask the paper nicely

    Answer: B! That's right! You need to take action by folding the paper. Just thinking about it will not make it fly!
