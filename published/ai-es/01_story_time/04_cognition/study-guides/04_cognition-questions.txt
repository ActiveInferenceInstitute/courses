Course — Module 04 — Think About It

What are you thinking about right now?
If you see someone crying, what does your brain guess happened?
How did Sally know it would rain when she saw grey clouds?
Can you guess what you will have for dinner tonight?
Why do you think the "Prediction Machine" in your head is useful?
If you drop a glass on the floor, what does your brain predict will happen?
Have you ever made a wrong guess? What happened next?
Do dogs think? What do you think they think about?
If you hear ice cream truck music, what picture pops into your head?
Is dreaming a kind of thinking? What do you think your brain is doing when you dream?
Draw a "Thinking Cap" for yourself. What color is it? What powers does it give you?
Finish this pattern by drawing what comes next: Star, Moon, Star, Moon, Star, ___
Imagine you are shaking a mystery gift box. It is heavy and rattles. Draw what you think is inside!
Tell a story about a time you predicted something and you were RIGHT. How did you know?
Tell a story about a time you predicted something and you were WRONG. What surprised you?
Draw Sally's brain as a map. Show the things Sally knows: "Clouds mean rain," "Fire is hot," "Ice cream is cold."
Play the "What Comes Next?" game with a friend. Make a pattern (clap, stomp, clap, stomp...) and see if they can predict the next move.
Imagine you are a detective. You find footprints in the mud. Draw what you think made them and explain your guess.
Draw two thought bubbles: one showing what you PREDICTED would happen, and one showing what ACTUALLY happened.
Write a silly story about a brain that was always wrong. "The brain said it would snow, but it was sunny! The brain said the cookie was spicy, but it was sweet!" What did the brain learn?
