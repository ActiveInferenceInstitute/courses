# Lab: The Prediction Pal

## Goal

Test your brain's power to predict the future!

## What You Need

* A ball (or a crumpled piece of paper)
* A clear cup of water
* A coin

## Steps

### 1. The Ball Drop

Hold the ball high up.

| Event | My Prediction (What will happen?) | What Happened? (Result) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Drop a ball** | It will fall down. | |
| **Turn off switch** | The light will go off. | |
| **Push a car** | | |

Were you right?

### 2. The Coin Flip

Look at the coin. Start flipping it.

* **Predict**: Will it be Heads or Tails?
* **Test**: Flip it! Were you right? Is this harder to predict than the ball?

### 3. The Water Tip

Look at the cup of water. Tilt it a little bit.

* **Predict**: When will the water spill?
* **Test**: Tilt it slowly over a sink. Watch your brain "know" exactly when to stop!
