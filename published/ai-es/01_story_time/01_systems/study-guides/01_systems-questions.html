Course — Module 01 — Think About It

  1. Can you find a system in your kitchen? What are its parts?
  2. What would happen to a bicycle system if it lost its chain?
  3. Is a pile of sand a system? Why or why not?
  4. How are you and your friends like a system when you play a game together?
  5. What is the "edge" of your house system?
  6. Can a system be part of a bigger system? (Think about a leaf on a tree in a forest!)
  7. Why do you think parts need to work together?
  8. Draw a system you love (like a video game console or a garden). What makes it work?
  9. If Squeaky the Squirrel left the forest, would the forest still be a system?
  10. What is your favorite system?
