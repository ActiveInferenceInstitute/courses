Lab: Design Your Own System

Goal

Today you are an inventor! You will think of a system and draw how its parts work together.

What You Need

 Paper
 Crayons, markers, or colored pencils
 Your imagination!

Steps

Choose Your System

Pick one idea:

 A Space Rocket
 A Robot Helper
 A Garden
 Or make up your own!

Draw the Parts

Draw your system. Make sure you show the different parts.

 If it's a Garden, draw the flowers, the dirt, the sun, and the watering can.
 If it's a Robot, draw its metal arms, its battery, and its computer brain.

Draw the Edge

Draw a big circle or line around your system to show what is Inside and what is Outside.

Show the Helping

Draw arrows to show how the parts help each other. (Example: Sun --> Flower)
