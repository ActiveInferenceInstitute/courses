Module 01: Systems — The Forest Team

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand what a System is (things working together).
  2. See how parts of a system help each other.
  3. Find examples of systems in your own life.

Introduction: The Forest Team

Once upon a time, there was a big, green forest. In this forest, there were tall Trees, busy Squirrels, and soft Soil.

One day, a little Squirrel named Squeaky asked a big old Oak Tree, "Mr. Tree, are you all alone?"

The Oak Tree rustled his leaves and laughed. "Oh no, Squeaky! I am never alone. I am part of a System."

"What is a System?" asked Squeaky.

"A System is like a team," said the Tree. "Look down at your feet. The Soil holds my roots so I don't fall over. The Rain gives me water to drink. And you, little Squirrel, you bury acorns that grow into new trees. We all work together. We are the Forest Team!"

Squeaky looked around. He saw bees visiting flowers. He saw birds building nests in branches. He realized that nothing was doing everything by itself. Everything was helping everything else.

Key Concepts

1. What is a System?

A System is a group of things that work together to do something special.

  • Example: A Bicycle is a system. It has wheels, a chain, handlebars, and pedals. If you take away the wheels, is it still a bicycle? No! The parts need each other to work.

2. Parts of a System

Every system has Parts.

  • Forest System Parts: Trees, animals, sun, rain, dirt.
  • Your Body System Parts: Heart, lungs, tummy, hands, feet.
  • School System Parts: Teachers, students, books, classrooms.

When the parts work together, they can do amazing things! A wheel can't go anywhere by itself, but a whole bicycle can take you to the park.

3. Inside and Outside

Every system has an Edge.

  • Your house has walls (the edge). Inside is your family. Outside is the street.
  • Your body has skin (the edge). Inside is you. Outside is the air.

The edge keeps the system safe and holds the parts together.

Activities

Activity 1: The Body Team

Stand up and wiggle your fingers. Now stomp your feet. Put your hand on your chest—can you feel your heart beating? All these parts are working together to make YOU! You are a wonderful system.

Activity 2: School Spies

Look around your classroom. Can you spot 3 parts of the "Classroom System"? (Hint: Desks? Whiteboard? Rug?)

Summary

A System is like a team. It is made of different parts that work together. A forest, a bicycle, and your own body are all systems. When parts help each other, they can do things they couldn't do alone!

Lab Protocol

Lab: Design Your Own System

Goal

Today you are an inventor! You will think of a system and draw how its parts work together.

What You Need

  • Paper
  • Crayons, markers, or colored pencils
  • Your imagination!

Steps

1. Choose Your System

Pick one idea:

  • A Space Rocket
  • A Robot Helper
  • A Garden
  • Or make up your own!

2. Draw the Parts

Draw your system. Make sure you show the different parts.

  • If it's a Garden, draw the flowers, the dirt, the sun, and the watering can.
  • If it's a Robot, draw its metal arms, its battery, and its computer brain.

3. Draw the Edge

Draw a big circle or line around your system to show what is Inside and what is Outside.

4. Show the Helping

Draw arrows to show how the parts help each other. (Example: Sun --> Flower)

Practice Quiz: Systems

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is a System? A) A pile of rocks B) A group of parts that work together C) A single leaf D) Nothing

  2. In the story, who was part of the Forest Team? A) A robot B) An alien C) An Oak Tree and a Squirrel D) A car

  3. What happens if you take a wheel off a bicycle? A) It goes faster B) It stops working as a system C) It turns into a skateboard D) It flies away

  4. Which one is a System? A) A cloud of dust B) Your body C) A broken toy D) A single grain of sand

  5. What keeps a system's parts inside? A) The Edge (boundary) B) The wind C) Magic D) Sticky tape

  6. Why do parts work together? A) To help the system do something special B) To make noise C) To sleep D) To hide

  7. Is a family a system? A) Yes, because they help each other B) No, because they are people C) Only on Tuesdays D) Only if they are robots

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  1. Name one part of the "School System" and say what it does.
  2. Draw a circle. Imagine it is the edge of a cell. What goes inside?
  3. How is a sports team like a system?
