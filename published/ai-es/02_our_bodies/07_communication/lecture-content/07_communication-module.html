Module 07: Communication — Face Talk

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand that we can talk without words.
  2. Learn about Face Talk (Smiles, Frowns) and Body Talk (Waving, Pointing).
  3. Know that helping others understand what you want is Communication.

Introduction: The Silent Signal

Captain Brain wants to tell a friend "Hello!" Does she need a mouth? No! She can use the Hand Agent to WAVE. She can use the Face Agent to SMILE.

The friend's brain sees the wave and smile. Prediction: "She is friendly! She wants to play!" Communication is a message from one Brain to another Brain.

Key Concepts

1. Sending Signals

You are always sending signals, even when you are quiet.

  • Slumped shoulders: "I am sad."
  • Big eyes: "I am scared!"
  • Red face: "I am angry!"

2. Reading Signals

Your Brain is a Signal Detective. It looks at other people's faces to guess what they are thinking. If Mom looks angry, what do you predict? (Maybe I am in trouble?)

3. Helping Each Other

Ants touch feelers to share food news. Bees dance to show where flowers are. Humans use words, faces, and hands to share ideas.

Activities

Activity 1: The Face Game

Make a face to show an emotion (Happy, Sad, Angry, Surprised). Can your friend guess what it is? How did they know? (Eyebrows up? Mouth down?)

Activity 2: Silent Charades

Act out an animal or job WITHOUT talking.

  • "Elephant" (Use arm as trunk).
  • "Chef" (Pretend to stir a pot). Your body is the message!

Summary

Communication helps us share our Goals with other Agents. We use our Face, Body, and Words to send signals. Listening and looking closely helps us understand what others feel.
