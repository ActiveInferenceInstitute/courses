Module 02: Agents — Captain Brain

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand that YOU are an Agent (the Boss of your body).
  2. Learn that your Body is like a ship, and your Brain is the Captain.
  3. Know that Agents have Goals (things they want to do).

Introduction: Captain Brain and the Body Ship

Meet Captain Brain! She lives in the tall tower (your head). She sails a wonderful ship called The Body. The Body Ship sails on the big blue Ocean called The World.

Captain Brain has a map. She looks through her spyglass (Eyes). "Land ho! I see a yummy apple!" She yells to the crew: "Legs, walk! Arms, reach!" The Body Ship moves. Captain Brain gets the apple. She is a smart Agent!

Key Concepts

1. Who is the Agent?

The Agent is YOU. Specifically, it's the part of you that thinks, chooses, and feels. Your body is your tool to move in the world.

2. The Goal

Every Agent has a Goal.

  • Captain Brain's Goal: Stay safe and find food (and have fun!).
  • Your Goal: Learn, play, and grow. Without a goal, the ship would just float in circles!

3. Action and Perception

  • Perception (Spyglass): Seeing the world coming in.
  • Action (Steering): Moving the body out into the world. Captain Brain needs BOTH to sail safely.

Activities

Activity 1: Simon Says (The Captain Speaks)

One person is "Captain Brain." Everyone else is a "Body Part."

  • "Captain says... touch your toes!" (The Body obeys)
  • "Captain says... blink your eyes!"
  • "Jump!" (Uh oh! Captain didn't say. Don't move!) This shows how the brain sends orders to the body.

Activity 2: The Goal Game

Stand on one side of the room. Place a toy on the other side. Goal: Get the toy without using your feet! Plan: Can you crawl? Roll? Hop? Your brain finds a way to reach the goal!

Summary

You are a special Agent. Your Brain is the Captain, your Body is the Ship, and you sail through the World. Use your spyglass (senses) and steering wheel (actions) to reach your Goals!

References

  • Inside Out (A movie about agents inside the brain!)
  • My Strong Mind by Niels van Hove

Lab Protocol

Lab: Simon Says (Captain Says)

Goal

To see how the Brain sends orders to the Body, and what happens if the order isn't clear!

What You Need

  • A group of friends or family.
  • One leader ("Captain Brain").

Steps

1. The Good Captain

The leader stands in front. Leader says: "Captain Says... Touch your nose!" Everyone must touch their nose. Leader says: "Captain Says... Jump!" Everyone jumps.

2. The Trick

Leader says: "Clap your hands!" (Without saying "Captain Says"). If you clap, you are out! Why? Because the Captain didn't give the official order!

3. Be the Boss

Switch leaders. Now YOU are Captain Brain. Give commands to your friends' bodies. "Captain Says... Dance like a chicken!"

What We Learned

Your brain is in charge. It has to pay attention to know when to act!

Practice Quiz

Practice Quiz: Captain Brain

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. Who is the Agent? A) The Chair B) The Rock C) YOU (The Brain/Body Team) D) The Water

    Answer: C! Great job! YOU are the agent because you can sense, think, and make choices.

  2. Where does Captain Brain live? A) In your foot B) In your head C) In your pocket D) On the moon

    Answer: B! That's right! Your brain lives in the tall tower at the top of your body -- your head!

  3. What is a Goal? A) Something you want to do or get B) A kind of fish C) Falling down D) Sleeping forever

    Answer: A! Nice work! A goal is what you are trying to do, like finding food, making a friend, or learning something new.

  4. If Captain Brain sees a yummy apple, what does she say? A) "Run away!" B) "Reach out and grab it!" C) "Close your eyes!" D) "Sing a song!"

    Answer: B! Exactly! Captain Brain gives orders to the body crew: "Legs, walk! Arms, reach!" And you get the apple.

  5. What are your "Spyglasses"? A) Your elbows B) Your hair C) Your senses (Eyes, Ears, Nose) D) Your shoes

    Answer: C! You got it! Your senses are like spyglasses that help Captain Brain see what is happening in the world.

  6. Who drives the Body Ship? A) The Wind B) Captain Brain C) A Ghost D) Nobody

    Answer: B! That's right! Captain Brain is the one steering the ship, telling your body where to go and what to do.

  7. Can a robot be an agent? A) Yes, if it has a computer brain and a goal B) No, never C) Only if it eats pizza D) Only on Fridays

    Answer: A! Smart thinking! A robot with a computer brain and a goal can sense and act, just like you.

  8. What does Captain Brain need BOTH of to sail safely? A) Pizza and cookies B) Perception (seeing the world) and Action (moving the body) C) Two hats D) Rain and snow

    Answer: B! Exactly! Captain Brain needs the spyglass (senses) to see what is out there AND the steering wheel (muscles) to move the body.

  9. What would happen if the Body Ship had no Captain Brain? A) It would sail perfectly B) It would just float around in circles with no direction C) It would fly to space D) It would turn into a submarine

    Answer: B! That's right! Without a captain to give orders, the ship has no one to make choices or set a course.

  10. In "Simon Says," who is the Captain Brain? A) The person giving orders B) The floor C) The ceiling D) Nobody

    Answer: A! You got it! The person saying "Captain says... touch your toes!" is acting like the brain, sending orders to the body.

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  1. Imagine you are Captain Brain. Draw yourself steering the ship!
  2. If your goal is to "Make a Friend," what actions should your body do? (Smile? Wave?)
  3. Why is it good to have a brain?
  4. Captain Brain uses a spyglass (eyes) to see the world. If your spyglass was foggy (like when you wake up), would it be harder to reach your goals? Why?
  5. If Captain Brain could give your body one superpower, what would it be? (Super speed? Super hearing?)
