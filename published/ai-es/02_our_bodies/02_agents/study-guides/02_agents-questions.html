Course — Module 02 — Think About It

  1. Who is the boss of your body?
  2. Can your hand move if your brain doesn't tell it to? (Reflexes?)
  3. What is a goal you have for today? (Eat lunch? Play a game?)
  4. If Captain Brain falls asleep, what does the Body Ship do?
  5. Why do we need eyes (spyglass)? Can we sail with our eyes closed?
  6. Is a car an agent? Does it have a brain? (No, the driver is the brain!)
  7. What happens if the Captain sees an iceberg (danger)?
  8. Can you be an agent if you can't move? (Yes, you can still think!)
  9. Do animals have a Captain Brain too?
  10. What is the best thing about being the Captain of your own ship?
