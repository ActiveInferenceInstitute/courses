# Practice Quiz: The Prediction Machine

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is your Brain always doing?
    A) Sleeping
    B) Predicting the future
    C) Counting backward from 100
    D) Singing opera

    **Answer: B!** Great job! Your brain is a prediction machine that is always asking "What will happen next?"

2. If you see a dark cloud, what is a good prediction?
    A) It will rain
    B) It will rain candy
    C) The sun will explode
    D) A dinosaur appears

    **Answer: A!** That's right! Your brain remembers that dark clouds usually mean rain. That is a smart prediction!

3. What happens when your prediction is WRONG?
    A) You vanish
    B) You feel Surprised
    C) You turn green
    D) Nothing

    **Answer: B!** Exactly! Surprise is your brain saying "Whoa! I did not expect that!" And that surprise helps you learn.

4. Why do we need memories?
    A) To help us guess what will happen next
    B) To fill up space
    C) They are heavy
    D) To forget things

    **Answer: A!** Nice work! Your brain uses memories like a map to predict the future. The more you remember, the better your guesses!

5. If you let go of a balloon, what do you predict?
    A) It will fall down (if full of air)
    B) It will float up (if full of helium)
    C) It will turn into a bird
    D) Both A or B

    **Answer: D!** Smart thinking! It depends on what is inside the balloon. Your brain uses what it knows to make the right prediction.

6. What is a "Pattern"?
    A) Something that repeats (A-B-A-B)
    B) A messy room
    C) A single shoe
    D) A noise

    **Answer: A!** That's right! A pattern is something that repeats in a way you can predict, like Clap-Stomp-Clap-Stomp.

7. Surprise helps us:
    A) Sleep
    B) Learn
    C) Eat
    D) Jump

    **Answer: B!** Exactly! When you are surprised, it means your prediction was wrong, and now your brain can update its map. That is learning!

8. Your brain builds a map called a "Generative Model." What does that map remember?
    A) Only your name
    B) Everything that happened before, like lemons being sour
    C) Nothing at all
    D) Only things that are blue

    **Answer: B!** Good thinking! Your brain remembers all kinds of things: what foods taste like, where your house is, and that fire is hot.

9. If you pull 1 block from a bag with 3 blue blocks and 1 red block, what color do you predict?
    A) Red (because it is special)
    B) Blue (because there are more blue ones)
    C) Green (because green is pretty)
    D) Purple (because it is your favorite)

    **Answer: B!** Nice! Since there are more blue blocks, your brain predicts blue is more likely. That is smart thinking!

10. When your prediction matches what really happens, how does your brain feel?
    A) Confused
    B) Happy and calm because the world makes sense
    C) Angry
    D) Sleepy

    **Answer: B!** That's right! When you guess correctly, your brain feels good because everything makes sense. No surprises needed!
