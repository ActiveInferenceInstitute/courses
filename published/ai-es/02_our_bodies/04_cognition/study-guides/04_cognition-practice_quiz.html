Practice Quiz: The Prediction Machine

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is your Brain always doing? A) Sleeping B) Predicting the future C) Counting backward from 100 D) Singing opera

  2. If you see a dark cloud, what is a good prediction? A) It will rain B) It will rain candy C) The sun will explode D) A dinosaur appears

  3. What happens when your prediction is WRONG? A) You vanish B) You feel Surprised C) You turn green D) Nothing

  4. Why do we need memories? A) To help us guess what will happen next B) To fill up space C) They are heavy D) To forget things

  5. If you let go of a balloon, what do you predict? A) It will fall down (if full of air) B) It will float up (if full of helium) C) It will turn into a bird D) Both A or B

  6. What is a "Pattern"? A) Something that repeats (A-B-A-B) B) A messy room C) A single shoe D) A noise

  7. Surprise helps us: A) Sleep B) Learn C) Eat D) Jump

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  1. What comes next: Square, Circle, Square, Circle, _____?
  2. If you smell cookies baking, what do you predict you will taste soon?
  3. Draw a picture of something you predict will happen tomorrow.
