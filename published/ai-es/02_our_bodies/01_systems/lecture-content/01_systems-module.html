Module 01: Systems — The Body Team

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand that your Body is a System.
  2. Meet the members of the Team: Heart, Lungs, Stomach, and Brain.
  3. See how they work together to keep you alive and happy.

Introduction: The Body Team

Imagine your body is like a big team of players.

  • The Pump (Heart): Keeps the red river flowing. Thump-thump!
  • The Bellows (Lungs): Brings in fresh air. Whoosh!
  • The Mixer (Stomach): Turns yummy food into energy. Gurgle!
  • The Captain (Brain): Tells everyone what to do.

If the Heart stops, the Brain gets dizzy. If the Lungs stop, the Heart gets tired. They all need each other. They are a System!

Key Concepts

1. What is a System?

A system is many parts working as ONE. Your bicycle is a system (wheels + pedals + chain). Your body is the most amazing system of all!

2. The Blanket (Skin)

Your skin is your Markov Blanket. It keeps your insides IN (blood, bones) and the outsides OUT (dirt, germs). It defines where "YOU" end and the "WORLD" begins.

3. Working Together

When you run:

  1. Legs move fast.
  2. Lungs breathe faster to get air.
  3. Heart pumps faster to move the air. All part of the same team!

Activities

Activity 1: The Heartbeat Check

Sit very still. Put two fingers on your neck or wrist. Can you feel the thump-thump? That is your System working! Now do 10 jumping jacks. Check again. Is it faster? Why?

Activity 2: The Skin Shield

Look at your arm. Touch your skin. It is soft but strong. Imagine if you didn't have skin... you would spill everywhere! Thank you, Skin, for keeping us safe!

Summary

Your body is a wonderful machine made of many parts. The Heart, Lungs, Stomach, and Brain work together as a System inside your Skin (Blanket) to keep you playing, learning, and growing.
