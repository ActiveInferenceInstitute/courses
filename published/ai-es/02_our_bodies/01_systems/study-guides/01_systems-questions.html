Course — Module 01 — Think About It

  1. Why does your heart beat faster when you run?
  2. What would happen if your bones were made of jelly?
  3. Why do we need to eat food? (Hint: Fuel for the system!)
  4. Can you tickle yourself? Why or why not? (Hint: Your brain predicts it!)
  5. What job does your skin do for your body system?
  6. How does your stomach help your legs run? (Food becomes energy!)
  7. Do robots have a heart? What is their "pump"? (A battery!)
  8. Why do you get tired at night? What does your body need?
  9. If you scrape your knee, how does your body system fix it?
  10. Which body part is the "Boss" of the whole system? (The Brain!)
  11. Draw your Body Team! Show the Heart, Lungs, Stomach, and Brain all working together inside your skin.
  12. Put your hand on your chest. Count your heartbeats for 10 seconds. Now do 10 jumping jacks. Count again. Draw a chart showing the difference!
  13. Imagine your body is a car. What is the engine? (Heart!) What is the fuel? (Food!) What are the tires? (Legs!) Draw your Body Car.
  14. Act out being a body part! Pick one (heart, lungs, stomach) and show what it does. Can your friend guess which one you are?
  15. Tell a story about what happens inside your body when you eat a sandwich. Where does the food go?
  16. Draw your body with a big line around it showing your skin. Label "Inside Me" and "Outside World."
  17. Imagine your lungs went on vacation. What would happen? Write a silly story about it.
  18. Make a list of five things your body system does without you even thinking about it (breathing, heartbeat, digestion...).
  19. Draw the Body Team as a group of friends. Give each organ a face and a name!
  20. Write a thank-you note to one of your body parts. Tell it why you are grateful for the work it does!
