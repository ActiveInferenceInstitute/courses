# Practice Quiz: The Body Team

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is a System?
    A) A messy room
    B) Many parts working together as one
    C) A single rock
    D) A cloud

2. Which part is the "Pump"?
    A) The Lungs
    B) The Brain
    C) The Heart
    D) The Feet

3. What does your Skin do?
    A) Holds your insides IN and keeps dirt OUT
    B) Tastes food
    C) Pumps blood
    D) Makes music

4. Why do Lungs breathe faster when you run?
    A) They are singing
    B) To bring more air to the working muscles
    C) To cool down your teeth
    D) Because they are bored

5. Who is the "Captain" of the Body Team?
    A) The Stomach
    B) The Big Toe
    C) The Brain
    D) The Elbow

6. Is a bicycle a system?
    A) Yes, it has parts (wheels, chain) that work together
    B) No, it is not alive
    C) Only if it is red
    D) No

7. What happens if one part of a system breaks (like a flat tire)?
    A) The whole system has trouble working
    B) Nothing
    C) It goes faster
    D) It turns into a bird

## Part B: Tell Us What You Think

1. Draw a picture of your Heart, Lungs, and Brain. Draw lines to connect them!
2. How does your body tell you it is hungry? (Stomach growling?)
3. Name three things your body needs to stay happy (like food, sleep...).
