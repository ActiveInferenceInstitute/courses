# Practice Quiz: Practice Power

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What helps the Brain build a strong path?
    A) Eating candy
    B) Sleeping all day
    C) Practice (doing it again and again)
    D) Yelling

2. When you first learn something (like bike riding), how does it feel?
    A) Easy
    B) Wobbly and hard
    C) Boring
    D) Cold

3. What happens when you make a mistake?
    A) Your Brain learns something new
    B) You fail forever
    C) Your ears fall off
    D) Nothing

4. What is "Muscle Memory"?
    A) Muscles have tiny brains
    B) Doing something automatically without thinking hard
    C) Forgetting how to walk
    D) Strong arms

5. If you want to be good at soccer, what should you do?
    A) Watch TV
    B) Practice kicking the ball every day
    C) Buy new shoes
    D) Eat pizza

6. Why is writing with your "Other Hand" hard?
    A) That hand is broken
    B) Your Brain hasn't built the path yet
    C) The pencil is heavy
    D) Because it is Tuesday

7. Does everyone learn at the same speed?
    A) Yes, exactly the same
    B) No, everyone is different
    C) Only robots learn fast
    D) Nobody learns
