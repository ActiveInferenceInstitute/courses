Lab: The Backward Memory

Goal

To feel how hard it is to learn a new way of moving (rewiring the brain).

What You Need

  • Paper and pencil.
  • A mirror (bathroom mirror or handheld).

Steps

1. The Easy Way

Look at the paper. Draw a Star. Easy, right? Your brain has a strong path for this.

2. The Hard Way

Put the paper in front of the mirror. Hold your hand so you can ONLY see it in the mirror (don't look at your real hand). Try to trace the Star (or draw a new one).

3. The Struggle

Your brain says "Go Right," but in the mirror, your hand goes "Left." It feels wobbly! Keep trying for 2 minutes. Does it get easier?

My Learning Log

Attempt How did it look? How did it feel?
Normal Star Perfect Easy
Mirror Star Squiggly mess! Weird! Frustrating!

What We Learned

When the world changes (mirror reverses it), our old predictions don't work. We have to learn a new way, and that takes practice!
