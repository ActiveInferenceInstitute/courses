Practice Quiz: Muscle Movers

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. Who pulls the strings in your body? A) The Wind B) Captain Brain C) The Cat D) The Shoes

  2. What are the "Strings"? A) Hair B) Nerves C) Spaghetti D) Bones

  3. What are the "Movers"? A) Muscles B) Teeth C) Ears D) Eyebrows

  4. Why do we move? A) To change the world (get food, play) B) To be annoying C) Because gravity pushes us D) No reason

  5. If you close your eyes and touch your nose, how do you know where your hand is? A) Magic B) Proprioception (Body Sense) C) You guessed D) You looked in a mirror

  6. Which is an ACTION? A) Seeing a ball B) Hearing a bird C) Kicking a soccer ball D) Smelling pizza

  7. What happens if the "strings" (nerves) get cut? A) You can't move that part B) You move faster C) You turn blue D) It tickles

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  1. Name 3 distinct ways you can move your arms (e.g., Flap, Wave, Punch).
  2. Try to walk without bending your knees. Is it hard? Why?
  3. Draw a stick figure running. Show where adding muscles would help!
