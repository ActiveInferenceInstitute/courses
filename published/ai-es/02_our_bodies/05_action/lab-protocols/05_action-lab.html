Lab: Mirror, Mirror

Goal

To practice "Action" (Moving) by copying exactly what you see "Perception").

What You Need

  • A partner (friend or parent).

Steps

1. The Mirror

Stand face-to-face. Decide who is the "Person" and who is the "Mirror." The Person moves slowly (lifts an arm, tilts head). The Mirror must do the EXACT same thing at the EXACT same time.

2. The Switch

Switch roles! Now YOU are the Person. Move weirdly! Can your Mirror keep up?

3. Fast Mode

Move faster! Jump! Wiggle! Does the Mirror make mistakes? It takes time for the signals to go Brain -> Nerve -> Muscle!

What We Learned

Moving takes brain power. Copying someone else takes even MORE brain power because you have to See (Perceive) and Move (Act) at the same time.
