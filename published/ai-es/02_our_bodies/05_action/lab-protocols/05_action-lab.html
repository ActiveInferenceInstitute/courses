Lab: Mirror, Mirror

Goal

Practice Action (Moving) by copying exactly what you see (Perception) in a mirror game with a partner!

What You Need

  • A partner (friend or parent)
  • Some space to move around

Steps

1. Pick Roles

Stand face-to-face with your partner. Decide who is the "Leader" (the Person) and who is the "Mirror" (the Copier).

2. Slow Mirror

The Leader moves very slowly: lift an arm, tilt your head, wiggle your fingers. The Mirror must do the EXACT same thing at the EXACT same time, like a real mirror!

(Example: Leader lifts right hand. Mirror lifts left hand -- because mirrors flip!)

3. Speed Up

The Leader starts moving faster. Can the Mirror keep up? Try jumping, crouching, making funny faces!

4. Switch Roles

Now switch! The Mirror becomes the Leader. Is it easier to lead or to follow?

5. Double Challenge

Both of you try to be the Mirror AND the Leader at the same time. Move together without deciding who leads! This is really hard because both brains have to See and Move at the same time.
