Lab: The Mystery Box

Goal

To use your senses (Touch) to guess what is hidden, without using Sight (Eyes).

What You Need

  • A cardboard box with a hole cut in the side (or just a bag/pillowcase).
  • Secret objects (spoon, rock, cotton ball, toy car, orange).

Steps

1. The Setup

Ask a friend or parent to hide 3 objects in the box/bag. Don't look!

2. The Touch Test

Put your hand inside. Grab one object. Feel it.

  • Is it hard or soft?
  • Is it round or square?
  • Is it cold or warm?
  • Is it smooth or bumpy?

3. The Guess

Tell your brain the clues. "It is round. It has bumpy skin. It smells... citrusy?" Guess: "It is an orange!" Pull it out. Were you right?
