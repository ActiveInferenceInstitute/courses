Lab: The Mystery Box

Goal

Use your sense of Touch to guess what is hidden inside a box, without using your Eyes!

What You Need

  • A cardboard box with a hole cut in the side (or a pillowcase or paper bag)
  • 5 secret objects (examples: spoon, rock, cotton ball, toy car, orange)
  • A friend or family member to hide the objects

Steps

1. The Setup

Ask your helper to put one secret object inside the box. Do not peek! Your Eyes Spy must stay off duty for this mission.

2. The Touch Investigation

Put your hand inside the box. Feel the object carefully. Ask yourself these questions:

  • Is it hard or soft?
  • Is it round or has corners?
  • Is it cold or warm?
  • Is it smooth or bumpy?
  • Is it heavy or light?

3. The Brain's Guess

Tell Captain Brain all the clues. "It is round. It has bumpy skin. It feels cold and heavy." Captain Brain makes a guess: "I think it is... an orange!"

4. The Reveal

Pull out the object. Were you right? If yes, great prediction! If no, surprise! What clue did you miss?

5. Repeat

Try with all five objects. Keep score!
