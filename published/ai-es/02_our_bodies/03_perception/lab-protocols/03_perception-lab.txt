Lab: The Mystery Box

Goal

To use your senses (Touch) to guess what is hidden, without using Sight (Eyes).

What You Need

 A cardboard box with a hole cut in the side (or just a bag/pillowcase).
 Secret objects (spoon, rock, cotton ball, toy car, orange).

Steps

The Setup

Ask a friend or parent to hide 3 objects in the box/bag.
Don't look!

The Touch Test

Put your hand inside. Grab one object.
Feel it.

 Is it hard or soft?
 Is it round or square?
 Is it cold or warm?
 Is it smooth or bumpy?

The Guess

Tell your brain the clues.
"It is round. It has bumpy skin. It smells... citrusy?"
Guess: "It is an orange!"
Pull it out. Were you right?

What We Learned

Your brain can see with your hands! It takes the touch clues and builds a picture in your mind.
