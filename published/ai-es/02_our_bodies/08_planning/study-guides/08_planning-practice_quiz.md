# Practice Quiz: Healthy Habits

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is Planning?
    A) Thinking about the past
    B) Thinking about what to do next to reach a goal
    C) Sleeping
    D) Crying

    **Answer: B!** Great job! Planning is using your brain to think ahead and figure out the best steps to reach your goal.

2. Why do we sleep?
    A) To be boring
    B) To recharge our Brain and Body batteries
    C) To hide from the sun
    D) Because we have to

    **Answer: B!** That's right! Sleep is how your body and brain recharge. Without it, your ship runs out of energy!

3. Which is a Healthy Habit?
    A) Eating only candy
    B) Never washing hands
    C) Brushing teeth every night
    D) Watching TV for 24 hours

    **Answer: C!** Exactly! Brushing your teeth every night keeps them strong and healthy. That is a great plan for the future!

4. Who is the "Future You"?
    A) A stranger
    B) You in a minute, an hour, or a year
    C) Your shadow
    D) A ghost

    **Answer: B!** Nice work! The Future You is still YOU, just later. Taking care of yourself now is a gift to your future self.

5. If your goal is "Strong Muscles," what is the best plan?
    A) Sit on the couch
    B) Exercise and play outside
    C) Eat cookies
    D) Wish for magic

    **Answer: B!** You got it! Exercise is the action that helps your muscles grow strong. Good planning means choosing actions that match your goal.

6. Why is breakfast important?
    A) It gives fuel for the school day
    B) It tastes yucky
    C) It makes you sleepy
    D) No reason

    **Answer: A!** That's right! Breakfast is fuel for your body, like putting gas in a car. Without it, your brain and body run on empty!

7. If you make a plan and it doesn't work, what should you do?
    A) Give up
    B) Make a NEW plan (Update the Model)
    C) Cry
    D) Break something

    **Answer: B!** Exactly! Good planners do not give up. They update their model and try a different path, just like a robot re-plans!

8. What happens if Captain Brain chooses Plan A: eat only candy and stay up all night?
    A) You feel amazing the next day
    B) You feel sick and tired the next day
    C) Nothing happens
    D) You turn into a superhero

    **Answer: B!** That's right! Bad planning leads to bad results. Eating only candy and skipping sleep makes you feel awful.

9. The Strawberry Experiment says: if you eat the strawberry NOW, you get 1. If you plant the seeds and wait, you get...
    A) Zero strawberries
    B) 10 strawberries later
    C) A pumpkin
    D) A pickle

    **Answer: B!** Smart thinking! Planning means sometimes waiting for a bigger reward instead of grabbing the small one right now.

10. What is the best way to take care of "Future You"?
    A) Ignore the future
    B) Make healthy choices today like eating well, sleeping, and exercising
    C) Only think about today
    D) Worry about everything

    **Answer: B!** Awesome! Every healthy choice you make today is a gift to the person you will be tomorrow. That is the power of planning!
