# Lab: The Future Me Poster

## Goal

To visualize your Future Self and plan the habits you need to get there.

## What You Need

* A big piece of paper.
* Crayons or markers.
* Old magazines (optional, for cutting out pictures).

## Steps

### 1. The Dream

Close your eyes. Imagine you are 20 years old.

* Are you an astronaut? A doctor? A dancer?
* Are you happy? Strong? Kind?

### 2. The Poster

Draw that person in the middle of the paper.
Write "FUTURE ME" at the top.

### 3. The Plan

Around the drawing, write or draw the things you need to do to get there.

* "Eat Veggies" (to be strong).
* "Read Books" (to be smart).
* "Be Nice" (to have friends).
