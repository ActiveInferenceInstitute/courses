Lab: The Future Me Poster

Goal

Imagine who you want to be when you grow up and plan the healthy habits that will get you there!

What You Need

  • A big piece of paper (poster size is best!)
  • Crayons, markers, or colored pencils
  • Old magazines for cutting out pictures (optional)
  • Tape or glue stick

Steps

1. Close Your Eyes and Dream

Close your eyes. Imagine you are 20 years old. Think about these questions:

  • What job do you have? (Astronaut? Doctor? Artist? Teacher?)
  • What do you look like? (Strong? Happy? Kind?)
  • What cool things can you do?

2. Draw Future You

In the middle of the poster, draw a big picture of "Future You." Make it colorful! Write "FUTURE ME" at the top in big letters.

3. Add Your Habit Plan

Around your drawing, write or draw the healthy habits you need to do TODAY to become that person:

  • "Eat Veggies" (to be strong) -- draw a carrot
  • "Read Books" (to be smart) -- draw a book
  • "Exercise" (to be fast) -- draw a pair of running shoes
  • "Sleep Well" (to recharge) -- draw a bed with ZZZ
  • "Be Kind" (to have friends) -- draw a heart

4. Make a Habit Tracker

At the bottom of the poster, make a small chart to track one habit this week.
