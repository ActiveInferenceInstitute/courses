1. What is a Budget?

    B) A plan for your numbers (Money/Tokens)

2. If you want a toy that costs 5 tokens, and you have 3, what do you need?
    A) 2 more tokens
    B) To cry
    C) 100 more tokens
    D) A sandwich

3. What happens if you spend your tokens on candy now?
    A) You can still buy the big toy later
    B) The tokens are gone, and you can't buy the toy
    C) The tokens come back by magic
    D) Nothing

4. Why is Saving hard?
    A) Because we want things NOW
    B) Because math is easier
    C) Because pockets are small
    D) It is easy

5. How can you earn tokens?
    A) By doing Actions (Work, Chores)
    B) By sleeping
    C) By watching TV
    D) By eating

6. Is Planning about the Past or the Future?
    A) Past
    B) Future
    C) Yesterday
    D) Last year

7. Who uses Planning?
    A) Only parents
    B) Only banks
    C) Everyone! (Even squirrels)
    D) Nobody
