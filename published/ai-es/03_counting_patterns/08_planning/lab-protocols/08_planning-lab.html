Lab: The Token Shop

Goal

To practice Earning and Spending to reach a Goal.

What You Need

  • 10 items (Paper clips, beans) to be "Tokens".
  • 3 Toys or Treats (Price them: 2, 5, and 10 tokens).

Steps

1. The Menu

Set up your store.

  • Small Treat: 2 Tokens.
  • Medium Toy: 5 Tokens.
  • Big Prize: 10 Tokens.

2. The Bank

You start with 0 Tokens. How do you get them? Action Menu:

  • Do 10 Jumping Jacks = 1 Token.
  • Clean up toys = 2 Tokens.
  • sing a song = 1 Token.

3. The Choice

Start working! Earn your tokens. When you have 2, do you spend them? Or save for the Big Prize?

What We Learned

You have to Work (Action) to earn numbers. You have to Plan (Save) to get the big things you want!
