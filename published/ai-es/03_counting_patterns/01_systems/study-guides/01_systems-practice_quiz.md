# Practice Quiz: Kingdom of Numbers

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What number comes after 4?
    A) 2
    B) 5
    C) 100
    D) Blue

2. What is a System?
    A) A mess
    B) Parts working together with rules
    C) A broken toy
    D) A sleepy cat

3. What does ZERO (0) mean?
    A) A lot
    B) Empty or None
    C) Ten
    D) A circle

4. Why do we count?
    A) To know "How Many"
    B) To make noise
    C) To be confusing
    D) To jump high

5. Which is a SET?
    A) One shoe
    B) A box of 12 crayons
    C) A cloud
    D) Air

6. If you sort buttons by COLOR, what is the Rule?
    A) Big ones here, small ones there
    B) Red ones here, Blue ones there
    C) Throw them
    D) Eat them

7. Can numbers change their order? (Does 5 ever come before 1?)
    A) Yes, on Tuesdays
    B) No, the System stays the same
    C) Only in space
    D) If you ask nicely
