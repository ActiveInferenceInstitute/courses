Lab: Guess How Many?

Goal

To practice Estimation (guessing close numbers) and compare it to Counting.

What You Need

  • A clear jar or cup.
  • Small items (Beans, Buttons, or Cereal).
  • Paper and pencil.

Steps

1. The Setup

Ask a friend to put a handful of beans in the jar. Don't count them yet!

2. The Estimate

Look at the jar. Think about it. Write down your Estimate: "I think there are ____ beans."

3. The Count

Pour them out. Count them exactly (1, 2, 3...). Write down the Real Number.

4. The Difference

How close were you?

  • Real Number - Estimate = Difference.
  • If you guessed 20 and there were 22, the difference is 2. You are a Super Estimator!

What We Learned

Estimation is faster than counting, but less exact. It's a great tool for making quick decisions!
