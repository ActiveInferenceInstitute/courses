# Course — Module 04 — Think About It

1. If I hold up a handful of sand, can you count the grains? (No! You estimate.)
2. How many people are in a stadium? (Estimation helps here!)
3. Why is "About 10" a useful answer?
4. If you flip a coin, can you be 100% sure it will be Heads?
5. What is "Luck"? (Just random chance!)
6. How many bites does it take to eat an apple? (Guess first, then eat!)
7. Is it better to count slowly or estimate quickly? (Depends on the Goal!)
8. Which is more: A bucket of bouncy balls or a bucket of peas?
9. If you roll a dice, what number might you get? (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6).
10. Can you guess what your friend is thinking of (a number)?
