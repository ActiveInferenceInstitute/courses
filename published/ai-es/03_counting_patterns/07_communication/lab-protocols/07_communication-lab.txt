Lab: Symbol Factory

Goal

To Create your own number system (Symbols) and use them to communicate.

What You Need

 Paper and markers.
 A partner.

Steps

The Design

Invent new symbols for numbers 1, 2, and 3.

 Example:
   1 = $\star$ (Star)
   2 = $\Delta$ (Triangle)
   3 = $\square$ (Box)

The Message

Write a math problem using YOUR symbols.

$\star + \Delta = ?$

The Decode

Give it to your partner. Can they solve it?
They have to use the "Key" (The list you made in Step 1).
Answer: $\star (1) + \Delta (2) = \square (3)$!

What We Learned

Symbols only work if everyone agrees on what they mean. That is why we all learn the same numbers in school!
