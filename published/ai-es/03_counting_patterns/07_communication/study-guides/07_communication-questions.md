# Course — Module 07 — Think About It

1. Why do we write numbers instead of drawing lines (|||||)? (It's faster to write "5"!)
2. What does a Red Light mean? (Stop! It's a symbol).
3. If you write a letter to a friend, are you Communicating?
4. Can animals read numbers?
5. What happens if you write a number messy and it looks like a different one? (Confusion!)
6. Who invented numbers? (Ancient people a long time ago).
7. Do you know any numbers in another language? (Uno, Dos, Tres...)
8. Why do clocks use numbers?
9. Is music written down with symbols? (Yes, notes!).
10. If you found a treasure map with "X" on it, what does X mean?
