Practice Quiz: The Counter

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. Who is the Agent? A) The Number 5 B) You (The Counter) C) The Pencil D) The Floor

  2. What is the "One-to-One" rule? A) Touch one thing, say one number B) Touch two things, say one number C) Count without touching D) Don't count

  3. If you have 5 cookies and eat 1, how many are left? A) 5 B) 4 C) 100 D) Zero

  4. What does the LAST number you say tell you? A) Nothing B) How many are in the whole group (Cardinality) C) The name of the group D) What time it is

  5. Why do Agents count? A) To solve problems (like missing cookies) B) To be confusing C) To make the number angry D) No reason

  6. Can you count sounds (like claps)? A) No, you can't see them B) Yes! (1 clap, 2 claps...) C) Only if they are loud D) Start over

  7. If you skip the number 3 (1, 2, 4, 5), is the answer right? A) Yes B) No, the System is broken! C) Maybe D) Skip 4 too

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  1. Count how many chairs are in your room. Write the number.
  2. Draw 4 apples. Cross one out. How many are left?
  3. If you were a King or Queen, what would you count? (Gold? Dragons?)
