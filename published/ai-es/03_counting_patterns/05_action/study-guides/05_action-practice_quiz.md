# Practice Quiz: Moving Math

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is "Moving Math"?
    A) Doing homework on a bus
    B) Using your body to count (Steps, Jumps)
    C) Dancing with a calculator
    D) Sleeping

2. If the Commander says "Jump 3 times," and you jump 4 times...
    A) You did it perfectly
    B) You miscounted (Surprise!)
    C) You are a kangaroo
    D) Nothing happened

3. How can you measure distance with your body?
    A) Use your nose
    B) Count your Steps (Pacing)
    C) Guess
    D) Ask a bird

4. Which shape can you make with your arms?
    A) A Circle (Big hug shape)
    B) A real car
    C) A dinosaur
    D) A cloud

5. Why do dancers count "1, 2, 3, 4"?
    A) To stay together in time
    B) Because they like numbers only
    C) To be loud
    D) They don't

6. If you act out a Number Pattern (Jump, Clap, Jump, Clap), what comes next?
    A) Sit
    B) Jump
    C) Sleep
    D) Eat

7. Is "Stop" an Action?
    A) No
    B) Yes! (It is the action of not moving)
    C) Only for cars
    D) Maybe

## Part B: Tell Us What You Think

1. Stand up. Take 5 steps forward. Did you count them?
2. Make a shape with your fingers (Triangle, Heart, Square).
3. Try to clap as fast as you can for 10 seconds. Was it a lot of claps?
