Practice Quiz: Robot Talk

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is the language of computers? A) English B) Binary (0s and 1s) C) Robot-speak D) French

  2. What does "0" usually mean to a computer? A) ON B) OFF C) Hungry D) Hello

  3. How do wireless robots talk? A) They shout B) They use invisible waves (Radio/Wi-Fi) C) They use long strings D) They don't

  4. What is a Swarm? A) A type of bee B) A group of robots working together C) A broken robot D) A sandwich

  5. Can a robot talk to a satellite in space? A) Yes, using powerful signals B) No, space is too far C) Only if it jumps high D) Maybe

  6. Which is a communication device? A) A Hammer B) A Walkie-Talkie C) A Shoe D) A Rock

  7. If two robots are talking and the Wi-Fi turns off, what happens? A) They keep talking B) They lose connection (Cannot talk) C) They explode D) Nothing

  1. Write your name. Now pretend you are a robot and write it in "Beeps" (Beep-Bop-Beep).
  2. Why is it good for robots to talk to each other? (Teamwork!).
