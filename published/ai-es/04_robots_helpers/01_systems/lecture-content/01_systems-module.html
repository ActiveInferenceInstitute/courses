Module 01: Systems — The Robot Factory

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand that a Robot is a System made of many parts.
  2. Learn the 3 main parts: Sensors (Eyes), Computer (Brain), and Actuators (Muscles).
  3. Know that the parts must work together or the robot won't work.

Introduction: The Robot Factory

Welcome to the Robot Factory! We are building a machine helper. Is a robot just a piece of metal? No! It is a System.

  • It needs Batters to have energy.
  • It needs Wires to send messages.
  • It needs Code to know what to do.

If you take out the battery, does the robot work? No! The System is broken. All parts must work as a team.

Key Concepts

1. Inputs (Sensors)

Robots need to see the world. They use Sensors (Cameras, Microphones, Bumpers). This is like your Eyes and Ears. It brings information IN.

2. Processing (The Computer)

Inside the robot is a computer chip. It thinks: "I see a wall. I should stop." This is the Controller.

3. Outputs (Actuators)

Robots need to move. They use Motors (Wheels, Arms, Grippers). This sends action OUT.

Activities

Activity 1: Be the Robot

Play with a partner.

  • Part 1: Use your "Sensors" (Close your eyes. Partner taps your shoulder).
  • Part 2: Use your "Computer" (Think: A tap means Turn Left).
  • Part 3: Use your "Actuators" (Turn your body left). You are a human system acting like a robot!

Activity 2: Design a Bot

Draw a robot on paper. Label its parts:

  • Where are the Sensors? (Eyes?)
  • Where is the Computer? (Head?)
  • Where are the Actuators? (Wheels? Legs?)
  • Where is the Power? (Battery pack?)

Summary

A robot is a special system that can Sense, Think, and Act. It is efficient because all its parts (Electronic and Mechanical) work together perfectly!
