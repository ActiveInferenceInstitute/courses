Course — Module 03 — Think About It

Can a robot taste ice cream? (Chemical sensors exist, but can they enjoy it?)
Do robots need glasses? (They need focus lenses!).
Can a robot see in the dark? (Yes! Better than us).
If a robot's camera is dirty, what happens? (Perception fails).
How does an automatic door know you are there? (Motion sensor).
Can a car see the road? (Self-driving cars use Lidar and Cameras).
Do robots feel pain? (They have touch sensors, but it doesn't "hurt").
Why do smoke alarms make a loud noise? (To tell humans the sensor found smoke).
Can a robot hear a dog whistle? (Yes, if the microphone is good enough).
Which sensor is most important for a robot walking? (Balance/Gyroscope).
