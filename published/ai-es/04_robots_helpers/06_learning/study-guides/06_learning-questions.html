Course — Module 06 — Think About It

  1. Does a robot go to High School? (No, it learns in a lab).
  2. Can a robot learn to play piano? (Yes, by practicing!).
  3. Do robots forget things? (Not usually, unless their hard drive breaks).
  4. Why does it take so long to teach a robot? (It needs thousands of examples).
  5. Can a robot learn from another robot? (Yes! They can share data).
  6. If a robot makes a mistake, is it embarrassed? (No, simple robotics have no feelings).
  7. How do self-driving cars learn? (By driving millions of miles on virtual roads).
  8. Can you teach a robot to lie? (If you show it that lying is the "Right" answer, yes. That's why we must be careful!).
  9. Is the internet a robot teacher? (Robots scan the internet to learn).
  10. What is better: A robot that knows everything, or a robot that can learn new things?
