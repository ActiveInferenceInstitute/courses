Practice Quiz: Smart Robots

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. How do new robots (AI) learn? A) By reading a book B) By looking at examples (Training Data) C) By sleeping D) By eating computer chips

  2. If a robot falls down, what should it do? A) Give up B) Learn from the mistake and try again C) Cry D) Dig a hole

  3. What is "Training Data"? A) Robot gym clothes B) Pictures and information used to teach the robot C) A train set D) Nothing

  4. How do you teach a robot to know a Cat? A) Show it a dog B) Show it thousands of cat pictures C) Tell it "Meow" D) Give it milk

  5. Why is feedback (Good Job/Bad Job) important? A) It helps the robot know if it is right B) It makes the robot happy C) It is loud D) It isn't

  6. Can robots get smarter? A) No, they stay the same forever B) Yes, with updates and learning C) Only if they go to college D) Maybe

  7. What is faster to train? A) A Human (Takes 18 years!) B) A Robot (Can look at 1,000,000 pictures in an hour!) C) A Turtle D) A Rock
