Practice Quiz: Learning (Fiber & Flow)

Part A: Multiple Choice

  1. Gauge swatching in Active Inference terms is: A) A waste of yarn B) Active learning — deliberately generating sensory evidence to update the generative model C) Only necessary for beginners D) A purely aesthetic exercise

  2. Yarn substitution skill develops because: A) All yarns are interchangeable B) The crocheter builds comparative generative models across many fiber encounters C) Patterns always specify alternative yarns D) Substitution does not require any material knowledge

  3. An experienced crocheter's mental library of materials functions as: A) A fixed, unchanging database B) A Bayesian prior that shapes predictions about new yarns C) Something only available in books D) A list of yarn brand preferences

  4. Large prediction errors from failed projects contribute to learning by: A) Discouraging future crochet attempts B) Producing the most significant model updates C) Having no effect on the crocheter's knowledge D) Only teaching about what not to buy

  5. Social learning in crochet circles accelerates model building by: A) Replacing the need for personal experience entirely B) Providing vicarious prediction errors from others' experiences C) Being unreliable and useless D) Only working for beginners

  6. The transition from beginner to expert crocheter involves: A) No change in the generative model B) Building increasingly deep and accurate material models through accumulated experience C) Simply crocheting faster D) Only learning more stitch patterns

  7. Keeping a yarn journal supports learning by: A) Being purely decorative B) Extending the internal generative model with external records C) Replacing the need to swatch D) Being required by law
