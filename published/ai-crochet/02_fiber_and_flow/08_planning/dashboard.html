Key Concepts

Palette Design as Expected Free Energy +

Choosing colors for a project is minimizing expected surprise in the finished fabric.

The crocheter imagines the finished piece and selects colors that minimize the gap between vision and reality. Each color choice is a prediction about how the palette will look worked up.
Relevance to Active Inference

Dye Lot Management +

Buying enough yarn from the same dye lot is planning to prevent future prediction error.

A dye lot mismatch mid-project creates prediction error in the fabric. Planning enough yarn from one lot is a preemptive action to minimize future surprise.
Relevance to Active Inference

Project-Yarn Matching +

Matching yarn properties to project requirements is planning under a generative model.

A baby blanket needs softness and washability. A market bag needs strength and structure. The crocheter's generative model of yarn properties guides the match.
Relevance to Active Inference

Iterative Planning Cycle +

Swatch, evaluate, revise — planning in crochet is an iterative active inference loop.

The crocheter swatches, evaluates the result against their vision, revises the plan (hook size, yarn choice, stitch pattern), and swatches again. This is active inference applied to planning.
Relevance to Active Inference
