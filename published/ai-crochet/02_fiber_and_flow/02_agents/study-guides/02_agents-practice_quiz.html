Part A: Multiple Choice

  6. Crafters describe yarn as having "personality" because: A) Yarn is a living organism B) Different materials participate differently in the agent-material dialogue C) It is a superstition with no basis in experience D) All yarn behaves identically

  7. The nested hierarchy of agents in crochet means: A) Only the brain makes decisions about stitches B) Fingertips, hands, arms, and the whole person each operate as agents at different scales C) The hook is the only true agent D) There is only one level of control in the body
