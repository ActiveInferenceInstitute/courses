Lab: Counting, Measuring, and Categorizing Data -- Data Investigation

Objective

Learning Goal: Use data collection and analysis to explore the concept of number systems through probability and pattern-finding.

This is a guided worksheet designed for middle school students (Grades 6-8).

Materials Needed

  • Pencil and worksheet
  • Calculator (optional)
  • Graph paper or blank paper
  • Dice, coins, or random number generator (optional)
  • Lab journal or notebook
