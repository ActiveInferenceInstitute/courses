Write a 400-word response defending the Active Inference interpretation. Include at least three empirical findings that distinguish predictions from commands, and explain why the distinction matters for understanding motor disorders.

2. Design an experiment that could distinguish between the "predictions not commands" framework and the classical motor command framework.
