Part A: Multiple Choice

1. A probability distribution must satisfy: A) All values are between -1 and 1 B) All probabilities are between 0 and 1, and they sum/integrate to 1 C) At least one value equals 1 D) Values can be negative

2. Bayes' theorem states P(s | o) =: A) P(o | s) + P(s) / P(o) B) P(o | s) × P(s) / P(o) C) P(s) / P(o | s) D) P(o) × P(s)
