Part 2: The Prediction-Error Cycle in Detail

Learning Goal: Trace the predict-compare-update cycle through a detailed scenario.

Scenario: You walk into a coffee shop expecting to order your regular drink, but the barista tells you they're out of oat milk.

Trace the steps:

  1. What was your prediction?
  2. What was the prediction error?
  3. What are your options: (a) update your beliefs, (b) act to change the situation? List specific examples of each.
  4. Which option do you choose, and why?

Part 3: The Self-Model

Learning Goal: Explore how the self-model shapes experience.

Instructions: Try the following brief exercise and reflect:

  1. Close your eyes and point to your right knee. How did you know where it was without looking? (This is proprioception — your body's self-model.)
  2. Now, recall a time you felt a "phantom" phone vibration — your phone wasn't actually buzzing. What does this tell you about your self-model's predictions?
  3. What would change in your daily experience if your self-model were disrupted?
