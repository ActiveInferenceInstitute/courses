Lab: Cognition and Belief Optimization

Learning Goal: Analyze how attention, biases, and belief updating shape cognitive experience.

Part 1: Attention as Precision

Exercise: For each scenario, identify what is being attended to, what precision is being increased, and what is being suppressed.

  1. A chess player concentrating on the board during a tournament in a noisy room
  2. A lifeguard scanning a crowded swimming pool
  3. A student scrolling social media during a lecture

For each, describe: What information has high precision? What has low precision? What are the consequences?

Part 2: Cognitive Biases in Action

Learning Goal: Identify cognitive biases in everyday reasoning and explain them using Active Inference.

Exercise: For each bias, give a real-life example, then explain it using terms from this module (precision, priors, prediction error, free energy).

  1. Confirmation bias: When has seeking confirming evidence led you (or someone you know) astray?
  2. Anchoring effect: Think of a negotiation or price comparison. How does the first number set the prior?
  3. Sunk cost fallacy: Why do people continue with a failing plan rather than switching? How does this relate to model commitment?

Part 3: The Dark Room Thought Experiment

Learning Goal: Engage critically with the Dark Room problem.

Discussion: Imagine an organism that truly only minimized surprise. Would it:

  1. Eat? Why or why not?
  2. Explore? Why or why not?
  3. Have any preferences? Why or why not?

Then explain: How do preferences (prior expectations about desirable states) solve the Dark Room problem?

Part 4: Clinical Application

Learning Goal: Apply the framework to understand cognitive differences.

Case study: Alex is a 19-year-old college student who has recently been diagnosed with generalized anxiety disorder. Alex constantly expects the worst: failing exams, losing friends, getting sick. Even when things go well, Alex finds it hard to update these negative expectations.

Use the Active Inference framework to analyze:

  1. What is happening with Alex's precision weighting?
  2. Why is Alex unable to update beliefs based on positive evidence?
