Key Concepts

Closed system +

Exchanges no matter or energy with the environment (very rare — almost nothing is truly closed). A sealed thermos is approximately closed.

Explore how Closed system connects to the other concepts in this module through the lecture and lab materials.

Centrality to module

Open system +

Exchanges both matter and energy with the environment. All living things are open systems — they need food, oxygen, and waste removal to survive.

Explore how Open system connects to the other concepts in this module through the lecture and lab materials.

Active states +

How the inside affects the boundary (e.g., your muscles moving your body, a cell's flagella propelling it)
