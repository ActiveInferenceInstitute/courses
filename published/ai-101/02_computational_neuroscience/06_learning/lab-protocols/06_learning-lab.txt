Part 3: Hippocampal Memory Consolidation

> Learning Goal: Understand the hippocampal-cortical consolidation process.

Scenario: You visit a new city for the first time.

During the day: What does the hippocampus encode? (events, spatial layout, emotional associations)
That night during sleep: What is replayed during sharp-wave ripples?
Over subsequent weeks: How does the memory transfer from hippocampus to cortex?
After a year: Can you recall the same details? What has been lost vs. retained? Why?

Part 4: STDP and Prediction

> Learning Goal: Connect spike-timing plasticity to prediction at the synaptic level.

Thought experiment: Neuron A fires 10ms before Neuron B, repeatedly.

What happens to the A→B synapse? (LTP or LTD?)
Now reverse: B fires 10ms before A. What happens to the A→B synapse?
Why does this rule make the synapse a predictor? What is A "predicting"?
How might this simple rule, operating across billions of synapses, implement a generative model?
