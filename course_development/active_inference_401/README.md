## Overview

Welcome to **Active Inference 401** — an advanced seminar for researchers pushing the boundaries of the Free Energy Principle and Active Inference. This curriculum engages with the primary literature, open research questions, and cutting-edge methodological challenges.

Expect rigorous mathematical formalism, critical analysis of foundational assumptions, and engagement with active debates in the field.

## Design Principles

1. **Research engagement**: Every module connects to active research questions
2. **Primary literature**: Content is grounded in the original papers, not summaries
3. **Critical analysis**: Students are encouraged to question foundational assumptions
4. **Methodological rigor**: Labs involve real data analysis, formal proofs, or research design
5. **Interdisciplinary breadth**: Philosophy, neuroscience, mathematics, and methodology in dialogue
