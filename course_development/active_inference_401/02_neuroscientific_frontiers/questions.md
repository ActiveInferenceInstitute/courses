# Study Questions: Neuroscientific Frontiers

1. How does the cortical hierarchy implement a hierarchical generative model? Map specific anatomical structures (V1→V2→V4→IT, A1→STG→STS) to levels of the inference hierarchy.

2. How does the canonical microcircuit (Bastos et al., 2012) provide a unifying neural architecture for predictive coding across cortical regions?

3. How do the four major neuromodulatory systems (dopamine, norepinephrine, acetylcholine, serotonin) each implement distinct aspects of precision weighting in Active Inference?

4. Compare the oscillatory signatures of prediction errors (gamma), predictions (beta), and precision modulation (alpha). What empirical evidence supports this mapping, and where does it break down?

5. How does the default mode network (DMN) implement self-modeling? Explain the functional contributions of mPFC, PCC, TPJ, hippocampus, and precuneus to the hierarchical self-model.

6. How does interoceptive inference ground emotional experience? Trace the pathway from peripheral signals to anterior insula to subjective feeling, and explain how interoceptive prediction errors produce anxiety, depression, or depersonalization.

7. Compare disruptions of the self-model across clinical conditions: depersonalization, Cotard's syndrome, phantom limb, anosognosia, and psychedelic-induced ego dissolution. What common inferential mechanism underlies these distinct phenomena?

8. How does Active Inference reconceptualize motor control as proprioceptive prediction error minimization rather than descending motor commands? What evidence from the stretch reflex, cerebellum, and basal ganglia supports this view?

9. Explain how the basal ganglia implement policy selection through the direct, indirect, and hyperdirect pathways, and how dopaminergic D1/D2 balance controls policy precision.

10. How does the hippocampus contribute to planning through replay, preplay, and prospective coding? What distinguishes these from mere memory retrieval?

11. How does the prefrontal cortex decompose expected free energy into pragmatic value (vmPFC) and epistemic value (anterior insula/frontopolar cortex)? What lesion evidence supports this mapping?

12. How do mirror neurons, interbrain synchronization, and the social brain network (mPFC, TPJ, STS, amygdala, anterior insula) implement social inference as "active inference about active inference"?

13. How do ERP components (N400, P600, MMN) serve as electrophysiological markers of prediction error at different levels of the linguistic hierarchy?

14. How does the Active Inference framework unify computational psychiatry — explaining schizophrenia (aberrant precision), depression (pessimistic priors), anxiety (overestimated volatility), autism (inflexible precision), and addiction (hijacked policy precision) through a common inferential vocabulary?

15. How does sleep implement Bayesian Model Reduction through synaptic homeostasis, sharp-wave ripple replay, and sleep spindle–slow oscillation coupling?

16. Explain how spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP) implements causal inference at the synaptic level, and how this relates to the Dirichlet parameter accumulation in Active Inference.

17. How does the concept of allostatic regulation extend Active Inference beyond homeostasis? What neural circuits implement allostatic prediction, and how does allostatic overload contribute to psychiatric symptoms?

18. Compare the explanatory power of Active Inference in neuroscience with alternative frameworks (classical reinforcement learning, Bayesian brain hypothesis, global neuronal workspace theory). What does Active Inference uniquely explain?

19. How might neuroscientific findings constrain or refine the mathematical formalisms of Active Inference? Give specific examples where neuroscience has driven theoretical revision.

20. Critically evaluate: Does the neuroscientific evidence for Active Inference constitute strong confirmation, or is it consistent with multiple theoretical frameworks? What experiment would decisively favor Active Inference over its competitors?
