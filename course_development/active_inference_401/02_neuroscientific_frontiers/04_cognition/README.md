## Learning Goals

1. **Analyze** how prefrontal cortex implements hierarchical planning and policy evaluation under Active Inference, distinguishing roles of dorsolateral PFC (policy selection), ventromedial PFC (expected value), and anterior cingulate cortex (precision of policy selection)
2. **Evaluate** the neuroscience of expected free energy decomposition: how the brain separates pragmatic value from epistemic value at the neural level, with reference to fMRI and single-unit recording evidence
3. **Formalize** the role of dopaminergic circuits in policy precision and confidence, connecting phasic dopamine to precision updates over policies and tonic dopamine to motivational vigor
4. **Compare** the Active Inference account of decision-making with reinforcement learning models, identifying specific neural predictions that differentiate the two frameworks

## Prerequisites

- Graduate-level knowledge of prefrontal cortex functional anatomy and decision-making circuits
- Understanding of dopaminergic signaling (phasic vs. tonic, mesolimbic vs. mesocortical pathways)
- Familiarity with expected free energy and its role in policy selection
