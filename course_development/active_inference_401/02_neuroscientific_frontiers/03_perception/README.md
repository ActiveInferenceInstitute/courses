## Learning Goals

1. **Analyze** how neural oscillations implement the distinction between predictions and prediction errors: evaluate the gamma-for-errors (ascending) and beta-for-predictions (descending) hypothesis with reference to MEG and intracranial evidence
2. **Evaluate** precision weighting as gain modulation of prediction error units, connecting alpha/beta desynchronization to attentional precision enhancement and examining neuromodulatory mechanisms
3. **Derive** the mathematical relationship between oscillatory phase-amplitude coupling and hierarchical message passing in predictive coding networks
4. **Design** an electrophysiological experiment that could disambiguate predictive coding from alternative accounts of oscillatory dynamics

## Prerequisites

- Graduate-level neurophysiology (oscillatory dynamics, EEG/MEG signal generation, spectral analysis)
- Understanding of canonical microcircuit architecture and laminar specificity of oscillatory generators
- Competence in time-frequency analysis and cross-frequency coupling methods
