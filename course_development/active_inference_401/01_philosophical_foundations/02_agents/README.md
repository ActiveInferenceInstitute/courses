## Learning Goals

1. **Analyze** the self-model in Active Inference as a generative model of one's own states (interoceptive, proprioceptive, narrative) and relate it to Hume's bundle theory, Metzinger's self-model theory, and Buddhist anatta
2. **Evaluate** the FEP's position on the hard problem of consciousness, distinguishing what it explains (the structure of experience) from what it does not (phenomenal consciousness per se)
3. **Formalize** agential minimalism -- the graduated ontology from mere physical system to self-conscious agent -- and assess the triviality objection
4. **Reconstruct** the compatibilist account of free will arising from Active Inference, where autonomy is defined as the degree to which internal dynamics are driven by the organism's own generative model

## Prerequisites

- Graduate-level philosophy of mind (consciousness, intentionality, personal identity)
- Familiarity with the Markov blanket formalism and the particular partition
- Knowledge of the variational free energy functional and its decomposition
