## Part 2: The Hard Problem Debate

> **Learning Goal:** Evaluate competing positions on consciousness within the FEP framework.

**Exercise**: Consider three positions:

**Position A (Optimist)**: "Active Inference will eventually explain consciousness because phenomenal experience just *is* the self-model in action."

**Position B (Pessimist)**: "Active Inference explains the *structure* of experience but can never explain why there is something it's like. The hard problem remains."

**Position C (Deflationist)**: "The hard problem is a pseudo-problem arising from confused concepts. Active Inference dissolves it by showing that 'experience' just means 'inference.'"

Write 150 words defending each position, then state which you find most compelling.
