## Learning Goals

1. **Situate** the Free Energy Principle within the Western epistemological tradition, from Cartesian dualism through Kantian constructivism to Bayesian epistemology
2. **Analyze** the FEP as a process epistemology in which knowledge is formalized as ongoing variational free energy minimization rather than static justified true belief
3. **Critically evaluate** the ontological status of Markov blankets as system-environment boundaries, distinguishing realist, constructivist, and structural realist interpretations
4. **Formalize** the self-evidencing circularity problem and connect it to Quine's naturalized epistemology and the hermeneutic circle

## Prerequisites

- Graduate-level epistemology (rationalism, empiricism, Bayesian epistemology)
- Formal probability theory (Bayes' theorem, conditional independence, graphical models)
- Familiarity with the FEP at the level of Parr, Pezzulo, & Friston (2022), chapters 1--4
