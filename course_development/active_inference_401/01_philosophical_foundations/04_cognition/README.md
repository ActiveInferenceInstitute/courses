## Learning Goals

1. **Evaluate** the relationship between Active Inference and the 4E cognition framework (Embodied, Embedded, Enacted, Extended), identifying points of convergence and genuine tension
2. **Analyze** the enactivist challenge: determine whether Active Inference honors the enactivist commitment to non-representationalism or covertly reinstates internal representations
3. **Formalize** the extended mind thesis through the Markov blanket formalism, examining whether cognitive processes can extend beyond the neural boundary
4. **Critique** the scope of computational explanation in cognitive science, adjudicating between radical enactivism and computational functionalism through the lens of the FEP

## Prerequisites

- Graduate-level philosophy of cognitive science (representationalism, anti-representationalism, functionalism)
- Understanding of the 4E cognition literature (Varela, Thompson, Clark, Chemero)
- Familiarity with Markov blankets and their role in defining system boundaries
