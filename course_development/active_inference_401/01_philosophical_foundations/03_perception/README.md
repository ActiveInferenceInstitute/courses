## Learning Goals

1. **Connect** Active Inference to the phenomenological tradition: map Husserl's intentionality onto generative models, Heidegger's ready-to-hand/present-at-hand onto prediction error dynamics, and Merleau-Ponty's embodied perception onto sensorimotor coupling
2. **Analyze** the transparency and opacity of perceptual inference as a function of precision-weighted prediction error magnitude
3. **Evaluate** the position of Active Inference between direct realism and indirect realism, articulating the notion of cybernetic realism or enacted directness
4. **Synthesize** Gibson's ecological optics with Active Inference, showing how direct perception is the limit case of efficient predictive processing

## Prerequisites

- Graduate-level phenomenology (Husserl, Heidegger, Merleau-Ponty)
- Understanding of predictive coding hierarchies and precision weighting
- Familiarity with ecological psychology (Gibson) and the affordance concept
