# Syllabus: Philosophical Foundations

> **Active Inference 401: Advanced PhD Seminar** | **Audience**: Advanced PhD students and researchers

## Course Description

Philosophical Foundations explores Active Inference through epistemology, phenomenology, 4e cognition. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn.

## Schedule

| Week | Module | Topic | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | [Systems](./01_systems/module.md) | Non-Equilibrium Steady States and Random Dynamical Systems | Seminar Discussion |
| Week 2 | [Agents](./02_agents/module.md) | The Bayesian Brain and Markov Decision Processes | Seminar Discussion |
| Week 3 | [Perception](./03_perception/module.md) | Predictive Coding and Neural Process Theories | Seminar Discussion |
| Week 4 | [Cognition](./04_cognition/module.md) | Variational Methods and Free Energy Minimization | Seminar Discussion |
| Week 5 | [Action](./05_action/module.md) | Path Integrals and Optimal Control | Seminar Discussion |
| Week 6 | [Learning](./06_learning/module.md) | Bayesian Mechanics and Structure Learning | Seminar Discussion |
| Week 7 | [Communication](./07_communication/module.md) | Collective Intelligence and Cultural Affordances | Seminar Discussion |
| Week 8 | [Planning](./08_planning/module.md) | Open Problems and Research Frontiers | Seminar Discussion |
