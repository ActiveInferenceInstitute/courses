# Course 4: Technology, AI & Active Inference

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](./README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../README.md) | [Resources](../resources/)

## Course Description

This course explores Active Inference through the lens of **computational thinking**. Students engage with the 8 core topics using guided coding lab activities designed for high school learners.

---

## Prerequisites

Courses 1-3.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Describe** the core concepts of Active Inference in accessible language
2. **Apply** Active Inference concepts to real-world examples
3. **Analyze** systems, agents, and prediction from a computational thinking perspective
4. **Evaluate** the strengths and limitations of the prediction framework
5. **Connect** ideas across modules to build a coherent understanding
