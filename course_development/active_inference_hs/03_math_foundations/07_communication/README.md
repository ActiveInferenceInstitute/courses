## Learning Objectives

1. **Compute** the information content (surprisal) of events using I = -log2(p).
2. **Calculate** the entropy of discrete probability distributions and identify which is more uncertain.
3. **Evaluate** KL divergence between two distributions and verify it is asymmetric.
4. **Apply** mutual information to quantify how much one variable reveals about another.
5. **Connect** surprisal, entropy, and KL divergence to free energy in Active Inference.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Module 06: Learning (prediction error, model updating)
- Logarithms (base 2), probability distributions

## Key Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Surprisal | I = -log2(p): the information content of an event, measured in bits |
| Entropy | H(X): the average surprisal of a distribution; measures uncertainty |
| KL Divergence | KL(P \|\| Q): measures how much distribution P diverges from Q |
| Mutual Information | I(X;Y): how much knowing Y reduces uncertainty about X |
