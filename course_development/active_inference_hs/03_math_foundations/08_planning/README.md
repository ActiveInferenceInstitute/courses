## Learning Objectives

1. **Construct** decision trees for two-step decision problems and evaluate branches by expected value.
2. **Apply** discount factors to compare immediate vs. delayed reward policies.
3. **Compute** a simplified expected free energy score combining pragmatic and epistemic value.
4. **Enumerate** and rank complete policies for a multi-step scenario.
5. **Explain** how Active Inference agents select policies proportional to exp(-G) over expected free energy.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Module 07: Communication (entropy, KL divergence, mutual information)
- Expected value, tree diagrams, basic exponents

## Key Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Decision Tree | A branching diagram showing sequential choices and their probabilistic outcomes |
| Discount Factor | gamma: a value between 0 and 1 that reduces the weight of future rewards |
| Expected Free Energy | G: a score combining expected loss and expected uncertainty over future steps |
| Policy | A complete sequence of planned actions from start to finish |
| Policy Selection | Choosing among policies based on their expected free energy scores |
