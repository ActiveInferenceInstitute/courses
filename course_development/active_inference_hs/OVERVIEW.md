# Active Inference for High School — Curriculum Overview

> **Quick Navigation**: [Everyday Life](./01_everyday_life/) | [Biology & Health](./02_biology_health/) | [Math Foundations](./03_math_foundations/) | [Technology & AI](./04_technology_ai/) | [Shared Resources](./resources/)

## Course Mission

This 4-course curriculum introduces **Active Inference** and the **Free Energy Principle (FEP)** to high school students. It takes learners from everyday intuition to biological understanding, mathematical reasoning, and computational implementation.

The curriculum uses a **Spiral Learning** approach: each of the 4 courses revisits the **same 8 core topics** from a different angle, building deeper understanding with every pass.

---

## 1. The Structure

### Disciplinary Tracks

1. **[Everyday Life](./01_everyday_life/README.md)**: Build the "Why" and "What". Focus on relatable real-world examples and intuition.
    * *Key Output*: Conceptual understanding and everyday applications.
2. **[Biology & Health](./02_biology_health/README.md)**: Explore the "How" in your body. Focus on neurons, homeostasis, and health.
    * *Key Output*: Understanding how prediction works in biological systems.
3. **[Math Foundations](./03_math_foundations/README.md)**: Learn the "Proof". Focus on probability, Bayes' theorem, and optimization.
    * *Key Output*: Mathematical reasoning for prediction and decision-making.
4. **[Technology & AI](./04_technology_ai/README.md)**: Build the "Machine". Focus on Python coding, simulations, and AI agents.
    * *Key Output*: Working code that demonstrates Active Inference principles.

### The 8 Core Topics

All courses follow this sequence:

1. **Systems**: Boundaries, parts, and wholes ([Map](./resources/cross_course_map.md#module-1-systems)).
2. **Agents**: Things that act on their own ([Map](./resources/cross_course_map.md#module-2-agents)).
3. **Perception**: How agents sense the world ([Map](./resources/cross_course_map.md#module-3-perception)).
4. **Cognition**: How agents think and believe ([Map](./resources/cross_course_map.md#module-4-cognition)).
5. **Action**: How agents make decisions and move ([Map](./resources/cross_course_map.md#module-5-action)).
6. **Learning**: How agents improve over time ([Map](./resources/cross_course_map.md#module-6-learning)).
7. **Communication**: How agents share information ([Map](./resources/cross_course_map.md#module-7-communication)).
8. **Planning**: How agents think ahead ([Map](./resources/cross_course_map.md#module-8-planning)).

## 4. For Teachers

This curriculum can be used in multiple ways:

1. **Full Sequence**: Complete all 4 courses over a school year (1 course per quarter).
2. **Single Course**: Use Course 1 as a standalone introduction in any class.
3. **Interdisciplinary**: Assign Courses 1–2 in science, Course 3 in math, Course 4 in CS.
4. **Self-Study**: Students can work through the material independently using the dashboards and self-assessment quizzes.
