## Applications

In High School, we see Communication manifest in:
*   **Specific Example 1**: Neurons communicate across synapses using an Active Inference logic: the presynaptic neuron releases neurotransmitters that the postsynaptic neuron compares against its predicted input level -- if the signal is unexpectedly strong (prediction error), the receiving neuron fires and propagates the surprise upward, which is how your nervous system transmits only the most informative signals rather than flooding the brain with redundant data.
*   **Specific Example 2**: When a cell detects DNA damage, it releases signaling proteins (like p53) that communicate to neighboring cells and the immune system, functioning as a biological broadcast that updates the tissue-level generative model from "all cells healthy" to "potential threat detected" -- triggering coordinated repair or programmed cell death to minimize future organismal surprise.
