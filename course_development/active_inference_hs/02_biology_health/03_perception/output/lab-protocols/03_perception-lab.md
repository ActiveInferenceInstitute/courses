## Part 2: The Predictive Brain Experiment

**Goal**: Experience how your nervous system uses top-down predictions.

**Experiment A -- Sensory adaptation**: Stare at a colored square for 30 seconds, then look at a white surface. Record the afterimage color.
- Why does your brain produce an afterimage? (Hint: neurons adapted to the color reduce their firing rate, and the brain's prediction is now "wrong.")

**Experiment B -- Two-point discrimination**: Using two pencil tips held close together, touch your fingertip, then your forearm. Can you feel two points in both places?

## Part 3: Predictive Coding in the Visual System

**Goal**: Understand how the visual cortex implements predictive coding.

Research the hierarchy of visual processing (V1, V2, V4, IT cortex). Answer:

1. What does the primary visual cortex (V1) detect? (Edges, orientations.)
2. What do higher areas detect? (Shapes, objects, faces.)
3. In predictive coding, higher areas send **predictions** down to lower areas. Lower areas send **prediction errors** up. Draw or describe this flow.
4. How does this explain why you can recognize a friend's face in a crowd almost instantly?
