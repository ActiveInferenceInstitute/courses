## Learning Objectives

1. **Identify** the cell membrane as a biological Markov blanket, naming its sensory components (receptors) and active components (transport proteins).
2. **Explain** how organ systems maintain boundaries that separate internal states from the external environment.
3. **Describe** nested biological systems (organelle, cell, tissue, organ, organism) as hierarchically composed Markov blankets.
4. **Analyze** homeostasis as a biological expression of free energy minimization using specific examples (thermoregulation, blood sugar).
5. **Predict** what happens when a biological boundary fails, connecting membrane damage to disease and system breakdown.

## Prerequisites

## Key Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Cell Membrane | Phospholipid bilayer that acts as the cell's Markov blanket |
| Receptor Proteins | Membrane proteins that serve as sensory states, detecting signals |
| Homeostasis | Active maintenance of preferred physiological conditions |
| Nested Systems | Systems within systems, each with its own boundary |
