Part 2: Chemotaxis -- The Simplest Agent

Goal: Understand bacterial chemotaxis as the most basic form of Active Inference.

E. coli bacteria swim toward nutrients and away from toxins using a process called chemotaxis. Research this process and answer:

What is the bacterium's "preferred state"? (Hint: high nutrient concentration.)
How does it "sense" the environment? (Receptor proteins on its surface.)
What are its two possible actions? (Run straight or tumble randomly.)
How does it "decide" which action to take? (Compare current vs. recent chemical concentration.)
Is this a generative model? The bacterium compares "expected" concentration to "actual" concentration -- what is this comparison called in Active Inference?
