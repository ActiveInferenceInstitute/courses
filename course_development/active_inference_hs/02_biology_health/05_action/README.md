## Learning Objectives

1. **Map** the reflex arc from stimulus to response, identifying each biological structure and its Active Inference role.
2. **Explain** voluntary movement as the fulfillment of proprioceptive predictions by the motor system.
3. **Demonstrate** proprioception and motor learning through safe physical experiments.
4. **Distinguish** reflexive action (spinal cord level) from voluntary action (cortical level) in terms of the prediction hierarchy.
5. **Analyze** movement disorders as disruptions in the brain's motor prediction system.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Module 04: Cognition (Biology & Health)
- Basic knowledge of the nervous system (neurons, spinal cord, brain regions)

## Key Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Reflex Arc | A fast sensory-motor loop processed at the spinal cord level |
| Proprioception | The sense of body position generated by muscle spindles and joint receptors |
| Motor Learning | Updating the cerebellar forward model through practice |
| Active Inference (Motor) | Action as the fulfillment of proprioceptive predictions |
