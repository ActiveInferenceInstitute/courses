## Learning Objectives

1. **Map** prefrontal cortex subdivisions (dorsolateral, ventromedial, orbitofrontal, anterior cingulate) to their specific planning roles.
2. **Assess** executive functions (working memory, inhibition, cognitive flexibility) through self-testing tasks.
3. **Explain** why delayed gratification is biologically harder for teenagers due to ongoing prefrontal cortex development.
4. **Analyze** the marshmallow test experiment as a demonstration of planning under competing drives.
5. **Design** a personal goal plan that accounts for the biological capacities and limitations of the adolescent brain.

## Prerequisites

- Completed all prior Biology & Health modules (01-07)
- Understanding of brain regions and neurotransmitter roles

## Key Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Executive Function | Cognitive capacities (working memory, inhibition, flexibility) implemented by the prefrontal cortex |
| Dorsolateral PFC | Region responsible for working memory and action sequencing |
| Delayed Gratification | Choosing a future reward over an immediate one, requiring forward simulation |
| Goal-Directed Behavior | Action guided by internally maintained goals rather than immediate stimuli |
