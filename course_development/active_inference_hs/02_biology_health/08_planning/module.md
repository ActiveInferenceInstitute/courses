## Applications

In High School, we see Planning manifest in:
*   **Specific Example 1**: Your body plans for future energy needs through the hormone insulin: after a meal, the pancreas predicts rising blood glucose and preemptively releases insulin to shuttle sugar into cells before levels spike dangerously -- this anticipatory regulation is biological planning that minimizes the expected free energy of metabolic imbalance.
*   **Specific Example 2**: When public health officials model the spread of a flu outbreak and pre-position vaccine supplies in regions predicted to be hit hardest, they are performing population-level Active Inference planning -- simulating multiple epidemic trajectories, evaluating each scenario's expected harm (free energy), and selecting the intervention strategy that minimizes predicted surprise across the healthcare system.
