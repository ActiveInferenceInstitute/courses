## Overview

How does a collection of cells become a living, breathing **agent**? Why does your heart beat faster when you're nervous, even before you "think" about the danger?

**Course 2: Biology & Health** explores the physical hardware of Active Inference. We move from the microscopic cell membrane to the complex loops of the human brain. You will learn how your body acts as a prediction machine to maintain stability (homeostasis) and navigate a changing world.

### Course Learning Goals

- **Master the Biological Boundary**: See how cell membranes and organs act as "Markov Blankets" to protect life from entropy.
- **Understand the Predictive Brain**: Explore how neurons carry "surprise" signals and how your cortex builds a mental map of reality.
- **Connect Health to Inference**: Analyze how stress, habits, and social interactions are driven by the brain's goal to minimize prediction error.
