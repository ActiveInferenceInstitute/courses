## Learning Objectives

1. **Define** a system and explain what separates it from its environment (Markov blanket).
2. **Identify** the internal states, sensory states, and active states of everyday systems such as a phone, a school, or a human body.
3. **Explain** homeostasis as a system's drive to maintain preferred states over time.
4. **Compare** systems at different scales (personal, household, community) using boundary-mapping.
5. **Evaluate** what happens when a system's boundary is disrupted or removed.

## Prerequisites

- No prior Active Inference knowledge required
- Basic familiarity with the idea that things can be "inside" or "outside" a boundary

## Key Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| System | A collection of states organized within a boundary |
| Markov Blanket | The boundary layer (sensory + active states) separating internal states from the environment |
| Internal States | Hidden states inside the system that the environment cannot directly observe |
| Homeostasis | The tendency of a system to maintain itself within preferred ranges |
