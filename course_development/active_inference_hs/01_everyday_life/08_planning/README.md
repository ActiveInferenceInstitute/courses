## Learning Objectives

1. **Define** planning as the process of simulating future action sequences using the generative model.
2. **Construct** a simple decision tree for a real upcoming decision, identifying policies and outcomes.
3. **Explain** counterfactual thinking as running the generative model under alternative action sequences.
4. **Distinguish** between risk (known probabilities) and ambiguity (unknown probabilities) in decision-making.
5. **Analyze** how the planning horizon is limited by the accuracy of the generative model.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Modules 01-07 (all prior Everyday Life modules)
- Understanding of policies, expected free energy, and epistemic vs. pragmatic action

## Key Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Planning | Evaluating policies by simulating future outcomes in the generative model |
| Counterfactual Thinking | Imagining what would have happened under a different action sequence |
| Risk | Uncertainty where the probabilities are known |
| Ambiguity | Uncertainty where the probabilities themselves are unknown |
| Planning Horizon | How many time steps into the future the agent can simulate |
