## Part 3: Communication Breakdown Analysis

**Goal**: Analyze a real miscommunication you witnessed or experienced.

Pick a moment where two people misunderstood each other (a text message gone wrong, an argument, a confusing instruction). Answer:

1. What was Person A's internal model of the situation?
2. What was Person B's internal model?
3. Where did the models diverge -- what prediction did each person make about the other?
4. How was the misunderstanding resolved (or not)?

## Part 4: Planning Under Uncertainty

**Goal**: Map out a decision you need to make this week using a simple decision tree.

1. State the decision (e.g., "Should I study for the test tonight or go to my friend's party?").
2. List two or three possible policies (sequences of actions).
3. For each policy, estimate the likely outcomes and how "surprising" each would be relative to your goals.
4. Which policy minimizes expected free energy (balances getting what you want with learning something new)?
