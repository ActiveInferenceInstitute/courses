## Learning Objectives

1. **Define** "generative model" as the brain's internal theory of how the world produces sensory data.
2. **Identify** latent (hidden) states that the brain infers but cannot directly observe in everyday situations.
3. **Explain** the phrase "the map is not the territory" and why mental models are always incomplete.
4. **Recognize** cognitive biases as systematic shortcuts in generative models that can lead to prediction errors.
5. **Apply** Bayesian updating by walking through a real example of changing a belief based on new evidence.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Modules 01-03 (Systems, Agents, Perception)
- Familiarity with the idea that the brain makes predictions

## Key Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Generative Model | Internal representation of how causes in the world produce sensory effects |
| Latent States | Hidden variables inferred by the brain that are not directly observable |
| Cognitive Bias | A systematic shortcut in reasoning that can distort predictions |
| Bayesian Updating | Revising a prior belief toward a posterior belief in light of new evidence |
