# Learning Pathways

Suggested study orders through the Active Inference for High School curriculum, based on your interests and background.

---

## Pathway 1: The Standard Path (Recommended)

Follow all 4 courses in order: Everyday Life → Biology & Health → Math Foundations → Technology & AI. Complete each course before moving to the next.

**Best for**: Most students. Builds from intuition to biology to math to coding.

```text
Life M1-M8 → Bio M1-M8 → Math M1-M8 → Tech M1-M8
```

**Time estimate**: 1 school year (1 course per quarter, ~2 weeks per module).

---

## Pathway 2: The Science Track

Start with Biology & Health alongside Everyday Life, then Math and Tech.

**Best for**: Students in AP Biology or honors science who want the biological grounding early.

```text
Week 1: Life M1 + Bio M1 (Systems)
Week 2: Life M2 + Bio M2 (Agents)
...continue paired through M8...
Then: Math M1-M8 → Tech M1-M8
```

---

## Pathway 3: The Math/CS Track

Start with Everyday Life (quickly), then do Math and Tech in parallel.

**Best for**: Students in AP Calculus, AP CS, or who love math and coding.

```text
Life M1-M8 (accelerated, 4 weeks)
Then paired:
  Math M1 + Tech M1
  Math M2 + Tech M2
  ...through M8...
Then: Bio M1-M8 (fill in biology perspective)
```

---

## Pathway 4: The Humanities Track

Focus on Everyday Life and Communication/Planning modules first, then selectively explore other courses.

**Best for**: Students more interested in psychology, philosophy, and social science.

```text
Life M1-M8 (full course)
Then deep dives:
  Bio M3 (Perception) + Bio M4 (Cognition) + Bio M7 (Communication)
  Math M4 (Bayes' Theorem) — the one essential math module
  Tech M7 (Multi-Agent Messaging) — see how AI "communicates"
```

---

## Pathway 5: Topic Deep Dives

Instead of going course by course, explore one topic across all 4 courses before moving on.

**Best for**: Independent learners and study groups.

```text
Week 1: Systems across all 4 courses
  Life M1 → Bio M1 → Math M1 → Tech M1
Week 2: Agents across all 4 courses
  Life M2 → Bio M2 → Math M2 → Tech M2
...continue through M8...
```

## Module Dependencies

Some modules build on concepts from earlier modules. Here's what you should know before starting each:

| Module | Prerequisite Knowledge |
|--------|----------------------|
| M1: Systems | None |
| M2: Agents | M1 (what a system is) |
| M3: Perception | M1-M2 (systems and agents) |
| M4: Cognition | M3 (how perception works) |
| M5: Action | M3-M4 (perception and cognition) |
| M6: Learning | M5 (how action selection works) |
| M7: Communication | M2 + M3 (agents and perception) |
| M8: Planning | M5 + M6 (action and learning) |

## For Teachers: Suggested Pacing

| Setting | Suggested Approach |
|---------|-------------------|
| Full-year elective | All 4 courses, 1 quarter each |
| Semester elective | Course 1 + Course 2 (or Course 3 or 4) |
| Science class integration | Course 2 (Biology & Health) modules as supplementary readings |
| Math class integration | Course 3 (Math Foundations) modules as application projects |
| CS class integration | Course 4 (Technology & AI) modules as coding projects |
| Independent study | Any pathway above, self-paced with dashboard quizzes |
