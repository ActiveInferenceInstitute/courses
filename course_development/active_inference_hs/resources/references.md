# References

> **Quick Navigation**: [Resources Home](./README.md) | [Glossary](./glossary.md) | [Curriculum Home](../README.md)

Curated references for the Active Inference for High School curriculum. Organized by module topic, with a mix of accessible resources (videos, websites, textbooks) and foundational papers.

---

## Foundational Texts

1. Parr, T., Pezzulo, G., & Friston, K. J. (2022). *Active Inference: The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, and Behavior*. MIT Press. — The definitive textbook, Chapters 1–3 are most relevant for HS.
2. Clark, A. (2016). *Surfing Uncertainty: Prediction, Action, and the Embodied Mind*. Oxford University Press. — Accessible introduction to predictive brains.
3. Seth, A. (2021). *Being You: A New Science of Consciousness*. Dutton. — Popular science treatment of how the brain constructs reality.
4. Friston, K. (2010). The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory? *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 11(2), 127–138.

## Citation Format

When citing in module content, use author-year format:

- Papers: `Author (Year)` or `Author et al. (Year)`
- Books: `Author (Year). *Title*. Publisher.`
- Videos: `📹 *Title* — Channel (Platform).`
