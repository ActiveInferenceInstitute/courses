What happens to EFE when the C-vector is all zeros? Which component vanishes and which remains?

Write the policy posterior equation $q(\pi)$. What role does the negative sign in front of γ play?

Why is softmax used to convert EFE values to policy probabilities instead of simply picking the minimum $G$?

In runpolicyinference(), what does the return value Gvalues contain? What is its shape?
