Study Questions: Cognition

What does each entry of the C-vector represent, and on what scale is it expressed (linear probability, log probability, or utility)?

Write the formula for the preferred observation distribution $\tilde{P}(o)$ in terms of the C-vector. What function converts C to a valid probability distribution?

If C = np.zeros(numobs), what does the risk term in EFE evaluate to? What type of behavior results?

Construct a C-vector for a 4-observation system where observation 2 is strongly preferred, observations 0 and 1 are neutral, and observation 3 is strongly avoided.

Explain the difference between $D$ (prior over initial states) and $q(s)$ (current posterior). When are they equal?

If $D = [1, 0, 0]$, the agent starts with zero entropy over initial states. What is the computational implication for the first state inference step?

What validation does GenerativeModel perform on the D-vector? Write the two conditions that must hold.

How does the E-vector enter the policy posterior formula? Write the equation for $q(\pi)$ with and without E.

If E = np.array([0.99, 0.005, 0.005]) and γ = 0.01, which policy will the agent almost certainly select? Why?

What happens to policy selection when γ = 0? Does the agent still use EFE, or does it rely entirely on E?

Design an experiment that isolates the effect of the C-vector: keep A, B, D, E, and γ fixed, vary only C, and predict how agent behavior changes.

Explain precision γ in terms of the inverse temperature of a Boltzmann distribution. What is the policy posterior equation in this form?

How would you use plotCpreferences() to verify that your C-vector matches your intended preference structure? What should you look for in the plot?

What does plotDprior() annotate on the bar chart beyond the prior probabilities? Why is this annotation useful?

Write code to create two agents with identical A, B, C, D but different E-vectors, run them in the same environment, and compare their action sequences.

At what value of γ does the policy posterior transition from near-uniform to near-deterministic? How would you determine this experimentally?

Use plotprecision_sweep() to visualize $q(\pi)$ across γ values. Describe the expected shape of the curves.

If C-vector entries are in log-probability, can negative C values occur? What do they mean?

How would you model an agent that prefers to stay in its current state? Which component(s) — C, D, E, or B — would you modify?

Explain why an agent with strong C-preferences and low γ might still act randomly. What is the mathematical mechanism?
