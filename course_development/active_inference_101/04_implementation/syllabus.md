# Syllabus: Implementation & Simulation

> **Active Inference 101: College First Semester** | **Audience**: College 1st semester undergraduates

## Course Description

Implementation & Simulation explores Active Inference through python, pymdp, agent-based modeling. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn.

## Schedule

| Week | Module | Topic | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | [Systems](./01_systems/module.md) | Dynamical Systems and Markov Blankets | Coding Assignment |
| Week 2 | [Agents](./02_agents/module.md) | Autonomous Agents and Generative Models | Coding Assignment |
| Week 3 | [Perception](./03_perception/module.md) | Predictive Processing and Bayesian Inference | Coding Assignment |
| Week 4 | [Cognition](./04_cognition/module.md) | Free Energy and Belief Optimization | Coding Assignment |
| Week 5 | [Action](./05_action/module.md) | Active Inference and Expected Free Energy | Coding Assignment |
| Week 6 | [Learning](./06_learning/module.md) | Structure Learning and Model Reduction | Coding Assignment |
| Week 7 | [Communication](./07_communication/module.md) | Multi-Agent Active Inference | Coding Assignment |
| Week 8 | [Planning](./08_planning/module.md) | Planning as Inference and Deep Models | Coding Assignment |
