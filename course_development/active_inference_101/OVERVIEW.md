# Active Inference 101: College First Semester — Pedagogical Overview

## Vision

This curriculum makes Active Inference accessible to College 1st semester undergraduates through four complementary lenses. Each course covers the same 8 foundational topics but from a different disciplinary perspective.

## Tone & Style

Rigorous but accessible. Full mathematical notation. Python/NumPy. Textbook-quality.
