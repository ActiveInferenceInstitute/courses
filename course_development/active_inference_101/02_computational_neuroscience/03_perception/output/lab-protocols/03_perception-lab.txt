Part 2: MMN Experiment Design

> Learning Goal: Design a mismatch paradigm to test predictive coding predictions.

Exercise: Design a simple oddball experiment for visual processing (not auditory):

What repeated stimulus would you use (the "standard")?
What deviant stimulus would you insert (the "oddball")?
