Part 3: Excitation-Inhibition Balance

> Learning Goal: Understand how populations produce oscillations through E-I balance.

Thought experiment: Imagine a small network of 10 excitatory and 10 inhibitory neurons, all connected to each other.

If only excitatory neurons were present, what would happen? (Hint: think runaway excitation)
If only inhibitory neurons were present, what would happen?
With both, how does the interplay produce oscillations?
What happens when the balance is disrupted? (Connect to epilepsy)
