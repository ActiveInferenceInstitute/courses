Part A: Multiple Choice

1. Hebbian learning states that: A) Neurons that fire together weaken their connections B) Neurons that fire together strengthen their connections (wire together) C) Learning only occurs during sleep D) Only motor neurons can learn

3. Dopamine neurons fire a burst when: A) An expected reward is received on time B) An unexpected reward is received (positive reward prediction error) C) Nothing happens D) The animal is sleeping
