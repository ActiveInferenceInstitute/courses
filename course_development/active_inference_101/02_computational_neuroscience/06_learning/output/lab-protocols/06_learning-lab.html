Lab: Synaptic Plasticity and Learning Circuits

Learning Goal: Trace learning mechanisms from molecular synaptic changes to behavioral outcomes.

Part 1: Hebbian Learning Scenarios

Exercise: For each scenario, identify what neural connections are being strengthened or weakened, and whether this is LTP or LTD:

  2. You stop flinching at a door that used to slam but was fixed
  3. A musician practices a difficult passage until it becomes automatic
  4. A phobia develops after a traumatic experience

Part 3: Hippocampal Memory Consolidation

Learning Goal: Understand the hippocampal-cortical consolidation process.

Scenario: You visit a new city for the first time.

  1. During the day: What does the hippocampus encode? (events, spatial layout, emotional associations)
  2. That night during sleep: What is replayed during sharp-wave ripples?
  3. Over subsequent weeks: How does the memory transfer from hippocampus to cortex?
  4. After a year: Can you recall the same details? What has been lost vs. retained? Why?

Part 4: STDP and Prediction

Learning Goal: Connect spike-timing plasticity to prediction at the synaptic level.

Thought experiment: Neuron A fires 10ms before Neuron B, repeatedly.

  1. What happens to the A→B synapse? (LTP or LTD?)
  2. Now reverse: B fires 10ms before A. What happens to the A→B synapse?
  3. Why does this rule make the synapse a predictor? What is A "predicting"?
  4. How might this simple rule, operating across billions of synapses, implement a generative model?
