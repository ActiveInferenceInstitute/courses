Part 3: Cerebellar Learning Simulation

> Learning Goal: Trace the error-correction circuit in the cerebellum.

Exercise: You're learning to throw darts at a target.

First throw: You aim for center, the dart lands far right. What is the climbing fiber error signal?
How does this error modify the parallel fiber–Purkinje cell synapses?
After 100 throws: What has happened to the prediction error? What has happened to cerebellar predictions?
If you suddenly switch to a heavier dart, what happens? How does the cerebellum adapt?
