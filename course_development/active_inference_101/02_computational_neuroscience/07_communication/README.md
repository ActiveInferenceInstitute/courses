## Neural Bases of Social Inference

Part of **Computational Neuroscience** -- this module maps social cognition onto the social brain network, mirror neurons, and neural synchrony.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

1. **Identify** the brain regions comprising the social brain network (TPJ, mPFC, STS, ACC) and their roles in Theory of Mind
2. **Analyze** mirror neurons as a neural mechanism for action understanding and basic social inference
3. **Evaluate** neural synchrony between communicating individuals as evidence for coupled Active Inference
4. **Synthesize** the role of oxytocin in modulating social precision and its implications for social bonding and social anxiety
5. **Apply** the computational neuroscience of communication to interpret hyperscanning studies of interacting brains

## Prerequisites

- Computational Neuroscience Module 04: Cognition (prefrontal cortex, neuromodulation)
- Cognitive Science Module 07: Communication (shared inference, Theory of Mind)

## Key Concepts

- **Mentalizing network**: TPJ and mPFC regions that support attributing mental states to others
- **Mirror neuron system**: Neurons that fire during both action execution and observation, supporting action prediction
- **Neural synchrony**: Alignment of brain oscillations between interacting individuals
- **Oxytocin**: Neuropeptide that increases the precision of social signals
- **Hyperscanning**: Simultaneous brain recording from multiple interacting people
