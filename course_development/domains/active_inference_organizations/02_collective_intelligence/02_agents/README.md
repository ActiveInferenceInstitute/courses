## Distributed Minds and Collective Intelligence

Part of **Section 2: Collective Intelligence**.

A team's intelligence is not the sum of its members' intelligence — it is an emergent property of how individuals coordinate their inference. This module examines how individual agency gives rise to collective agency, the conditions under which groups outperform individuals, and the pathologies that destroy collective intelligence.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Explain** how collective agency emerges from individual agent coordination
2. **Apply** the conditions for wise crowds: diversity, independence, and aggregation
3. **Distinguish** productive collaboration from groupthink as a matter of precision settings
4. **Design** team roles that distribute the inference task effectively
5. **Build** psychological safety as the foundation of collective intelligence

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Emergence**: Team capabilities that exceed individual capabilities
- **Wisdom of crowds**: When and why groups outperform individuals
- **Psychological safety**: Enabling dissent and diverse perspectives
- **Role design**: Distributing sensing, reasoning, and action across members
- **Groupthink**: When model alignment goes too far

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Section 2, Module 01 (Teams as Systems)
- Familiarity with team collaboration in any professional context

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Google's Project Aristotle** — psychological safety as the key predictor of team performance
- **Wikipedia** — large-scale collective agency through distributed editing and governance
- **Bay of Pigs** — groupthink destroying collective intelligence in elite advisory groups
