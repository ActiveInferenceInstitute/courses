## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Individual agency aggregating into collective intelligence through coordination and model alignment
- **Topics**: Distributed agency, wisdom of crowds, collective inference, team roles, psychological safety
- **Lab Style**: Workshop — collective agency assessment and role design
- **Audience**: Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists, team leads
- **Tone**: Professional, collaborative, team-focused

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module examines how individual agents coordinate to produce collective intelligence greater than the sum of its parts. All content should:

1. **Show emergence of collective agency**: Team intelligence is not the sum of individual IQ — it emerges from coordination, diversity, and psychological safety
2. **Apply conditions for wise crowds**: Diversity of perspective, independence of judgment, decentralized information, and aggregation mechanisms
3. **Distinguish beneficial from pathological group dynamics**: Wisdom of crowds versus groupthink — same phenomenon (model alignment), different precision settings
4. **Map team roles as distributed inference**: Each member contributes specialized sensing, reasoning, or action capacity
5. **Emphasize psychological safety**: Edmondson's research showing that the best teams are those where members feel safe to express dissenting views

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Distributed agency**: Collective intelligence emerging from coordinated individual inference
- **Model alignment**: Shared generative models enabling prediction of teammates' behavior
- **Wisdom of crowds**: Diversity + independence + aggregation as optimal collective inference
- **Psychological safety**: Maintaining appropriate precision on dissenting signals
- **Role specialization**: Distributing the inference task across team members
