# Lab: Collective Intelligence Workshop — Team Dynamics Assessment

## Objective

> **Learning Goal:** Conduct a comprehensive team-level analysis using the Active Inference framework, assessing how your team functions as a collective inference agent — its boundary, shared model, coordination mechanisms, and learning capacity.

This workshop is designed to be run with an actual team (4-10 members). If working individually, select a team you have recently been part of and complete the assessment from memory.

---

## Scenario

Your team has been asked to conduct a self-assessment of its collective intelligence capacity. The goal is to identify the team's strengths and weaknesses as a coordinated inference system and develop specific interventions to improve collective performance.

## Step 2: Shared Mental Model Assessment (15 min)

Have each team member independently answer these questions, then compare answers:

1. What is the team's primary mission? (one sentence)
2. What are the top 3 risks to the team's success?
3. Who is our most important stakeholder?
4. What is our biggest current challenge?

## Step 5: Intervention Design (15 min)

1. **Quick win** (implement this week): A small change to improve one dimension of collective intelligence
2. **Medium-term** (implement this quarter): A structural change to team coordination or communication
3. **Strategic** (implement over 6 months): A fundamental change to how the team learns or manages its boundary
