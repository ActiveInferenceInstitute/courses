# Syllabus: Collective Intelligence

> **Active Inference for Organizations** | **Audience**: Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists

## Course Description

Collective Intelligence explores Active Inference through teams, culture, shared mental models. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn.

## Schedule

| Week | Module | Topic | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | [Systems](./01_systems/module.md) | Organizations as Living Systems | Workshop |
| Week 2 | [Agents](./02_agents/module.md) | Individual and Collective Agency | Workshop |
| Week 3 | [Perception](./03_perception/module.md) | Market Signals and Environmental Scanning | Workshop |
| Week 4 | [Cognition](./04_cognition/module.md) | Organizational Beliefs and Strategy | Workshop |
| Week 5 | [Action](./05_action/module.md) | Strategic Action and Implementation | Workshop |
| Week 6 | [Learning](./06_learning/module.md) | Organizational Learning and Adaptation | Workshop |
| Week 7 | [Communication](./07_communication/module.md) | Internal Communication and Culture | Workshop |
| Week 8 | [Planning](./08_planning/module.md) | Futures Thinking and Organizational Resilience | Workshop |
