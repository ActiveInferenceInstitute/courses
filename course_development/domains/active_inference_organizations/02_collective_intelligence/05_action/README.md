# Module 05: Action — Team Coordination and Collective Action

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## How Groups Act Together

Part of **Section 2: Collective Intelligence**.

Collective action is more than individuals acting in parallel — it is synchronized, interdependent behavior that produces emergent outcomes. This module examines coordination mechanisms, division of labor, stigmergic coordination, and how teams resolve the tension between individual autonomy and collective alignment.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Distinguish** collective action from parallel individual action
2. **Map** explicit and implicit coordination mechanisms in your team
3. **Analyze** division of labor and its coordination costs
4. **Apply** stigmergy as indirect coordination through environmental traces
5. **Balance** individual autonomy with collective alignment in team design

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Synchronized action**: Interdependent team behavior producing emergent outcomes
- **Coordination mechanisms**: Plans, protocols, shared models, and environmental cues
- **Division of labor**: Functional, geographic, and temporal partitioning of tasks
- **Stigmergy**: Coordination through shared work artifacts (kanban boards, code commits, documents)
- **Autonomy-alignment tension**: How much freedom individual agents need vs. how much coherence the team requires

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Section 2, Modules 01-04

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Open-source software development** — stigmergic coordination at massive scale
- **Jazz improvisation** — implicit coordination through shared musical models
- **Emergency room teams** — rapid coordination under extreme uncertainty
