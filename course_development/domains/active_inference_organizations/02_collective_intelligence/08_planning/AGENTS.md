## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Collective planning as participatory futures work, harnessing distributed knowledge for robust strategy
- **Topics**: Participatory planning, collective foresight, emergent strategy (Mintzberg), bottom-up intelligence
- **Lab Style**: Workshop — participatory scenario planning and collective roadmapping
- **Audience**: Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists, team leads
- **Tone**: Professional, collaborative, futures-oriented

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module examines how teams and organizations plan together — harnessing the distributed knowledge and diverse models of the entire team to construct richer scenario spaces and more robust strategies. All content should:

1. **Frame participatory planning as collective inference**: Planning that draws on many agents' generative models produces richer predictions than executive-only planning
2. **Apply emergent strategy (Mintzberg)**: Not all strategy is deliberate — some emerges from the pattern of decisions across many distributed agents
3. **Design collective foresight processes**: Participatory scenario planning, futures workshops, strategy jams, crowd-sourced competitive intelligence
4. **Balance top-down and bottom-up**: Leadership provides direction (priors), but frontline agents provide ground truth (sensory data). Good planning integrates both
5. **Build collective commitment**: Plans developed participatively generate greater buy-in because agents helped construct the shared model

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Participatory policy selection**: Multiple agents contributing to the evaluation of strategic options
- **Emergent strategy**: Patterns of action that arise from distributed agent decisions, not top-down planning
- **Collective foresight**: Using diverse generative models to construct richer scenario spaces
- **Top-down/bottom-up integration**: Combining leadership priors with frontline sensory evidence
- **Commitment through participation**: Agents who help build the plan internalize it as part of their own generative model
