## How Teams Plan Together

Part of **Section 2: Collective Intelligence**.

Planning is too important to be left to executives alone. This module covers participatory planning processes, collective foresight methods, the concept of emergent strategy (Mintzberg), and how to design planning processes that balance top-down direction with bottom-up intelligence.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Design** participatory planning processes that harness distributed team knowledge
2. **Apply** emergent strategy concepts — recognizing that some strategy emerges from patterns of distributed decisions
3. **Facilitate** collective foresight sessions (scenario workshops, futures jams, strategy competitions)
4. **Integrate** top-down direction (leadership priors) with bottom-up intelligence (frontline observations)
5. **Build** collective commitment to plans through participatory development

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Participatory planning**: Drawing on many agents' models for richer strategic predictions
- **Emergent strategy**: Strategy arising from patterns of distributed decisions, not just deliberate planning
- **Collective foresight**: Using diverse perspectives to construct more robust scenario spaces
- **Top-down/bottom-up balance**: Integrating leadership vision with operational reality
- **Commitment through participation**: Greater buy-in from co-created plans

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Section 2, Modules 01-07
- Experience with strategic planning or team roadmapping

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Mintzberg's emergent strategy at Honda** — how Honda's US motorcycle strategy emerged from frontline agent decisions
- **Lego's crowdsourced product development** — participatory foresight through Lego Ideas platform
- **W. L. Gore & Associates** — bottom-up strategy in a non-hierarchical organization
