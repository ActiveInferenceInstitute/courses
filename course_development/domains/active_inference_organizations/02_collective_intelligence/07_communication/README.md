## How Teams Communicate Across Boundaries

Part of **Section 2: Collective Intelligence**.

The hardest communication problem in organizations is not within teams — it is between teams that have different expertise, terminology, and mental models. This module examines inter-team communication protocols, boundary objects, alignment workshops, and the challenge of translating across specialist boundaries.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Analyze** cross-team communication as the challenge of bridging different generative models
2. **Design** boundary objects that are meaningful to multiple teams with different expertise
3. **Create** alignment protocols (demo days, cross-functional reviews) for regular model synchronization
4. **Diagnose** translation failures between specialist teams (engineering vs. marketing, strategy vs. operations)
5. **Build** organizational coherence across specialized team boundaries

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Model bridging**: Creating shared representations across specialist boundaries
- **Boundary objects**: Prototypes, dashboards, roadmaps that bridge different team models
- **Alignment protocols**: Structured inter-team synchronization processes
- **Translation failure**: When teams with different expertise cannot communicate effectively
- **Organizational coherence**: Enterprise-level alignment despite internal specialization

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Section 2, Modules 01-06
- Experience working across team or departmental boundaries

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Spotify's chapters and guilds** — cross-cutting communication structures for specialized alignment
- **Product-engineering handoffs** — boundary objects (specifications, user stories) bridging design and development
- **Hospital interdisciplinary rounds** — regular alignment across nursing, medical, and allied health teams
