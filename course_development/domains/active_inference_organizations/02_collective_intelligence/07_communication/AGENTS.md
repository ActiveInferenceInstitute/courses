## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Cross-team communication as bridging different generative models through shared representations
- **Topics**: Inter-team communication, boundary objects, alignment workshops, translation across specialties
- **Lab Style**: Workshop — cross-team alignment exercise and boundary object design
- **Audience**: Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists, team leads
- **Tone**: Professional, collaborative, cross-functional

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module examines the hardest communication challenge: communicating between teams with different expertise, terminology, and mental models. All content should:

1. **Frame cross-team communication as model bridging**: Different teams have different generative models — communication across team boundaries requires building shared representations
2. **Design boundary objects**: Artifacts (prototypes, dashboards, specifications, roadmaps) that are meaningful to multiple teams despite their different models
3. **Create alignment protocols**: Regular inter-team meetings, demo days, cross-functional workshops designed to synchronize models
4. **Address translation failures**: When engineers and marketers talk past each other, when strategy and operations have incompatible models
5. **Build organizational coherence**: How communication across team boundaries maintains the enterprise as a unified inference agent despite internal specialization

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Model bridging**: Building shared representations across different specialist models
- **Boundary objects**: Artifacts meaningful to multiple teams with different models
- **Cross-team alignment**: Regular synchronization of generative models across team boundaries
- **Translation failure**: Communication breakdown due to incompatible specialist models
- **Organizational coherence**: Maintaining enterprise-level model alignment across specialized teams
