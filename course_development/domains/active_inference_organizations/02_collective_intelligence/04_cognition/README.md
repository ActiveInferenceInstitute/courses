## How Teams Think Together

Part of **Section 2: Collective Intelligence**.

Teams build shared mental models that enable coordinated understanding and rapid decision-making. This module examines team mental model alignment, the value and risk of cognitive diversity, groupthink as a precision pathology, and how dialogue and deliberation processes build shared cognition.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Explain** team cognition as the alignment of individual generative models into a shared collective model
2. **Assess** cognitive diversity within a team and its effects on collective inference quality
3. **Diagnose** groupthink as excessive precision on consensus with insufficient precision on dissent
4. **Design** deliberation processes (devil's advocacy, dialectical inquiry) that harness cognitive diversity
5. **Facilitate** collective sensemaking sessions for ambiguous strategic situations

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Shared mental models**: Aligned beliefs enabling coordinated team action
- **Cognitive diversity**: Different expertise and thinking styles enriching collective reasoning
- **Groupthink diagnosis**: When consensus-seeking overrides critical evaluation
- **Structured deliberation**: Devil's advocacy, red teaming, dialectical inquiry
- **Collective sensemaking**: Joint interpretation of ambiguous organizational signals

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Section 2, Modules 01-03
- Experience participating in team discussions and decision-making

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Challenger disaster** — suppression of engineering concerns by groupthink dynamics
- **IDEO's design thinking** — structured processes for productive cognitive diversity
- **Amazon's "disagree and commit"** — institutionalizing constructive dissent in team cognition
