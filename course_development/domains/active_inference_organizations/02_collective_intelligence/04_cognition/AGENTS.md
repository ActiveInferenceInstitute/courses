## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Team cognition as shared sensemaking and aligned generative models
- **Topics**: Shared mental models, cognitive diversity, groupthink, dialogue and deliberation, team sensemaking
- **Lab Style**: Workshop — mental model alignment exercise and groupthink diagnosis
- **Audience**: Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists, team leads
- **Tone**: Professional, collaborative, evidence-based

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module examines how teams think together — building shared mental models that enable coordinated understanding and rapid decision-making. All content should:

1. **Frame team cognition as model alignment**: Team members aligning their individual generative models into a collective model used for prediction and coordinated action
2. **Value cognitive diversity**: Teams with diverse cognitive styles, expertise, and perspectives generate better hypotheses and more robust models
3. **Diagnose groupthink as precision pathology**: Excessive precision on consensus (high weight on group agreement) with insufficient precision on dissenting evidence
4. **Design deliberation processes**: Structured dialogue, devil's advocacy, dialectical inquiry as methods for productive cognitive diversity
5. **Connect to Weick's sensemaking**: Teams constructing shared meaning from ambiguous signals through iterative discussion and interpretation

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Shared mental model**: Aligned generative models across team members
- **Cognitive diversity**: Different priors and models producing richer collective inference
- **Groupthink**: Excessive precision on consensus, insufficient precision on dissent
- **Collective sensemaking**: Joint interpretation of ambiguous signals
- **Model negotiation**: Dialogue as the process of reconciling different individual models
