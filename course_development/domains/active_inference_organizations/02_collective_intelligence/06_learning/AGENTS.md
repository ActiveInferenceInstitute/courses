## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Team learning as collective model updating through shared experience and knowledge management
- **Topics**: Knowledge creation (Nonaka), communities of practice, transactive memory, institutional knowledge preservation
- **Lab Style**: Workshop — knowledge management audit and transactive memory mapping
- **Audience**: Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists, team leads
- **Tone**: Professional, collaborative, knowledge-management focused

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module examines how teams learn and remember — creating, sharing, storing, and retrieving knowledge collectively. All content should:

1. **Frame collective learning as shared model updating**: The team's generative model improves based on joint experience, not just individual learning
2. **Apply Nonaka's knowledge creation**: Socialization (tacit-to-tacit), externalization (tacit-to-explicit), combination (explicit-to-explicit), internalization (explicit-to-tacit) as modes of collective model updating
3. **Map transactive memory**: Who knows what on the team — the distributed memory system where members store knowledge about others' expertise
4. **Address knowledge loss**: When team members leave, institutional knowledge is lost unless explicitly captured and transferred
5. **Design learning infrastructure**: After-action reviews, retrospectives, knowledge bases, pair programming, mentorship as collective learning mechanisms

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Collective model updating**: Team-level learning from shared prediction errors
- **Knowledge creation**: Nonaka's SECI model as collective generative model refinement
- **Transactive memory**: Distributed knowledge storage across team members
- **Institutional memory**: Preserving the team's generative model through personnel changes
- **Communities of practice**: Informal learning networks that cross team boundaries
