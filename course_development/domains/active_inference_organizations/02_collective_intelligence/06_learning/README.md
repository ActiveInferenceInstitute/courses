# Module 06: Learning — Knowledge Management and Collective Memory

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## How Teams Learn and Remember

Part of **Section 2: Collective Intelligence**.

A team's knowledge exceeds what any member knows — but only if the team has systems for creating, sharing, storing, and retrieving knowledge collectively. This module covers Nonaka's knowledge creation framework, transactive memory systems, communities of practice, and the challenge of preserving institutional knowledge through personnel changes.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Explain** collective learning as team-level generative model updating
2. **Apply** Nonaka's SECI model (socialization, externalization, combination, internalization)
3. **Map** transactive memory in a team — who knows what and who knows who knows what
4. **Diagnose** knowledge loss risks when team members depart
5. **Design** knowledge management systems that preserve and grow institutional knowledge

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Collective learning**: Team-level model updating beyond individual learning
- **Knowledge creation (SECI)**: Four modes of converting between tacit and explicit knowledge
- **Transactive memory**: The team's distributed knowledge directory
- **Institutional knowledge**: Preserving the team's generative model over time
- **Communities of practice**: Cross-team learning networks

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Section 2, Modules 01-05
- Experience with knowledge sharing in team settings

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Toyota's A3 process** — systematic externalization of problem-solving knowledge
- **McKinsey's knowledge management** — institutional memory at scale through practice documents
- **NASA post-Apollo knowledge loss** — institutional memory degradation over decades
