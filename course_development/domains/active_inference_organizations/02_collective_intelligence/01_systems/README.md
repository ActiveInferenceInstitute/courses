## The Collective Markov Blanket

Part of **Section 2: Collective Intelligence**.

Teams are not just collections of individuals — they are systems in their own right, with their own Markov blankets, internal states, and adaptive behaviors. This module examines how team boundaries form, what makes a team function as a coherent system versus a loose collection, and how multi-scale organization enables collective intelligence.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Explain** how a team constitutes a system with its own Markov blanket
2. **Analyze** boundary permeability — how open or closed the team is to external influence
3. **Understand** multi-scale organization — individuals within teams within departments
4. **Identify** when a team is coherent (unified system) versus fragmented (loose collection)
5. **Apply** boundary management principles to team design and collaboration

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Team as system**: Shared internal states, collective boundary, joint sensing, coordinated action
- **Boundary permeability**: Open vs. closed teams and the trade-offs of each
- **Multi-scale nesting**: Hierarchical inference across organizational levels
- **Team coherence**: Shared model, clear boundary, joint attention, coordinated action, collective learning

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Section 1, Module 01 (Organizational Systems) or equivalent understanding
- Experience working in teams of any kind

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Pixar's Braintrust** — adaptive boundary permeability for creative review
- **Agile scrum teams** — designed coherence through ceremonies and shared artifacts
- **Surgical teams** — tight coordination with clear role boundaries
