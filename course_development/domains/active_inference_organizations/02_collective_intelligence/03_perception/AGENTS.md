# Station: Perception (Collective Intelligence)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Teams as distributed sensing systems that combine diverse observations for richer perception
- **Topics**: Distributed perception, wisdom of crowds, collective signal detection, perceptual diversity
- **Lab Style**: Workshop — collective sensing exercise and diversity audit
- **Audience**: Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists, team leads
- **Tone**: Professional, collaborative, evidence-based

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module examines how teams perceive collectively — combining distributed observations to infer hidden states that no individual could detect alone. All content should:

1. **Show the value of perceptual diversity**: Teams with diverse backgrounds, expertise, and cognitive styles detect more signals and reduce collective blind spots
2. **Apply collective signal detection**: How aggregating independent observations improves the signal-to-noise ratio beyond what any individual achieves
3. **Distinguish independence from isolation**: The wisdom of crowds requires independent initial judgments, but also mechanisms for aggregation and discussion
4. **Address pathologies of collective perception**: Shared blind spots, cascade effects (anchoring on the first opinion), and authority bias
5. **Design better collective sensing**: Techniques like Delphi method, pre-mortem analysis, and structured debate that improve collective perception

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Distributed sensing**: Multiple agents observing different aspects of the environment
- **Perceptual diversity**: Different generative models catching different signals
- **Signal aggregation**: Combining independent observations for better inference
- **Collective blind spots**: When all team members share the same perceptual limitations
- **Joint attention**: The team directing collective perception toward the same phenomena
