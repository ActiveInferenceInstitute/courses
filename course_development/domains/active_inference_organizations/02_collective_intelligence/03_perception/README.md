## How Teams See Together

Part of **Section 2: Collective Intelligence**.

A team's perception can exceed the sum of its members' perceptions — if the team is designed to leverage perceptual diversity. This module covers distributed perception, the wisdom of crowds, collective signal detection, and how diversity of perspective creates a richer organizational sensory apparatus.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Explain** how distributed perception enables teams to detect signals no individual could
2. **Apply** wisdom-of-crowds principles to organizational sensing
3. **Analyze** how perceptual diversity improves collective signal detection
4. **Identify** collective blind spots and cascade effects that degrade team perception
5. **Design** collective sensing processes (Delphi, pre-mortem, structured debate)

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Distributed sensing**: Combining observations from multiple vantage points
- **Perceptual diversity**: Different backgrounds and expertise catching different signals
- **Wisdom of crowds**: Independence, diversity, and aggregation for superior collective judgment
- **Collective blind spots**: When homogeneity creates shared perceptual gaps
- **Structured sensing**: Delphi method, pre-mortem, red team/blue team exercises

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Section 2, Modules 01-02 (Systems and Agents in teams)
- Familiarity with team decision-making processes

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Intelligence community** — structured analytic techniques for reducing collective bias
- **Prediction markets** — aggregating distributed beliefs for better forecasting
- **Medical diagnostic teams** — diverse specialist perspectives improving diagnostic accuracy
