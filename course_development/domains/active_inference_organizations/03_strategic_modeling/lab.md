# Lab: Strategic Modeling Exercise — Competitive War Game Simulation

## Objective

> **Learning Goal:** Conduct a comprehensive competitive strategy analysis using Active Inference principles, modeling competitors as inference agents, evaluating strategic options under uncertainty, and stress-testing your strategy against multiple competitive scenarios.

This exercise simulates a strategic planning session where you must analyze a competitive landscape, model key competitors, and develop a robust strategy that accounts for uncertainty and competitive dynamics.

---

## Scenario

Your organization operates in an industry undergoing disruption. A new technology, shifting customer preferences, or regulatory change is reshaping the competitive landscape. You must develop a strategic response.

Choose one of the following scenarios (or substitute your own):

- **FinServe Bank**: A mid-sized bank facing fintech disruption — digital-native competitors are capturing younger customers with superior mobile experiences and lower fees
- **PharmaCorp**: A pharmaceutical company whose blockbuster drug loses patent protection in 18 months while its pipeline has only early-stage candidates
- **RetailCo**: A brick-and-mortar retailer competing against e-commerce while dealing with rising commercial rents and changing consumer behavior

## Step 4: Competitive Response Modeling (10 min)

Predict how competitors will respond to your strategic move:

1. If you execute Option A, how will Competitor A respond?
2. How will that response change your position?
3. What is your counter-response?
4. What information would you need to reduce uncertainty about competitor reactions?

## Step 5: Strategic Recommendation (10 min)

Synthesize your analysis into a board-level strategic recommendation:

1. **Recommended strategy**: Which option, and why?
2. **Key assumptions to monitor**: What beliefs must hold for this strategy to succeed?
3. **Trigger signals**: What observations would cause you to switch strategies?
4. **Exploration investments**: What epistemic actions should accompany the strategy?
5. **Timeline and milestones**: Key decision points over the next 12-24 months
