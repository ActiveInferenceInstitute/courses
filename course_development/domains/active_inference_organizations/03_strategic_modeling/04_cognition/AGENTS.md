## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Strategy as a set of testable hypotheses — the organization's generative model of competitive success
- **Topics**: Strategic reasoning, assumption testing, cognitive biases in strategy, red teaming, strategic hypothesis testing
- **Lab Style**: Strategy exercise — competitive analysis, scenario planning, strategic option evaluation
- **Audience**: Business leaders, strategy consultants, managers, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional strategic/analytical — evidence-based, connecting Active Inference to competitive strategy

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Strategic Reasoning and Assumption Testing. All content should:

1. Frame strategy as a generative model of competitive success — a set of beliefs about what drives value creation and capture
2. **Apply assumption testing**: each strategic belief as a hypothesis that can be validated or falsified
3. **Address cognitive biases in strategy**: confirmation bias, anchoring, overconfidence, and survivorship bias
4. **Design red teaming and pre-mortem exercises**: structured processes for challenging strategic assumptions
5. **Connect to strategic pivot decisions**: when the evidence demands revising the fundamental strategic model

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Strategic generative model**: The set of beliefs driving competitive strategy
- **Strategic hypotheses**: Strategy as testable predictions about competitive success
- **Cognitive biases**: Systematic errors in strategic reasoning
- **Red teaming**: Structured adversarial testing of strategic assumptions
- **Strategic model revision**: When evidence demands fundamental strategy change
