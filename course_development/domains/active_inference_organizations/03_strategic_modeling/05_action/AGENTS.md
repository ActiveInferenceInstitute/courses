# Station: Action (Strategic Modeling)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Strategic action as policy execution in a multi-agent environment where competitors respond to your moves
- **Topics**: Competitive moves, first-mover advantage, strategic timing, competitive dynamics, market entry
- **Lab Style**: Strategy exercise — competitive analysis, scenario planning, strategic option evaluation
- **Audience**: Business leaders, strategy consultants, managers, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional strategic/analytical — evidence-based, connecting Active Inference to competitive strategy

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Strategic Moves and Competitive Action. All content should:

1. **Frame competitive moves as policy execution**: pricing decisions, market entry, product launches, M&A as strategic actions
2. Analyze first-mover advantage vs. fast-follower strategy as exploration vs. exploitation trade-offs
3. **Model competitive dynamics**: action-reaction sequences, escalation, and competitive spirals
4. **Apply strategic timing**: when to act depends on uncertainty levels and competitor readiness
5. **Address strategic commitment**: irreversible actions that signal intent and shape competitor behavior

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Competitive moves**: Strategic actions in a multi-agent environment
- **First-mover vs. fast-follower**: Exploration vs. exploitation in market entry
- **Competitive dynamics**: Action-reaction sequences between rival firms
- **Strategic timing**: Acting based on uncertainty levels and environmental readiness
- **Strategic commitment**: Irreversible actions that signal intent to competitors
