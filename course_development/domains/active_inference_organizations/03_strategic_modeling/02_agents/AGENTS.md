# Station: Agents (Strategic Modeling)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Competitors and stakeholders as inference agents with their own models, goals, and adaptive behavior
- **Topics**: Competitor modeling, stakeholder analysis, game theory, theory of mind, strategic interaction
- **Lab Style**: Strategy exercise — competitive analysis, scenario planning, strategic option evaluation
- **Audience**: Business leaders, strategy consultants, managers, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional strategic/analytical — evidence-based, connecting Active Inference to competitive strategy

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Competitive Agents and Stakeholder Modeling. All content should:

1. Model competitors as inference agents — each with their own generative model, preferred states, and action capacity
2. **Apply stakeholder analysis through the Active Inference lens**: each stakeholder as an agent whose behavior must be predicted
3. **Connect to game theory**: competitive interactions as multi-agent policy selection under mutual uncertainty
4. **Develop theory of mind for organizations**: predicting competitor behavior by modeling their generative model
5. **Address strategic surprise**: when a competitor's action reveals that your model of them was wrong

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Competitor as inference agent**: Modeling rivals' generative models and predicted policies
- **Stakeholder modeling**: Each stakeholder as an agent with beliefs, preferences, and actions
- **Strategic theory of mind**: Predicting competitor behavior by modeling their model
- **Game-theoretic interaction**: Multi-agent policy selection under mutual uncertainty
- **Strategic surprise**: Competitor actions that violate your model — requiring revision
