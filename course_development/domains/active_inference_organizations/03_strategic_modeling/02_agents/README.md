## Learning Objectives

1. Model competitors as inference agents with their own generative models and preferred states
2. Conduct stakeholder analysis through the Active Inference lens
3. Apply strategic theory of mind — predicting competitor behavior by modeling their model
4. Connect competitive interaction to game-theoretic concepts of mutual inference
5. Diagnose strategic surprises as failures in competitor modeling

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Competitor modeling**: Inferring rivals' generative models and predicted actions
- **Stakeholder as agent**: Each stakeholder with beliefs, preferences, and behavior
- **Strategic theory of mind**: What do they think we think they will do?
- **Game-theoretic dynamics**: Nash equilibria and competitive spirals
- **Strategic surprise**: When competitor behavior violates your model

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Sections 1-2 (Organizational Systems and Collective Intelligence)
