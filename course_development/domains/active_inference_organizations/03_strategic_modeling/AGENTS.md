## Domain Guidelines

This course applies Active Inference to competitive strategy and market dynamics. The central insight is that strategy is not just planning — it is **inference under uncertainty**: firms must infer hidden states (competitor intentions, market trajectory, regulatory direction), select policies (strategic options) that minimize expected free energy, and update their models as the competitive landscape evolves.

### Key Organizational Active Inference Concepts

- **Multi-agent inference**: Markets as environments where multiple firms simultaneously infer, act, and learn — each modeling the others' behavior
- **Strategic generative model**: The firm's model of its competitive environment — who the key players are, what drives their behavior, and how the market evolves
- **Competitive prediction error**: When a competitor acts in a way the firm's model did not anticipate — requiring model revision
- **Strategic expected free energy**: Evaluating strategic options on both pragmatic value (achieving goals) and epistemic value (reducing uncertainty about the competitive environment)
- **Dynamic capabilities (Teece)**: The organization's capacity to sense opportunities, seize them, and transform itself — mapped to Active Inference sensing, action, and model updating
