# Station: Perception (Strategic Modeling)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Strategic perception as inferring competitive dynamics from indirect, ambiguous, and potentially deceptive signals
- **Topics**: Competitive intelligence, weak signal detection, strategic early warning, market sensing systems
- **Lab Style**: Strategy exercise — competitive analysis, scenario planning, strategic option evaluation
- **Audience**: Business leaders, strategy consultants, managers, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional strategic/analytical — evidence-based, connecting Active Inference to competitive strategy

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Competitive Intelligence and Market Sensing. All content should:

1. Frame competitive intelligence as sensory inference — detecting competitor moves, market shifts, and emerging threats from noisy signals
2. **Introduce weak signal detection**: identifying early indicators of strategic change before they become obvious trends
3. **Address the deception problem**: competitors may deliberately send misleading signals (bluffing, misdirection)
4. **Build strategic early warning systems**: systematic processes for monitoring key competitive variables
5. **Connect to the precision concept**: which competitive signals does the organization trust, and why?

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Competitive intelligence**: Inferring hidden competitive states from observable signals
- **Weak signal detection**: Sensing early indicators of strategic change before trend confirmation
- **Strategic deception**: Competitors sending misleading signals to manipulate your model
- **Early warning systems**: Systematic monitoring of competitive environment variables
- **Strategic precision**: Which competitive signals the organization trusts and weights heavily
