## Learning Objectives

1. Frame strategic risk management as managing prediction uncertainty across competitive landscapes
2. Apply scenario planning — evaluating strategy robustness against multiple plausible futures
3. Use real options thinking — investing in strategic flexibility and future optionality
4. Assess existential strategic risks to organizational viability
5. Design adaptive strategies with revision triggers and contingency branches

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Strategic risk**: Competitive uncertainty affecting strategic decisions
- **Scenario planning**: Multi-model strategy stress testing
- **Real options**: Investing in flexibility and future strategic optionality
- **Existential risk**: Threats to organizational survival as a competitive entity
- **Adaptive strategy**: Plans designed to evolve as the environment changes

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Sections 1-2 (Organizational Systems and Collective Intelligence)
- Familiarity with basic strategy concepts (competitive advantage, market analysis)

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Shell's scenario planning** — 50 years of systematic multi-future strategy evaluation
- **Amazon's real options approach** — AWS, logistics, and healthcare as strategic options
- **Blockbuster vs. Netflix** — failure to manage existential strategic risk
