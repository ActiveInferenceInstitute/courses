# Station: Planning (Strategic Modeling)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Long-term strategic planning as managing uncertainty across multiple time horizons and possible futures
- **Topics**: Strategic risk, scenario planning, real options, long-term positioning, existential risk
- **Lab Style**: Strategy exercise — competitive analysis, scenario planning, strategic option evaluation
- **Audience**: Business leaders, strategy consultants, managers, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional strategic/analytical — evidence-based, connecting Active Inference to competitive strategy

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Strategic Risk Management and Long-Term Positioning. All content should:

1. Frame strategic risk management as managing prediction uncertainty across the competitive landscape
2. **Apply scenario planning**: constructing multiple plausible futures and evaluating strategy robustness against each
3. **Introduce real options thinking**: making small, reversible investments that create future strategic flexibility
4. **Address existential strategic risk**: threats to the organization's continued existence as a viable competitive entity
5. **Design adaptive strategy**: plans with built-in revision triggers and contingency branches

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Strategic risk**: Uncertainty about future competitive states that affects strategic decisions
- **Scenario planning**: Multi-model evaluation of strategy robustness across possible futures
- **Real options**: Investments in strategic flexibility that reduce future expected free energy
- **Existential risk**: Threats to the organization's viability as a competitive entity
- **Adaptive strategy**: Plans with built-in trigger signals and contingency branches
