# Syllabus: Strategic Modeling

> **Active Inference for Organizations** | **Audience**: Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists

## Course Description

Strategic Modeling explores Active Inference through scenario planning, risk, decision frameworks. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn.

## Schedule

| Week | Module | Topic | Activity |

| Week 3 | [Perception](./03_perception/module.md) | Market Signals and Environmental Scanning | Strategy Exercise |
| Week 4 | [Cognition](./04_cognition/module.md) | Organizational Beliefs and Strategy | Strategy Exercise |

| Week 8 | [Planning](./08_planning/module.md) | Futures Thinking and Organizational Resilience | Strategy Exercise |
