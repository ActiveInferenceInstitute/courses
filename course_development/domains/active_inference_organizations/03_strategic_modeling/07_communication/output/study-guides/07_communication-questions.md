# Strategic Modeling — Module 07: Communication — Discussion Questions

> 20 questions on strategic narrative, signaling theory, brand positioning, and competitive communication.

---

1. How does Tesla's strategic communication simultaneously manage investor, customer, and competitor perceptions?
2. When is strategic ambiguity (being deliberately vague) a rational communication strategy?
3. How do credible signals differ from "cheap talk" in competitive communication? Give examples.
4. How does brand equity function as a shared generative model between the firm and its customers?
5. When should a company pre-announce a product or strategy? What are the risks of signaling too early?
6. How do investor narratives shape capital allocation decisions? What makes a narrative credible?
7. How does competitive signaling create a "conversation" between firms without direct communication?
8. When is it advantageous to signal weakness rather than strength? Give examples.
9. How do earnings calls function as a signaling mechanism between firms and capital markets?
10. What happens when a firm's strategic narrative diverges from its operational reality?
11. Analyze the strategic narrative of a company you know well. What story is it telling? To whom?
12. Design a competitive signaling strategy for a firm entering a market dominated by an incumbent.
13. Compare the strategic communication of two direct competitors. What does each signal?
14. Identify a case where a firm's strategic communication backfired. What went wrong?
15. How do ESG (environmental, social, governance) communications function as strategic signals?
16. Design a brand positioning strategy using the concept of "model alignment" between the firm and its target customers.
17. When a competitive signal is misinterpreted, what consequences can follow?
18. How do social media and viral communication change the dynamics of strategic signaling?
19. Is a company's leadership blog post a credible signal or cheap talk? What determines which?
20. Design a strategic communication plan for an organization undergoing a major pivot. How do you manage narratives for investors, employees, and customers simultaneously?
