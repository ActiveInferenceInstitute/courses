## Learning Objectives

1. Frame external communication as active inference — shaping stakeholder models
2. Build strategic narratives that position the organization in its competitive landscape
3. Analyze competitive signaling — communications designed to influence competitor behavior
4. Design stakeholder communication strategies for investors, customers, regulators, and employees
5. Diagnose narrative failure — when the organizational story diverges from reality

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Strategic narrative**: The competitive story told to stakeholders
- **Active communication**: Shaping how others model the organization
- **Competitive signaling**: Threats, commitments, and signals to competitors
- **Stakeholder segmentation**: Different messages for different audience models
- **Narrative-reality gap**: Credibility erosion when story and evidence diverge

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Sections 1-2 (Organizational Systems and Collective Intelligence)
- Familiarity with basic strategy concepts (competitive advantage, market analysis)

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Tesla's strategic narrative** — shaping investor and public models of the EV future
- **WeWork's narrative failure** — a story that diverged dramatically from financial reality
- **Intel Inside campaign** — shaping end-consumer models of a B2B component
