## Conventions

- **Perspective**: External communication as active inference — shaping stakeholder models to align with organizational goals
- **Topics**: Strategic narrative, investor relations, brand positioning, stakeholder communication, competitive signaling
- **Lab Style**: Strategy exercise — competitive analysis, scenario planning, strategic option evaluation
- **Audience**: Business leaders, strategy consultants, managers, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional strategic/analytical — evidence-based, connecting Active Inference to competitive strategy

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Strategic Narrative and Stakeholder Communication. All content should:

1. **Frame external communication as active inference**: shaping how stakeholders (investors, customers, competitors) model your organization
2. **Build strategic narratives**: the story the organization tells about its competitive position and future trajectory
3. **Analyze competitive signaling**: communications designed to influence competitor behavior (threats, commitments, signals of strength)
4. **Design stakeholder communication**: different messages for different audiences, each targeting specific model updates
5. **Address narrative failure**: when the organization's story diverges from reality, creating credibility gaps

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Strategic narrative**: The story that shapes how stakeholders model the organization
- **Active communication**: Shaping stakeholder generative models through targeted messaging
- **Competitive signaling**: Communications designed to influence competitor models and behavior
- **Stakeholder model management**: Different messages targeting different audience models
- **Narrative-reality gap**: When the story diverges from evidence, eroding credibility
