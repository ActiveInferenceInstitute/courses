## Learning Objectives

1. Define the competitive ecosystem as the relevant system for strategic modeling
2. Apply Porter's Five Forces as a model of environmental pressures on the firm's inference task
3. Understand ecosystem dynamics — co-evolution, network effects, and platform economics
4. Frame market competition as multi-agent inference under uncertainty
5. Identify when the competitive system itself is undergoing structural change

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Competitive ecosystem**: The firm-environment coupled system
- **Porter's Five Forces**: Environmental pressures as inference constraints
- **Platform dynamics**: Network effects and winner-take-all competition
- **Multi-agent markets**: Distributed inference across competing firms
- **Industry disruption**: When the rules of the competitive game change

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Sections 1-2 (Organizational Systems and Collective Intelligence)
- Familiarity with basic strategy concepts (competitive advantage, market analysis)

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Apple's platform ecosystem** — self-reinforcing network effects and boundary management
- **Airline industry** — Porter's Five Forces in a mature, competitive market
- **Uber vs. taxi industry** — platform disruption of a regulated ecosystem
