# Station: Systems (Strategic Modeling)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: The firm-environment coupled system as the relevant unit of strategic analysis
- **Topics**: Competitive ecosystems, Porter's Five Forces, market dynamics, multi-agent inference, platform economics
- **Lab Style**: Strategy exercise — competitive analysis, scenario planning, strategic option evaluation
- **Audience**: Business leaders, strategy consultants, managers, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional strategic/analytical — evidence-based, connecting Active Inference to competitive strategy

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Competitive Ecosystems. All content should:

1. Frame the competitive ecosystem as the relevant system — not the organization alone, but the organization and its environment as a coupled dynamical system
2. Apply Porter's Five Forces as environmental pressures shaping the firm's inference task
3. **Model ecosystem dynamics**: co-evolution, network effects, platform economics, and winner-take-all dynamics
4. Frame market competition as multi-agent inference under uncertainty
5. Identify when the competitive system itself is changing (industry disruption, convergence, platform emergence)

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Firm-environment system**: The coupled dynamical system of organization and competitive landscape
- **Competitive Markov blanket**: The firm's strategic boundary in its market
- **Multi-agent inference**: Multiple firms simultaneously modeling each other
- **Ecosystem dynamics**: Co-evolution, network effects, and platform economics
- **Industry disruption**: Structural change in the competitive system itself
