## Learning Objectives

1. Frame strategic learning as updating the competitive model based on market outcomes
2. Apply Teece's dynamic capabilities framework through the Active Inference lens
3. Design strategic experiments that validate or falsify competitive assumptions
4. Analyze competitive learning asymmetries — why some firms learn faster from competition
5. Understand strategic co-evolution between the firm and its competitive environment

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Strategic model updating**: Learning from competitive outcomes
- **Dynamic capabilities**: Sensing, seizing, transforming as inference operations
- **Strategic experimentation**: Hypothesis-driven competitive learning
- **Learning asymmetry**: Differential learning rates creating competitive advantage
- **Co-evolution**: Firm and environment mutually adapting over long horizons

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Sections 1-2 (Organizational Systems and Collective Intelligence)
- Familiarity with basic strategy concepts (competitive advantage, market analysis)

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Toyota's lean production** — decades of strategic learning embedded in organizational routines
- **Netflix's culture of experimentation** — A/B testing as strategic learning at scale
- **Nokia vs. Apple** — differential learning rates in the smartphone transition
