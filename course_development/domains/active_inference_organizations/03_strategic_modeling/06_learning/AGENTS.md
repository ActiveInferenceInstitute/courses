# Station: Learning (Strategic Modeling)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Strategic learning as updating the competitive model based on market feedback and competitive outcomes
- **Topics**: Dynamic capabilities, strategic adaptation, competitive learning, strategic experimentation
- **Lab Style**: Strategy exercise — competitive analysis, scenario planning, strategic option evaluation
- **Audience**: Business leaders, strategy consultants, managers, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional strategic/analytical — evidence-based, connecting Active Inference to competitive strategy

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Adaptive Strategy and Strategic Learning. All content should:

1. **Frame strategic learning as model updating**: refining the competitive model based on market outcomes
2. **Apply dynamic capabilities (Teece)**: sensing opportunities, seizing them, and transforming the organization
3. **Design strategic experiments**: small-scale tests that validate or falsify competitive assumptions
4. **Address competitive learning asymmetries**: some organizations learn from competition faster than others
5. **Connect to strategic evolution**: how organizations co-evolve with their competitive environment over long time horizons

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Strategic model updating**: Refining competitive assumptions based on market outcomes
- **Dynamic capabilities**: Sensing, seizing, and transforming as Active Inference operations
- **Strategic experimentation**: Small-scale tests that reduce competitive uncertainty
- **Competitive learning asymmetry**: Differential learning rates across competing firms
- **Strategic co-evolution**: Firm and environment adapting to each other over time
