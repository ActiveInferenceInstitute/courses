## Overview

Welcome to **Active Inference for Organizations**. This curriculum teaches Active Inference — the theory that all living systems work by constantly predicting and acting to minimize surprise.

**Tone**: Business/management. Strategic planning, team dynamics, organizational cognition.

## Design Principles

1. **Business language first**: FEP terminology always introduced after organizational parallel
2. **Case-study driven**: Real-world examples from multiple industries
3. **Actionable**: Every module includes tools, frameworks, or exercises you can use Monday morning
4. **Light formalism**: Diagrams and conceptual models, not dense mathematics
5. **Strategic framing**: Active Inference as a lens for understanding organizations, not a prescription
