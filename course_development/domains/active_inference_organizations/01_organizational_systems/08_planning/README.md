## Strategic Planning Under Uncertainty

Part of **Section 1: Organizational Systems**.

How does an organization plan for an uncertain future? This module covers strategic planning as policy selection — choosing sequences of actions that minimize expected free energy over an extended time horizon. You will learn scenario planning as multi-model evaluation, build resilience assessments, and navigate the fundamental paradox between commitment and adaptability.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Frame** strategic planning as policy selection under expected free energy
2. **Apply** temporal depth — matching planning horizon to industry dynamics
3. **Use** scenario planning as multi-model evaluation for robust strategy
4. **Assess** organizational resilience: absorptive, adaptive, and transformative capacity
5. **Navigate** the commitment-adaptability paradox in strategic resource allocation

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Policy selection**: Choosing strategy as selecting the action sequence with lowest expected free energy
- **Temporal depth**: How far ahead the organization's model can meaningfully predict
- **Scenario planning**: Constructing multiple futures and evaluating strategies against each
- **Resilience**: Absorbing surprise while maintaining identity and functioning
- **Commitment-adaptability**: Investing deeply versus maintaining strategic optionality

## Prerequisites
