# Lab: Organizational Systems Diagnostic — Full Boundary Assessment

## Objective

> **Learning Goal:** Conduct a comprehensive organizational systems analysis, integrating all eight Active Inference concepts (systems, agents, perception, cognition, action, learning, communication, planning) to diagnose an organization's adaptive health and identify high-priority improvement areas.

This capstone lab synthesizes the entire Organizational Systems section into a single diagnostic exercise. You will play the role of an organizational consultant conducting a rapid assessment for a client.

---

## Scenario

You have been retained by the leadership team of a mid-sized company facing strategic uncertainty. Revenue growth has slowed, employee engagement survey scores have declined for two consecutive quarters, and a new competitor has entered the market with a disruptive product. The CEO asks: "Is our organization fundamentally healthy, or do we have structural problems?"

Choose one of the following organizations to diagnose (or substitute your own):

- **GreenLeaf Manufacturing**: A 500-person sustainable packaging company whose core customer base is shifting to e-commerce packaging requirements
- **MedBridge Health Systems**: A regional hospital network navigating the transition from fee-for-service to value-based care
- **NovaTech Solutions**: A 200-person B2B SaaS company whose founding product is mature and whose growth depends on launching a new product line
