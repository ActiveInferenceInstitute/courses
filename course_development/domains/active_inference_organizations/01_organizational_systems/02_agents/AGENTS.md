# Station: Agents (Organizational Systems)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Organizations as purposive inference agents with goals, beliefs, and action capacity
- **Topics**: Corporate agency, distributed inference, roles as specialization, incentives as precision allocation
- **Lab Style**: Case study — agent coherence assessment and incentive diagnosis
- **Audience**: Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional business/management — strategic and evidence-based

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module establishes that organizations are not merely systems — they are **agents** that maintain generative models, have preferred states (goals), take purposive action, and update beliefs. All content should:

1. **Distinguish system from agent**: A system has boundaries; an agent has goals, beliefs, and acts to fulfill them
2. **Explore distributed agency**: Corporate intentionality emerges from many individuals coordinating through roles and incentives
3. **Frame roles as inference specialization**: Sales, R&D, Finance, HR each handle a specific subset of the organizational inference task
4. **Treat incentives as precision allocation**: Compensation structures and KPIs determine which signals agents attend to
5. **Address agency breakdown**: Principal-agent problems, organizational drift, groupthink as failure modes of organizational agency

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Agent**: A system that maintains a generative model, has preferred states, and minimizes free energy
- **Distributed agency**: Corporate intentionality emerging from coordinated individual agents
- **Precision allocation**: Incentives determining which signals receive organizational attention
- **Agency coherence**: Whether subunit goals align with enterprise goals
- **Strong vs. weak agency**: Deliberate strategy versus emergent organizational behavior
