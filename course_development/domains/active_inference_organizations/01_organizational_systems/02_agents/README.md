## The Organization as an Inference Agent

Part of **Section 1: Organizational Systems**.

This module moves beyond the organization-as-system view to the organization-as-agent view. Organizations have goals, form beliefs about their environment, and take purposive action. Under Active Inference, an agent is any system that minimizes free energy by maintaining a generative model and acting to confirm its predictions. Here we examine how corporate intentionality emerges from distributed individual agency, how roles partition the inference task, and how incentive structures allocate organizational attention.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Explain** how an organization qualifies as an agent under the Active Inference framework
2. **Describe** how corporate intentionality emerges from distributed individual agency
3. **Analyze** how roles and incentive structures partition the organizational inference task
4. **Distinguish** between strong agency (deliberate strategy) and weak agency (emergent behavior)
5. **Identify** when organizational agency breaks down — subunit goals conflicting with enterprise goals

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Corporate agency**: The firm as a purposive entity with beliefs, goals, and action capacity
- **Distributed inference**: How individual agents aggregate into organizational intelligence
- **Roles as specialization**: Sales, R&D, Finance, HR as inference subspecialties
- **Incentives as precision**: Compensation and KPIs determining what gets attended to
- **Agency breakdown**: Principal-agent problems, drift, groupthink, analysis paralysis

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Module 01 (Systems) or equivalent understanding of organizational Markov blankets
- Familiarity with basic organizational roles and incentive structures

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Amazon** — two-pizza teams and distributed agency aligned by leadership principles
- **Wells Fargo (2016)** — incentive misalignment as precision misallocation leading to fraud
- **Enron** — organizational agency breakdown through misaligned subunit optimization
