# Notation Table: Active Inference for Organizations

> Standard symbols and notation used throughout the curriculum.
> This curriculum uses **light conceptual notation** — diagrams and organizational mappings, not dense mathematics.
> Where formal notation is introduced, it is always grounded in a business parallel first.

---

## Core Symbols

| Symbol / Concept | Plain English | Formal Meaning | Organizational Translation | First Introduced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **System** | A thing with a boundary | An entity with internal and external states separated by a Markov blanket | A firm, division, or team with defined boundaries | C1 M1 |
| **Agent** | Something that acts to achieve goals | A system that minimizes free energy through perception and action | An individual, team, or organization pursuing strategic objectives | C1 M2 |
| **Prediction** | A guess about what will happen | Expected sensory input under the generative model | Sales forecast, market projection, scenario | C1 M3 |
| **Prediction Error** | When reality ≠ expectation | Difference between predicted and actual observations | Missed forecast, strategic surprise, budget variance | C1 M3 |
| **Surprise** | An unexpected outcome | Negative log probability of an observation under the model | Black swan event, market disruption, regulatory shock | C1 M3 |
| **Action** | Making something happen | Changing the world to match predictions | Strategy execution, product launch, hiring | C1 M5 |
| **Learning** | Getting better over time | Updating model parameters based on prediction error | Organizational learning, post-mortem analysis, training | C1 M6 |
| **Generative Model** | Mental model of how the world works | Joint probability distribution over hidden states and observations | Organization's assumptions about markets, customers, competitors | C1 M1 |
| **Markov Blanket** | Boundary between inside and outside | Statistical partition into internal, external, sensory, and active states | Organizational boundary — what flows in (data) and out (products) | C1 M1 |
| **Free Energy** | Tension or misalignment | Upper bound on surprise; divergence between model and reality | Gap between strategy and market reality | C1 M1 |
| **Precision** | Confidence in a signal | Inverse variance of a probability distribution | Trust in data, confidence in market research, signal quality | C2 M3 |
| **Expected Free Energy** | How good a plan looks | Combined pragmatic + epistemic value of a future action | ROI estimate that also accounts for what we'll learn | C3 M5 |
| **Epistemic Value** | Information gained | Expected reduction in uncertainty from an action | Value of running a pilot, market test, or experiment | C3 M3 |
| **Pragmatic Value** | Goal achievement | Expected alignment with preferred outcomes | Revenue impact, market share gain, cost reduction | C3 M5 |
| **Policy** | Plan of action | Sequence of actions selected to minimize EFE | Strategic plan, quarterly roadmap, implementation timeline | C3 M8 |
| **Nested Systems** | Systems within systems | Hierarchical Markov blankets at multiple scales | Teams within divisions within the enterprise | C2 M1 |
| **Prior** | What we believe before new evidence | Prior probability distribution | Strategic assumption, industry conventional wisdom | C1 M4 |
| **Posterior** | Updated belief after new evidence | Posterior probability distribution | Revised forecast, updated market model | C1 M4 |

## Diagrammatic Conventions

Throughout the curriculum, diagrams use these conventions:

| Element | Representation | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Markov blanket boundary | Dashed border | Org boundary diagram |
| Information flow (sensory) | Arrow **into** the system | Market data, customer feedback |
| Information flow (active) | Arrow **out of** the system | Products, communications, strategy |
| Internal states | Nodes inside the boundary | Culture, knowledge, capabilities |
| External states | Nodes outside the boundary | Competitors, regulators, customers |
| Prediction error | Red highlighted gap | Forecast vs. actual |
| Free energy minimization | Downward-pointing arrow | Strategic adaptation over time |
