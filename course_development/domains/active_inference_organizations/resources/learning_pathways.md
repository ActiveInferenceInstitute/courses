# Learning Pathways: Active Inference for Organizations

> Suggested routes through the curriculum for business leaders, managers, and organizational scientists.
> Choose the pathway that best matches your role, learning style, and time constraints.

---

## Pathway 1: Sequential (Recommended for Beginners)

Complete one course fully before starting the next. This builds understanding from structural fundamentals to digital applications.

```
[C1: Organizational Systems] → [C2: Collective Intelligence] → [C3: Strategic Modeling] → [C4: Digital Transformation]
         ↓                              ↓                              ↓                             ↓
    "What is the org?"          "How do teams work?"         "How do we decide?"         "How do we integrate AI?"
```

**Time**: ~40 hours total (10 hours per course)
**Best for**: Learners new to Active Inference who want the full picture.

---

## Pathway 2: Parallel (Comparative)

Study the same module across all 4 courses before moving to the next. This builds deep understanding of each topic from multiple angles.

```
M1 across all 4 courses → M2 across all 4 courses → ... → M8 across all 4 courses
```

**Time**: ~40 hours total
**Best for**: Academics, researchers, and comparative thinkers who want to see how the same principle applies in different contexts.

## Pathway 3: Executive Track

A targeted pathway for senior leaders focused on strategic decision-making and organizational design.

| Step | Module | Course | Why |
|------|--------|--------|-----|
| 1 | Systems (M1) | C1: Org Systems | Understand your organization as an inference engine |
| 2 | Cognition (M4) | C1: Org Systems | Map your organization's mental models |
| 3 | Planning (M8) | C3: Strategic Modeling | Apply EFE to strategic planning |
| 4 | Action (M5) | C3: Strategic Modeling | Evaluate strategy execution |
| 5 | Learning (M6) | C1: Org Systems | Build organizational learning loops |
| 6 | Systems (M1) | C4: Digital Transform | Frame digital transformation |

**Time**: ~15 hours
**Best for**: CEOs, VPs, board members, and strategy directors.
