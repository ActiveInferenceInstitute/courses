## Purpose

These shared resources serve as the single source of truth for terminology, notation, and references across all courses in Active Inference for Organizations. They provide the connective tissue that ensures consistency and coherence across the four course sections.

## Maintenance Rules

1. Update the glossary when new terms are introduced in any module.
2. Keep the notation table synchronized with all courses.
3. Verify cross-course map links after any structural changes.
4. All tables must use proper MD060-compliant formatting (spaces around pipes).

## Organizational Active Inference Reference Framework

### Core Theoretical References

- **Friston, K.** (2010). The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory? *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 11(2), 127-138. -- The foundational paper on the free energy principle
- **Parr, T., Pezzulo, G., & Friston, K. J.** (2022). *Active Inference: The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, and Behavior*. MIT Press. -- The comprehensive textbook on Active Inference
- **Ramstead, M. J. D., Badcock, P. B., & Friston, K. J.** (2018). Answering Schrodinger's question: A free-energy formulation. *Physics of Life Reviews*, 24, 1-16. -- Active Inference applied to social and cultural systems

### Social and Organizational Extensions

- **Constant, A., et al.** (2019). Regimes of expectations: An active inference model of social conformity and decision making. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 10, 679. -- Active Inference applied to social norms and institutional expectations
- **Veissiere, S. P. L., et al.** (2020). Thinking through other minds: A variational approach to cognition and culture. *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*, 43, e90. -- Active Inference and social cognition
- **Friston, K., & Frith, C.** (2015). A duet for one. *Consciousness and Cognition*, 36, 390-405. -- Active Inference and social interaction as coupled inference

### Organizational and Management Theory

- **Senge, P. M.** (1990). *The Fifth Discipline*. Doubleday. -- Systems thinking in organizations
- **Weick, K. E.** (1995). *Sensemaking in Organizations*. Sage. -- Organizational sensemaking as inference
- **Argyris, C.** (1977). Double loop learning in organizations. *Harvard Business Review*, 55(5), 115-125. -- Single-loop and double-loop organizational learning
- **Nonaka, I., & Takeuchi, H.** (1995). *The Knowledge-Creating Company*. Oxford University Press. -- Knowledge creation and management
- **Teece, D. J.** (2007). Explicating dynamic capabilities. *Strategic Management Journal*, 28(13), 1319-1350. -- Dynamic capabilities framework
- **Porter, M. E.** (1980). *Competitive Strategy*. Free Press. -- Industry structure and competitive forces
- **March, J. G.** (1991). Exploration and exploitation in organizational learning. *Organization Science*, 2(1), 71-87. -- The fundamental exploration-exploitation trade-off
- **Simon, H. A.** (1947). *Administrative Behavior*. Macmillan. -- Bounded rationality in organizations
- **Cyert, R. M., & March, J. G.** (1963). *A Behavioral Theory of the Firm*. Prentice-Hall. -- Behavioral economics of organizations
- **Edmondson, A. C.** (1999). Psychological safety and learning behavior in work teams. *Administrative Science Quarterly*, 44(2), 350-383. -- Psychological safety and team learning

## Content Guidelines

All resources should:

1. Use consistent terminology across the glossary, notation table, and course content
2. Bridge Active Inference technical language with organizational management vocabulary
3. Provide clear cross-references between related concepts across all four course sections
4. Maintain accessibility for business professionals who may not have prior Active Inference exposure
