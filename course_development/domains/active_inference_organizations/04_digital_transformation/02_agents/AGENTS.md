## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Organizations as ecosystems of human and artificial agents that must coordinate inference and action
- **Topics**: Human-AI collaboration, algorithmic agents, automation decisions, augmentation vs. replacement, AI governance
- **Lab Style**: Implementation plan — technology assessment, architecture design, migration planning
- **Audience**: Business leaders, CTOs, digital transformation leaders, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional technology/business — connecting Active Inference to digital strategy and implementation

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Human and AI Agents in Organizations. All content should:

1. Frame AI as a new class of organizational agent — with its own generative model, action capacity, and learning ability
2. Distinguish augmentation (AI enhancing human capabilities) from automation (AI replacing human tasks)
3. Design delegation frameworks: which decisions should be human, which algorithmic, and which hybrid?
4. Address AI governance: accountability, transparency, bias, and alignment of AI agent goals with organizational goals
5. Connect to the future of work: how AI agents reshape organizational roles, skills, and career paths

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **AI as organizational agent**: Algorithmic systems with their own models, predictions, and actions
- **Augmentation vs. automation**: AI enhancing human capability vs. replacing human tasks
- **Human-AI delegation**: Framework for allocating decisions between human and algorithmic agents
- **AI alignment**: Ensuring algorithmic agent goals align with organizational preferred states
- **Hybrid inference**: Human and AI agents contributing complementary capabilities to organizational cognition
