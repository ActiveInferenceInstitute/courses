# Digital Transformation — Module 02: Agents — Discussion Questions

> 20 questions on human-AI collaboration, delegation of inference, and the future of work.

---

1. How does the spectrum of digital agents (tools → assistants → collaborators → autonomous) map to different organizational functions?
2. Using the radiology AI case, explain why human-AI teams outperform either alone.
3. Which organizational decisions should be delegated to AI and which should remain human? What criteria should guide this choice?
4. How do AI failure modes (distribution shift, edge cases, alignment) differ from human failure modes (bias, fatigue)?
5. What organizational changes are required before an AI system can be effectively deployed?
6. How does "automation anxiety" affect organizational readiness for digital transformation?
7. When should an organization move from AI-as-tool to AI-as-collaborator?
8. How do you evaluate whether an AI system's generative model is well-calibrated for organizational needs?
9. Compare how a startup and a traditional organization should approach human-AI team design differently.
10. What governance structures are needed when AI systems make decisions that affect employees?
11. Design a human-AI team for a customer service function. Specify roles, escalation triggers, and oversight.
12. Map the decisions in your organization by suitability for AI delegation (high volume + rule-based vs. low volume + judgment-heavy).
13. Design a process for monitoring AI agent performance within an organization.
14. What training do human team members need to collaborate effectively with AI systems?
15. Compare the trust-building process for a new human team member versus a new AI system.
16. How should compensation, evaluation, and career paths change in organizations with AI colleagues?
17. Design an AI deployment framework that manages the transition from pilot to full-scale organizational integration.
18. When has an organization given too much autonomy to an AI system? How would you detect this?
19. How do cultural differences across organizations affect the acceptance and integration of AI agents?
20. Design a "delegation of inference" framework that determines which decisions are automated at what level.
