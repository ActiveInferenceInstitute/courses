## Learning Objectives

1. Frame AI systems as a new class of organizational agent with distinct capabilities and limitations
2. Distinguish augmentation from automation and identify appropriate use cases for each
3. Design delegation frameworks specifying which decisions are human, algorithmic, or hybrid
4. Address AI governance — accountability, transparency, bias, and goal alignment
5. Analyze how AI agents reshape organizational roles, skills, and the future of work

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **AI as agent**: Algorithmic systems with models, predictions, and action capacity
- **Augmentation vs. automation**: Enhancing humans vs. replacing human tasks
- **Delegation frameworks**: Allocating decisions between human and algorithmic agents
- **AI governance**: Accountability, transparency, bias, and alignment
- **Future of work**: How AI reshapes organizational roles and career paths

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Sections 1-3 (Organizational Systems, Collective Intelligence, Strategic Modeling)
- Basic familiarity with digital technology concepts (APIs, databases, cloud computing, machine learning)

## Recommended Case Studies

- **GitHub Copilot** — AI augmentation of software development
- **Autonomous vehicles at Waymo** — full automation of the driving task
- **JPMorgan's COIN system** — AI agents handling contract review at scale
