# Station: Planning (Digital Transformation)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Planning digital transformation as a multi-year organizational evolution requiring technical, cultural, and strategic coordination
- **Topics**: Technology roadmapping, digital maturity assessment, build vs. buy, migration planning, change management
- **Lab Style**: Implementation plan — technology assessment, architecture design, migration planning
- **Audience**: Business leaders, CTOs, digital transformation leaders, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional technology/business — connecting Active Inference to digital strategy and implementation

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Digital Transformation Roadmapping. All content should:

1. Frame digital transformation roadmapping as long-term policy selection: choosing the sequence of technology investments that maximizes organizational adaptive capacity
2. Design digital maturity assessments: where is the organization today, and what capabilities must it develop?
3. Apply build vs. buy vs. partner decisions: when to develop internally, purchase solutions, or form partnerships
4. Address migration planning: transitioning from legacy systems while maintaining operational continuity
5. Connect to organizational change management: technology change requires corresponding changes in culture, skills, and processes

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Technology roadmapping**: Long-term policy selection for digital capability development
- **Digital maturity**: Assessment of current organizational technology capability and gaps
- **Build vs. buy vs. partner**: Strategic option evaluation for technology acquisition
- **Migration planning**: Transitioning from legacy systems while maintaining operational continuity
- **Change management**: Aligning organizational culture, skills, and processes with technology evolution
