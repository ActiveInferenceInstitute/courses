## Learning Objectives

1. Frame digital transformation roadmapping as long-term policy selection for technology investment
2. Conduct digital maturity assessments to identify current capabilities and gaps
3. Apply build vs. buy vs. partner frameworks for technology acquisition decisions
4. Plan system migrations that maintain operational continuity during transition
5. Integrate technology change management with organizational culture and skill development

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Technology roadmapping**: Multi-year policy selection for digital capability building
- **Digital maturity**: Assessing current state and defining target state for technology capability
- **Build vs. buy vs. partner**: Strategic options for technology acquisition
- **Migration planning**: Legacy-to-modern system transitions with operational continuity
- **Change management**: Cultural, skill, and process adaptation alongside technology change

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Sections 1-3 (Organizational Systems, Collective Intelligence, Strategic Modeling)
- Basic familiarity with digital technology concepts (APIs, databases, cloud computing, machine learning)

## Recommended Case Studies

- **ING Bank's agile transformation** — multi-year roadmap from traditional bank to technology company
- **US Air Force digital transformation** — large-scale legacy system migration in a complex organization
- **Domino's Pizza** — from pizza company to technology company over a decade of digital transformation
