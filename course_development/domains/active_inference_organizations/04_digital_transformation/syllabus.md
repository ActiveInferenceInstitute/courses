# Syllabus: Digital Transformation

> **Active Inference for Organizations** | **Audience**: Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists

## Course Description

Digital Transformation explores Active Inference through ai integration, data-driven organizations. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn.
