## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Digital technology massively expanding organizational sensing capacity — but also creating new perceptual pathologies
- **Topics**: Data pipelines, analytics platforms, IoT sensing, real-time dashboards, data quality, sensor networks
- **Lab Style**: Implementation plan — technology assessment, architecture design, migration planning
- **Audience**: Business leaders, CTOs, digital transformation leaders, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional technology/business — connecting Active Inference to digital strategy and implementation

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Data Analytics and Organizational Sensing. All content should:

1. Frame data analytics as digital organizational perception — technology expanding sensing coverage, speed, and resolution
2. Design data pipelines as digital sensory channels: collection, transformation, and delivery of environmental signals
3. Address data quality as signal fidelity — garbage in, garbage out as a precision problem
4. Introduce IoT and sensor networks as physical-world sensing apparatus
5. Diagnose digital perceptual pathologies: data overload, dashboard blindness, metric fixation, and false precision

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Digital sensing**: Technology expanding organizational perception coverage, speed, and resolution
- **Data pipelines**: Digital sensory channels collecting, transforming, and delivering signals
- **Data quality**: Signal fidelity and reliability in digital sensing systems
- **IoT and sensors**: Physical-world sensing apparatus extending the organizational boundary
- **Digital perceptual pathologies**: Data overload, dashboard blindness, metric fixation
