## Learning Objectives

1. Frame data analytics as digital organizational perception — expanding sensing capacity
2. Design data pipelines as digital sensory channels for organizational intelligence
3. Evaluate data quality as signal fidelity — a precision problem for organizational inference
4. Understand IoT and sensor networks as physical-world sensing extensions
5. Diagnose digital perceptual pathologies: data overload, dashboard blindness, metric fixation

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Digital sensing**: Technology-amplified organizational perception
- **Data pipelines**: The infrastructure of digital sensory channels
- **Data quality**: Ensuring signal fidelity in digital sensing
- **IoT sensing**: Physical-world data collection extending organizational reach
- **Digital perceptual pathologies**: Overload, blindness, and false precision

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Sections 1-3 (Organizational Systems, Collective Intelligence, Strategic Modeling)
- Basic familiarity with digital technology concepts (APIs, databases, cloud computing, machine learning)

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Netflix's real-time analytics** — digital sensing driving content and recommendation decisions
- **Tesla's fleet telemetry** — millions of vehicles as a distributed sensor network
- **Target's predictive analytics** — inferring customer pregnancy from purchasing patterns
