# Digital Transformation

> Part of **Active Inference for Organizations** | [Curriculum Home](../README.md)

## Overview

**Digital Transformation** explores Active Inference through the lens of ai integration, data-driven organizations. This course is designed for Business leaders, managers, organizational scientists.

**Tone**: Business/management. Strategic planning, team dynamics, organizational cognition.
