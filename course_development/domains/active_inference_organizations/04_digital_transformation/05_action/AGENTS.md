# Station: Action (Digital Transformation)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Digital technology executing organizational actions — from simple automation to fully autonomous systems
- **Topics**: RPA, intelligent automation, workflow orchestration, autonomous systems, human-machine handoffs
- **Lab Style**: Implementation plan — technology assessment, architecture design, migration planning
- **Audience**: Business leaders, CTOs, digital transformation leaders, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional technology/business — connecting Active Inference to digital strategy and implementation

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Process Automation and Digital Action. All content should:

1. Frame automation as algorithmic action: technology executing organizational policies at scale and speed
2. Distinguish levels of automation: manual, assisted, supervised, conditional, and full automation
3. Design human-machine handoffs: clear protocols for when automated systems should defer to human judgment
4. Address automation failure modes: brittle automation breaking on edge cases, automation complacency, and skill atrophy
5. Connect to organizational design: how automation changes roles, processes, and organizational structure

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Algorithmic action**: Technology executing organizational policies at machine scale and speed
- **Automation levels**: Manual through full autonomy — a spectrum of human-machine action allocation
- **Human-machine handoffs**: Protocols for transitioning between automated and human-driven execution
- **Automation failure modes**: Brittleness, complacency, and skill atrophy
- **Process orchestration**: Coordinating human and automated actions in complex workflows
