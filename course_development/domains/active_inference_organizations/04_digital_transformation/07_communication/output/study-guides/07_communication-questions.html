Digital Transformation — Module 07: Communication — Discussion Questions

20 questions on digital channels, omnichannel communication, API architecture, and collaboration platforms.

  1. How does Disney's MagicBand exemplify omnichannel communication coherence?
  2. When does adding more communication channels improve organizational coordination versus creating fragmentation?
  3. How does API architecture parallel organizational communication design?
  4. What are the most common failures in omnichannel customer communication?
  5. How do digital collaboration tools (Slack, Teams) change organizational power dynamics?
  6. When should communication be synchronous (real-time) versus asynchronous?
  7. How does "channel proliferation" in digital organizations create the paradox of more communication but less understanding?
  8. What happens when system-to-system (API) communication fails? How does this ripple through the organization?
  9. Compare how remote-first and office-first organizations should design their digital communication architectures.
  10. How does searchability of digital communication change organizational memory?
  11. Map the communication channels in your organization. Is there redundancy? Is there fragmentation?
  12. Design an omnichannel communication architecture for a customer-facing organization.
  13. Identify your organization's most critical API dependencies. What happens if they fail?
  14. Assess whether your organization has "tool fatigue" from too many digital communication platforms.
  15. Design a communication protocol for a distributed team spanning multiple time zones.
  16. How should organizations manage the boundary between formal and informal digital communication?
  17. Compare the communication effectiveness of email, Slack, video calls, and shared documents for different types of organizational communication.
  18. When should auto-notifications be designed to reduce information loading versus ensuring awareness?
  19. Design a "digital communication health" assessment for your organization.
  20. How will large language models (ChatGPT, copilots) change organizational communication patterns?
