# Lab: Digital Transformation Assessment — Organizational Technology Readiness

## Objective

> **Learning Goal:** Conduct a comprehensive digital transformation assessment for an organization, evaluating current digital maturity, identifying high-value transformation opportunities, and developing a phased implementation roadmap that addresses technology, people, and process dimensions.

This exercise puts you in the role of a digital transformation consultant conducting a readiness assessment and creating an implementation plan.

---

## Scenario

An organization is considering a significant digital transformation initiative. Leadership knows they need to "go digital" but lacks a clear understanding of where they stand, what to prioritize, and how to sequence the transformation.

Choose one of the following organizations (or substitute your own):

- **CityGov Municipal Services**: A city government looking to digitize citizen services — permit applications, utility billing, public records — currently running on legacy systems built in the 2000s
- **MediCare Clinics**: A regional healthcare provider with 15 clinics that wants to implement telehealth, electronic health records, and AI-assisted diagnostics while maintaining patient trust
- **Heritage Manufacturing**: A 100-year-old industrial manufacturer that needs to implement IoT monitoring, predictive maintenance, and supply chain digitalization to compete with born-digital competitors
