## Learning Objectives

1. Define sociotechnical systems and explain why digital transformation is about system design, not technology adoption
2. Apply the Markov blanket concept to understand how technology reshapes organizational boundaries
3. Distinguish digitization, digitalization, and digital transformation as three levels of organizational change
4. Analyze technical debt as accumulated model error in organizational infrastructure
5. Design human-technology interfaces that leverage complementary human and algorithmic capabilities

## Key Organizational Concepts

- **Sociotechnical systems**: Integrated human-technology organizational design
- **Digital Markov blanket**: Technology constituting the organizational boundary
- **Three levels of change**: Format, process, and business model transformation
- **Technical debt**: Accumulated technology model error requiring future correction
- **Human-technology complementarity**: Combining human and machine strengths

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Sections 1-3 (Organizational Systems, Collective Intelligence, Strategic Modeling)
- Basic familiarity with digital technology concepts (APIs, databases, cloud computing, machine learning)

## Recommended Case Studies

- **Amazon's algorithmic organization** — technology as the organizational boundary itself
