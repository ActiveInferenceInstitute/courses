## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Digital organizations as hybrid sociotechnical systems where technology reshapes the organizational Markov blanket
- **Topics**: Sociotechnical systems, digital boundaries, digitization vs. digitalization vs. transformation, technical debt, human-technology interface
- **Lab Style**: Implementation plan — technology assessment, architecture design, migration planning
- **Audience**: Business leaders, CTOs, digital transformation leaders, organizational scientists
- **Tone**: Professional technology/business — connecting Active Inference to digital strategy and implementation

## Domain Integration Guidelines

This module covers Digital Organizations as Sociotechnical Systems. All content should:

1. Frame digital transformation as system redesign, not technology adoption — changing the organization's Markov blanket through technology
2. Distinguish three levels of digital change: digitization (format), digitalization (process), and digital transformation (business model)
3. Analyze technology as Markov blanket: APIs, databases, and algorithms as organizational boundary components
4. Address technical debt as accumulated model error — the gap between what systems assume and what they need to do
5. Design human-technology interfaces that leverage complementary strengths of human and algorithmic agents

## Active Inference Concepts for This Module

- **Sociotechnical system**: Human and technology components forming an integrated organizational whole
- **Digital Markov blanket**: Technology (APIs, databases, algorithms) as organizational boundary
- **Three levels of digital change**: Digitization, digitalization, and digital transformation
- **Technical debt**: Accumulated model error in organizational technology infrastructure
- **Human-technology interface**: Integration points between human and algorithmic agents
