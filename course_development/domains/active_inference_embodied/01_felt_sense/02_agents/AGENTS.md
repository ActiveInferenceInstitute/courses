# Station: Agents (Felt Sense)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Body awareness & interoception
- **Topics**: Agents as embodied selves -- the one who notices, the one who acts
- **Lab Style**: Somatic Exercise
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module introduces **agents** through the first-person experience of being a living, sensing, acting body. Content must:

- Present the agent not as an abstract decision-maker but as the felt sense of being someone -- the experiential quality of aliveness and agency.
- Distinguish between voluntary and involuntary bodily processes to illuminate how agency is a spectrum, not a binary. The heartbeat acts without your will; reaching for a cup involves intention.
- Connect to interoception: the agent's sense of self is grounded in the continuous stream of internal body signals (heartbeat, breath, gut).
- Reference the minimal self (Gallagher) -- the pre-reflective, bodily sense of being a subject of experience.
- Integrate Active Inference: the agent is a system that minimizes free energy by predicting its sensory states and acting to confirm those predictions.
- Introduce morphological computation: the body's physical structure contributes to the agent's capacities. The shape of your hand is part of how you grasp.
- Lab activities should involve noticing the felt difference between doing and being done to, sensing intention before movement.
