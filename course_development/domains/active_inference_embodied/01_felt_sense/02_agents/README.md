# Module 02: Agents

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## You Are the One Who Notices

Part of **Felt Sense**.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Experience** the minimal self as the pre-reflective, bodily sense of being a subject of experience
2. **Distinguish** voluntary from involuntary bodily processes and explore agency as a spectrum
3. **Sense** motor intention -- the felt impulse that precedes deliberate movement
4. **Understand** the embodied agent as a system that minimizes free energy through prediction and action
5. **Explore** morphological computation: how the body's physical structure contributes to the agent's capacities
6. **Investigate** the felt difference between acting and being acted upon through active and passive touch

## Key Concepts

- **Minimal self**: The most basic, pre-reflective sense of being someone (Gallagher)
- **Agency spectrum**: From fully involuntary (heartbeat) to semi-voluntary (breath) to fully voluntary (reaching)
- **Motor intention**: The body's readiness to move before movement begins
- **Active Inference agent**: A system that predicts its sensory states and acts to confirm those predictions
- **Morphological computation**: The body's physical form doing computational work (hand shape enabling grasp)
- **Active versus passive touch**: How agency changes the quality of perceptual experience
