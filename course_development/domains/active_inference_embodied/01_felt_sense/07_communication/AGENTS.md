## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Body awareness & interoception
- **Topics**: Communication as resonance between bodies -- co-regulation, mirroring, and intercorporeality
- **Lab Style**: Somatic Exercise
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module introduces **communication** as a fundamentally embodied, intercorporeal process. Content must:

- Frame communication not as the transmission of information between disembodied minds but as the resonance, synchrony, and mutual influence between living bodies.
- Present co-regulation as the foundation of embodied communication: two bodies in proximity naturally influence each other's autonomic states. A calm presence can soothe; an anxious presence can agitate.
- Connect to Active Inference: communication involves the alignment of generative models between agents. When bodies synchronize (breathing together, moving in rhythm), they are minimizing free energy across a shared Markov blanket.
- Reference intercorporeality (Merleau-Ponty): the lived experience of being bodily present with another person, where the boundary between self and other becomes fluid.
- Introduce participatory sense-making (Di Paolo & De Jaegher): meaning arises through the dynamic interaction between embodied agents, not through the decoding of pre-formed messages.
- Discuss mirror neurons and empathic resonance as embodied mechanisms for understanding others through felt simulation.
- Lab activities should involve partner exercises: mirroring movement, synchronizing breath, noticing how proximity and orientation affect felt connection.
