# Module 07: Communication

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Resonance Between Bodies

Part of **Felt Sense**.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Experience** communication as bodily resonance -- the mutual influence between living bodies before and beyond words
2. **Explore** co-regulation: how proximity and presence affect autonomic states between people
3. **Practice** mirroring exercises that reveal how bodies align their generative models through movement
4. **Investigate** intercorporeality (Merleau-Ponty): the fluid felt boundary between self and other in shared space
5. **Connect** participatory sense-making (Di Paolo & De Jaegher) to the experience of meaning emerging through bodily interaction
6. **Discover** how voice, breath, and vibration create physical coupling between communicating bodies

## Key Concepts

- **Co-regulation**: Two bodies mutually influencing each other's autonomic and emotional states
- **Intercorporeality**: The lived experience of being bodily present with another, where self-other boundaries shift
- **Participatory sense-making**: Meaning arising through dynamic interaction rather than message decoding
- **Mirror movement**: Alignment of generative models through synchronized action
- **Interpersonal Markov blanket**: The felt boundary between self and other that shifts with distance and orientation
- **Embodied empathy**: Understanding others through felt resonance and bodily simulation

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Modules 01-06 recommended
- A partner is strongly recommended (solo alternatives provided)
