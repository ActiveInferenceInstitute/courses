> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Feeling the Boundary of Your Skin

Part of **Felt Sense**.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Experience** the body's boundary (skin) as a living Markov blanket that separates internal from external states
2. **Distinguish** interoception (sensing the inside) from exteroception (sensing the outside) through direct bodily awareness
3. **Explore** how proprioception contributes to the body's sense of itself as a bounded system
4. **Recognize** allostasis as the body's predictive regulation -- maintaining itself by anticipating rather than merely reacting
5. **Apply** the 4E cognition framework (embodied, embedded, enacted, extended) to the concept of a system boundary
6. **Investigate** how tool use and environmental coupling can extend or shift the felt boundary of the system

## Key Concepts

- **System boundary as skin**: The Markov blanket is not an abstraction -- it is the felt surface of your body, where inside meets outside
- **Interoception and exteroception**: Two streams of sensory information that define the system's relationship to itself and its environment
- **Proprioception**: The body's sense of its own position, posture, and movement in space
- **Allostasis**: Predictive self-regulation that maintains the system's viability over time
- **4E cognition**: Systems are embodied (have bodies), embedded (situated), enacted (maintained through activity), and extended (can incorporate tools)

## Prerequisites

- No prior knowledge required
- Willingness to attend to bodily sensations
