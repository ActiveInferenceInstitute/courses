> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Body awareness & interoception
- **Topics**: Systems as lived boundaries -- the skin, the breath, the felt edge of self
- **Lab Style**: Somatic Exercise
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module introduces **systems** through the direct experience of having a body with a boundary. Content must:

- Frame the Markov blanket as the skin -- the living membrane where inside meets outside. The system boundary is not abstract; it is the felt surface of your body.
- Draw on interoception (sensing the inside) and exteroception (sensing the outside) as the two sides of the system boundary.
- Connect to proprioception: the body's sense of its own position and posture is one way the system "knows" its own internal states.
- Reference allostasis as the system's predictive regulation -- the body maintaining itself not by reacting but by anticipating.
- Integrate 4E cognition: the system is embodied (has a body), embedded (situated in an environment), enacted (maintained through ongoing activity), and extended (its boundary can shift with tools and technology).
- Use phenomenological language drawn from Merleau-Ponty and Gendlin. Avoid formal notation.
- Lab activities should involve actual body-boundary awareness exercises, not computational simulations.
