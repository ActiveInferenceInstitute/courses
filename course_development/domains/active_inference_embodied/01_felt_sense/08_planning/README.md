# Module 08: Planning

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Listening to What Wants to Happen

Part of **Felt Sense**.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Experience** planning as the body's preparation for future action -- postural adjustments, weight shifts, and autonomic changes that precede deliberate thought
2. **Explore** felt evaluation of possible futures: how the body simulates upcoming actions and generates somatic markers of preference
3. **Practice** emergent movement as planning without a plan -- the body selecting action policies in real time
4. **Understand** allostatic planning: how the body predicts future regulatory demands and adjusts in advance
5. **Connect** expected free energy to the felt sense of bodily readiness -- which futures the body leans toward or pulls away from
6. **Investigate** how the habitual body (Merleau-Ponty) provides a repertoire of action possibilities that support embodied planning

## Key Concepts

- **Embodied anticipation**: The body preparing for action before conscious intention forms
- **Expected free energy**: The body's felt evaluation of which possible futures will reduce uncertainty
- **Allostatic planning**: Predictive regulation of future homeostatic needs (hunger, arousal, rest)
- **Emergent movement**: Action arising from the body's real-time free energy minimization without deliberation
- **Kinesthetic anticipation**: The forward-looking quality built into movement (Sheets-Johnstone)
- **Habitual body as planning resource**: Past bodily experience shaping present readiness for future action

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Modules 01-07 recommended
- A space where you can stand and move
