# Module 04: Cognition

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## The Body Thinks Too

Part of **Felt Sense**.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Challenge** the assumption that thinking happens only in the brain by experiencing cognition as a whole-body process
2. **Identify** somatic markers -- the body's felt signals (gut feelings, chest tightness) that guide decision-making
3. **Explore** how posture, gesture, and breath shape cognitive processes in real time
4. **Apply** the 4E cognition framework to understand thinking as embodied, embedded, enacted, and extended
5. **Connect** interoceptive inference to cognitive clarity: how internal body signals participate in reasoning and attention
6. **Experience** the "aha" moment as a bodily event -- the felt resolution of prediction error

## Key Concepts

- **Embodied cognition**: Thinking is shaped by the body's morphology, senses, and movement repertoire (Shapiro, Gallagher)
- **Somatic markers**: Body-based feeling-states that guide decisions before conscious deliberation (Damasio)
- **Interoceptive inference**: Internal body signals as integral to cognitive processing, not mere background noise
- **Gesture and thought**: Hand movements as a constituent part of thinking, not just expression
- **Breath and cognition**: Respiratory patterns modulating arousal, attention, and cognitive performance
- **4E cognition**: Cognition as embodied, embedded, enacted, and extended
