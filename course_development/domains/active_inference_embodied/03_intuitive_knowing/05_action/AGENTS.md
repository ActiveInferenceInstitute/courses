## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Pattern-feeling without numbers
- **Topics**: Action as intuition-guided movement -- acting from bodily wisdom rather than deliberation
- **Lab Style**: Reflective Journaling
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **action** through the lens of embodied intuition, gut feelings, and the body's capacity for pattern recognition without conscious analysis. Content must:

- Frame action through the experience of intuitive, non-deliberate movement: the hand that reaches for the right tool, the feet that find the path, the body that adjusts its posture in conversation.
- Connect to the flow state (Csikszentmihalyi): the experience of action arising effortlessly from deep engagement, where the body's intuitive knowledge guides behavior.
- Present skilled intuitive action as the body's highly refined generative model producing accurate predictions with minimal error.
- Reference Varela's 'ethical know-how': the capacity to act wisely in complex situations through embodied intuition rather than rule-following.
- Integrate Active Inference: intuitive action is the selection of policies that minimize expected free energy, guided by well-calibrated somatic priors.
- Journaling activities should involve reflecting on experiences of intuitive action and contrasting them with over-deliberated action.
