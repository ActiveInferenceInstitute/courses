# Lab: Resonance Between Bodies

## Objective

Experience embodied communication through reflective journaling that explores co-regulation, mirroring, and resonance between bodies through the lens of pattern-feeling without numbers.

## Prerequisites

- A quiet, comfortable space for 25 minutes
- Willingness to engage in reflective journaling
- No prior experience required

## Part 1: Arriving (5 minutes)

Sit quietly with your journal. Take three slow breaths. Before writing anything, check in with your body. Notice any sensations, feelings, or subtle knowings that are present right now.

If with a partner, sit facing each other. If alone, recall a recent meaningful interaction. Notice: communication between bodies begins before words, through presence, rhythm, and resonance.
