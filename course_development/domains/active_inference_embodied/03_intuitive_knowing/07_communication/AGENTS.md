## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Pattern-feeling without numbers
- **Topics**: Communication as empathic knowing -- intuiting another's state through the body
- **Lab Style**: Reflective Journaling
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **communication** through the lens of embodied intuition, gut feelings, and the body's capacity for pattern recognition without conscious analysis. Content must:

- Frame communication through empathic intuition: the capacity to sense another person's emotional state, intentions, and needs through bodily cues.
- Connect to mirror neuron research and embodied simulation theory: understanding others by internally simulating their actions and feeling states.
- Present empathic knowing as the body's generative model extending to predict another person's internal states.
- Reference De Jaegher's participatory sense-making: meaning arising through embodied interaction, not just observation.
- Integrate Active Inference: empathic communication is the alignment of generative models through shared interoceptive and motor signals.
- Journaling activities should involve recording experiences of empathic intuition and reflecting on what bodily cues informed the understanding.
