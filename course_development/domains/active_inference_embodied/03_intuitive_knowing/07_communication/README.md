# Module 07: Communication

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Resonance Between Bodies

Part of **Intuitive Knowing**.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Experience** communication as embodied resonance through pattern-feeling without numbers
2. **Explore** co-regulation: how bodies mutually influence each other's states through presence
3. **Practice** mirroring and synchrony as forms of generative model alignment
4. **Investigate** intercorporeality and the fluid boundary between self and other
5. **Connect** participatory sense-making to the embodied foundations of shared meaning

## Key Concepts

- **Embodied communication**: Communication as experienced through the body, not just theorized about
- **Active Inference**: Communication as a component of the free energy minimization process
- **4E Cognition**: Communication as embodied, embedded, enacted, and extended
- **Interoception and proprioception**: How inner sensing shapes communication
- **Sensorimotor coupling**: The inseparability of communication from bodily engagement
- **Pattern-feeling without numbers**: The specific lens this course brings to communication

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Modules 01-06 recommended
- Willingness to engage in reflective journaling activities

## Estimated Time

- Module reading: 20 minutes
- Lab (reflective journaling): 25 minutes
- Questions and reflection: 15 minutes
