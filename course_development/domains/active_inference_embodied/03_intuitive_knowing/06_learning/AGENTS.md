## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Pattern-feeling without numbers
- **Topics**: Learning as deepening intuition -- how experience refines the body's implicit knowledge
- **Lab Style**: Reflective Journaling
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **learning** through the lens of embodied intuition, gut feelings, and the body's capacity for pattern recognition without conscious analysis. Content must:

- Frame learning as the progressive deepening of embodied intuition: with experience, the body's pattern recognition becomes more refined, more subtle, more reliable.
- Connect to expertise research: experts perceive patterns that novices cannot, act without deliberation, and have intuitions that consistently prove accurate.
- Present the transition from novice to expert as a journey from explicit, rule-following cognition to implicit, intuition-guided action (Dreyfus).
- Reference the body's accumulation of implicit knowledge through repeated engagement with complex environments.
- Integrate Active Inference: intuitive learning is the slow refinement of the generative model's deep parameters through extensive experience.
- Journaling activities should involve reflecting on the evolution of personal intuitions over time in a specific domain.
