## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Pattern-feeling without numbers
- **Topics**: Perception as felt pattern recognition -- knowing before you can say why
- **Lab Style**: Reflective Journaling
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **perception** through the lens of embodied intuition, gut feelings, and the body's capacity for pattern recognition without conscious analysis. Content must:

- Frame perception through the lens of intuitive pattern recognition: the body perceives wholes before parts, patterns before details.
- Connect to the Gestalt tradition: perception naturally organizes into meaningful patterns -- figures against grounds, groupings, closures.
- Present intuitive perception as the generative model's prior predictions shaping what is perceived before attention narrows.
- Reference 'thin-slicing' research: the body's capacity to make accurate judgments from minimal information, drawing on deep pattern recognition.
- Integrate Active Inference: intuitive perception is high-precision prior predictions that accurately anticipate sensory input.
- Journaling activities should involve recording first impressions and gut reactions, then analyzing what patterns the body detected.
