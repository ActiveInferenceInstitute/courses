# Module 03: Perception

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## What Does Surprise Feel Like?

Part of **Intuitive Knowing**.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Experience** perception as an active, embodied process through pattern-feeling without numbers
2. **Explore** sensorimotor contingencies: how what you perceive changes with how you move and attend
3. **Feel** prediction error as bodily surprise and investigate how the body updates its predictions
4. **Connect** interoceptive and exteroceptive perception as two modes of embodied inference
5. **Investigate** precision weighting: how attention amplifies specific sensory channels

## Key Concepts

- **Embodied perception**: Perception as experienced through the body, not just theorized about
- **Active Inference**: Perception as a component of the free energy minimization process
- **4E Cognition**: Perception as embodied, embedded, enacted, and extended
- **Interoception and proprioception**: How inner sensing shapes perception
- **Sensorimotor coupling**: The inseparability of perception from bodily engagement
- **Pattern-feeling without numbers**: The specific lens this course brings to perception

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Modules 01-02 recommended
- Willingness to engage in reflective journaling activities
