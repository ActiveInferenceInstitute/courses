## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Pattern-feeling without numbers
- **Topics**: Systems as felt patterns -- recognizing boundaries and structures through intuition
- **Lab Style**: Reflective Journaling
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **systems** through the lens of embodied intuition, gut feelings, and the body's capacity for pattern recognition without conscious analysis. Content must:

- Frame the system boundary not through explicit identification but through the intuitive sense of where one thing ends and another begins.
- Connect to the body's capacity for pattern recognition: we intuitively sense the edges of situations, relationships, and environments without formal analysis.
- Present the Markov blanket as the intuited boundary -- the felt sense of containment, of being within something, of being separate.
- Reference Gendlin's felt sense: the body's holistic, pre-conceptual apprehension of a situation as a whole system.
- Integrate Active Inference: intuitive pattern recognition is the generative model operating at levels below explicit awareness, generating predictions that are experienced as 'gut feelings.'
- Journaling activities should involve writing about the felt sense of boundaries in life situations -- where does this relationship end? Where does this project begin?
