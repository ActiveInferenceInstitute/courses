# Lab: Intuitive Knowing -- Reflective Journaling Series

## Objective

Develop embodied understanding of Active Inference through reflective journaling practices focused on embodied intuition, gut feelings, and the body's capacity for pattern recognition without conscious analysis. This lab series introduces the intuitive knowing perspective as a way of directly experiencing the principles of embodied cognition.

## Prerequisites

- A quiet space where you can engage in reflective journaling for 30 minutes
- A journal and pen
- No prior experience required -- only willingness to attend to bodily experience

## Part 1: Orientation (5 minutes)

Sit with your journal. Take three breaths. Before writing, check in with your body -- what is the overall quality of your felt sense right now? Begin with a brief free-write about your current bodily state.

## Part 2: Core Exploration (10 minutes)

Write continuously about your bodily experience for 10 minutes. Do not censor or edit. Let the writing follow the body's lead. When you notice a felt sense, stay with it and describe it in as much detail as you can.

In Active Inference terms, you are increasing the precision weighting of interoceptive and proprioceptive signals, making the body's ongoing free energy minimization process visible to awareness.

## Part 3: Connection to Active Inference (10 minutes)

Now bring the following Active Inference concepts to your bodily experience:

1. **Prediction**: What is your body predicting right now? What does it expect to happen next?
2. **Surprise**: Where in your body do you notice surprise -- sensations that defy expectation?
3. **Action**: How is your body acting to reduce surprise? What adjustments is it making?
4. **Learning**: What has your body learned from this practice session? What predictions have updated?
