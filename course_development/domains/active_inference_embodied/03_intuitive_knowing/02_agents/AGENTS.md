> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Pattern-feeling without numbers
- **Topics**: Agents as intuitive selves -- the body's wisdom about who is acting and who is acted upon
- **Lab Style**: Reflective Journaling
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **agents** through the lens of embodied intuition, gut feelings, and the body's capacity for pattern recognition without conscious analysis. Content must:

- Present agency through the intuitive sense of authorship: the pre-reflective knowing of when you are the one doing something versus when something is happening to you.
- Connect to Dreyfus's phenomenology of expertise: the skilled practitioner does not deliberate but acts from intuitive bodily knowing.
- Frame the intuitive agent as one who has internalized their generative model so deeply that predictions and actions flow without conscious effort.
- Reference somatic markers: the body's intuitive signals that guide action selection.
- Integrate Active Inference: intuitive agency is high-precision, well-calibrated prediction that requires minimal conscious correction.
- Journaling activities should involve reflecting on moments of intuitive action -- when did you just know what to do?
