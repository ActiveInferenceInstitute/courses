## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Pattern-feeling without numbers
- **Topics**: Planning as trusting intuition -- letting the body's wisdom guide future action
- **Lab Style**: Reflective Journaling
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **planning** through the lens of embodied intuition, gut feelings, and the body's capacity for pattern recognition without conscious analysis. Content must:

- Frame planning through the lens of trusting embodied intuition: the body often knows what to do next before the conscious mind can articulate a plan.
- Connect to the concept of kairos (right timing): the intuitive sense of when to act, when to wait, and when to change course.
- Present allostatic planning through the body's felt anticipation of needs and readiness for action.
- Reference decision-making research showing that intuitive judgments often outperform analytical reasoning in complex, uncertain situations.
- Integrate Active Inference: intuitive planning is the evaluation of expected free energy through high-precision interoceptive priors rather than explicit simulation.
- Journaling activities should involve body-based decision-making exercises: sensing what the body leans toward and trusting that signal.
