# Notation Table: Active Inference for Embodied Experience

> Standard symbols and notation used throughout the curriculum.
> Level: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding

## Core Active Inference Symbols

| Symbol | Plain English | Embodied Meaning | Formal Meaning | First Introduced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| System | A thing with a boundary | Your body as a living envelope separating inside from outside | An entity with internal and external states separated by a Markov blanket | Course 1, M1 |
| Agent | Something that acts | You, the embodied being who senses and moves | A system that minimizes free energy through perception and action | Course 1, M2 |
| Prediction | A guess about what will happen | The body's anticipation of what it will feel next | Expected sensory input generated by the generative model | Course 1, M3 |
| Surprise | When reality does not match expectation | That jolt when the ground is not where your foot expected it | Negative log probability of an observation under the model | Course 1, M3 |
| Action | Making something happen | Moving your body to bring the world closer to your expectations | Changing external states to reduce prediction error | Course 1, M5 |
| Learning | Getting better over time | The body acquiring new habits, postures, and sensitivities | Updating model parameters to improve future predictions | Course 1, M6 |

## Embodied Cognition Key Terms

| Term | Plain English | Embodied Meaning | First Introduced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interoception | Sensing the inside | Feeling your heartbeat, breath, gut sensations, temperature | Course 1, M1 |
| Exteroception | Sensing the outside | Seeing, hearing, touching, tasting, smelling the world | Course 1, M3 |
| Proprioception | Sensing your own position | Knowing where your limbs are without looking | Course 1, M1 |
| Allostasis | Predictive regulation | The body adjusting in advance of anticipated needs | Course 2, M1 |
| Markov Blanket | A statistical boundary | The skin, senses, and muscles that separate you from the world | Course 1, M1 |
| Sensorimotor Contingency | Action-perception pairing | How moving your hand changes what you feel | Course 1, M3 |
| Morphological Computation | Body as computer | How the shape of your hand helps you grasp without thinking | Course 4, M1 |
| Enactivism | Cognition through action | Understanding arises from doing, not just from thinking | Course 3, M1 |
| Affordance | Action possibility | What the environment offers your body (a chair affords sitting) | Course 4, M3 |
| Somatic Marker | Body-based signal | A gut feeling that guides decisions before conscious thought | Course 1, M4 |
| Felt Sense | Bodily knowing | The vague, holistic bodily awareness of a situation | Course 1, M1 |
| 4E Cognition | Four dimensions of mind | Cognition as embodied, embedded, enacted, and extended | Course 1, M1 |
