# Learning Pathways: Active Inference for Embodied Experience

> Suggested routes through the curriculum for anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding of embodied cognition.

## Pathway 1: Sequential (Recommended)

Complete one course fully before starting the next. This pathway builds from inner sensation outward to movement in the world.

1. **Felt Sense** -- Begin with interoception and body awareness. Learn to notice the boundary of your skin, the sense of being an embodied agent, and how surprise registers in the body before the mind.
2. **Living Presence** -- Deepen through breath and heartbeat. Explore how the autonomic nervous system sustains your presence, how mindfulness practice reveals the body's ongoing self-regulation.
3. **Intuitive Knowing** -- Discover pattern-recognition without numbers. Through reflective journaling, explore how the body knows things before conscious reasoning, how gut feelings relate to prediction, and how embodied intuition guides action.
4. **Moving Through the World** -- Culminate in full-body engagement. Through dance, yoga, and martial arts perspectives, experience how action and perception are inseparable, how the body thinks through movement, and how embodied planning unfolds in real time.

## Pathway 3: Interest-Driven

Start with whichever course matches your background or curiosity, then follow connections.

- **If you are a meditator or contemplative practitioner**: Start with Course 2 (Living Presence), then Course 1 (Felt Sense).
- **If you are a dancer, athlete, or movement practitioner**: Start with Course 4 (Moving Through the World), then Course 1 (Felt Sense).
- **If you are a therapist or counselor**: Start with Course 1 (Felt Sense), then Course 3 (Intuitive Knowing).
- **If you are a scientist or philosopher**: Start with Course 3 (Intuitive Knowing), then Course 2 (Living Presence).

## Pathway 4: Thematic Threads

Follow a specific theme across all modules and courses.

- **The Interoception Thread**: Course 1 M1, Course 2 M1, Course 1 M3, Course 2 M3 -- tracing how inner sensing shapes the boundary, perception, and action.
- **The Sensorimotor Thread**: Course 4 M1, Course 4 M3, Course 4 M5, Course 1 M5 -- following the coupling of movement and perception.
- **The Social Body Thread**: All M7 modules across courses -- exploring how bodies communicate, resonate, and co-regulate.
- **The Embodied Planning Thread**: All M8 modules across courses -- understanding how the body prepares for and unfolds into future action.
