# Syllabus: Moving Through the World

> **Active Inference: Embodied Experience** | **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding

## Course Description

Moving Through the World explores Active Inference through dance, yoga, martial arts. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn.
