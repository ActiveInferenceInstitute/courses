# Lab: Moving Through the World -- Movement Lab Series

## Objective

Develop embodied understanding of Active Inference through movement lab practices focused on full-body movement, sensorimotor coupling, and the inseparability of action and perception. This lab series introduces the moving through the world perspective as a way of directly experiencing the principles of embodied cognition.

## Prerequisites

- A quiet space where you can engage in movement lab for 30 minutes
- Comfortable clothing and space to move
- No prior experience required -- only willingness to attend to bodily experience

## Part 1: Orientation (5 minutes)

Stand comfortably. Shake out your hands and feet. Roll your neck and shoulders. Take three breaths. Begin to move gently, finding your body's natural rhythm.

Through the lens of dance, yoga, martial arts, notice what becomes visible about your embodied experience that might otherwise go unnoticed.

## Part 2: Core Exploration (10 minutes)

Move continuously for 10 minutes, exploring different qualities: slow and fluid, sharp and quick, heavy and grounded, light and airy. Let each quality teach you something about how the body relates to the principles of Active Inference.

In Active Inference terms, you are increasing the precision weighting of interoceptive and proprioceptive signals, making the body's ongoing free energy minimization process visible to awareness.

## Part 3: Connection to Active Inference (10 minutes)

Now bring the following Active Inference concepts to your bodily experience:

1. **Prediction**: What is your body predicting right now? What does it expect to happen next?
2. **Surprise**: Where in your body do you notice surprise -- sensations that defy expectation?
3. **Action**: How is your body acting to reduce surprise? What adjustments is it making?
4. **Learning**: What has your body learned from this practice session? What predictions have updated?
