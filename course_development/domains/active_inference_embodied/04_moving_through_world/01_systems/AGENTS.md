## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Dance, yoga, martial arts
- **Topics**: Systems as movement boundaries -- the body in space, the space around the body
- **Lab Style**: Movement Lab
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **systems** through the lens of full-body movement, sensorimotor coupling, and the inseparability of action and perception. Content must:

- Frame the system boundary through movement: the kinesphere (the space the body can reach) defines the system's active boundary.
- Connect to Laban Movement Analysis: the body creates and inhabits spatial patterns that define its relationship with the environment.
- Present the Markov blanket as a dynamic, movement-defined boundary: the system's edge shifts with every gesture, step, and turn.
- Reference proxemics: how the body's spatial behavior (personal space, territorial behavior) reflects the system's boundary maintenance.
- Integrate Active Inference: the moving system continuously updates its predictions about spatial relationships, and prediction error drives exploration.
- Movement activities should involve kinesphere exploration, boundary-sensing in pairs, and spatial orientation exercises.
