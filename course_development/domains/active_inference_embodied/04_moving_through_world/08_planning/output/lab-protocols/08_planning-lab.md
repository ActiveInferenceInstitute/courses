# Lab: Listening to What Wants to Happen

## Objective

Explore embodied planning through movement lab that reveals how the body anticipates, prepares for, and evaluates future action through the lens of dance, yoga, martial arts.

## Prerequisites

- A quiet, comfortable space for 25 minutes
- Willingness to engage in movement lab
- No prior experience required

## Part 1: Arriving (5 minutes)

Stand in a comfortable position. Shake out your hands and feet gently. Roll your shoulders. Take three breaths. Begin to sway gently, finding your body's natural rhythm of movement.

Notice: your body is already planning. It is adjusting posture, regulating breath, and anticipating what comes next. Through dance, yoga, martial arts, attend to the body's forward-looking quality.

## Part 2: Felt Futures (7 minutes)

Think of two possible activities. For each, let the body simulate the experience. Through dance, yoga, martial arts, notice the somatic response -- does the body lean toward one future or pull away?


<div style="border: 1px solid #ccc; border-radius: 4px; min-height: 96px; padding: 8px; margin: 8px 0; background-color: #fafafa;"><em style="color: #999; font-size: 0.85em;">Write your response here</em></div>
(Describe the bodily response to each imagined future. Did the body have a preference? How did dance, yoga, martial arts inform this evaluation?)

## Part 3: Emergent Action (7 minutes)

Rather than planning what to do next, ask the body: What wants to happen? Wait. Let a movement or action emerge without deliberation. Through dance, yoga, martial arts, trust the body's intelligence.

## Part 4: Reflection (5 minutes)

Through dance, yoga, martial arts, reflect on the relationship between planning and presence.
