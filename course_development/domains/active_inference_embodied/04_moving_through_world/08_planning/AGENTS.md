# Station: Planning (Moving Through the World)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Dance, yoga, martial arts
- **Topics**: Planning as choreographic intelligence -- the body composing sequences of future action
- **Lab Style**: Movement Lab
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **planning** through the lens of full-body movement, sensorimotor coupling, and the inseparability of action and perception. Content must:

- Frame planning through choreographic thinking: the capacity to compose, anticipate, and sequence movement in time and space.
- Connect to the martial artist's reading of the opponent: planning as the real-time anticipation of another's movement and the preparation of appropriate responses.
- Present the yogi's practice design as embodied planning: sequencing postures to create a coherent journey through the body.
- Reference the concept of motor imagery: the body's capacity to simulate movement without executing it, as a form of embodied planning.
- Integrate Active Inference: choreographic planning is the evaluation of action sequences by simulating their expected sensory outcomes (expected free energy) through motor imagery.
- Movement activities should involve choreographic exercises, improvisation with constraints, and the practice of motor imagery for movement planning.
