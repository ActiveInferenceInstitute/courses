# Module 08: Planning

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Listening to What Wants to Happen

Part of **Moving Through the World**.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Experience** planning as embodied anticipation through dance, yoga, martial arts
2. **Explore** the body's preparation for future action through postural and autonomic adjustments
3. **Practice** felt evaluation of possible futures using somatic markers and bodily readiness
4. **Understand** allostatic planning: how the body predicts and prepares for future regulatory demands
5. **Connect** expected free energy to the felt sense of which futures the body leans toward

## Key Concepts

- **Embodied planning**: Planning as experienced through the body, not just theorized about
- **Active Inference**: Planning as a component of the free energy minimization process
- **4E Cognition**: Planning as embodied, embedded, enacted, and extended
- **Interoception and proprioception**: How inner sensing shapes planning
- **Sensorimotor coupling**: The inseparability of planning from bodily engagement
- **Dance, yoga, martial arts**: The specific lens this course brings to planning

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Modules 01-07 recommended
- Willingness to engage in movement lab activities

## Estimated Time

- Module reading: 20 minutes
- Lab (movement lab): 25 minutes
- Questions and reflection: 15 minutes
