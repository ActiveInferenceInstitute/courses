## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Dance, yoga, martial arts
- **Topics**: Perception as sensorimotor exploration -- perceiving through and because of movement
- **Lab Style**: Movement Lab
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **perception** through the lens of full-body movement, sensorimotor coupling, and the inseparability of action and perception. Content must:

- Frame perception as fundamentally constituted by movement: you see by moving your eyes, hear by turning your head, feel by running your fingers across surfaces.
- Connect to Gibson's ecological approach: perception is the detection of information that specifies action possibilities (affordances).
- Present sensorimotor contingencies through movement practices: how the martial artist perceives openings, the dancer perceives rhythm, the yogi perceives alignment.
- Reference O'Regan and Noë: perceptual experience is constituted by the perceiver's mastery of sensorimotor contingencies.
- Integrate Active Inference: active perception is the generation of sensory predictions through motor action and the updating of those predictions through sensory feedback.
- Movement activities should involve blindfolded movement, tactile exploration through gesture, and perception-action coupling exercises.
