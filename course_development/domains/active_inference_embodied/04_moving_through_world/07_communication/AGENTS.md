# Station: Communication (Moving Through the World)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Dance, yoga, martial arts
- **Topics**: Communication as movement dialogue -- bodies in conversation through gesture, rhythm, and contact
- **Lab Style**: Movement Lab
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **communication** through the lens of full-body movement, sensorimotor coupling, and the inseparability of action and perception. Content must:

- Frame communication through movement-based interaction: contact improvisation, partner dance, martial arts sparring, and group movement as forms of embodied conversation.
- Connect to the concept of kinesthetic empathy: understanding another's movement by feeling it resonate in your own body.
- Present movement dialogue as participatory sense-making in action: meaning emerging through the dynamic negotiation of shared movement.
- Reference the role of rhythm in social coordination: how synchronized movement creates social bonds and shared meaning.
- Integrate Active Inference: movement dialogue is the real-time alignment of two generative models through continuous exchange of motor and sensory signals.
- Movement activities should involve contact improvisation, partner mirroring, group rhythm exercises, and movement-based conversation.
