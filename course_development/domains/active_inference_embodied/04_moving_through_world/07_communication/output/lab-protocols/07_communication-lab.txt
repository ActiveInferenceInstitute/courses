Lab: Resonance Between Bodies

Objective

Experience embodied communication through movement lab that explores co-regulation, mirroring, and resonance between bodies through the lens of dance, yoga, martial arts.

Prerequisites

A quiet, comfortable space for 25 minutes
Willingness to engage in movement lab
No prior experience required

Part 1: Arriving (5 minutes)

Stand in a comfortable position. Shake out your hands and feet gently. Roll your shoulders. Take three breaths. Begin to sway gently, finding your body's natural rhythm of movement.

If with a partner, sit facing each other. If alone, recall a recent meaningful interaction. Notice: communication between bodies begins before words, through presence, rhythm, and resonance.

Part 2: Synchrony (7 minutes)

With a partner: breathe together, move together, mirror each other's gestures. Without a partner: move in synchrony with music, or mirror your own movements in reflection. Through dance, yoga, martial arts, notice how synchrony changes the felt relationship.

Part 3: Proximity and Presence (7 minutes)

Explore how distance affects felt connection. Move closer and farther from a partner or an object. Through dance, yoga, martial arts, notice how the interpersonal Markov blanket -- the felt boundary between self and other -- shifts.

Part 4: Reflection (5 minutes)

Through dance, yoga, martial arts, reflect on what communication means before and beyond words.
