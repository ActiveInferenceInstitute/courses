## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Dance, yoga, martial arts
- **Topics**: Learning as skill embodiment -- how practice transforms the body and its possibilities
- **Lab Style**: Movement Lab
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **learning** through the lens of full-body movement, sensorimotor coupling, and the inseparability of action and perception. Content must:

- Frame learning through the progressive embodiment of skill: the novice's awkward imitation becoming the expert's fluid mastery.
- Connect to motor learning research: stages of skill acquisition from cognitive (thinking about each step) to associative (linking steps) to autonomous (flowing without thought).
- Present the concept of motor schema: the body's learned templates for action that can be adapted to novel situations.
- Reference the tradition of deliberate practice: how focused, repetitive engagement with challenging movement patterns restructures the body-mind system.
- Integrate Active Inference: skill learning is the systematic reduction of motor prediction error through repeated parameter updating of the generative model.
- Movement activities should involve learning a simple movement sequence, noticing the stages of acquisition, and observing how the body transforms through practice.
