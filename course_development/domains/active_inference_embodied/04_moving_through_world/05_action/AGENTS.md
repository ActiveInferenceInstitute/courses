# Station: Action (Moving Through the World)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Dance, yoga, martial arts
- **Topics**: Action as whole-body expression -- the full integration of intention, movement, and environment
- **Lab Style**: Movement Lab
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **action** through the lens of full-body movement, sensorimotor coupling, and the inseparability of action and perception. Content must:

- Frame action through the peak experience of skilled, whole-body movement: the dancer's leap, the martial artist's strike, the yogi's balanced pose.
- Connect to flow states in movement: the experience of seamless action-perception coupling where effort dissolves and skill manifests.
- Present morphological computation through movement practice: how the body's physical structure shapes and enables skilled action.
- Reference the concept of mushin (no-mind) in martial arts: action that arises from deep training without conscious deliberation.
- Integrate Active Inference: skilled action is the body operating with highly accurate motor predictions, minimal prediction error, and seamless sensorimotor coordination.
- Movement activities should involve full-body movement sequences, improvisation with environmental constraints, and exploration of effort versus ease in practiced skills.
