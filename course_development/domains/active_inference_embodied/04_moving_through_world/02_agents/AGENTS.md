> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Dance, yoga, martial arts
- **Topics**: Agents as moving selves -- agency expressed through the body in motion
- **Lab Style**: Movement Lab
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **agents** through the lens of full-body movement, sensorimotor coupling, and the inseparability of action and perception. Content must:

- Present the agent through the experience of being a body in motion: the mover, the dancer, the practitioner who inhabits movement.
- Connect to martial arts philosophy: the concept of the centered self (hara, dantian) as the embodied locus of agency.
- Frame agency through movement initiation: the capacity to start, stop, redirect, and modulate movement from an internal locus.
- Reference the phenomenology of dance: the dancer's experience of being both the creator and the creation of movement.
- Integrate Active Inference: the moving agent generates motor predictions, receives proprioceptive feedback, and continuously adjusts -- a complete active inference loop visible in every step.
- Movement activities should involve centering exercises, movement initiation from stillness, and exploration of the felt locus of agency.
