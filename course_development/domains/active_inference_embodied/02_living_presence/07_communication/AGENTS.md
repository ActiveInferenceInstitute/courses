# Station: Communication (Living Presence)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Breath, heartbeat, embodied being
- **Topics**: Communication as breath synchrony and shared presence -- meeting another in embodied stillness
- **Lab Style**: Mindfulness Practice
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **communication** through the lens of breath awareness, autonomic regulation, and the living body's continuous self-maintenance. Content must:

- Frame communication through shared mindful presence: two people sitting together in silence, attending to breath, and noticing the subtle influence they have on each other.
- Connect to breath synchrony as the foundation of embodied communication: research shows that co-present individuals spontaneously synchronize their respiratory and cardiac rhythms.
- Present co-regulation through contemplative dyad practices: sitting together, walking together, and sharing silence.
- Reference the concept of intersubjectivity from the phenomenological tradition: shared experience arising through embodied co-presence.
- Integrate Active Inference: shared presence involves the mutual alignment of generative models through synchronized interoceptive rhythms.
- Activities should involve dyad meditation, synchronized walking, and shared silence exercises.
