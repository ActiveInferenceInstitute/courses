# Station: Action (Living Presence)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Breath, heartbeat, embodied being
- **Topics**: Action as breath-led movement -- moving from presence rather than intention
- **Lab Style**: Mindfulness Practice
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **action** through the lens of breath awareness, autonomic regulation, and the living body's continuous self-maintenance. Content must:

- Frame action through breath-initiated movement: letting the breath lead, the body follows. Action begins with the respiratory rhythm.
- Connect to the relationship between breath and movement found in yoga (vinyasa), tai chi, and contemplative dance.
- Present action as arising from present-moment awareness rather than from deliberate planning.
- Reference the autonomic-somatic interface: how the breath bridges the voluntary and involuntary nervous systems.
- Integrate Active Inference: breath-led action is a form of minimizing free energy through alignment of interoceptive predictions with motor execution.
- Activities should involve breath-synchronized movement sequences and walking meditation.
