# Station: Perception (Living Presence)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Breath, heartbeat, embodied being
- **Topics**: Perception as present-moment sensing -- attending to what is here, now, in the body
- **Lab Style**: Mindfulness Practice
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **perception** through the lens of breath awareness, autonomic regulation, and the living body's continuous self-maintenance. Content must:

- Frame perception through mindful awareness: perception in this course is not about identifying objects but about attending to the raw sensory qualities of the present moment.
- Connect to interoceptive and exteroceptive streams: what is happening inside (breath, heartbeat, temperature) and what is reaching from outside (sound, light, air movement).
- Present prediction error as the gap between expectation and present-moment experience -- what mindfulness traditions call 'beginner's mind.'
- Reference precision weighting through attention: mindful attention increases the precision of sensory signals, making subtle sensations vivid.
- Integrate Active Inference: mindful perception is a shift in precision weighting from prior predictions to sensory evidence.
- Activities should involve sustained present-moment awareness exercises -- body scans, sound baths, and open-monitoring meditation.
