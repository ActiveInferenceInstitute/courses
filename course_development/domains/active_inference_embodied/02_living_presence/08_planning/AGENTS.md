# Station: Planning (Living Presence)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Breath, heartbeat, embodied being
- **Topics**: Planning as attentive unfolding -- letting the next moment arise from present-moment awareness
- **Lab Style**: Mindfulness Practice
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **planning** through the lens of breath awareness, autonomic regulation, and the living body's continuous self-maintenance. Content must:

- Frame planning through contemplative non-doing: rather than projecting into the future, attend so fully to the present that the next step arises naturally.
- Connect to the contemplative concept of 'wu wei' or effortless action: skillful engagement that emerges from deep presence rather than anxious deliberation.
- Present allostatic planning through the lens of mindful eating, mindful movement, and attentive self-care.
- Reference the role of interoceptive awareness in anticipating bodily needs: recognizing hunger, fatigue, and emotional states before they become urgent.
- Integrate Active Inference: contemplative planning is the evaluation of expected free energy through direct felt experience rather than abstract simulation.
- Activities should involve mindful intention-setting practices and body-based decision-making exercises.
