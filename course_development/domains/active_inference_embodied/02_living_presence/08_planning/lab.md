# Lab: Listening to What Wants to Happen

## Objective

Explore embodied planning through mindfulness practice that reveals how the body anticipates, prepares for, and evaluates future action through the lens of breath, heartbeat, embodied being.

## Prerequisites

- A quiet, comfortable space for 25 minutes
- Willingness to engage in mindfulness practice
- No prior experience required

## Part 1: Arriving (5 minutes)

Sit comfortably and close your eyes. Take three slow breaths, letting each exhale be longer than the inhale. Bring your attention to the natural rhythm of your breathing without trying to change it.

Notice: your body is already planning. It is adjusting posture, regulating breath, and anticipating what comes next. Through breath, heartbeat, embodied being, attend to the body's forward-looking quality.

Think of two possible activities. For each, let the body simulate the experience. Through breath, heartbeat, embodied being, notice the somatic response -- does the body lean toward one future or pull away?

Rather than planning what to do next, ask the body: What wants to happen? Wait. Let a movement or action emerge without deliberation. Through breath, heartbeat, embodied being, trust the body's intelligence.
