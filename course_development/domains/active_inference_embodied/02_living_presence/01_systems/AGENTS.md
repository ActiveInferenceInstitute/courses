## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Breath, heartbeat, embodied being
- **Topics**: Systems as the breathing boundary -- the body as a self-regulating living system
- **Lab Style**: Mindfulness Practice
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **systems** through the lens of breath awareness, autonomic regulation, and the living body's continuous self-maintenance. Content must:

- Frame the system boundary through breath: with each inhale, the world enters; with each exhale, the self extends outward. The Markov blanket is not static but rhythmically pulsing.
- Connect to autonomic regulation: the system maintains itself through continuous physiological adjustment -- heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, and respiratory rate.
- Present allostasis as the system's predictive self-maintenance: the body anticipates its own needs and adjusts before demands arise.
- Reference interoceptive awareness of cardiac and respiratory rhythms as direct perception of the system's internal dynamics.
- Integrate Active Inference: the living system minimizes free energy by maintaining its internal states within viable ranges through ongoing regulatory action.
- Mindfulness activities should involve sustained breath awareness as a window into the system's self-regulation.
