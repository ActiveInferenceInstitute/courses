# Module 01: Systems

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Feeling the Boundary of Your Skin

Part of **Living Presence**.

## Learning Objectives

1. **Experience** the body's boundary through the lens of breath, heartbeat, embodied being as an embodied Markov blanket
2. **Distinguish** interoception (sensing the inside) from exteroception (sensing the outside) through direct awareness
3. **Explore** how breath, heartbeat, embodied being reveals the body's self-regulatory boundaries
4. **Recognize** allostasis as the body's predictive regulation that maintains system viability
5. **Apply** the 4E cognition framework (embodied, embedded, enacted, extended) to system boundaries

## Key Concepts

- **Embodied systems**: Systems as experienced through the body, not just theorized about
- **Active Inference**: Systems as a component of the free energy minimization process
- **4E Cognition**: Systems as embodied, embedded, enacted, and extended
- **Interoception and proprioception**: How inner sensing shapes systems
- **Sensorimotor coupling**: The inseparability of systems from bodily engagement
- **Breath, heartbeat, embodied being**: The specific lens this course brings to systems

## Prerequisites

- No prior knowledge required
- Willingness to engage in mindfulness practice activities

## Estimated Time

- Module reading: 20 minutes
- Lab (mindfulness practice): 25 minutes
- Questions and reflection: 15 minutes
