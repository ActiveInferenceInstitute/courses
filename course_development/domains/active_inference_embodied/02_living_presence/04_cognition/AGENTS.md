# Station: Cognition (Living Presence)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Breath, heartbeat, embodied being
- **Topics**: Cognition as mindful awareness -- thinking that arises from the body's living presence
- **Lab Style**: Mindfulness Practice
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **cognition** through the lens of breath awareness, autonomic regulation, and the living body's continuous self-maintenance. Content must:

- Present cognition not as abstract thought but as the quality of awareness that arises from sustained attention to bodily presence.
- Connect to the mindfulness tradition: non-judgmental awareness of thoughts as they arise, persist, and pass away.
- Reference interoceptive contributions to cognition: how cardiac phase, respiratory rhythm, and visceral state influence the quality and content of thought.
- Frame somatic markers through mindful observation: noticing the bodily feeling-tone (vedana) that accompanies every cognitive event.
- Integrate Active Inference: cognition is the generative model's ongoing updating in response to prediction errors, observable in real time through mindful attention.
- Activities should involve noting practice (labeling thoughts and sensations as they arise) and body-scan awareness during cognitive tasks.
