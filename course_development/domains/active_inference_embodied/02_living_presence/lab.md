# Lab: Living Presence -- Mindfulness Practice Series

## Objective

Develop sustained awareness of the body's living processes through mindfulness practices that attend to breath, heartbeat, and autonomic regulation. This lab introduces the living body as the foundation for understanding Active Inference through direct contemplative experience.

## Prerequisites

- A quiet space where you can sit undisturbed for 30 minutes
- Comfortable seating (cushion or chair)
- No prior meditation experience required

## Part 1: Arriving Through Breath (5 minutes)

Sit comfortably. Close your eyes. Without changing your breathing, simply observe its natural rhythm. Notice: the breath moves on its own. You did not decide to breathe. Your body has been breathing since birth without your conscious direction.

In Active Inference, this is the generative model's most fundamental prediction: the body expects to continue breathing, and the autonomic system acts to fulfill that prediction.

## Part 2: Heart Awareness (5 minutes)

Turn your attention to your heartbeat. Can you feel it without touching your chest? Some people feel it in the chest, others in the throat, wrists, or belly. If you cannot feel it at first, be patient -- interoceptive sensitivity increases with practice.

Count your heartbeats for 30 seconds. Now count your breaths for 30 seconds. Notice the relationship between these two rhythms. They are not independent; the heart and breath are coupled through the autonomic nervous system.

## Part 3: Autonomic Landscape (7 minutes)

Scan through the body's self-regulating processes:

1. **Temperature**: Where are you warmest? Coolest? The body maintains a temperature gradient, warming the core and allowing the extremities to vary.
2. **Digestion**: Can you sense any activity in the belly? Gurgling, warmth, stillness? Digestion is one of the body's most energy-intensive regulatory processes.
3. **Muscular tone**: Where is there holding? Where is there release? The body maintains postural tone continuously, even when you think you are relaxed.
4. **Alertness**: What is your current level of arousal? Wide awake? Drowsy? The body regulates alertness through the sympathetic-parasympathetic balance.

{fill:textarea}(Describe the autonomic landscape you discovered. What surprised you about your body's ongoing self-regulation?)

## Part 4: Breath and Regulation (8 minutes)

Explore how the breath can modulate the body's regulatory state:

1. **Extended exhale**: Breathe in for 4 counts, out for 8 counts. Repeat 5 times. Notice what happens to your heart rate, your muscular tension, your alertness. The extended exhale activates the parasympathetic nervous system.
2. **Energizing breath**: Breathe in for 6 counts, out for 3 counts. Repeat 5 times. Notice the shift toward alertness and activation.
3. **Return to natural**: Let the breath return to its own rhythm. Notice how the body re-establishes its preferred baseline.

In Active Inference, you have just used action (changing your breathing) to modify your internal states, reducing the difference between your current state and a preferred state. This is active inference in its most direct form.

Return to simply sitting. Breath happening. Heart beating. Body regulating. You are not doing any of this, and yet you are intimately present to all of it.
