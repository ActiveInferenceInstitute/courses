# Station: Agents (Living Presence)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Breath, heartbeat, embodied being
- **Topics**: Agents as living, breathing selves -- the felt sense of being alive and present
- **Lab Style**: Mindfulness Practice
- **Audience**: Anyone seeking qualitative, felt understanding
- **Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential

## Embodied Cognition Content Guidelines

This module explores **agents** through the lens of breath awareness, autonomic regulation, and the living body's continuous self-maintenance. Content must:

- Present the agent through the felt quality of being alive: the warmth of blood flowing, the rhythm of the heart, the rise and fall of breath.
- Connect to interoceptive self-awareness: the agent knows itself through its own internal states. Cardiac awareness, respiratory sensitivity, and visceral feeling ground the sense of self.
- Reference Seth's interoceptive self: the experience of being a self is fundamentally an experience of being a body.
- Integrate Active Inference: the agent's generative model is, at its core, a model of the body's own viability.
- Distinguish between the automatic self-regulation of the agent (autonomic) and the deliberate attention to that regulation (mindful awareness).
- Mindfulness activities should involve heart-rate awareness and breath-counting as practices of attending to the agent's own life processes.
