## Overview

Welcome to **Active Inference: Embodied Experience**. This curriculum teaches Active Inference — the theory that all living systems work by constantly predicting and acting to minimize surprise.

**Tone**: Somatic, poetic, experiential. First-person felt sense. Meditative. Zero equations, zero code.

## Design Principles

1. **Body is the text**: Every concept is grounded in felt, bodily experience
2. **No equations, no code**: Understanding comes through sensing, not calculating
3. **Somatic traditions honored**: References Gendlin, Feldenkrais, Alexander, mindfulness
4. **Poetic precision**: Language is beautiful and precise — somatic rigor is real rigor
5. **First-person voice**: "Notice..." "Feel..." "Allow..." — the reader is invited, not lectured
