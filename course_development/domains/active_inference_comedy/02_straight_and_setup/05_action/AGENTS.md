## Conventions

- **Perspective**: The performer's embodied action repertoire — vocal, physical, and temporal actions as boundary crossings
- **Topics**: Vocal action parameters, physical comedy, timing as embodied action, the microphone as blanket extension
- **Lab Style**: Performance Workshop — embodied delivery exercises exploring how action parameters change meaning
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Performance is embodied action. Every word is produced by muscles and modulated by posture, gesture, and spatial positioning. This module catalogs the performer's action repertoire and connects each action to its effect on the audience's inference. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Vocal actions** (pitch, volume, speed, prosody) are connected to the precision weights they communicate to the audience
- **Physical actions** (gesture, posture, facial expression) are analyzed as embodied generative models the audience reads directly
- **Timing** is treated as the most sophisticated action — the bodily management of temporal precision that creates prediction windows
- The **microphone** is analyzed as a technological extension of the Markov blanket — amplifying some channels while filtering others
