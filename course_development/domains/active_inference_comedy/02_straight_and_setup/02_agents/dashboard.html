Key Concepts

The Straight Man's Discipline +

Holding the model with iron certainty while the world goes mad. Abbott maintains sky-high precision on his priors, suppressing every natural tendency to accommodate or clarify. This is cognitive discipline of a high order.

The straight man's role is defined by sustained precision maintenance against social pressure to adapt — many actors describe it as harder than the comic's role.

Centrality to module

The Comic's Rapid Inference +

Costello runs a volatile, rapidly-updating generative model while simultaneously performing its output (confusion, frustration, bewilderment) with precise timing. The chaos is controlled chaos.

The comic must rapidly generate plausible interpretations, express genuine confusion, escalate emotionally, and respond to audience reactions — all in real time.

Centrality to module

The Coupled Agent System +

Abbott and Costello share a meta-model invisible to the audience. Their coupling is maintained through micro-signals: timing adjustments, eye contact, breath coordination — a private communication channel.

Both performers know the names, the structure, and where the laughs should come. This shared knowledge operates behind the Markov blanket of character.

Centrality to module

Solo Performers as Internal Duos +

A solo stand-up comedian embodies both roles — narrating as the straight man, acting out as the comic. The switch between roles is a switch between precision profiles.

The audience tracks both archetypes within a single performer, creating a dynamic internal dialogue.
