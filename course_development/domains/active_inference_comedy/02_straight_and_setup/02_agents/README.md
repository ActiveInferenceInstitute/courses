## Learning Objectives

1. **Experience** the straight man's cognitive discipline — holding high-precision priors under the social pressure to accommodate
2. **Analyze** the comic's apparent chaos as sophisticated, rapid-fire inference under uncertainty
3. **Identify** the micro-signals that couple a comedy duo (timing cues, eye contact, breath synchronization)
4. **Distinguish** between the character's model and the performer's meta-model in both the straight man and comic roles
5. **Apply** agent archetype analysis to modern comedy formats: sitcoms, late night, improv, and solo stand-up as internal duo

## Key Terms

- **Straight man**: The high-precision agent — holds the model with iron certainty while the comic destabilizes around them
- **Comic**: The volatile agent — runs a rapidly-updating model while performing its outputs (confusion, frustration) with precision
- **Coupled agent system**: Two agents connected through private micro-signals invisible to the audience
- **Precision profile**: An agent's characteristic pattern of precision assignment across beliefs — what they hold certain and what they hold loosely
- **Meta-model**: The performer's model of the performance itself, running simultaneously with the character's model
