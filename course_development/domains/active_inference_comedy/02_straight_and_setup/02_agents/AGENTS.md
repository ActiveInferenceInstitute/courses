# Agent Guidelines: Agents (Straight & Setup)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: The straight man and the comic as agent archetypes from the performer's perspective — what it takes to BE each role
- **Topics**: Agent archetypes, precision profiles, cognitive discipline, the duo as a coupled agent system
- **Lab Style**: Performance Workshop — inhabiting the straight man and comic roles through embodied performance
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

This module shifts from analyzing agents to inhabiting them. The straight man's discipline (holding high-precision priors under siege) and the comic's virtuosity (running a volatile model while performing its outputs with precision) are both demanding cognitive feats. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- The **straight man's role** is presented as actively difficult — maintaining certainty requires cognitive discipline, not passivity
- The **comic's apparent chaos** is revealed as sophisticated, rapid-fire inference under uncertainty
- The **duo as a coupled system** is described through the micro-signals invisible to the audience (timing adjustments, eye contact, breath coordination)
- The content invites the reader to try inhabiting these roles, not just analyze them
