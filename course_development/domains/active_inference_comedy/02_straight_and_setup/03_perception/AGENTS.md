# Agent Guidelines: Perception (Straight & Setup)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Reading the room through the performer's senses — what the performer actually perceives from the stage
- **Topics**: Multi-modal sensory fusion, audience state estimation, precision-weighted perception, Abbott's perceptual strategy
- **Lab Style**: Performance Workshop — sensory exercises that train audience perception skills
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

From the stage, perception is active inference about a complex social system. The performer fuses auditory, visual, haptic, and temporal signals to estimate the audience's hidden state in real time. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Reading the room** is formalized as active perceptual inference — estimating hidden states from sparse, noisy observations
- The performer's **multi-modal sensory world** is vividly described (laughter, silence, coughs, body language, energy)
- **Abbott's perceptual strategy** (setting precision on Costello's signals to near-zero) is explained as a deliberate precision-weighting choice
- Crowd work is analyzed as **epistemic action** — performing acts designed to gather perceptual data about the audience's model
