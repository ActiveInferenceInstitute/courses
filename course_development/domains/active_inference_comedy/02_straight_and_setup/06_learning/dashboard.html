Key Concepts

Rehearsal as Model Refinement +

Rehearsal refines the performer's generative model — from coarse structure to precise timing, emphasis, and physicality. Each rehearsal iteration tightens the model's precision on lower-level processes.

Early rehearsals establish structure; later rehearsals optimize timing and delivery at increasingly fine-grained levels.

Centrality to module

Audience as Training Signal +

The audience provides noisy, aggregate, real-time feedback. Laughter, silence, groans, and energy shifts serve as evaluation signals that the performer uses to update their action policy across performances.

The audience is the comedian's training data — but the signal is noisy and requires many performances to extract reliable information.

Centrality to module

Exploration vs. Exploitation +

Early career: broad sampling, high variance, many failures (exploration). Seasoned career: proven material, reliable delivery, optimized sets (exploitation). The tension is never fully resolved.

Finding your voice is the convergence of a long-horizon policy optimization — settling into a style that reliably minimizes free energy.

Centrality to module

Deliberate Practice +

Deliberate practice targets specific weaknesses: timing drills, vocal exercises, crowd work practice. It isolates component skills for focused improvement, unlike ordinary performance which blends everything.

The comedy equivalent of running scales is isolating delivery parameters and drilling them independently.
