# Agent Guidelines: Learning (Straight & Setup)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Building a performance through rehearsal — the performer's learning trajectory from open mic to mastery
- **Topics**: Rehearsal as model refinement, audience feedback as training signal, the exploration-exploitation arc of a comedy career
- **Lab Style**: Performance Workshop — deliberate practice exercises targeting specific comedy skills
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Learning in comedy is learning by performance. Each show is a training episode, each audience reaction is a signal, and the comedian's career is a long trajectory through action-policy space. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Rehearsal** is presented as model refinement — progressively increasing precision on lower-level features until they become automatic
- **Audience feedback** is treated as the comedian's primary training signal — a form of supervised learning where the audience evaluates
- The **career learning trajectory** is mapped onto the exploration-exploitation trade-off: early exploration, mid-career transition, late exploitation
- **Mentorship** is analyzed as compressed model transfer — decades of experience transmitted through direct guidance
