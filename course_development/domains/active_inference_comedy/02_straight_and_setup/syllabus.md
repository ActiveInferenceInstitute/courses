# Syllabus: Straight & Setup

## Course Description

Shifts perspective from external analysis to the embodied experience of performing comedy. Examines what it feels like to be a straight man, a comic, and the cognitive and physical demands of live performance.

## Weekly Schedule

| Week | Topic | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Systems | The Performer-Audience System |
| 2 | Agents | Straight Man and Comic as Agent Archetypes |
| 3 | Perception | Reading the Room Through the Performer's Senses |
| 4 | Cognition | The Straight Man's Cognitive Discipline |
| 5 | Action | The Straight Man's Strategic Delivery |
| 6 | Learning | Building a Performance Through Rehearsal |
| 7 | Communication | The Performer's Communication Toolkit |
| 8 | Planning | Building a Set: Action Policy Design |

## Assessment

- **Study Questions**: 20 per module (160 total)
- **Practice Quizzes**: 1 per module (8 total)
- **Labs**: 1 per module (8 total)
