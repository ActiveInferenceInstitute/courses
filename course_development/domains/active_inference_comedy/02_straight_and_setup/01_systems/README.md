## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** the performer-audience system from the performer's embodied perspective — not as an abstraction but as a lived experience
2. **Identify** nested Markov blankets in performance: body, stage, and venue as concentric boundaries filtering information
3. **Analyze** coupling dynamics between performer and audience — what a "warm room" and a "cold room" feel like and why
4. **Reframe** stage fright as the performer's model predicting high free energy in an approaching system
5. **Connect** abstract systems theory to the physical, sensory reality of standing on stage

## Key Terms

- **Performer-audience coupling**: The bidirectional connection between performer actions and audience responses
- **Nested Markov blankets**: Concentric boundaries (body, stage, venue) that filter information at different scales
- **Warm room / cold room**: Performer's terms for tight coupling (responsive audience) vs. loose coupling (unresponsive audience)
- **Reading the room**: Estimating the coupling strength between performer and audience in real time
- **System formation**: The moment when performer and audience lock into a coupled dynamical system

## Prerequisites
