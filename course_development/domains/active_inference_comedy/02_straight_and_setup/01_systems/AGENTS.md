# Agent Guidelines: Systems (Straight & Setup)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: The performer-audience system from the inside — what it feels like to be a node in a comedy system
- **Topics**: Performer-audience coupling, nested Markov blankets, system formation from the performer's perspective
- **Lab Style**: Performance Workshop — embodied exercises where you experience being part of the system
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Where Course 1 analyzed the system from the outside, Straight & Setup puts you inside it. The performer is not an observer of the comedy system — they ARE the system. This module emphasizes the **embodied, felt experience** of being coupled to an audience. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- The **performer-audience system** is presented from the first-person perspective — what the performer sees, hears, and feels
- **Nested Markov blankets** (body, stage, venue) are described as concentric layers the performer navigates
- **Coupling dynamics** (warm room vs. cold room) are grounded in the performer's sensory experience
- Stage fright is treated as a rational anticipation of system failure, not irrational anxiety
- Second-person voice is used where appropriate: "Step onto the stage. You are half of a system now."
