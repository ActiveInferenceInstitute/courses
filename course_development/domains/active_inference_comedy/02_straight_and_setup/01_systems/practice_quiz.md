# Practice Quiz: Systems in Straight & Setup

## Part A: Multiple Choice

1. From the performer's perspective, the Markov blanket is experienced as:
A) An abstract mathematical concept
B) The voice, body, and timing — the physical channels through which performer and audience interact
C) The audience's reaction alone
D) The scripts and rehearsals before the show

2. A "warm room" in performer terminology corresponds to:
A) A room with high temperature
B) Loose coupling between performer and audience
C) Tight coupling — every action gets an immediate reaction
D) An audience that has already seen the routine

3. Stage fright, according to this module, is best described as:
A) An irrational fear unrelated to the system
B) The performer's model predicting high free energy in the approaching system
C) A sign that the performer is unprepared
D) An external state imposed by the venue

4. Which of the following is NOT identified as a nested Markov blanket in performance?
A) The performer's body
B) The stage
C) The audience's collective memory
D) The venue

5. When the performer "reads the room," they are:
A) Reading a script
B) Performing inference about external states from sensory signals at the performance boundary
C) Deliberately ignoring the audience
D) Counting seats

6. In ensemble comedy, inter-performer blankets are maintained through:
A) Written scripts only
B) Eye contact, verbal cues, and physical positioning
C) Audience feedback alone
D) Separate dressing rooms

7. The concept of the performer as a "fixed reference point" means:
A) The performer doesn't move on stage
B) The performer maintains high-precision priors that anchor the system's dynamics
C) The performer always stands in the same spot
D) The performer never changes their material

## Part B: Short Answer

1. Explain the difference between experiencing the Markov blanket from the outside (Course 1) and from the inside (Course 2). Use a specific example from a performance scenario.

2. A comedian performs in two venues: a 50-seat club and a 5,000-seat arena. How does the change in venue Markov blanket affect the performer's coupling dynamics with the audience? What adjustments must the performer make?

3. Why is the first laugh in a performance described as a pivotal moment for the system? Explain using the concepts of coupling confirmation and free energy reduction.
