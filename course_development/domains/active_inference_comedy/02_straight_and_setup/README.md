# Course 02: Straight & Setup

> **Quick Navigation**: [Curriculum Home](../OVERVIEW.md) | [Resources](../resources/README.md)

## 🎭 Amber (#F2A900)

**Perspective**: The performers as agents — Abbott vs. Costello from the inside

Shifts perspective from external analysis to the embodied experience of performing comedy. Examines what it feels like to be a straight man, a comic, and the cognitive and physical demands of live performance.

## Learning Goals

By completing Straight & Setup, you will be able to:

1. Analyze "Who's on First?" through the performers as agents — abbott vs. costello from the inside
2. Apply Active Inference concepts to the specific dynamics that Straight & Setup foregrounds
3. Connect theoretical understanding to practical comedy craft
