## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** dual-track cognition in performance — running a character model and a performer meta-model simultaneously
2. **Analyze** cognitive load management through rehearsal — how automatization frees resources for real-time adaptation
3. **Apply** theory of mind as the comedian's core skill — modeling audience expectations and predicting when predictions will fail
4. **Explain** corpsing (breaking character) as a meta-model breach — internal amusement leaking through the character's Markov blanket
5. **Evaluate** why the straight man's role is often described as harder than the comic's — sustained precision maintenance under social pressure

## Key Terms

- **Dual-track cognition**: Running character-level beliefs and performer-level monitoring simultaneously
- **Meta-model**: The performer's model of the performance itself — tracking timing, audience response, and strategic adjustments
- **Cognitive load**: The total demand on working memory during performance — reduced through rehearsal and automatization
- **Theory of mind**: The ability to model another agent's beliefs, expectations, and likely responses
- **Corpsing**: Breaking character by laughing — a meta-model breach where the performer's state leaks through the character boundary

## Prerequisites

- Modules 01-03 in Straight & Setup — the performer's system, agent roles, and perceptual world
- Comfort with the idea that "effortless" performance requires enormous cognitive effort
