# Agent Guidelines: Communication (Straight & Setup)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: The performer's multi-channel communication toolkit — voice, body, face, silence, and the spaces between sentences
- **Topics**: Multi-channel communication, vocal instruments, silence as communication, cross-channel contradiction
- **Lab Style**: Performance Workshop — exercises isolating and combining different communication channels
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Comedy performance is a multi-channel communication act optimized for maximum prediction error delivery. The performer communicates through every available channel simultaneously, and the audience integrates across channels. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Multi-channel communication** is catalogued: verbal, prosodic, gestural, postural, facial, and temporal channels
- **Cross-channel contradiction** (saying something funny with a serious face) is analyzed as an additional source of prediction error
- **Silence** is treated as a deliberate communication act — its meaning determined by context and audience expectations
- The difference between full-channel (live performance), reduced-channel (podcast), and single-channel (text) comedy is explored
