# Agent Guidelines: Action (Timing & Tension)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: The physics of punchline delivery — temporal action control at the millisecond level
- **Topics**: Delivery timing, temporal action precision, the mechanics of "landing" a joke, rhythm in delivery
- **Lab Style**: Timing Experiment — practicing delivery with varied timing and measuring impact
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Punchline delivery is temporal action at its most precise. The comedian must control the exact moment of prediction error delivery relative to the audience's precision window. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Delivery timing** is analyzed at the millisecond level — the difference between landing and bombing can be a quarter-second
- **Temporal action precision** connects the performer's motor control to the audience's temporal expectations
- The concept of **landing** a joke is formalized as delivering the prediction error at peak temporal precision
- **Rhythm in delivery** is treated as the temporal skeleton of comedy — the underlying beat that the punchline plays against
