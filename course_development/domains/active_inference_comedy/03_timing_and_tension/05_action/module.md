## Introduction

Delivering a punchline is a physical act with microsecond precision. The comedian's larynx, diaphragm, tongue, and lips must execute a precisely timed sequence of movements that produce specific sounds at specific moments. A stand-up closer is as physically precise as a musician's fermata or a diver's final twist.

This module examines the physics of punchline delivery — the embodied actions through which timing becomes real.

## Key Concepts

### The Physics of Punchline Delivery

At the heart of this module is the physics of punchline delivery — the way action operates within precision dynamics and temporal control. In 'Who\'s on First?', this manifests as the interplay between performers, audience, and the comedy tradition, examined through the temporal, social, and evolutionary dimensions that Timing & Tension foregrounds.

### Active Inference Formalization

Active Inference provides the formal toolkit for understanding action in this context. The key constructs are:

- **Generative model**: The agent's internal model of how action works in comedy — what patterns to expect, what signals to attend to, and what actions to take.
- **Prediction error**: The mismatch between expected and observed action dynamics — the signal that drives updating and adaptation.
- **Precision weighting**: The confidence assigned to predictions about action — higher precision means stronger expectations and larger prediction errors when they fail.
- **Expected free energy**: The anticipated surprise and information gain associated with future actions related to action.
