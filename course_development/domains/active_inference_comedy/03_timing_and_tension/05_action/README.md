## Learning Objectives

1. **Analyze** punchline delivery at the millisecond level — how quarter-second differences determine whether a joke lands
2. **Connect** temporal action precision to the audience's precision window — delivering the prediction error at peak anticipation
3. **Formalize** "landing" a joke as delivering prediction error at the moment of maximum temporal precision in the audience
4. **Identify** rhythm in delivery as the temporal skeleton of comedy — the underlying beat that content plays against
5. **Practice** varying delivery timing on the same joke and observing how timing changes the comedy's impact

## Key Terms

- **Delivery timing**: The precise temporal placement of the punchline relative to the audience's expectations
- **Landing a joke**: Delivering the prediction error at the peak of the audience's temporal precision window
- **Temporal action precision**: The performer's motor control over the exact moment of key utterances
- **Rhythm in delivery**: The underlying temporal pattern (beat, pace, cadence) that structures the delivery of comedy
- **Timing break**: A deliberate violation of established delivery rhythm to generate temporal surprise
