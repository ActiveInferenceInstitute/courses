# Agent Guidelines: Learning (Timing & Tension)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Learning to time — the developmental trajectory from novice (bad timing) to expert (invisible timing)
- **Topics**: Timing acquisition, feedback loops in temporal learning, the automation of temporal control, timing across media
- **Lab Style**: Timing Experiment — comparing timing in early vs. late performances of the same material
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Timing is learned, not innate. The comedian develops temporal precision through thousands of performances, each providing feedback about what temporal patterns work. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Timing acquisition** is modeled as the refinement of a temporal generative model through experience
- **Feedback loops** in temporal learning are traced: audience reaction latency tells the performer whether their timing was right
- The **automation of timing** is explained as procedural encoding — expert timing feels effortless because it has left conscious control
- The distinction between **timing in different media** (live, recorded, text) is explored as different temporal learning environments
