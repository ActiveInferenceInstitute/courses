Key Concepts

Timing Acquisition Stages +

Novices learn explicit rules ('pause for 2 seconds'). Intermediates develop feel ('wait until it feels right'). Experts have internalized timing as embodied prediction — automatic and adaptive.

The progression mirrors Active Inference model refinement: from high-uncertainty explicit policies to low-uncertainty habitual policies.

Centrality

Temporal Model Updating +

Each performance updates the comedian's timing model. Prediction errors from audience responses refine internal temporal predictions — what works, what's too fast, what's too slow.

Learning timing is Bayesian updating of temporal generative models through performance experience.

Centrality

Cross-Context Timing Transfer +

Expert timing transfers across contexts — a comedian who times well in clubs can adapt to theaters, podcasts, TV. The underlying temporal model generalizes across venues.

Transfer learning in timing: the generative model captures abstract temporal principles, not context-specific rules.
