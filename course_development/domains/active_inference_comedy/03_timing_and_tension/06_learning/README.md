## Learning Objectives

1. **Model** timing acquisition as the refinement of a temporal generative model through performance experience
2. **Trace** feedback loops in temporal learning — how audience reaction latency informs the performer about their timing
3. **Explain** the automation of expert timing as procedural encoding — why great timing feels effortless to the master
4. **Compare** temporal learning across media: live performance, recorded comedy, and written humor as different learning environments
5. **Analyze** Abbott and Costello's timing refinement across decades of "Who's on First?" performances

## Key Terms

- **Timing acquisition**: The developmental process of learning temporal precision through performance experience
- **Temporal feedback loop**: The cycle of delivery -> audience reaction -> timing adjustment that refines temporal control
- **Procedural encoding**: The transition of timing from conscious effort to automatic, embodied skill
- **Temporal generative model**: The performer's internal model of when events should occur for maximum comedic effect
- **Media-specific timing**: The different temporal constraints and learning signals in live, recorded, and written comedy

## Prerequisites

- Modules 01-05 in Timing & Tension — temporal systems, agents, perception, cognition, and action
