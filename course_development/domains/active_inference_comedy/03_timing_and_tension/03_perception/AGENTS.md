## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Precision windows in comedic timing — how temporal expectations shape what the audience perceives
- **Topics**: Temporal perception, precision windows, the pause as precision builder, anticipation as perceptual state
- **Lab Style**: Timing Experiment — manipulating pause lengths and measuring their effect on perceived humor
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

The pause before the punchline is not dead air — it is a precision window during which the audience's temporal predictions accumulate confidence. The longer the pause (up to a point), the higher the precision on the temporal prediction, and the larger the prediction error when the punchline arrives. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Precision windows** are explained as temporal intervals during which the audience builds confidence about what comes next
- The **pause-punchline relationship** is analyzed as precision accumulation followed by prediction error delivery
- **Anticipation** is treated as a perceptual state with measurable physiological correlates (held breath, leaned-forward posture, focused attention)
- The distinction between **too-early** (low precision, weak prediction error) and **too-late** (precision decays, attention drifts) is clearly drawn
