## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Comedy as a temporal dynamical system — where timing IS the system, not decoration on it
- **Topics**: Temporal systems, precision dynamics, rhythmic structure, acceleration patterns, the clock of comedy
- **Lab Style**: Timing Experiment — measuring pauses, mapping rhythmic patterns, analyzing temporal structure
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Timing is the system itself. In "Who's on First?", the temporal arrangement of exchanges is not incidental to the comedy — it IS the comedy. A half-beat early kills the laugh; a half-beat late amplifies it. This module examines comedy as a **temporal dynamical system** where precision weighting operates on temporal predictions. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Temporal precision** is the central concept — the audience builds expectations about WHEN things will happen, and comedy exploits these temporal predictions
- The **acceleration of exchanges** in "Who's on First?" is mapped as a temporal system parameter that increases audience arousal
- **Rhythmic structure** in comedy is connected to precision accumulation — regular rhythms build temporal precision, making violations more surprising
- All concepts are grounded in measurable temporal features (pause durations, exchange rates, laugh onset times)
