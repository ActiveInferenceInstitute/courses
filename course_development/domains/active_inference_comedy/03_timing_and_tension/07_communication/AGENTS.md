# Agent Guidelines: Communication (Timing & Tension)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Rhythmic communication in comedy — how temporal patterns carry meaning beyond words
- **Topics**: Rhythm as communication, tempo shifts as signals, call-and-response patterns, the musicality of comedy dialogue
- **Lab Style**: Timing Experiment — analyzing rhythmic patterns in comedy dialogue and their communicative function
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Rhythm communicates. A comedian who speeds up communicates urgency; one who slows down communicates significance. The temporal structure of dialogue carries meaning independent of content. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Rhythm as communication** is treated as a distinct channel — temporal patterns communicate mood, energy, status, and intent
- **Tempo shifts** are analyzed as communicative signals — acceleration communicates escalation, deceleration communicates emphasis
- **Call-and-response patterns** in "Who's on First?" are examined as rhythmic communication structures
- The **musicality of comedy dialogue** is explored — comedians describe good dialogue as having "rhythm," and this is literally true
