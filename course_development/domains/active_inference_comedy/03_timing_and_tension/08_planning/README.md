## Learning Objectives

1. **Design** the temporal architecture of a performance — planning where energy peaks, valleys, builds, and releases occur
2. **Apply** build-peak-resolve structures as temporal planning templates that manage audience arousal
3. **Scale** timing principles from the joke level (seconds) to the set level (minutes) to the special level (hour)
4. **Analyze** where Abbott and Costello placed "Who's on First?" in their full shows and why
5. **Evaluate** the temporal design of modern comedy specials as deliberately architected arousal management systems

## Key Terms

- **Temporal architecture**: The deliberate design of a performance's timing structure
- **Build-peak-resolve**: A temporal planning template where energy accumulates, climaxes, and releases
- **Energy pacing**: Managing audience arousal across the duration of a performance
- **Temporal scaling**: Timing dynamics that recur at different time scales (seconds, minutes, years)
- **Arousal management**: Using temporal structure to keep the audience in the optimal engagement zone
