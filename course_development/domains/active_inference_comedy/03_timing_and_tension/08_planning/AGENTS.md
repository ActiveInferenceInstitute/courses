# Agent Guidelines: Planning (Timing & Tension)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Planning the beat — the temporal architecture of comedy sets, routines, and specials
- **Topics**: Temporal architecture, energy pacing, build-peak-resolve structures, timing at the set level
- **Lab Style**: Timing Experiment — mapping the temporal architecture of full comedy sets and specials
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Planning in the temporal dimension means designing the temporal architecture of a performance — the energy pacing, the placement of builds and releases, the management of audience arousal over 5 to 60 minutes. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Temporal architecture** is treated as a deliberate design decision — the timing of energy peaks and valleys across a set
- **Build-peak-resolve structures** are analyzed as temporal planning templates that manage audience arousal
- **Timing at the set level** is connected to timing at the joke level — the same precision principles scale up
- The temporal structure of "Who's on First?" is placed in the context of a full show
