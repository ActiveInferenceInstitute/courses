# Lab: Planning Exercise (Timing & Tension)

## Objective

Design and evaluate a planned comedy structure, optimizing for expected free energy.

## Materials

* A recording of 'Who\'s on First?' or another comedy performance
* Pen and paper or text editor
* A partner for exercises (recommended)
* Timer
* Optional: recording device

## Prerequisites

* Module 08: Planning lecture (module.md)
* Familiarity with Active Inference concepts

## Steps

### Part 1: Observation (15 minutes)

1. Watch a 5-minute comedy segment with specific attention to planning the beat: temporal architecture.
2. Annotate at least 5 moments where planning dynamics from the Timing & Tension perspective are visible.
3. For each moment, identify the Active Inference concept at work (prediction error, precision, model updating, etc.).

### Part 2: Active Practice (20 minutes)

4. With a partner, create a short comedy exercise that highlights planning the beat: temporal architecture.
5. Focus on how precision dynamics and temporal control shapes the comedy experience.
6. Perform the exercise three times, noting improvements and changes in each iteration.
7. After each iteration, discuss: what changed? What was the audience's response? How did planning dynamics shift?

### Part 3: Analysis (15 minutes)

8. Compare your exercise to the professional performance observed in Part 1.
9. Identify three specific ways the professional performance demonstrated more sophisticated use of planning the beat: temporal architecture.
10. For each difference, explain what the professional's generative model likely includes that yours does not.

### Part 4: Synthesis (10 minutes)

11. Write a brief reflection connecting your experience to the module's key concepts.
12. Address: How does the Timing & Tension perspective change the way you understand planning in comedy?
13. Propose one modification to your exercise that would amplify planning the beat: temporal architecture effects.

## Discussion Questions

* How did the practical experience change your theoretical understanding of planning the beat: temporal architecture?
* What aspects of planning in comedy can only be understood through the lens of precision dynamics and temporal control?
* How does Active Inference help explain what you observed and experienced?
* Connect your findings to the broader curriculum: how does this lab relate to other modules in Timing & Tension?

## Wrap-Up

You have experienced planning the beat: temporal architecture through hands-on practice. The observation-practice-analysis cycle mirrors the Active Inference loop: perceive, act, update, repeat. Carry your updated model of planning into the remaining modules.
