Key Concepts

Anticipatory Processing +

The audience's brain generates temporal predictions — when the next beat should arrive. Comedy exploits these predictions by confirming, delaying, or accelerating the expected beat.

Anticipation is the cognitive engine of timing: the audience is always predicting the WHEN, not just the WHAT.

Centrality

The Cognitive Beat +

Internal rhythmic timing that both performer and audience entrain to. When the cognitive beats align, the system resonates. When the performer deliberately breaks the beat, surprise amplifies.

The beat is not musical — it is a cognitive rhythm emerging from the regularity of language, gesture, and emotional cadence.

Centrality

Tension as Accumulated Prediction +

Tension builds as the audience accumulates unresolved predictions. The longer the setup, the higher the precision, the greater the prediction error when resolution finally arrives.

Tension is cognitive free energy — unresolved prediction that demands resolution. Comedy is the art of managing this tension.
