# Agent Guidelines: Cognition (Timing & Tension)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Anticipation and the cognitive beat — how the audience's temporal cognition creates the scaffold for comedy
- **Topics**: Cognitive rhythm, beat detection in dialogue, anticipatory processing, temporal prediction hierarchies
- **Lab Style**: Timing Experiment — identifying cognitive beats in comedy and measuring anticipatory responses
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

The audience's brain runs a temporal prediction engine that detects beats, anticipates patterns, and generates surprise when the rhythm breaks. Comedy exploits this engine by establishing cognitive rhythms and then violating them. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Cognitive rhythm** is explained as the brain's temporal prediction model — detecting and extrapolating regular patterns in dialogue
- **Beat detection** in comedy is connected to musical beat detection — the same neural machinery processes both
- **Anticipatory processing** is shown to have multiple time horizons — the audience simultaneously predicts the next syllable, the next exchange, and the routine's arc
- **Temporal prediction hierarchies** are connected to joke structure — setups establish rhythms at one level, punchlines violate them at another
