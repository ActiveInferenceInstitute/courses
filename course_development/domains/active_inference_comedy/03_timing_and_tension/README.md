## Learning Goals

By completing Timing & Tension, you will be able to:

1. Analyze "Who's on First?" through perception and precision in comedy — the microsecond art of comedic timing
2. Apply Active Inference concepts to the specific dynamics that Timing & Tension foregrounds
3. Connect theoretical understanding to practical comedy craft
