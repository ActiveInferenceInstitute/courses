# Syllabus: Timing & Tension

## Course Description

Zooms into the temporal dimension of comedy. Examines how precision windows, anticipation dynamics, and rhythmic communication create the felt pulse of humor.

## Weekly Schedule

| Week | Topic | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Systems | Temporal Systems in Comedy |
| 2 | Agents | The Timer and the Timed |
| 3 | Perception | Precision Windows in Comedic Timing |
| 4 | Cognition | Anticipation and the Cognitive Beat |
| 5 | Action | The Physics of Punchline Delivery |
| 6 | Learning | Learning to Time: From Novice to Expert |
| 7 | Communication | Rhythmic Communication in Comedy |
| 8 | Planning | Planning the Beat: Temporal Architecture |

## Assessment

- **Study Questions**: 20 per module (160 total)
- **Practice Quizzes**: 1 per module (8 total)
- **Labs**: 1 per module (8 total)
