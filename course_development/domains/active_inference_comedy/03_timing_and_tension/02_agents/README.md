## Learning Objectives

1. **Distinguish** agents by their temporal precision profiles — Abbott as metronomic timer, Costello as reactive responder
2. **Analyze** tempo control as a form of agency — the straight man's power derives partly from controlling the temporal dimension
3. **Map** the audience's temporal tracking: how they build predictions about when the next exchange, confusion, and laugh will occur
4. **Connect** the comedy duo to the drummer-soloist metaphor: one keeps time, the other plays against it
5. **Evaluate** how temporal agent roles shift in solo stand-up, improv, and ensemble comedy

## Key Terms

- **Temporal precision profile**: An agent's characteristic timing pattern — regular vs. variable, leading vs. following
- **The timer**: The agent who sets and controls the tempo (Abbott, the straight man)
- **The timed**: The agent who reacts to and plays against the established tempo (Costello, the comic)
- **Tempo control as agency**: The power to set the pace of exchange, controlling when prediction errors can occur
- **Temporal tracking**: The audience's ongoing estimation of when the next significant event will occur
