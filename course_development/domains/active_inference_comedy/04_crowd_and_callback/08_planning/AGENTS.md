# Agent Guidelines: Planning (Crowd & Callback)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Domain AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Social comedy -- audience, ensemble, cultural transmission
- **Topics**: Audience-adaptive planning, demographic awareness, universal vs. niche comedy, festival vs. club planning
- **Lab Style**: Audience Dynamics Lab
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Planning for a crowd means planning for heterogeneity. The comedian must design sets that generate prediction error across DIVERSE generative models -- different ages, cultures, comedy literacy levels, and expectations. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- Every Active Inference concept is grounded in a concrete comedic experience from the Crowd & Callback perspective
- The social dynamics and collective inference lens reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points
- "Who's on First?" is referenced substantively through the Crowd & Callback perspective
- Comedy terminology is used correctly alongside Active Inference formalism

## Key Mappings for This Module

| Comedy Concept | Active Inference Mapping |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Audience-adaptive planning | Designing comedy sets that work for heterogeneous audiences with diverse models |
| Universal comedy | Material that generates prediction error across the widest possible range of generative models |
| Niche comedy | Material that targets specific generative models -- high prediction error for the in-group, none for outsiders |
