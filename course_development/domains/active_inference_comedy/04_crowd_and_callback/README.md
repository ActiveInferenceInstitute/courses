# Course 04: Crowd & Callback

> **Quick Navigation**: [Curriculum Home](../OVERVIEW.md) | [Resources](../resources/README.md)

## 👥 Teal (#009B8D)

**Perspective**: Social comedy — audience, ensemble, cultural transmission

Expands from individual agents to collective dynamics. Examines the audience as a social organism, crowd work as interaction, and comedy as cultural communication.

## Learning Goals

By completing Crowd & Callback, you will be able to:

1. Analyze "Who's on First?" through social comedy — audience, ensemble, cultural transmission
2. Apply Active Inference concepts to the specific dynamics that Crowd & Callback foregrounds
3. Connect theoretical understanding to practical comedy craft
