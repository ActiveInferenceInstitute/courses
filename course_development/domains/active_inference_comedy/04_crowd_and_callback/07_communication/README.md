## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** social communication through comedy through the lens of Active Inference and social dynamics and collective inference
2. **Analyze** how communication manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Crowd & Callback perspective
3. **Apply** shared laughter as communication to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** comedy as truth-telling to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Crowd & Callback perspective on communication reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Social communication through comedy**: Using humor to establish shared models, signal group membership, and transmit cultural knowledge
- **Shared laughter as communication**: Collective laughter as a signal that 'we share a model' -- social bonding through synchronized prediction error
- **Comedy as truth-telling**: The social function of comedy in communicating uncomfortable truths through humor
- **In-group humor**: Comedy that works only for those who share specific cultural priors -- it signals group membership
- **Comedy as social glue**: The bonding function of shared laughter -- synchronizing models across a social group

## Prerequisites

- Prior modules in Crowd & Callback and foundational concepts from Courses 01-02
- Familiarity with Active Inference concepts of generative models, prediction error, and precision weighting
