## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Social comedy -- audience, ensemble, cultural transmission
- **Topics**: Cultural learning, comedy conventions, audience sophistication, comedy literacy, genre expectations
- **Lab Style**: Audience Dynamics Lab
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Comedy both depends on and creates cultural learning. The audience must LEARN comedy conventions (setup-punchline structure, callback patterns, genre expectations) before they can fully participate. Meanwhile, comedy TEACHES cultural norms by violating them. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- Every Active Inference concept is grounded in a concrete comedic experience from the Crowd & Callback perspective
- The social dynamics and collective inference lens reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points
- "Who's on First?" is referenced substantively through the Crowd & Callback perspective
- Comedy terminology is used correctly alongside Active Inference formalism
