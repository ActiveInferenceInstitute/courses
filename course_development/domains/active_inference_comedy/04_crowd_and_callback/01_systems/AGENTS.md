## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Social comedy -- audience, ensemble, cultural transmission
- **Topics**: Comedy ecosystem, multi-agent systems, feedback loops, emergent properties, cultural embedding
- **Lab Style**: Audience Dynamics Lab
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

The comedy ecosystem is a complex adaptive system. The comedian exists within a web of relationships -- audience, venue, other comedians, bookers, cultural critics. This module treats the ECOSYSTEM as the system, not just the performer-audience dyad. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- Every Active Inference concept is grounded in a concrete comedic experience from the Crowd & Callback perspective
- The social dynamics and collective inference lens reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points
- "Who's on First?" is referenced substantively through the Crowd & Callback perspective
- Comedy terminology is used correctly alongside Active Inference formalism

## Key Mappings for This Module

| Comedy Concept | Active Inference Mapping |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Comedy ecosystem | The full web of relationships (audience, venue, other comedians, culture) that shapes what comedy is possible |
| Multi-agent system | A system with multiple interacting inference agents, each maintaining their own generative model |
| Emergent comedy | Humor that arises from system-level dynamics rather than any single agent's actions |
