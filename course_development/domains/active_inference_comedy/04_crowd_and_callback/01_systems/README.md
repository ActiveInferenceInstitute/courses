## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** comedy ecosystem through the lens of Active Inference and social dynamics and collective inference
2. **Analyze** how systems manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Crowd & Callback perspective
3. **Apply** multi-agent system to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** emergent comedy to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Crowd & Callback perspective on systems reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Comedy ecosystem**: The full web of relationships (audience, venue, other comedians, culture) that shapes what comedy is possible
- **Multi-agent system**: A system with multiple interacting inference agents, each maintaining their own generative model
- **Emergent comedy**: Humor that arises from system-level dynamics rather than any single agent's actions
- **Cultural embedding**: How the surrounding culture constrains and enables the comedy system
- **Feedback loops**: Bidirectional influence between performer, audience, venue, and comedy community

## Prerequisites

- Prior modules in Crowd & Callback and foundational concepts from Courses 01-02
- Familiarity with Active Inference concepts of generative models, prediction error, and precision weighting
