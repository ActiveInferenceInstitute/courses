# Agent Guidelines: Crowd & Callback

> **Quick Navigation**: [Domain AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) | [Course README](./README.md)

## Course-Specific Conventions

- **Perspective**: Social comedy — audience, ensemble, cultural transmission
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending
- **Grounding**: All concepts grounded in "Who's on First?" and comedy craft

## Content Standards

1. Every `module.md` must connect to the Crowd & Callback perspective
2. Every `questions.md` must ground questions in specific comedy examples
3. Every `practice_quiz.md` must include both MC and short-answer sections
4. Every `lab.md` must include hands-on exercises with discussion questions
