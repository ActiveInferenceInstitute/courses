## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Social comedy -- audience, ensemble, cultural transmission
- **Topics**: Collective inference, audience as agent, social contagion of laughter, herd behavior in comedy
- **Lab Style**: Audience Dynamics Lab
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

The audience is not a passive recipient -- it is a collective inference agent. This module examines how individual audience members synchronize into a collective that responds as a unified (though heterogeneous) agent. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- Every Active Inference concept is grounded in a concrete comedic experience from the Crowd & Callback perspective
- The social dynamics and collective inference lens reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points
- "Who's on First?" is referenced substantively through the Crowd & Callback perspective
- Comedy terminology is used correctly alongside Active Inference formalism

## Key Mappings for This Module

| Comedy Concept | Active Inference Mapping |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Collective agent | A group of individuals whose synchronized inference produces coherent collective behavior |
| Social contagion | The spread of laughter, attention, and mood through an audience via social coupling |
| Audience heterogeneity | The fact that different audience members maintain different generative models |
