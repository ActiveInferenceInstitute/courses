## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** collective agent through the lens of Active Inference and social dynamics and collective inference
2. **Analyze** how agents manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Crowd & Callback perspective
3. **Apply** social contagion to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** audience heterogeneity to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Crowd & Callback perspective on agents reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Collective agent**: A group of individuals whose synchronized inference produces coherent collective behavior
- **Social contagion**: The spread of laughter, attention, and mood through an audience via social coupling
- **Audience heterogeneity**: The fact that different audience members maintain different generative models
- **Synchronization**: The process by which individual responses align into collective laughter or silence
- **First laugher**: The audience member with highest epistemic confidence who responds first, seeding social contagion
