## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** crowd work through the lens of Active Inference and social dynamics and collective inference
2. **Analyze** how action manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Crowd & Callback perspective
3. **Apply** social action to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** heckler management to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Crowd & Callback perspective on action reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Crowd work**: Direct interaction with audience members -- the comedian's primary social epistemic action
- **Social action**: Actions that operate ON and WITH the social system rather than just across it
- **Heckler management**: Responding to unsolicited audience input -- a test of real-time social action skills
- **Callback as social action**: Deploying a callback that rewards the audience's collective memory with fresh prediction error
- **Audience participation**: Structuring the performance so the audience becomes an active part of the comedy system

## Prerequisites

- Prior modules in Crowd & Callback and foundational concepts from Courses 01-02
- Familiarity with Active Inference concepts of generative models, prediction error, and precision weighting
