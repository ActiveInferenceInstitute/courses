# Agent Guidelines: Action (Crowd & Callback)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Social comedy -- audience, ensemble, cultural transmission
- **Topics**: Crowd work, social action, audience participation, callbacks as social action, heckler management
- **Lab Style**: Audience Dynamics Lab
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Crowd work is the comedian's most social form of action -- directly engaging audience members to generate comedy in real time. This module treats crowd work as SOCIAL epistemic action: gathering data about the collective audience model through direct interaction. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- Every Active Inference concept is grounded in a concrete comedic experience from the Crowd & Callback perspective
- The social dynamics and collective inference lens reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points
- "Who's on First?" is referenced substantively through the Crowd & Callback perspective
- Comedy terminology is used correctly alongside Active Inference formalism

## Key Mappings for This Module

| Comedy Concept | Active Inference Mapping |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Crowd work | Direct interaction with audience members -- the comedian's primary social epistemic action |
| Social action | Actions that operate ON and WITH the social system rather than just across it |
| Heckler management | Responding to unsolicited audience input -- a test of real-time social action skills |
