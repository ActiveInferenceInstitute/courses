## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Social comedy -- audience, ensemble, cultural transmission
- **Topics**: Social perception, audience-to-audience inference, laughter as social signal, the room as perceptual field
- **Lab Style**: Audience Dynamics Lab
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

In live comedy, the audience perceives not just the performer but each other. Laughter from the person next to you changes your perception of the joke. This module examines SOCIAL perception -- how the audience uses each other as evidence. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- Every Active Inference concept is grounded in a concrete comedic experience from the Crowd & Callback perspective
- The social dynamics and collective inference lens reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points
- "Who's on First?" is referenced substantively through the Crowd & Callback perspective
- Comedy terminology is used correctly alongside Active Inference formalism

## Key Mappings for This Module

| Comedy Concept | Active Inference Mapping |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Social perception | Perceiving through social channels -- using others' reactions as evidence for one's own inference |
| Laughter as evidence | Hearing others laugh provides evidence that the joke was funny, updating the individual's model |
| Audience-to-audience inference | The process by which audience members infer the joke's quality from each other's responses |
