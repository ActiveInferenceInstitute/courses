## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** social perception through the lens of Active Inference and social dynamics and collective inference
2. **Analyze** how perception manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Crowd & Callback perspective
3. **Apply** laughter as evidence to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** audience-to-audience inference to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Crowd & Callback perspective on perception reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Social perception**: Perceiving through social channels -- using others' reactions as evidence for one's own inference
- **Laughter as evidence**: Hearing others laugh provides evidence that the joke was funny, updating the individual's model
- **Audience-to-audience inference**: The process by which audience members infer the joke's quality from each other's responses
- **The room as perceptual field**: The venue as a shared perceptual environment where social signals propagate
- **Social precision**: The weight assigned to social evidence -- how much others' reactions influence your own

## Prerequisites

- Prior modules in Crowd & Callback and foundational concepts from Courses 01-02
- Familiarity with Active Inference concepts of generative models, prediction error, and precision weighting
