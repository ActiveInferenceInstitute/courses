# Agent Guidelines: Agents (Riff & Repertoire)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: The evolution of comedy -- improvisation, variation, and tradition
- **Topics**: Improvisation as agency, real-time adaptation, the 'yes-and' principle, ensemble improvisation
- **Lab Style**: Improvisation Studio
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

The improviser is the purest adaptive agent in comedy. With no script, no rehearsal, and no safety net, the improviser must generate comedy in real time through real-time inference, action selection, and model updating. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- Every Active Inference concept is grounded in a concrete comedic experience from the Riff & Repertoire perspective
- The evolution, improvisation, and tradition lens reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points
- "Who's on First?" is referenced substantively through the Riff & Repertoire perspective
- Comedy terminology is used correctly alongside Active Inference formalism

## Key Mappings for This Module

| Comedy Concept | Active Inference Mapping |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Improviser as adaptive agent | The comedian operating without a script -- pure real-time inference and action |
| Yes-and principle | The improv rule of accepting and building on offers -- a policy of model expansion rather than contraction |
| Real-time adaptation | Adjusting the generative model and action policy moment-to-moment based on live observations |
