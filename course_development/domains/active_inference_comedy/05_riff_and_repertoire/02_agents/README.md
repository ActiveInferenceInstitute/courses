## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** improviser as adaptive agent through the lens of Active Inference and evolution, improvisation, and tradition
2. **Analyze** how agents manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Riff & Repertoire perspective
3. **Apply** yes-and principle to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** real-time adaptation to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Riff & Repertoire perspective on agents reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Improviser as adaptive agent**: The comedian operating without a script -- pure real-time inference and action
- **Yes-and principle**: The improv rule of accepting and building on offers -- a policy of model expansion rather than contraction
- **Real-time adaptation**: Adjusting the generative model and action policy moment-to-moment based on live observations
- **Ensemble improvisation**: Multi-agent improv where agents must maintain shared models without pre-coordination
- **Offer and acceptance**: The improv pattern where one agent generates a model element and another integrates it

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with Active Inference concepts of generative models, prediction error, and precision weighting
