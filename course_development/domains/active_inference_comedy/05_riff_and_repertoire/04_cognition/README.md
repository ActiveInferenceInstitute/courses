## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** creative cognition through the lens of Active Inference and evolution, improvisation, and tradition
2. **Analyze** how cognition manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Riff & Repertoire perspective
3. **Apply** joke generation to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** model recombination to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Riff & Repertoire perspective on cognition reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Creative cognition**: The cognitive process of generating novel, useful combinations from existing model elements
- **Joke generation**: The creative act of constructing setups and punchlines that produce prediction error
- **Model recombination**: Combining elements from different generative models to create novel, surprising configurations
- **Frame collision**: Forcing two incompatible interpretive frames into the same utterance -- the cognitive engine of jokes
- **Comedic imagination**: The ability to envision scenarios, connections, and interpretations that generate humor

## Prerequisites

- Prior modules in Riff & Repertoire and foundational concepts from Courses 01-02
- Familiarity with Active Inference concepts of generative models, prediction error, and precision weighting
