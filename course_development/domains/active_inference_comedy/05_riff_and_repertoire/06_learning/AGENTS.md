## Conventions

- **Perspective**: The evolution of comedy -- improvisation, variation, and tradition
- **Topics**: Tradition evolution, accumulated learning, comedy lineages, style transmission, innovation cycles
- **Lab Style**: Improvisation Studio
- **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious
- **Tone**: Smart, antic, deeply technical, never condescending

## Active Inference Focus

Comedy traditions evolve through ACCUMULATED LEARNING across generations. Each comedian inherits a tradition, adds to it, and passes it on. 'Who's on First?' carries the DNA of vaudeville cross-talk, refined across a century. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- Every Active Inference concept is grounded in a concrete comedic experience from the Riff & Repertoire perspective
- The evolution, improvisation, and tradition lens reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points
- "Who's on First?" is referenced substantively through the Riff & Repertoire perspective
- Comedy terminology is used correctly alongside Active Inference formalism
