## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** tradition evolution through the lens of Active Inference and evolution, improvisation, and tradition
2. **Analyze** how learning manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Riff & Repertoire perspective
3. **Apply** comedy lineage to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** style transmission to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Riff & Repertoire perspective on learning reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Tradition evolution**: The gradual change of comedy conventions through accumulated performer learning
- **Comedy lineage**: The chain of influence from mentor to student across generations of comedians
- **Style transmission**: How comedy styles propagate from one performer to another and across generations
- **Innovation cycle**: The pattern of convention establishment, refinement, violation, and new convention establishment
- **Accumulated learning**: The body of knowledge, technique, and convention that builds across generations of performers
