# Syllabus: Riff & Repertoire

## Course Description

Takes the longest view, examining comedy as an evolving system. Explores improvisation, creative cognition, cultural transmission, and the building of a comedy career over decades.

## Weekly Schedule

| Week | Topic | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Systems | Comedy as an Evolving System |
| 2 | Agents | The Improviser as Adaptive Agent |
| 3 | Perception | Perceiving Novelty in Familiar Forms |
| 4 | Cognition | Creative Cognition in Comedy |
| 5 | Action | Improvised Action: Real-Time Policy Selection |
| 6 | Learning | The Evolution of a Comedy Tradition |
| 7 | Communication | Transmitting Comedy Across Generations |
| 8 | Planning | Long-Horizon Planning: Building a Comedy Career |

## Assessment

- **Study Questions**: 20 per module (160 total)
- **Practice Quizzes**: 1 per module (8 total)
- **Labs**: 1 per module (8 total)
