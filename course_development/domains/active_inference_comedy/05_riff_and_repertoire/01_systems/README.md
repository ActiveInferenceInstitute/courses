## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** evolving system through the lens of Active Inference and evolution, improvisation, and tradition
2. **Analyze** how systems manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Riff & Repertoire perspective
3. **Apply** comedy variation to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** cultural selection to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Riff & Repertoire perspective on systems reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Evolving system**: A system that changes over time through variation, selection, and transmission
- **Comedy variation**: The mutations and adaptations that comedy material undergoes across performances and generations
- **Cultural selection**: The process by which audiences and culture select which comedy survives and which dies
- **Medium evolution**: How comedy adapts to new media (vaudeville -> radio -> TV -> internet -> social media)
- **Comedy tradition**: The accumulated, transmitted body of comedy knowledge, conventions, and material
