## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** comedy transmission through the lens of Active Inference and evolution, improvisation, and tradition
2. **Analyze** how communication manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Riff & Repertoire perspective
3. **Apply** oral tradition to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** media preservation to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Riff & Repertoire perspective on communication reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Comedy transmission**: The process of communicating comedy material, techniques, and traditions across time
- **Oral tradition**: The transmission of comedy through direct person-to-person communication -- mentorship, workshops, backstage culture
- **Media preservation**: How recording technologies preserve comedy for transmission beyond live performance
- **Viral propagation**: The spread of comedy through digital sharing -- memes, clips, and social media
- **Comedy education**: Formal and informal systems for transmitting comedy knowledge to new practitioners

## Prerequisites

- Prior modules in Riff & Repertoire and foundational concepts from Courses 01-02
- Familiarity with Active Inference concepts of generative models, prediction error, and precision weighting
