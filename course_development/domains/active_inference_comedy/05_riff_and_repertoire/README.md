# Course 05: Riff & Repertoire

> **Quick Navigation**: [Curriculum Home](../OVERVIEW.md) | [Resources](../resources/README.md)

## 🎵 Coral (#FF6F61)

**Perspective**: The evolution of comedy — improvisation, variation, tradition

Takes the longest view, examining comedy as an evolving system. Explores improvisation, creative cognition, cultural transmission, and the building of a comedy career over decades.

## Learning Goals

By completing Riff & Repertoire, you will be able to:

1. Analyze "Who's on First?" through the evolution of comedy — improvisation, variation, tradition
2. Apply Active Inference concepts to the specific dynamics that Riff & Repertoire foregrounds
3. Connect theoretical understanding to practical comedy craft
