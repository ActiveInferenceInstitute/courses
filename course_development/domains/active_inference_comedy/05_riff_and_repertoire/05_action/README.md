## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand** improvised action through the lens of Active Inference and evolution, improvisation, and tradition
2. **Analyze** how action manifests in "Who's on First?" from the Riff & Repertoire perspective
3. **Apply** real-time policy selection to practical comedy craft and performance
4. **Connect** riffing to broader patterns in comedy as cultural practice
5. **Evaluate** how the Riff & Repertoire perspective on action reveals dynamics invisible from other vantage points

## Key Terms

- **Improvised action**: Selecting comedy actions in real time without a predetermined script or plan
- **Real-time policy selection**: Choosing the next move from a vast action space based on current observations
- **Riffing**: Spontaneous comedic exploration -- epistemic foraging through the comedy state space
- **Comedy risk**: The possibility of failure inherent in improvised action -- the higher the risk, the bigger the potential reward
- **Action space exploration**: Sampling novel actions to discover new comedy possibilities beyond the known repertoire

## Prerequisites

- Prior modules in Riff & Repertoire and foundational concepts from Courses 01-02
- Familiarity with Active Inference concepts of generative models, prediction error, and precision weighting
