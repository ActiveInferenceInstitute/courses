## Learning Objectives

1. **Recognize** every utterance, pause, and gesture in comedy as an action in the Active Inference sense
2. **Analyze** Costello's questions as epistemic actions that tragically reproduce the same ambiguity they were designed to resolve
3. **Distinguish** between pragmatic actions (achieving goals) and epistemic actions (gathering information) in comedic dialogue
4. **Map** the action repertoire of "Who's on First?" — every line classified by action type and predicted downstream effect
5. **Evaluate** why Costello's epistemic strategy fails: the action space does not contain a move that resolves the ambiguity

## Key Terms

- **Action**: Any behavior that crosses the Markov blanket from internal to external states
- **Epistemic action**: An action taken to gather information and reduce uncertainty
- **Pragmatic action**: An action taken to achieve a goal in the world
- **Epistemic foraging**: Exploring the environment through actions designed to maximize information gain
- **Action policy**: A planned sequence of actions designed to minimize expected free energy

## Prerequisites

- Modules 01-04 (Systems through Cognition) — understanding of the system, its agents, and their cognitive dynamics
- The insight that questions are not just requests for information — they are strategic moves in a coupled system
