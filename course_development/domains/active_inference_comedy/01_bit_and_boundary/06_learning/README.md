## Learning Objectives

1. **Explain** why "Who's on First?" is funny even when the audience knows every word in advance
2. **Distinguish** between model learning (Costello never learns) and performance learning (the comedians refine across thousands of shows)
3. **Apply** the concept of habituation and novelty to the persistence of comedy — why some jokes die on repetition and others survive
4. **Analyze** structural humor as comedy whose architecture generates fresh prediction error independent of content knowledge
5. **Evaluate** the role of performer learning in refining a routine's timing, emphasis, and energy across hundreds of performances

## Key Terms

- **Model learning**: Updating the generative model based on new evidence — what Costello fails to do within the routine
- **Performance learning**: Refining action policies through repeated experience — what the performers do across shows
- **Habituation**: Decreased response to repeated stimuli — the enemy of comedy
- **Structural humor**: Comedy where the architecture of the exchange generates prediction error on every pass
- **Novelty**: The presence of unexplained variance that drives continued engagement

## Prerequisites

- Modules 01-05 (Systems through Action) — full understanding of the routine's structure and dynamics
- Some experience watching or listening to the same comedy more than once
