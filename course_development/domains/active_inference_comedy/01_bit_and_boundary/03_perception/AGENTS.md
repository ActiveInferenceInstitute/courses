## Active Inference Focus

Perception in "Who's on First?" is not passive hearing — it is active inference. The same acoustic signal ("Who's on first") generates different percepts depending on the listener's generative model. This module reveals that **comedy exploits perceptual ambiguity**: the routine works because English allows "Who" to be parsed as either a question word or a proper noun, and neither parsing is wrong given the sensory data alone. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Perceptual inference** is presented as a process of mapping observations to hidden states through a generative model
- **Likelihood degeneracy** is clearly explained — the same observation (the sound "Who") is equally consistent with multiple hidden causes
- **Comedic timing** is connected to the temporal dynamics of perception — when the prediction error registers matters as much as whether it registers
- Every concept is grounded in the specific auditory experience of hearing the routine
