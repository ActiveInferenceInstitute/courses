# Syllabus: Bit & Boundary

> **Active Inference & Comedy** | **Audience**: Comedians, performers, comedy scholars, cognitive scientists, and the curious

## Course Description

Bit & Boundary explores Active Inference through the routine as a system — the mechanics of a comedic exchange. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how performers, audiences, and jokes form interacting dynamical systems — all through Abbott & Costello's "Who's on First?" and the wider craft of comedy.
