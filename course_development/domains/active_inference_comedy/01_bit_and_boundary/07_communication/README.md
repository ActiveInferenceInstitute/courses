## Learning Objectives

1. **Analyze** "Who's on First?" as a systematic violation of Gricean maxims of communication
2. **Distinguish** between communication failure at the character level and communicative success at the performance level
3. **Apply** the concept of pragmatic inference to comedic exchange — how listeners infer meaning beyond literal content
4. **Identify** the specific maxim violated in each exchange of the routine (quantity, quality, relation, manner)
5. **Evaluate** how engineered ambiguity functions as a comedy technique — deliberately constructing utterances that are literally true but pragmatically misleading

## Key Terms

- **Gricean maxims**: The cooperative principles of conversation — quantity (say enough), quality (be truthful), relation (be relevant), manner (be clear)
- **Pragmatic inference**: Inferring the speaker's intended meaning beyond the literal content of their words
- **Communicative failure**: When the listener's model of the speaker's intent fails to match the speaker's actual intent
- **Engineered ambiguity**: Deliberately constructing utterances that allow multiple valid interpretations
- **Implicature**: What is implied but not explicitly stated in an utterance
