## Active Inference Focus

The comedy of "Who's on First?" depends on a cognitive paradox: Costello receives abundant evidence that his model is wrong, yet he never updates. This module explains **why** through the lens of Active Inference. The key insight is that Costello's model is not irrational — it is locally optimal given his priors. The word "Who" genuinely functions as a question word in standard English, and Costello's model has overwhelming prior evidence for this parsing. Abbott's usage (as a proper noun) is the anomaly, not Costello's confusion. Agents working on this module must ensure that:

- **Belief perseveration** is explained through model evidence, not stupidity — Costello's model is structurally reasonable
- **Model evidence** (the likelihood of observations under a given model) is shown to favor Costello's parsing at every exchange
- The **straight man's cognitive rigidity** is distinguished from the comic's — both are rigid, but for different reasons
- The cognitive dynamics are traced exchange-by-exchange through the routine
