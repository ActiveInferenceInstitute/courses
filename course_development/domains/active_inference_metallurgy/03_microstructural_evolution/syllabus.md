# Syllabus: Microstructural Evolution

> Part of **Active Inference for Metallurgy**

## Course Overview

This unit covers Active Inference through the lens of nucleation, grain growth, precipitation, and characterization. It is designed for Microscopists, characterization engineers, and microstructure scientists.

## Schedule

| Week | Module | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Systems | Grain Boundaries as System Boundaries |
| 2 | Agents | Nuclei as Autonomous Agents |
| 3 | Perception | Microscopy and EBSD as Perceptual Systems |
| 4 | Cognition | Microstructure Prediction and Computational Modeling |
| 5 | Action | Grain Growth and Coarsening as Microstructural Action |
| 6 | Learning | Adaptive Characterization and 4D Imaging |
| 7 | Communication | Grain Boundary Networks and Communication Pathways |
| 8 | Planning | Microstructure Engineering and Design |

## Assessment

- Weekly practice quizzes (self-assessment)
- Laboratory exercises (hands-on application)
- Discussion questions (analytical and applied)

## Prerequisites

- Undergraduate-level materials science or physical metallurgy
- Basic thermodynamics (Gibbs free energy, phase diagrams)
- Familiarity with Python for computational exercises
