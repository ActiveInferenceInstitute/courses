# Process Optimization

> Part of **Active Inference for Metallurgy** | [Curriculum Home](../README.md)

## Overview

**Process Optimization** explores Active Inference through the lens of heat treatment, welding, additive manufacturing, industry 4.0. This course is designed for Process engineers, manufacturing engineers, and Industry 4.0 specialists.
