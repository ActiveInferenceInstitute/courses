# Syllabus: Process Optimization

> Part of **Active Inference for Metallurgy**

## Course Overview

This unit covers Active Inference through the lens of heat treatment, welding, additive manufacturing, industry 4.0. It is designed for Process engineers, manufacturing engineers, and Industry 4.0 specialists.

## Schedule

| Week | Module | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Systems | Manufacturing Process as Inference System |
| 2 | Agents | Sensors and Controllers as Process Agents |
| 3 | Perception | In-Situ Monitoring and Non-Destructive Testing |
| 4 | Cognition | Digital Twin Cognition and Process Simulation |
| 5 | Action | Heat Treatment and Quenching as Process Action |
| 6 | Learning | Closed-Loop Control and Adaptive Manufacturing |
| 7 | Communication | SCADA, IoT, and Industrial Data Pipelines |
| 8 | Planning | Process Route Optimization and the Digital Thread |

## Assessment

- Weekly practice quizzes (self-assessment)
- Laboratory exercises (hands-on application)
- Discussion questions (analytical and applied)

## Prerequisites

- Undergraduate-level materials science or physical metallurgy
- Basic thermodynamics (Gibbs free energy, phase diagrams)
- Familiarity with Python for computational exercises
