# Active Inference for Metallurgy — Pedagogical Overview

## Vision

This curriculum makes Active Inference accessible to materials scientists and metallurgical engineers through four complementary lenses. Each course covers the same 8 foundational topics but from a different disciplinary perspective — from atomic-scale thermodynamics to factory-floor process optimization.

## Tone & Style

Engineering-focused. Thermodynamic rigor meets shop-floor pragmatism. Phase diagrams, diffusion equations, microstructural characterization, and digital twins.

## Course Map

### Course 1: Foundations of Metallurgical Systems

**Perspective**: Crystal structures, defects, and fundamental thermodynamics

### Course 2: Thermodynamic Inference

**Perspective**: Phase equilibria, transformation kinetics, and CALPHAD

### Course 3: Microstructural Evolution

**Perspective**: Nucleation, grain growth, precipitation, and characterization

### Course 4: Process Optimization & Smart Manufacturing

**Perspective**: Heat treatment, welding, additive manufacturing, Industry 4.0

## Cross-Course Connections

The same 8 topics appear in every course, building a spiral of understanding. See [Cross-Course Map](./resources/cross_course_map.md) for detailed connections.

## How to Use This Curriculum

- **Sequential**: Work through one course start to finish.
- **Parallel**: Study the same module across all 4 courses.
- **Exploratory**: Use the [Learning Pathways](./resources/learning_pathways.md).
