# Syllabus: Metallurgical Systems

> Part of **Active Inference for Metallurgy**

## Course Overview

This unit covers Active Inference through the lens of crystal structures, defects, and fundamental thermodynamics. It is designed for Materials science graduate students and metallurgical engineers.

## Schedule

| Week | Module | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Systems | Crystal Lattices as Markov Blankets |
| 2 | Agents | Atoms and Defects as Agents |
| 3 | Perception | X-Ray Diffraction and Spectroscopic Sensing |
| 4 | Cognition | Lattice Energy and First-Principles Cognition |
| 5 | Action | Deformation and Alloying as Material Action |
| 6 | Learning | The Arc of Alloy Development |
| 7 | Communication | Diffusion as Atomic Communication |
| 8 | Planning | Alloy Design as Strategic Planning |

## Assessment

- Weekly practice quizzes (self-assessment)
- Laboratory exercises (hands-on application)
- Discussion questions (analytical and applied)

## Prerequisites

- Undergraduate-level materials science or physical metallurgy
- Basic thermodynamics (Gibbs free energy, phase diagrams)
- Familiarity with Python for computational exercises
