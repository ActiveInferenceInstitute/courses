# References: Active Inference for Metallurgy

> Recommended reading and resources for materials scientists, metallurgical engineers, and process engineers.
> Organized by category: foundational Active Inference, metallurgy and materials science, thermodynamics, and applied intersections.

---

## Active Inference & Free Energy Principle — Foundations

1. Friston, K. (2010). The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory? *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 11(2), 127–138.
2. Parr, T., Pezzulo, G., & Friston, K. J. (2022). *Active Inference: The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, and Behavior*. MIT Press.
3. Da Costa, L., Parr, T., Sajid, N., Vesber, S., Ryan, V., & Friston, K. (2020). Active inference on discrete state-spaces: A synthesis. *Journal of Mathematical Psychology*, 99, 102447.
4. Smith, R., Friston, K. J., & Whyte, C. J. (2022). A step-by-step tutorial on active inference and its application to empirical data. *Journal of Mathematical Psychology*, 107, 102632.

## Metallurgy & Materials Science — Core Texts

1. Porter, D. A., Easterling, K. E., & Sherif, M. Y. (2021). *Phase Transformations in Metals and Alloys* (4th ed.). CRC Press.
2. Callister, W. D., & Rethwisch, D. G. (2020). *Materials Science and Engineering: An Introduction* (10th ed.). Wiley.
3. Reed-Hill, R. E., & Abbaschian, R. (2009). *Physical Metallurgy Principles* (4th ed.). Cengage.
4. Asm International. (2004). *ASM Handbook, Volume 3: Alloy Phase Diagrams*. ASM International.
5. Christian, J. W. (2002). *The Theory of Transformations in Metals and Alloys* (3rd ed.). Pergamon.
6. Bhadeshia, H. K. D. H., & Honeycombe, R. W. K. (2017). *Steels: Microstructure and Properties* (4th ed.). Butterworth-Heinemann.

## Thermodynamics & Phase Equilibria

1. Hillert, M. (2007). *Phase Equilibria, Phase Diagrams and Phase Transformations: Their Thermodynamic Basis* (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press.
2. Lukas, H. L., Fries, S. G., & Sundman, B. (2007). *Computational Thermodynamics: The Calphad Method*. Cambridge University Press.
3. Saunders, N., & Miodownik, A. P. (1998). *CALPHAD (Calculation of Phase Diagrams): A Comprehensive Guide*. Pergamon.
4. DeHoff, R. T. (2006). *Thermodynamics in Materials Science* (2nd ed.). CRC Press.

## Characterization & Microscopy

1. Schwartz, A. J., Kumar, M., Adams, B. L., & Field, D. P. (2009). *Electron Backscatter Diffraction in Materials Science* (2nd ed.). Springer.
2. Williams, D. B., & Carter, C. B. (2009). *Transmission Electron Microscopy: A Textbook for Materials Science* (2nd ed.). Springer.
3. Suryanarayana, C., & Norton, M. G. (2013). *X-Ray Diffraction: A Practical Approach* (2nd ed.). Springer.

## Process Engineering & Smart Manufacturing

1. ASM International. (2002). *ASM Handbook, Volume 4: Heat Treating*. ASM International.
2. DebRoy, T., Wei, H. L., Zuback, J. S., Mukherjee, T., Elmer, J. W., Milewski, J. O., ... & Zhang, W. (2018). Additive manufacturing of metallic components – process, structure and properties. *Progress in Materials Science*, 92, 112–224.
3. Kou, S. (2003). *Welding Metallurgy* (2nd ed.). Wiley-Interscience.
4. Tao, F., Zhang, H., Liu, A., & Nee, A. Y. C. (2019). Digital twin in industry: state-of-the-art. *IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics*, 15(4), 2405–2415.

## Active Inference Applied to Physical Systems

1. Ramstead, M. J. D., Badcock, P. B., & Friston, K. J. (2018). Answering Schrödinger's question: A free-energy formulation. *Physics of Life Reviews*, 24, 1–16.
2. Friston, K. (2019). A free energy principle for a particular physics. arXiv preprint arXiv:1906.10184.
3. Kauffman, S. A. (1993). *The Origins of Order: Self-Organization and Selection in Evolution*. Oxford University Press.
4. Prigogine, I., & Stengers, I. (1984). *Order Out of Chaos: Man's New Dialogue with Nature*. Bantam Books.

## Computational Tools

1. Otis, R., & Liu, Z.-K. (2017). pycalphad: CALPHAD-based computational thermodynamics in Python. *Journal of Open Research Software*, 5(1), 1.
2. Steinbach, I. (2009). Phase-field models in materials science. *Modelling and Simulation in Materials Science and Engineering*, 17(7), 073001.
3. Bachmann, F., Hielscher, R., & Schaeben, H. (2010). Texture analysis with MTEX – free and open source software toolbox. *Solid State Phenomena*, 160, 63–68.
