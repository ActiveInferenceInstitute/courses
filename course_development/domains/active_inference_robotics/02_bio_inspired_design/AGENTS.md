## Identity

- **Course**: Bio-Inspired Design

- **Audience**: Robotics engineers and researchers
- **Tone**: Engineering-focused. Hardware + software. ROS2, sensor fusion, control theory.

## Course Theme

This course examines Active Inference through the lens of biological inspiration for robotic design. Every module connects the eight Active Inference spine concepts (systems, agents, perception, cognition, action, learning, communication, planning) to biomimicry & neural architectures, providing robotics engineers with both theoretical understanding and practical design skills.

## Conventions

All modules in this course must:

1. Use language appropriate for robotics engineers and researchers with diverse technical backgrounds.
2. Frame all concepts through the lens of biomimicry & neural architectures.
3. Include design challenge activities that involve hands-on design, analysis, and implementation.
4. Adhere to notation standards in [../resources/notation_table.md](../resources/notation_table.md).
5. Cross-reference the shared [../resources/glossary.md](../resources/glossary.md).
6. Maintain the Active Inference integration pattern: sensorimotor loops, proprioceptive inference, and motor commands as predictions.
7. Connect to practical robotic applications using industry-standard tools and frameworks.
