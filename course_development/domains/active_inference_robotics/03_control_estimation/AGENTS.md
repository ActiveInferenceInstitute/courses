## Identity

- **Course**: Control & Estimation
- **Number**: 3
- **Perspective**: Kalman filters, PID, MPC, active inference
- **Lab Type**: Simulation Lab
- **Audience**: Robotics engineers and researchers
- **Tone**: Engineering-focused. Hardware + software. ROS2, sensor fusion, control theory.

## Course Theme

This course examines Active Inference through the lens of mathematical control and estimation theory. Every module connects the eight Active Inference spine concepts (systems, agents, perception, cognition, action, learning, communication, planning) to kalman filters, pid, mpc, active inference, providing robotics engineers with both theoretical understanding and practical design skills.

## Conventions

All modules in this course must:

1. Use language appropriate for robotics engineers and researchers with diverse technical backgrounds.
2. Frame all concepts through the lens of kalman filters, pid, mpc, active inference.
3. Include simulation lab activities that involve hands-on design, analysis, and implementation.
4. Adhere to notation standards in [../resources/notation_table.md](../resources/notation_table.md).
5. Cross-reference the shared [../resources/glossary.md](../resources/glossary.md).
6. Maintain the Active Inference integration pattern: sensorimotor loops, proprioceptive inference, and motor commands as predictions.
7. Connect to practical robotic applications using industry-standard tools and frameworks.
