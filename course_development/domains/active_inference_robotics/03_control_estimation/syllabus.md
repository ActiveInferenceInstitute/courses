# Syllabus: Control & Estimation

> **Active Inference for Robotics** | **Audience**: Robotics engineers and researchers

## Course Description

Control & Estimation explores Active Inference through kalman filters, pid, mpc, active inference. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn.

## Schedule

| Week | Module | Topic | Activity |

| Week 8 | [Planning](./08_planning/module.md) | Autonomous Navigation and Mission Planning | Simulation Lab |
