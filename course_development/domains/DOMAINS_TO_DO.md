## How to Use This Tracker

1. **Propose a domain:** Add a row with 💡 status and a brief note
2. **Research phase:** Change to 🔬 if the domain needs investigation before scoping
3. **Plan the course:** Change to 📋 when scoped and ready for development
4. **Start building:** Change to 🔨 and create the domain folder
5. **Complete:** Change to ✅ when the course is fully reviewed and published
6. **Assign a lead:** Fill in the Lead column when someone takes ownership
