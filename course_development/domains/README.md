## Overview

These domain curricula apply Active Inference and the Free Energy Principle to specific professional, creative, and research fields. Each follows the standard 8-topic spiral structure (Systems, Agents, Perception, Cognition, Action, Learning, Communication, Planning) across 4–5 specialized units.

The domain courses serve as **proof of universality** — demonstrating that the same mathematical framework that explains neural computation can illuminate comedy timing, crochet pattern cognition, metallurgical phase transformations, robotic control, organizational strategy, embodied practices, and inventive engineering.

## Theory Semantics

The Free Energy Principle is not confined to neuroscience — it applies wherever systems maintain boundaries and act on predictions. Each domain course demonstrates this universality through a distinctive lens:

- **Embodied Cognition**: The body is an inference engine. Interoception, proprioception, and felt sense are forms of active inference. The Markov blanket is the skin, the breath, the boundary between self and world. Perception of one's own body is the most primal inference: "What are my internal states?"
- **Organizations**: An organization is a collective agent with a shared generative model. Departments form nested Markov blankets, with inter-departmental communication as boundary states. Strategy is policy selection under expected free energy — choosing actions that both achieve goals (pragmatic) and reduce market uncertainty (epistemic).
- **Robotics**: A robot is a physically instantiated active inference agent. Sensors are observation channels (the A matrix), actuators are action channels (policy execution), and the control loop implements the perception-action cycle. Bio-inspired designs show how biological inference strategies translate into engineered systems.
- **Crochet Circles**: A crochet circle is a nested system of active inference agents. Each crocheter maintains a generative model (the pattern) and minimizes prediction error with every stitch. The hook-yarn interface is a Markov blanket. The circle itself is a collective agent with shared generative models, transmitting technique through observation and instruction.
- **Inventions**: An invention process is active inference made tangible. The inventor maintains a generative model of a desired solution and iterates prototypes to minimize the gap between prediction and reality. Each design cycle is a perception-action loop; patent analysis reveals the genealogy of generative models across technical lineages.
- **Metallurgy**: A metallurgical system is an inference process at the atomic scale. Phase transformations minimize free energy (in the thermodynamic sense that directly parallels the FEP). Crystal structures are emergent Markov blankets — grain boundaries separate internal lattice states from external states. Heat treatment is policy selection under thermodynamic constraints.
- **Comedy**: A comedic performance is prediction error engineered for pleasure. The comedian builds expectations (generative models in the audience) and then violates them (punchlines as precision-weighted prediction errors). Timing is the precision of the error signal. The room itself is a collective inference agent — when the audience laughs, they are minimizing surprise *together*.
