# Lab: Innovation Communication Design

## Objective

Design a multi-stakeholder communication strategy for your invention, crafting messages that transmit your generative model to different audiences with appropriate precision, evidence, and emotional resonance. You will practice writing marketing copy, structuring investor pitches, and designing brand narratives — all grounded in Active Inference communication theory.

## Materials and Prerequisites

- Your invention concept and all previous analyses from Modules 01-06
- Understanding of Active Inference communication from Section 1

## Discussion and Debrief

1. How did articulating your core model change your understanding of what you are communicating? Was the compression exercise (three sentences to one sentence) difficult? What got lost?

2. Compare your marketing messages at the three precision levels. How does precision level change the type of adopter you are addressing?

3. How did structuring your pitch around the investor's inference framework change your approach compared to presenting information in the order you find most compelling?

4. What was the hardest part of designing brand consistency? Where are the most likely points of inconsistency in your actual operations?

5. Did you identify any stakeholder communication conflicts? How would you resolve them without being dishonest to any stakeholder?
