Lab: Stakeholder Inference Mapping

Objective

Model the key stakeholders in your invention's ecosystem as Active Inference agents, analyze their generative models, and design strategies that reduce free energy across multiple agent types. You will develop a multi-stakeholder strategy that accounts for the diverse inference processes of users, investors, regulators, and competitors.

Materials and Prerequisites

  • Your invention concept and ecosystem map from Module 01
  • Familiarity with adoption curve theory (Rogers' diffusion of innovations)
  • Understanding of Active Inference agent concepts from Section 1

Discussion and Debrief

  1. Which stakeholder's generative model was most difficult for you to construct? What does this difficulty reveal about gaps in your understanding of your ecosystem?

  2. How did explicitly modeling stakeholders as inference agents change your strategy compared to thinking about them as simple "targets" or "obstacles"?

  3. Were there stakeholders you had not considered before this exercise? How might their inclusion change your approach?

  4. Discuss the concept of the "chasm" with peers. Do different inventions face different types of chasms? Is the chasm always between early adopters and early majority, or can it appear at other transitions?

  5. How would you maintain and update these stakeholder models over time as your invention progresses from concept to market? What evidence would trigger a significant model revision?
