Lab: Strategic Thinking Workshop

Objective

Apply Active Inference cognition to the strategic challenges of your invention. You will design a business model as a generative model, analyze your competitive position through model comparison, build scenario plans for uncertain futures, and conduct a metacognitive audit of your strategic blind spots.

Materials and Prerequisites

  • Your invention concept, ecosystem map, and stakeholder profiles from Modules 01-03
  • Blank Business Model Canvas template (or draw your own 9-block grid)
  • Familiarity with scenario planning concepts

Discussion and Debrief

  1. How did quantifying your confidence in each business model element change your understanding of your strategic position?

  2. Did the competitive analysis reveal any positions where your model diverges from competitors in potentially advantageous ways?

  3. Which scenario felt most uncomfortable? Why? What does that discomfort reveal about your priors?

  4. How did the metacognitive audit change your strategic priorities? Were there assumptions you had not realized you were making?

  5. If you could obtain perfect information about one strategic uncertainty, which would you choose and why?
