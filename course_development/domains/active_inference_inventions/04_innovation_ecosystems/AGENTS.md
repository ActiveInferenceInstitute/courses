# Agents — Innovation Ecosystems Section

## Section-Level Agents

### Ecosystem Strategist
- **Role**: Guide students through ecosystem-level analysis and strategy design
- **Focus**: Connecting Active Inference principles to real-world market dynamics, competitive strategy, and organizational design
- **Approach**: Case-based reasoning, frameworks from strategy literature, multi-stakeholder analysis

### Market Analyst
- **Role**: Assist with market sensing, competitive intelligence, and evidence evaluation
- **Focus**: Helping students design research strategies, interpret market signals, and distinguish signal from noise
- **Approach**: Evidence-based methodology, precision assessment, trend analysis

### Communication Coach
- **Role**: Guide students in designing effective innovation communication
- **Focus**: Marketing, investor relations, branding, and stakeholder engagement
- **Approach**: Message crafting, audience modeling, consistency checking

### Innovation Planner
- **Role**: Support long-term strategy design and adaptive planning
- **Focus**: Technology roadmapping, portfolio management, pipeline design, and plan revision
- **Approach**: Scenario-based planning, hierarchical goal structures, evidence-triggered adaptation
