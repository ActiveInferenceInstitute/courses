# Practice Quiz: Innovation Ecosystems

## Part A: Multiple Choice

1. An innovation ecosystem is best described as:
A) A group of companies competing in the same market
B) A multi-agent Active Inference system where each stakeholder maintains a generative model and acts to minimize its own free energy
C) The natural environment in which an invention operates
D) A government program supporting innovation

2. According to Rogers' diffusion of innovation, early adopters accept new inventions because:
A) They are less intelligent than the majority
B) Their generative models have lower precision on existing solutions, making them more receptive to novelty
C) They receive financial incentives
D) They have no alternative options

3. Tesla's "master plan" of starting with a luxury car and scaling down illustrates:
A) A fixed plan that cannot adapt
B) Staged action sequencing where each stage resolves specific uncertainties and builds capability for the next
C) Random market experimentation
D) Cost-based pricing strategy

4. Customer discovery interviews provide evidence that is:
A) Always more precise than quantitative surveys
B) Rich in causal insight but lower in statistical precision than large-sample quantitative data
C) Useless for strategic decisions
D) Only valuable for consumer products

5. Instagram's pivot from Burbn (a location-sharing app) to a photo-sharing app is an example of:
A) Parameter learning -- adjusting features within the same product model
B) Structure learning -- fundamentally changing the generative model of what the product is
C) Failure -- the company lost its original vision
D) Communication strategy -- rebranding without changing the product

6. Open-source strategies succeed by:
A) Eliminating all intellectual property
B) Sharing the generative model with the ecosystem, enabling distributed development and adaptation that generates more value than protection would
C) Making the product free so no one competes
D) Attracting government funding

7. A long-term innovation portfolio should balance:
A) Only exploitation of current products
B) Only exploration of new possibilities
C) Exploitation of current advantages and exploration of new possibilities, with the balance shifting as market uncertainty changes
D) Equal investment in all possible projects

## Part B: Short Answer

1. Explain how Moderna's mRNA platform illustrates the difference between an invention being technically ready and the ecosystem being ready for adoption. What ecosystem conditions changed during the COVID-19 pandemic that enabled rapid deployment?

2. A startup's product receives enthusiastic user feedback but struggles with customer acquisition costs. Using Active Inference concepts, explain whether this is a product problem (the generative model is wrong about user value) or an ecosystem problem (the generative model is wrong about the acquisition channel). What evidence would distinguish between these two diagnoses?

3. Describe how Wikipedia's open editing model functions as a distributed Active Inference system. Who are the agents, what are the generative models, what are the prediction errors, and how are they resolved?
