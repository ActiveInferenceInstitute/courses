Lab: Designing Your Learning Engine

Objective

You will build customer feedback loops, practice pivot analysis, and design knowledge management processes — all grounded in Active Inference learning principles.

Discussion and Debrief

How does distinguishing between parameter updating and model restructuring change your approach to processing negative market feedback?

What was the most surprising insight from designing your customer feedback loop? Were there high-precision evidence sources you had not considered?

In the pivot analysis exercise, how difficult was it to honestly evaluate the evidence against your current model? What cognitive biases did you notice in yourself?

How would you maintain your knowledge management system under the pressure of rapid startup growth? What compromises are acceptable?

Compare your learning organization scorecard with a peer's. What structural differences between your organizations explain different learning rates?
