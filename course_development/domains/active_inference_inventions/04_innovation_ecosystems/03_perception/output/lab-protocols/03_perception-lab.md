# Lab: Market Sensing Design Workshop

## Objective

Design a comprehensive market sensing strategy for your invention using Active Inference perception principles. You will practice structuring customer discovery conversations for maximum precision, analyzing competitive signals, and building a trend detection framework — all treating market research as active inference about your ecosystem.

## Materials and Prerequisites

- Your invention concept and ecosystem map from Modules 01-02
- Access to web search for basic competitive and market research
- Familiarity with Bayesian updating concepts

## Discussion and Debrief

1. How did the prediction-first approach change your research design compared to starting with "What do I need to know?"

2. Which of your predictions do you feel least confident about? What sensing activity would most efficiently reduce that uncertainty?

3. How did reverse-engineering your competitor's model from their actions change your understanding of the competitive landscape?

4. What was the most surprising insight from the trend detection exercise? Did any trends turn out to be more noise than signal upon closer analysis?

5. How would you adapt your sensing portfolio as your invention moves from concept to prototype to launch? What new sensing activities become necessary at each stage?
