# Lab: Designing Your Iteration Roadmap

## Objective

Plan a structured sequence of prototype iterations for your invention project, specifying what each iteration tests, what decisions hinge on the results, and how the action-perception loop will drive convergence toward a working solution.

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with the action-perception loop, MVP concept, A/B testing, and pivot-or-persevere decisions from this module's lecture
- Your prototype specification from Module 01 and test results (real or hypothetical) from Modules 03-04
- Your current list of unresolved design questions

## Materials

- Large sheet of paper or whiteboard for roadmap visualization
- Sticky notes or index cards
- Your prototype and test records from prior modules

## Discussion and Debrief

1. **MVP discipline**: Was it difficult to strip your prototype down to the minimum viable version? What psychological pull did you feel to include "just one more feature"?

2. **A/B clarity**: Did designing the A/B test force you to clarify what you are actually uncertain about? Was the design choice harder or easier to define than you expected?

3. **Pivot honesty**: Were you able to honestly assess whether your project shows persevere or pivot signals? What made this assessment emotionally challenging?

4. **Roadmap realism**: Does your iteration roadmap feel achievable? Where are the resource bottlenecks? What could you do to speed up the loop frequency?

5. **Loop speed**: If you could run the action-perception loop twice as fast (half the iteration time), how would that change your strategy? What would you sacrifice for speed?
