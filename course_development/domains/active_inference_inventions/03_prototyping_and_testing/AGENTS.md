# AGENTS.md — Section 3: Prototyping and Testing

## Section Context

This is Section 3 of 4 in the Active Inference for Inventions course. The section theme is "Building, testing, and iterating — physical and digital prototypes, feedback loops, and learning from failure."

## Content Generation Guidelines

### Module Structure

Each module follows the Active Inference spine (systems, agents, perception, cognition, action, learning, communication, planning) applied to the prototyping and testing domain. All content should:

- Connect Active Inference concepts to practical prototyping and testing activities
- Use real invention case studies (Dyson, Wright Brothers, SpaceX, IDEO, Toyota, etc.)
- Support the "bring your own invention" pedagogy — labs work for any background
- Maintain the emerald green (#10b981) accent color for all dashboards
