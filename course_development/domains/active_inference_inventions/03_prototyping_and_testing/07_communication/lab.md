# Lab: Creating Your Invention's Documentation System

## Objective

Build a documentation system for your invention project that captures your generative model as an external representation — including design decisions, version history, test results, and uncertainties — so that another competent agent could continue your work.

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with lab notebooks, version control, decision records, and uncertainty communication from this module's lecture
- Your invention project materials (prototype, test data, design notes from prior modules)
- A documentation medium (physical notebook, digital document, or wiki)

## Materials

- Lab notebook or digital equivalent
- Camera (phone camera is fine) for prototype photography
- Your prototype (or detailed drawings/descriptions)
- All test records from prior modules

## Discussion and Debrief

1. **Documentation debt**: How much of your invention's knowledge currently exists only in your head? Was this exercise a wake-up call about documentation gaps?

2. **Decision amnesia**: Were you able to reconstruct the rationale for your key design decisions? Had you forgotten some of the reasoning? What does this suggest about the value of contemporaneous documentation?

3. **Uncertainty honesty**: Was it uncomfortable to formally acknowledge what you do not know? Why might investors, managers, or collaborators benefit from seeing your uncertainty map?

4. **Version control gaps**: What information about previous prototype versions has been lost? How would having that information change your current work?

5. **The future reader**: If you revisit this project in five years, will your documentation make sense to future-you? What would future-you wish you had recorded?
