# Lab: Building Your Comprehensive Test Plan

## Objective

Create a comprehensive test plan for your invention project that prioritizes hypotheses by expected free energy, specifies test protocols, allocates resources, and integrates regulatory requirements. By the end of this lab, you will have a structured document that guides your entire testing phase.

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with expected free energy prioritization, test protocol design, resource allocation, and regulatory testing from this module's lecture
- Your iteration roadmap from Module 05, learning system from Module 06, and documentation framework from Module 07
- A clear understanding of your invention's remaining uncertainties

## Materials

- All project documentation from prior modules
- Notebook or digital document for the test plan
- Calculator for resource estimation

## Discussion and Debrief

1. **Priority surprises**: Did the expected free energy ranking produce a different testing order than your intuition? What does this suggest about how you naturally prioritize?

2. **Protocol discipline**: Was it difficult to specify success and failure criteria in advance? What is the temptation you feel to leave these vague?

3. **Resource reality**: After mapping resources to testing needs, do you have enough to test everything? What trade-offs are you forced to make?

4. **Testing debt**: Are you carrying any testing debt — important tests you have deferred because they are inconvenient or expensive? What is the risk?

5. **Adaptability**: How will your test plan change as early tests resolve uncertainties? Is the plan structured enough to provide guidance but flexible enough to adapt?
