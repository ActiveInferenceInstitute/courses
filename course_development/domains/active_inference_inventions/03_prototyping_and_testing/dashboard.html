Section 3: Prototyping and Testing

Building, testing, and iterating — physical and digital prototypes, feedback loops, and learning from failure

Active Inference for Inventions

Every invention begins as a mental model. Prototyping makes that model physical, testable, and accountable to reality. This section applies Active Inference to the full cycle of building prototypes, testing them against the world, interpreting what the results mean, iterating based on evidence, and planning what to test next. Eight modules follow the Active Inference spine from systems through planning, each addressing a distinct aspect of the prototyping and testing process.
