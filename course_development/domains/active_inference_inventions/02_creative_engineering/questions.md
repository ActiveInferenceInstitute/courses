# Study Questions: Creative Engineering

1. How does Bell Labs' physical layout and interdisciplinary culture illustrate the concept of a creative system with a Markov blanket? What were the sensory and active states of this system?

2. Compare the Shenzhen hardware ecosystem to a centralized corporate R&D lab as creative systems. How do their system boundaries, information flows, and iteration speeds differ?

3. Describe the three inventor archetypes (tinkerer, systematic inventor, accidental discoverer) in Active Inference terms. What differs in each archetype's generative model, prior precision, and exploration strategy?

4. How did the complementary generative models of Wozniak (engineering) and Jobs (design and communication) enable the Apple I and Macintosh? What would each have produced alone?

5. Explain how the Swiffer cleaning system was invented through ethnographic observation. What prediction errors did the observers detect that existing product designers had missed, and why?

6. The Shinkansen bullet train's nose redesign was inspired by a kingfisher's beak. What cognitive process enables this kind of cross-domain transfer, and how does it relate to the structure of the inventor's generative model?

7. Describe Gutenberg's invention of the printing press as analogical reasoning. What was the source domain, what was the target domain, and what structural mapping connected them?

8. How does TRIZ's concept of contradiction resolution relate to Active Inference? Use Samsung's foldable display as an example of resolving a contradiction between two desirable but seemingly incompatible features.

9. Jeff Hawkins tested the Palm Pilot concept with a wooden block. In Active Inference terms, what hypothesis was this prototype testing, and why was a wooden block sufficient to test it?

10. The Apollo 13 CO2 scrubber was built from duct tape, cardboard, and spacesuit hoses. How does the concept of bricolage illustrate creative action under extreme constraint? How did the constraint structure the solution space?

11. Explain the biomimicry approach to invention using the Eastgate Centre in Harare. How does nature's generative model (termite mound ventilation) transfer to the architectural domain?

12. What is the exploration-exploitation dilemma in creative work, and how does Active Inference's expected free energy framework resolve it? When should an inventor explore, and when should they exploit?

13. Describe the double diamond design process in Active Inference terms. What role does divergent thinking play (exploration), and what role does convergent thinking play (exploitation)?

14. How does deliberate practice expand an inventor's generative model? Compare an inventor who reads 100 patents in their domain to one who reads 100 patents across 10 unrelated domains.

15. Why is the creative pitch difficult from an Active Inference perspective? What is the fundamental communication challenge when the inventor's generative model includes concepts that the audience's model does not?
