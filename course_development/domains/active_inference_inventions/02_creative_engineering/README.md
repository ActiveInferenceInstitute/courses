# Section 2: Creative Engineering

## Theme

The creative process of inventing -- ideation, cognitive reframing, and generating novel solutions through Active Inference.

## Overview

This section examines the internal creative process through the lens of Active Inference. Where Section 1 established foundational concepts (what is an invention?), Section 2 asks: how do we actually invent? Each module applies one pillar of the Active Inference framework to a specific aspect of creative practice, building a comprehensive theory of inventive cognition and action.

The central insight of this section is that creativity is not mysterious -- it is the systematic operation of generative models under uncertainty. Perception detects prediction errors that others miss. Cognition transforms models through analogy, combination, and contradiction resolution. Action externalizes models into testable form. Learning updates models from experience. Communication aligns models between people. Planning manages the balance between exploring and exploiting the solution space.

## Learning Approach

All labs use a "bring your own invention" approach -- students apply each module's concepts to their own projects, regardless of domain. Whether working on a mechanical device, software application, social program, artistic installation, or business process, the Active Inference framework applies.
