Lab: Mapping Your Creative System

Objective

Map the creative system in which you currently operate (or intend to operate) as an Active Inference system. By the end of this lab, you will have a concrete diagram of your creative system's Markov blanket, an inventory of its constraints and affordances, and an actionable plan for modifying the system to better support your inventive goals.

Materials / Prerequisites

  • Your own invention idea or project (any domain, any stage)
  • Paper and pen/pencil (or digital drawing tool)
  • A timer
  • Access to your actual workspace or a clear memory of it
  • Completed reading of Module 01: The Creative System

Discussion / Debrief Questions

  1. Which element of your creative system analysis surprised you most? Was it an input you did not realize you were relying on, a constraint you had not recognized, or an affordance you were not using?

  2. Compare your creative system to one of the case studies from the module reading (Bell Labs or Shenzhen). What structural feature do they have that your system lacks? Is it feasible to add it?

  3. How does the distinction between "smooth" and "rugged" free energy landscapes apply to your current creative system? Is your system better suited for incremental or radical innovation? Is that what you need?

  4. If you were designing a creative system from scratch for your invention project — with unlimited resources — what would it look like? How does it differ from your current system, and what is the most important single difference?

  5. Consider the relationship between your creative system and the broader ecosystem (your industry, your community, the state of technology). How does the broader system constrain or enable what your creative system can achieve?
