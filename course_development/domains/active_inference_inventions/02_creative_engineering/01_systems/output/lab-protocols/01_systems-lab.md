# Lab: Mapping Your Creative System

## Objective

Map the creative system in which you currently operate (or intend to operate) as an Active Inference system. By the end of this lab, you will have a concrete diagram of your creative system's Markov blanket, an inventory of its constraints and affordances, and an actionable plan for modifying the system to better support your inventive goals.

## Materials / Prerequisites

- Your own invention idea or project (any domain, any stage)
- Paper and pen/pencil (or digital drawing tool)
- A timer
- Access to your actual workspace or a clear memory of it
- Completed reading of Module 01: The Creative System
