Three Modes of Inventive Learning

Parameter learning refines accuracy within your existing model (calibrating cost estimates). Structure learning changes the model itself (realizing user behavior is driven by social norms, not individual choice). Policy learning expands your action repertoire (learning to solder, code, or interview). Effective inventive learning combines all three.

Deliberate Practice for Invention

Systematic, effortful improvement targeting specific weaknesses with feedback. Edison maintained daily experiment quotas and detailed notebooks. Effective inventive practice includes problem-framing exercises, analogical reasoning training, prototyping drills, and failure analysis. Target weaknesses, not strengths — comfort is the enemy of growth.

Patent Database Mining

The global patent database (100M+ documents) is a training set for inventive thinking. Each patent documents the prior model (Background), the prediction error (Problem), and the updated model (Solution). Reading patents through this lens extracts structural principles of inventive thinking that transfer across domains.

Biomimicry: Nature's Generative Models

3.8 billion years of natural selection have refined biological solutions to engineering challenges. Termite mounds inform building ventilation (Eastgate Centre: 90% energy savings). Kingfisher beaks inform bullet train noses. Key principle: transfer functional relationships, not surface morphology. Understand the physics, not just the shape.

The Learning Spiral

Inventive learning spirals through perception (notice), cognition (reason), action (build), and learning (update model). Each cycle raises capability because learning changes subsequent perception. All four phases are necessary — skipping action means reading without building; skipping cognition means building without reasoning. The spiral flattens without deliberate management.
