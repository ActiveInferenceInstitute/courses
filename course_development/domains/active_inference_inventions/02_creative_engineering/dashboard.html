Section 2: Creative Engineering

The creative process of inventing -- ideation, cognitive reframing, and generating novel solutions through Active Inference

Active Inference for Inventions

This section examines how inventors perceive problems, generate solutions, build prototypes, learn from experience, communicate ideas, and plan creative work. Each module applies one pillar of Active Inference to a specific aspect of the creative process.
