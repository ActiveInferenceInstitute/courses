Prediction Error as the Engine of Learning

Learning is driven by the gap between prediction and observation. A failed prototype that reveals specific model inaccuracies is more informative than a success that confirms what was already believed. The discipline is treating every deviation as data, not disappointment.

Parameter Learning

Adjusting specific values within a correct model structure. Edison testing filament materials, WD-40 refining 40 formulations, a baker adjusting oven temperature. Characterized by incremental convergence toward optimal values. Risk: converging on a local optimum when a different structure might be better.

Structure Learning

Changing the model's causal architecture when prediction errors resist parameter adjustment. Dyson shifting from bags to cyclones, Netflix from DVDs to streaming, sail to steam propulsion. More difficult and disruptive than parameter learning, but the source of breakthrough inventions.

The Learning Trajectory

Inventions follow a trajectory: early structure learning (discarding wrong approaches), followed by parameter optimization within the chosen structure, converging toward diminishing returns. Recognizing where you are on this trajectory helps allocate resources between exploration and optimization.

Building a Learning Protocol

Systematic documentation: record predictions before testing, observe carefully during testing, analyze prediction errors after testing, update the model, and plan the next iteration. This mirrors the scientific method adapted for invention and enables cumulative, team-wide learning.
