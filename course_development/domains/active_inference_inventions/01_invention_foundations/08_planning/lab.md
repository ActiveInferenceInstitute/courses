# Lab: Building Your Invention Roadmap

## Objective

Create an adaptive roadmap for your invention project that uses Active Inference principles: stage-gated development, expected free energy evaluation, explicit decision points, and built-in plan revision mechanisms. By the end of this lab, you will have a practical, evidence-driven project plan that balances learning with progress.

## Materials and Prerequisites

- All previous lab outputs (system map, agent profile, prediction error hunt, causal model, prototype plans, learning log, communication strategy)
- Paper or digital planning tool (a simple spreadsheet or even a hand-drawn timeline works)
- No project management background required

## Discussion and Debrief

1. **Epistemic vs. pragmatic balance**: Does your plan weight learning early and results later? Or does it try to produce results before critical uncertainties are resolved? How would you adjust?

2. **Decision quality**: Are your go/no-go criteria specific enough to actually make decisions? Or are they vague enough that you could justify any decision? Sharpen them.

3. **Adaptiveness**: How resilient is your plan to unexpected results? If Stage 1 produces a surprise, can your plan accommodate it without total replanning?

4. **Planning horizon humility**: Did you plan too far ahead? Is there detail in your later stages that you cannot possibly know yet?

5. **First step clarity**: Can you take the first action immediately? If not, what is blocking you, and how can you remove the blocker?
