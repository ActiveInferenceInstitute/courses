# Active Inference for Inventions

A domain-specific Active Inference curriculum that teaches the Free Energy Principle and Active Inference through the lens of **invention and innovation**. This course guides individuals and working groups — regardless of background or skill level — in using Active Inference as a framework for their own inventive practice.

## Course Structure

The course is organized into four sections, each containing eight modules that follow the Active Inference spine (Systems, Agents, Perception, Cognition, Action, Learning, Communication, Planning):

### Section 1: Invention Foundations
Understanding inventions as adaptive systems — problem spaces, boundaries, and the inventor-environment relationship.

### Section 2: Creative Engineering
The creative process of inventing — ideation, cognitive reframing, and generating novel solutions through Active Inference.

### Section 3: Prototyping and Testing
Building, testing, and iterating — physical and digital prototypes, feedback loops, and learning from failure.

### Section 4: Innovation Ecosystems
Scaling and evolving inventions — intellectual property, teams, communication, market planning, and societal impact.

## Audience

This course is designed for:
- **Engineers and scientists** seeking a principled framework for invention
- **Artists and designers** exploring creative problem-solving
- **Entrepreneurs** building products and businesses
- **Students** from any discipline interested in invention
- **Working groups and teams** collaborating on inventive projects

## Prerequisites

No prior knowledge of Active Inference is required. Each module introduces the relevant Active Inference concepts alongside their application to invention.

## Module Files

Each module contains:
- `module.md` — Lecture content
- `lab.md` — Hands-on lab exercise
- `questions.md` — 20 study and discussion questions
- `practice_quiz.md` — Self-assessment quiz (7 MC + 3 short answer)
- `dashboard.html` — Interactive web dashboard
