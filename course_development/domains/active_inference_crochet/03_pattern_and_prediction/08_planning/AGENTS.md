## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Pattern reading, design, mathematical structure of crochet
- **Topics**: Planning
- **Lab Style**: Pattern Design Workshop
- **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious
- **Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible

## Content Guidelines

This module covers **yardage estimation and shaping** as planning under uncertainty — using the generative model to predict resource needs, project dimensions, and construction sequences before committing to stitches.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Estimating total yardage for a project | Predictive modeling of resource requirements |
| Planning increases/decreases for shaping | Spatial prediction — modeling curvature and fit |
| Choosing construction method (top-down, bottom-up, modular) | Policy selection — choosing the action sequence that minimizes expected difficulty |
| Swatching to predict finished dimensions | Hypothesis testing — calibrating the model before committing |
| Scheduling a large project across weeks | Temporal planning under uncertainty |
| Accounting for yarn chicken (will you have enough?) | Risk assessment under the generative model |

### Craft Integrity

- Use real planning challenges: estimating skeins for an afghan, planning shaping for a garment, deciding whether to work flat or in the round.
- Acknowledge "yarn chicken" (the tension of running low on yarn near the end of a project) as a genuinely felt planning problem.
- Respect the mathematical structure of crochet planning — stitch counts, row gauges, and dimensional calculations are real applied mathematics.
