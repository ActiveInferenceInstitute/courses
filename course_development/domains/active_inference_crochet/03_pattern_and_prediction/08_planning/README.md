## Learning Goals

1. **Explain** yardage estimation as prediction under uncertainty — using partial information to forecast total material needs
2. **Describe** shaping calculations (increases, decreases, construction order) as planning under a generative model
3. **Identify** construction method choice (top-down vs bottom-up, joined vs seamless) as selecting between different generative model architectures
