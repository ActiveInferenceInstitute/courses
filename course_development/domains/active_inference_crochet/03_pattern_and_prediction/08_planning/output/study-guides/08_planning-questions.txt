What hidden variables does blocking introduce? Why must these be anticipated during the planning phase?

How does the choice between modular construction (separate pieces joined) and seamless construction (one piece) affect the error correction process?

Explain why time estimation for a project requires your model to operate at a higher level of abstraction than stitch-level planning.

How does the choice of construction method (top-down vs. bottom-up) reflect different tradeoffs between planning complexity and feedback frequency?

Explain why planning for lace projects requires predicting the post-blocking dimensions, not the pre-blocking dimensions. What makes this cognitively challenging?

How does the concept of "ease" in garment design (planned extra room beyond body measurements) reflect the designer's model of acceptable prediction range?

A crocheter weighs their remaining yarn mid-project to determine if they have enough. What type of real-time model updating is this?

How does planning for a gift (unknown recipient measurements) differ from planning for yourself? What additional uncertainty must the model account for?
