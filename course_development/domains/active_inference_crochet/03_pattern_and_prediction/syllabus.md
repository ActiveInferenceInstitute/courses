# Syllabus: Pattern & Prediction

> **Active Inference for Crochet Circles** | **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious

## Course Description

Pattern & Prediction explores Active Inference through pattern reading, design, and the mathematical structure of crochet. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn — all through the structured, creative world of crochet patterns.
