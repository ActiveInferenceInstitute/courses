# Study Questions: Pattern & Prediction

1. In what sense is a crochet pattern a generative model? Identify the inputs, transformations, and predicted outputs of a typical pattern.

2. Where is the Markov Blanket of a crochet pattern? What does it separate, and how does it mediate between the designer's intentions and the crocheter's actions?

3. Describe pattern reading as inference. When you read "ch 3, dc in next st," what prediction are you generating, and how do you verify it?

4. How does an experienced crocheter "see" the finished object in the pattern before stitching it? What does this ability reveal about the richness of their generative model?

5. Explain pattern modification as model updating. If you decide to add 4 extra rows to a sweater body, what other aspects of the pattern must be rechecked or adjusted?

6. How does the concept of gauge connect pattern (generative model) to execution (generative process)? What happens when gauge does not match?

7. Compare stitch charts and written instructions as two encodings of the same generative model. What information is easier to extract from each format?

8. What makes a pattern "well-written" versus "poorly-written" in Active Inference terms? Consider robustness, clarity of predictions, and accommodation of different generative processes (different crocheters).

9. Describe the process of designing an original pattern as model construction. What must the designer predict about future crocheters who will use the pattern?

10. How does the stitch abbreviation system (sc, dc, ch, inc, dec, etc.) function as a shared symbolic language? What Active Inference concept does a shared notation support?

11. When a pattern instruction is ambiguous (e.g., "work even for 6 inches" without specifying stitch), what kind of uncertainty does the crocheter face? How do they resolve it?

12. Explain yardage estimation as a planning problem. What variables must the crocheter's model include, and what are the consequences of underestimation versus overestimation?

13. How does a pattern's difficulty level relate to the precision demands it places on the crocheter's generative model? Why are lace patterns "harder" than dishcloth patterns in Active Inference terms?

14. Describe the experience of encountering an error in a published pattern. What prediction error does this produce, and how does the crocheter determine whether the error is in the pattern or in their execution?

15. How do Ravelry project notes (where crocheters share their modifications and results) function as shared updates to a pattern's generative model?
