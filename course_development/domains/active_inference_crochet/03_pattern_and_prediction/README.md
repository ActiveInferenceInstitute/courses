# Pattern & Prediction

> Part of **Active Inference for Crochet Circles** | [Curriculum Home](../README.md)

## Overview

**Pattern & Prediction** explores Active Inference through the lens of pattern reading, design, and the mathematical structure of crochet. This course is designed for fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious.

**Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible.
