# Course AGENTS: Pattern & Prediction

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course README](./README.md) | [Curriculum AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Identity

- **Course**: Pattern & Prediction
- **Number**: 3
- **Perspective**: Pattern reading, design, mathematical structure of crochet
- **Lab Type**: Pattern Design Workshop
- **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious
- **Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible

## Conventions

All modules in this course must:

1. Use language appropriate for fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious.
2. Frame all concepts through the lens of pattern reading, design, and the mathematical structure of crochet.
3. Include pattern design workshop activities.
4. Adhere to notation standards in [../resources/notation_table.md](../resources/notation_table.md).
5. Cross-reference the shared [../resources/glossary.md](../resources/glossary.md).
