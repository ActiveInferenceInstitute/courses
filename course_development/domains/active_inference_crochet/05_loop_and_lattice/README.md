# Course 5: Loop & Lattice

> **Active Inference for Crochet Circles** | **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious

## Topology, Neural Networks, and the Geometry of Crochet

Every piece of crochet fabric is a topological surface. Every neural network is a graph. This course reveals the deep structural parallels between the networks you crochet with your hands and the networks that process information in brains and machines. From hyperbolic ruffles to hidden layers, from Mobius strips to backpropagation, Loop & Lattice invites you to see your crochet hook as a tool for building mathematical structures — and to see mathematics as something you have been doing with your hands all along.
