# Syllabus: Loop & Lattice

> **Active Inference for Crochet Circles** | **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious

## Course Description

Loop & Lattice explores Active Inference through the topology of crocheted surfaces, neural network architecture, and the deep geometry connecting fabric and computation. Over 8 modules, students will discover how the networks they build with hook and yarn mirror the networks that process information in brains and machines — and how Active Inference provides the framework that unites both.
