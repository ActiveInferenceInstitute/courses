# Active Inference: Crochet Circles — Pedagogical Overview

## Vision

This curriculum makes Active Inference accessible to fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious through five complementary lenses drawn from the practice and culture of crochet. Each course covers the same 8 foundational topics but from a different aspect of the craft — from the mechanics of a single stitch to the social dynamics of a crafting circle to the topology of surfaces and neural networks.

A crochet circle is a nested system of active inference agents. Each crocheter maintains a generative model (the pattern) and minimizes prediction error with every stitch. The hook-yarn interface is a Markov blanket. The circle itself is a collective agent with shared generative models — pattern libraries, technique traditions, and communal knowledge. Frogging (undoing work) is the quintessential prediction-error response.

## Tone & Style

Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible. Like learning from a knowledgeable crafter sitting next to you in the circle. Stitch-based metaphors throughout. Real craft knowledge — no dumbing down the fiber arts or the science.

## Cross-Course Connections

The same 8 topics appear in every course, building a spiral of understanding from the individual stitch to the geometry of surfaces. A concept introduced at the hook-and-yarn level in Stitch & Structure reappears as a material property in Fiber & Flow, a symbolic structure in Pattern & Prediction, a group dynamic in Circle & Community, and a topological principle in Loop & Lattice. See [Cross-Course Map](./resources/cross_course_map.md) for detailed connections.

## How to Use This Curriculum

- **Sequential**: Work through one course start to finish — begin with Stitch & Structure for a hands-on foundation.
- **Parallel**: Study the same module across all 4 courses to see how one Active Inference concept manifests at every scale.
- **Exploratory**: Use the [Learning Pathways](./resources/learning_pathways.md) to follow your crafting interests.
- **Circle-Based**: Use Circle & Community alongside any other course for a group study experience.
