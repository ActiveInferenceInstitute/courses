## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Materials, yarn properties, creative flow states
- **Topics**: Planning
- **Lab Style**: Materials Exploration
- **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious
- **Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible

## Content Guidelines

This module examines how crocheters **plan** material choices: color palette design as expected free energy minimization, dye lot management as planning under uncertainty, and yardage estimation as predictive modeling with real consequences.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Designing a color palette | Expected free energy minimization — choosing colors to maximize predicted satisfaction |
| Dye lot management and buying enough | Planning under material uncertainty |
| Yardage estimation for a project | Predictive modeling of resource needs |
| Choosing yarn for a gift project | Planning with recipient preferences as model constraints |
| Stash organization | Resource model management for future projects |
| Deciding between luxury yarn and budget option | Cost-benefit evaluation under the generative model |

### Craft Integrity

- Acknowledge that material planning is both practical and emotional — choosing yarn is about budget and yardage but also about excitement, tactile pleasure, and creative vision.
- Use real planning scenarios: enough yarn for a blanket, matching dye lots across multiple skeins, choosing colors that will look good together in stripes.
- Respect the stash as a planning resource, not a problem — every crocheter's stash reflects their generative model of future projects.
- Connect the anxiety of "will I have enough yarn?" to genuine planning under uncertainty.
