How does the choice between pragmatic value (achieving the goal) and epistemic value (learning something new) shape project selection?

Describe how a crocheter plans for the visual impact of color pooling in a variegated yarn. What variables must they consider?

Why is buying yarn for a large project a planning challenge? What risks must the crocheter manage?

How does planning a gift project differ from planning a project for yourself? What additional predictions must be generated?

Describe the planning process for a multi-color project. How do decisions about color sequence, transition, and proportion interact?

Why do experienced crocheters plan their stash purchases? How is stash building a form of long-term project planning?

How does seasonal planning (starting a winter project in summer) require the crocheter to predict future preferences and needs?
