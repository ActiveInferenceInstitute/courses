# Syllabus: Fiber & Flow

> **Active Inference for Crochet Circles** | **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious

## Course Description

Fiber & Flow explores Active Inference through materials, yarn properties, and creative flow states. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn — all through the tactile world of yarn and fiber.
