Study Questions: Action (Fiber & Flow)

  1. Describe tension adjustment as a form of active inference. How do the hands continuously correct to maintain desired fabric properties?

  2. How does yarn feeding through the fingers function as an action policy? What does each feeding method optimize for?

  3. Walk through the perception-action cycle of a single pull-through stitch. What predictions are generated and tested?

  4. Why does switching between yarns (e.g., from cotton to silk) require a change in action policy? What specifically must the hands recalibrate?

  5. How is letting go (releasing tension, releasing yarn) an active inference action rather than a passive event?

  6. Compare the action parameters needed for crocheting with a bulky wool versus a fine lace-weight silk. How do the hands adjust?

  7. Why does gauge sometimes change between the beginning and end of a crochet session? Relate this to changes in the action policy over time.

  8. How does hook size interact with the crocheter's action policy? Why might changing hook size require a period of adjustment?

  9. Describe a situation where the crocheter must consciously override their automatic tension (e.g., transitioning from ribbing to lace within the same piece).

  10. How does the physical weight of the growing fabric affect the crocheter's action parameters? What adjustments are needed as the piece gets heavier?

  11. Why do different stitch types (single crochet, double crochet, treble crochet) require different action policies even with the same yarn?

  12. How is the crocheter's grip on the hook itself an action parameter? Compare pencil grip versus knife grip in terms of action control.

  13. Explain why a crocheter's tension tends to be tighter when they are stressed or anxious. How does emotional state affect action policies?

  14. How does the act of turning the work at the end of a row represent a policy transition? What predictions are tested during the turn?

  15. Describe the action challenges of working in the round versus working in rows. How do the hands adapt?

  16. Why is it difficult to maintain consistent tension when crocheting with very thick yarn on a very small hook (or vice versa)?

  17. How does the concept of yarn over (wrapping yarn around the hook) differ as an action between someone who yarn-overs from front to back versus back to front?

  18. Explain how the magic ring (adjustable loop) technique requires a different action sequence than a chain-start. What makes it harder for beginners?

  19. How does the action of joining a new skein of the same yarn differ from joining a completely different yarn? What predictions change?

  20. If you were designing a crochet ergonomics guide, what action parameters would you focus on to minimize hand strain? How does active inference inform ergonomics?
