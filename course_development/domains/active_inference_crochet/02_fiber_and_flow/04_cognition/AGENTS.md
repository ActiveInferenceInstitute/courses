## Content Guidelines

This module explores the cognitive dimension of working with materials: **flow state** as low prediction error, anticipating yarn behavior as a deep generative model, and the meditative quality of crochet as embodied cognition where hand and mind become one.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Flow state during repetitive stitching | Low prediction error, high model precision |
| Anticipating how yarn will behave before working it | Predictive model running ahead of action |
| "The yarn tells you what it wants to be" | Perceptual inference guiding action selection |
| Meditative, rhythmic crochet | Stable attractor state with minimal free energy |
| Adjusting expectations for an unfamiliar yarn | Model updating when prior predictions fail |
| Planning a color transition in your mind | Internal simulation of future material states |

### Craft Integrity

- Honor the meditative quality of crochet — flow state is not just a metaphor for low prediction error, it is the actual phenomenology of skilled crafting.
- Acknowledge that "the yarn tells you what it wants to be" is a genuine report of perceptual inference, not anthropomorphism.
- Connect the deep satisfaction of rhythmic crochet to the Active Inference principle that organisms seek states of low surprise.
- Respect material cognition as real cognition — knowing fiber is a form of knowledge.
