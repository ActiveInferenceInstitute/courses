## Learning Goals

1. **Describe** flow state as a condition of low prediction error between the crocheter and their material
2. **Explain** how anticipating yarn behavior constitutes a deep generative model
3. **Recognize** how material knowledge functions as embodied cognition in the fiber arts
4. **Experience** the shift between effortful attention (high prediction error) and flow (low prediction error)
5. **Reflect** on how the meditative quality of crochet relates to predictive alignment between maker and material

## Key Crochet Terminology

- **Flow state**: The absorbed, timeless feeling when stitching proceeds without conscious effort
- **Autopilot stitching**: Working familiar stitches without deliberate attention — deep model running smoothly
- **Material resistance**: The pushback yarn gives when worked — too tight, too loose, too slippery
- **Yarn behavior**: How a fiber responds to hooking, tension, blocking, and wear
- **Creative vision**: The crocheter's mental model of the finished piece guiding material choices

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Modules 01-03 in this course
- Experience with at least two different fiber types

## Materials Needed

- Crochet hook and a familiar, comfortable yarn (your flow-state favorite)
- A second, unfamiliar yarn (different fiber, weight, or texture)
- Timer or clock for timed stitching exercises
- Notebook for reflective observations
