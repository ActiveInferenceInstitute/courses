## Active Inference Integration

This course shifts attention from the stitch to the **material** — the yarn itself as a system, the crocheter's hands as agents that know fiber, and the flow state that emerges when material and maker are deeply attuned.

| Fiber Experience | Active Inference Concept | Teaching Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Feeling yarn weight and twist | Sensory evidence from the material system | Blind yarn identification exercises |
| Discovering a knot mid-skein | Surprise / prediction error | Notice the felt disruption when continuity breaks |
| Flow state during repetitive stitching | Low prediction error, high precision alignment | Timed meditative crochet sessions |
| Substituting one yarn for another | Generative model transfer and adaptation | Swatch the same pattern in different fibers |
| Color palette selection | Multi-step prediction and preference modeling | Color mood boards alongside crochet |
| Dye lot matching | Precision of visual prediction | Compare skeins from same and different dye lots |
