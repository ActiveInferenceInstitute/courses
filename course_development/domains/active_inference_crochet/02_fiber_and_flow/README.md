# Fiber & Flow

> Part of **Active Inference for Crochet Circles** | [Curriculum Home](../README.md)

## Overview

**Fiber & Flow** explores Active Inference through the lens of materials, yarn properties, creative flow states. This course is designed for fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious.

**Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible.
