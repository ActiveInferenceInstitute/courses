# Station: Systems (Fiber & Flow)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Materials, yarn properties, creative flow states
- **Topics**: Systems
- **Lab Style**: Materials Exploration
- **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious
- **Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible

## Content Guidelines

This module presents yarn itself as a **system** — with internal states (fiber composition, twist direction, ply structure), external states (humidity, storage conditions, dye lot context), and a boundary (the skein wrapper, the fiber surface). The focus is on the material before it becomes fabric.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Fiber composition (wool, cotton, acrylic) | Internal states of the yarn system |
| Skein label (weight, yardage, dye lot) | Encoded system description |
| Yarn meeting humidity, heat, or handling | External states acting on the system |
| The fiber surface where dye bonds | Markov blanket of the material |
| Dye lot variation | System-level prediction uncertainty |
| Blocking and washing behavior | System response to environmental perturbation |

### Craft Integrity

- Treat yarn as a material with its own character, not merely a tool for the crocheter.
- Use real fiber properties: wool felts, cotton has no memory, acrylic is heat-sensitive, silk drapes.
- Discuss the skein label as the first communication about the system — what it tells and what it conceals.
- Invite learners to handle different fibers and notice what their hands discover before reading any label.
