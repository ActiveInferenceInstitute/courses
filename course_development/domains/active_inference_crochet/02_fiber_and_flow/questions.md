# Study Questions: Fiber & Flow

1. How is a skein of yarn a system in the Active Inference sense? Identify its internal states, external states, and Markov Blanket.

2. When you switch from crocheting with wool to crocheting with cotton, what specific adjustments does your generative model make? How do your hands "recalibrate"?

3. Why does superwash wool behave differently from untreated wool when washed? Describe this difference in terms of the yarn's internal states and how they respond to environmental perturbation.

4. What does it mean for yarn to have "memory"? How does the presence or absence of memory in a fiber (e.g., acrylic versus cotton) affect the crocheter's predictions about the finished fabric?

5. Describe the experience of creative flow in crochet using Active Inference language. What is the state of the generative model during flow, and what disrupts it?

6. How does a gauge swatch with an unfamiliar yarn serve as a materials exploration? What does the crocheter learn about the yarn's generative process that they could not learn from reading the label?

7. When choosing yarn for a project, what predictions is the crocheter making about the relationship between material properties and the finished object? Give three specific examples.

8. Explain yarn substitution as model transfer. If a pattern calls for a DK-weight merino and you substitute a DK-weight cotton, what aspects of the generative model remain valid and what must be updated?

9. How does working with hand-dyed yarn require a different precision strategy than working with commercially dyed yarn? What predictions must be held loosely?

10. Describe the sensory experience of three different fiber types (e.g., wool, silk, acrylic) in terms of the information they convey through the Markov Blanket (touch, sight, sound).

11. Why might a crocheter "fall out of flow" when transitioning from the body of a garment to a complex colorwork section? What changes in the relationship between skill and challenge?

12. How does the weight of the yarn in your lap provide information about remaining yardage? Is this perception or inference?

13. Describe how humidity affects the generative process of a wool yarn system. What changes at the Markov Blanket, and how might the crocheter notice?

14. How does blocking (wetting and reshaping a finished piece) reveal the difference between the crocheter's generative model and the actual state of the fabric? What prediction errors become visible?

15. If you were designing a materials exploration lab for a crochet circle, what three fiber comparisons would you choose to best illustrate the concept of different generative processes?
