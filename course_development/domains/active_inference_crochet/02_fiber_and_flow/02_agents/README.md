## Learning Goals

1. **Recognize** the crocheter's hands as embodied active inference agents that sense and act on yarn
2. **Explain** how tactile expertise develops as a refined generative model of material behavior
3. **Connect** material sensitivity to precision weighting in the Active Inference framework
4. **Practice** blind fiber identification to reveal the depth of your embodied material model
5. **Reflect** on how your personal fiber preferences reveal your generative model's precision landscape

## Key Crochet Terminology

- **Hand (of yarn)**: The overall tactile quality — softness, stiffness, drape, bounce
- **Memory**: A fiber's tendency to return to its original shape after being stretched
- **Halo**: The fuzzy bloom around certain yarns (mohair, angora) that changes their visual and tactile character
- **Superwash**: Wool processed to resist felting; changes the fiber's behavioral predictions
- **Fiber blend**: A yarn combining multiple fibers (e.g., 80% acrylic / 20% wool), creating hybrid properties

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Module 01 (Systems) in this course
- Willingness to handle and compare multiple yarn types

## Materials Needed

- Five or more yarn samples across different fiber types and weights
- Blindfold or eye mask for tactile identification exercises
- Crochet hook appropriate for the yarns
- Notebook for recording tactile observations
