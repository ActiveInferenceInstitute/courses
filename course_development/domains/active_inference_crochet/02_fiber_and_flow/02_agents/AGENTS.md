- **Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible

## Content Guidelines

This module focuses on the crocheter's **hands** as embodied agents that sense and act on yarn. Tactile expertise develops as a refined generative model of material behavior — experienced hands know what a fiber will do before the mind names it.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Hands that "know" a yarn by touch | Embodied generative model of material properties |
| Adjusting grip for slippery vs. grippy yarn | Agent adapting action policy to material context |
| Preferring certain fibers | Precision-weighted sensory preferences |
| Expert's ability to predict yarn behavior | Deep generative model from accumulated experience |
| Beginner struggling with unfamiliar fiber | Sparse model, high prediction error |
| Yarn shopping by feel | Epistemic action — sampling the environment for information |

### Craft Integrity

- Position the hands as intelligent agents in their own right — the fingers that test twist, drape, and bounce are doing sophisticated inference.
- Acknowledge that material knowledge is deeply personal: two crocheters may handle the same yarn differently and both be correct.
- Connect yarn preferences to the Active Inference idea that agents seek states they can predict — we gravitate toward materials our models handle well.
