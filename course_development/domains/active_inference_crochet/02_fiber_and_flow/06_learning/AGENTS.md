## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Materials, yarn properties, creative flow states
- **Topics**: Learning
- **Lab Style**: Materials Exploration
- **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious
- **Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible

## Content Guidelines

This module explores how crocheters **learn** about materials through hands-on experience: discovering fiber properties through iterative experimentation, building yarn substitution knowledge as a learned generative model, and developing the deep material intuition that comes only from working with many different yarns.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Swatching with a new yarn | Hypothesis testing — generating predictions and observing outcomes |
| Building yarn substitution knowledge | Learned generative model from accumulated material encounters |
| Discovering that wool felts when agitated | Surprise-driven model expansion |
| Developing fiber preferences over time | Prior updating through repeated experience |
| First time working with lace-weight yarn | Operating with a sparse material model |
| Stash diving and rediscovering yarns | Re-engaging predictions about previously encountered materials |

### Craft Integrity

- Frame material learning as lifelong and open-ended — every new yarn is a learning opportunity.
- Acknowledge the role of happy accidents in fiber learning: the felted mistake that becomes a design technique.
- Use real yarn substitution scenarios (replacing discontinued yarn, adjusting for budget, choosing local fiber) as grounded examples of model transfer.
- Celebrate the stash as a physical record of the crocheter's evolving material model.
