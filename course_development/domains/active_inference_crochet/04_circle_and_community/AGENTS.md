## Active Inference Integration

This course scales Active Inference from the individual crocheter to the **social group**. The crochet circle is a multi-agent system where members share generative models, transmit craft knowledge, and collectively reduce uncertainty through collaboration.

| Circle Experience | Active Inference Concept | Teaching Approach |
|---|---|---|
| The circle gathering and establishing its rhythm | Multi-agent system forming a collective Markov blanket | Observe how a circle begins — who sits where, what is unpacked first |
| Noticing when someone is struggling | Social perception as prediction error detection | Reflect on how you notice another crafter's frustration |
| Teaching a new stitch to a newcomer | Transmitting a generative model from one agent to another | Practice teaching and notice what is hard to convey |
| Group problem-solving on a tricky pattern | Collective inference — pooling multiple generative models | Bring a challenge to the circle and solve it together |
| Storytelling over stitches | Cultural model transmission through narrative | Share a project story and notice what it teaches |
| Organizing a crochet-along | Coordinated multi-agent planning over time | Plan a real or hypothetical group project |
