## Learning Goals

1. **Explain** how collective knowledge in a crochet circle exceeds what any individual member knows, and how this distributed cognition emerges through interaction
2. **Describe** group problem-solving as a process of collective active inference — pooling generative models, comparing predictions, and converging on solutions
3. **Identify** the shared mental models that circles develop about difficulty levels, techniques, and craft norms, and explain how these models coordinate group behavior
