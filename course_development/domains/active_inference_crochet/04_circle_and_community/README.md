# Circle & Community

> Part of **Active Inference for Crochet Circles** | [Curriculum Home](../README.md)

## Overview

**Circle & Community** explores Active Inference through the lens of social dynamics, teaching, cultural transmission in crochet circles. This course is designed for fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious.

**Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible.
