## Learning Goals

1. **Describe** how social perception in a crochet circle functions as active inference — reading who needs help, noticing frustration or flow in others, sensing the emotional temperature of the group
2. **Identify** the shared sensory environment of a circle — the sounds of hooks, conversation rhythm, visual cues from others' work — and explain how these signals inform each agent's generative model
3. **Analyze** how prediction errors in social perception (misreading a mood, misjudging skill level) drive learning about fellow circle members
