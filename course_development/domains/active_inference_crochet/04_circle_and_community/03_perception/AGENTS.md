- **Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible

## Content Guidelines

This module explores **social perception** in the crochet circle — how crafters read the room while they stitch, noticing who needs help, sensing frustration or flow in others, and tracking the emotional temperature of the group through peripheral awareness.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Noticing a fellow crafter's sigh or frown | Social prediction error detection |
| Sensing the group mood without anyone speaking | Multi-modal social perception |
| Glancing at others' projects to gauge progress | Epistemic action — sampling the social environment |
| Hearing the rhythm of hooks change (faster or slower) | Auditory social perception |
| Detecting when someone is about to ask for help | Predictive social modeling |
| Reading body language while hands are busy | Dual-channel perception (craft + social) |

### Craft Integrity

- Acknowledge that crocheters in a circle are doing two things at once: crafting and socializing. Both involve perception, and neither is secondary.
- Use real social observations: the way a crafter holds up their work for feedback, the change in posture when someone encounters a problem, the shared laughter when someone confesses to frogging.
- Respect the subtlety of social perception in craft spaces — these groups often develop deep emotional attunement.
