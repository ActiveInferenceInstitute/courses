## Learning Goals

1. **Identify** the Markov blanket of a crochet circle — who belongs, what resources are shared, and where the boundary lies between the group and the wider world
2. **Distinguish** between the internal states (shared knowledge, ongoing projects, social bonds), external states (wider crafting community, yarn shops, online resources), and blanket states (meeting space, regular schedule, shared materials) of a crochet circle
3. **Explain** how a crochet circle maintains its identity over time even as members come and go
