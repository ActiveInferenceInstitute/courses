# Station: Planning (Circle & Community)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Social dynamics, teaching, cultural transmission in crochet circles
- **Topics**: Planning
- **Lab Style**: Group Activity
- **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious
- **Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible

## Content Guidelines

This module covers **organizing a crochet-along** and other group projects — how circles coordinate collective action across weeks or months, evaluate options by weighing predicted difficulty and satisfaction, and synthesize all Active Inference concepts in the service of shared planning.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Choosing a group project together | Collective policy selection under expected free energy |
| Organizing a crochet-along (CAL) | Coordinated temporal planning across multiple agents |
| Assigning squares for a community blanket | Distributing action policies across agents |
| Setting milestones and check-in dates | Temporal prediction points for monitoring collective progress |
| Adapting the plan when members fall behind | Collective model updating in response to prediction error |
| Celebrating the finished group project | Achieving the predicted outcome — collective free energy minimization complete |

### Craft Integrity

- Use real group planning scenarios: organizing a charity blanket drive, planning a year-long crochet-along, coordinating a group holiday gift project.
- Respect the social complexity of group planning — different members have different availability, skill levels, and aesthetic preferences.
- Acknowledge that the best group plans accommodate uncertainty — someone will fall behind, someone will want to change their color, and that is part of the process.
- This module synthesizes all eight topics: use it to bring the whole course together.
