## Learning Goals

1. **Describe** the dual-channel communication system of a crochet circle — hands working (physical inference) while voices engage in social inference (conversation) — and explain how both channels function simultaneously
2. **Analyze** storytelling traditions in circles as a form of cultural transmission that binds the group's shared generative model
3. **Explain** how modern communication channels (social media, pattern sharing, online communities) extend the circle's communicative reach and change its dynamics
