# Station: Communication (Circle & Community)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Social dynamics, teaching, cultural transmission in crochet circles
- **Topics**: Communication
- **Lab Style**: Group Activity
- **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious
- **Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible

## Content Guidelines

This module explores the **dual-channel communication** of the crochet circle — hands working while voices engage in conversation, stories woven alongside stitches, and the way modern communication technologies extend the circle's reach.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Conversations while stitching | Dual-channel communication — craft and social running in parallel |
| Storytelling traditions in circles | Narrative as cultural model transmission |
| Sharing project updates ("show and tell") | Visual communication of model state |
| Online forums, Ravelry, social media crochet groups | Extended communication channels beyond physical proximity |
| The comfortable silence of crafting together | Low-bandwidth but high-value social signal (mutual presence) |
| Asking for and receiving help across the table | Real-time bidirectional model transmission |

### Craft Integrity

- Honor the unique communicative space of the crochet circle — it is one of the few contexts where deep conversation happens while hands are busy with meaningful work.
- Acknowledge the role of silence — crafting together in comfortable quiet is a form of communication in itself.
- Use real communication scenarios: the project show-and-tell, the troubleshooting conversation, the shared recipe alongside the shared pattern.
- Respect online craft communities as genuine extensions of the circle, not lesser substitutes.
