## Learning Goals

1. **Recognize** each circle member as an autonomous inference agent with their own generative model — shaped by their unique skill level, project history, and craft preferences
2. **Describe** how the diversity of generative models within a circle creates conditions for collective learning and mutual enrichment
3. **Analyze** the crochet circle as a collective agent whose group-level behavior emerges from the interactions of individual agents
