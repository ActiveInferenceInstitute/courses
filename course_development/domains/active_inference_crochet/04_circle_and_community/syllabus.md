# Syllabus: Circle & Community

> **Active Inference for Crochet Circles** | **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious

## Course Description

Circle & Community explores Active Inference through social dynamics, teaching, cultural transmission in crochet circles. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn — all through the warm, collaborative world of the crochet circle.
