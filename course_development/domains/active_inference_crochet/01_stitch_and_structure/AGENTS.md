## Active Inference Integration

This course grounds Active Inference at the most fundamental level of crochet: the individual stitch. Every concept maps directly onto the physical experience of hook meeting yarn.

| Crochet Experience | Active Inference Concept | Teaching Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Pulling yarn through a loop | Action as policy execution | Have learners slow down and notice each micro-action |
| Feeling tension on the yarn | Sensory state at the Markov blanket | Eyes-closed stitching exercises |
| Counting stitches at row end | State estimation and belief updating | Count, predict, compare, reflect |
| Frogging a mistake | Prediction error correction | Intentional error-and-repair exercises |
| The working edge of the fabric | Markov blanket boundary | Identify what is inside, outside, and at the boundary |
| Gauge swatch | Prior precision calibration | Swatch before and after, compare predictions to outcomes |
