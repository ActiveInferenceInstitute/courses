# Syllabus: Stitch & Structure

> **Active Inference for Crochet Circles** | **Audience**: Fiber artists, crafters, crochet circle participants, and the curious

## Course Description

Stitch & Structure explores Active Inference through individual crocheter, stitch mechanics, tension & gauge. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn — all through the rhythmic, tactile world of crochet.
