- **Tone**: Warm, crafty, hands-on, accessible

## Content Guidelines

This module explores how crocheters **perceive** the state of their work through fingers, eyes, and rhythm. Yarn tension is proprioception. Dropped stitch detection is surprise. Texture discrimination is precision weighting. The crocheter's sensory world is rich, multi-modal, and deeply embodied.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Yarn tension through the fingers | Proprioceptive sensory state |
| Detecting a dropped stitch | Surprise (prediction error) |
| Distinguishing yarn textures by touch | Precision-weighted sensory processing |
| Eyes-closed stitching | Reliance on non-visual sensory channels |
| Stitch counting by rhythm | Temporal perceptual inference |
| "Feeling" a mistake before seeing it | Pre-conscious prediction error detection |

### Craft Integrity

- Emphasize the embodied, tactile nature of crochet perception — this is a craft you feel as much as see.
- Invite learners to close their eyes and notice what their fingers already know.
- Respect the depth of experienced crocheters' tactile knowledge: distinguishing fiber content, weight, and twist by touch alone is genuine perceptual expertise.
- Connect the physical sensation of tension to the formal concept of precision weighting without reducing one to the other.
