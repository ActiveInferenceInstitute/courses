## Learning Goals

1. **Sense** how yarn tension provides continuous proprioceptive feedback during crochet
2. **Detect** dropped stitches and errors through the feeling of surprise in your hands
3. **Distinguish** between different yarn textures and weights by touch alone
4. **Explain** how precision weighting determines which sensory channels the crocheter trusts most
5. **Practice** eyes-closed stitching to reveal the depth of your tactile generative model

## Key Crochet Terminology

- **Tension**: The tightness with which yarn is held between the fingers, controlling stitch size and fabric feel
- **Tension fingers**: The fingers of the non-hook hand that regulate yarn flow and provide feedback
- **Dropped stitch**: A stitch that has slipped off the hook or been missed, causing a gap
- **Hand (of yarn)**: The tactile quality of a yarn — how it feels between the fingers
- **Ply**: The number of strands twisted together to form a yarn; affects texture and behavior

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Modules 01-02 or comfort with basic crochet stitches
- Willingness to close your eyes and trust your hands

## Materials Needed

- Crochet hook (size H/8 or 5.0mm)
- Two or three different yarn types (e.g., acrylic, cotton, wool) in similar weight
- A small work-in-progress (at least 10 rows of single crochet)
- Blindfold (optional, for eyes-closed exercises)
