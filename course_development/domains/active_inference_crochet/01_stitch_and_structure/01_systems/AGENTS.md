## Content Guidelines

This module introduces the crochet project as a **system** in the Active Inference sense. The central metaphor is the hook-yarn boundary as a Markov blanket — the living edge where internal states (finished fabric) and external states (unworked yarn, environment) meet.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Working edge / live loops on hook | Markov blanket (sensory-active boundary) |
| Completed rows behind the hook | Internal states |
| Unworked yarn, pattern, environment | External states |
| Gauge swatch | System calibration |
| Frogging (ripping back) | Boundary dissolution and repair |

### Craft Integrity

- Always describe the system from the crocheter's physical perspective: hook in hand, yarn threaded through tension fingers, fabric growing row by row.
- Use the tangibility of the crochet boundary to make the abstract concept of a Markov blanket concrete and felt.
- When discussing internal and external states, ground them in things the crocheter can point to: the finished rows, the ball of yarn, the pattern page.
