## Learning Goals

1. **Explain** how reading a crochet pattern engages a generative model that predicts fabric structure
2. **Describe** mental stitch counting as a form of internal simulation
3. **Connect** the experience of "thinking with your hands" to embodied cognition in Active Inference
4. **Recognize** the difference between beginner cognition (stitch-by-stitch) and expert cognition (pattern-level thinking)
5. **Practice** mental previewing of a row before stitching it

## Key Crochet Terminology

- **Pattern repeat**: A sequence of stitches that recurs across a row or round, forming the basic unit of the design
- **Stitch dictionary**: A reference book containing instructions for hundreds of different stitch patterns
- **Reading the work**: Visually examining your fabric to understand its structure and detect issues
- **Mental model**: The crocheter's internal picture of what the finished piece should look like
- **Row counter**: A tool for tracking which row you are on — offloading cognitive load to the environment

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Modules 01-03 or comfort with basic stitches and tension management
- Experience with at least one multi-row crochet project

## Materials Needed

- Crochet hook (size H/8 or 5.0mm)
- Medium-weight (worsted) yarn
- A written crochet pattern with at least two different stitch types
- Row counter or pen and paper for tracking
