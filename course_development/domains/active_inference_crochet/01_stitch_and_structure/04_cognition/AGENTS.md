## Content Guidelines

This module explores the cognitive dimension of crochet: how reading a pattern is running a generative model, how mental stitch counting is internal simulation, and how "thinking with your hands" reflects embodied cognition in the Active Inference framework.

### Key Mappings

| Crochet Concept | Active Inference Concept |
|---|---|
| Reading a pattern before stitching | Loading the generative model |
| Mentally previewing a row | Internal simulation (running the model forward) |
| "Thinking with your hands" | Embodied cognition / extended generative model |
| Losing your place in a pattern | Model state uncertainty |
| Deciding which stitch to use next | Hypothesis selection |
| Understanding why a stitch creates a certain texture | Causal inference within the generative model |

### Craft Integrity

- Honor the cognitive sophistication of crochet — pattern reading, mental modeling, and hand-mind coordination are genuine forms of thinking.
- Emphasize that "thinking with your hands" is not a metaphor but a description of how the generative model extends into the body.
- Connect the experience of losing count or losing place in a pattern to uncertainty and model state confusion.
- Acknowledge that beginners think explicitly about each stitch, while experts think at the level of pattern repeats, sections, or whole garments.
