# Study Questions: Stitch & Structure

1. Where is the Markov Blanket of a crochet project? Describe the internal states, external states, sensory states, and active states in terms of the physical components of your work-in-progress.

2. What happens to the Markov Blanket when you set your work down and the live loop slips off the hook? Why does the fabric begin to unravel, and what does this tell you about system boundaries?

3. In what sense is the crocheter an Active Inference agent? What is their generative model, and how does each stitch serve as both a prediction and an action?

4. How does yarn tension function as precision in the Active Inference framework? Describe what happens to your stitching when tension is too tight (over-precision) and too loose (under-precision).

5. Explain frogging (ripping back) as a prediction error response. At what point does the accumulated prediction error become large enough to trigger frogging rather than smaller corrections?

6. How does a gauge swatch calibrate the crocheter's generative model? What specific predictions does the swatch test, and what actions follow if the gauge does not match?

7. Why is a stitch marker an epistemic action rather than a pragmatic action? What uncertainty does it reduce, and how does it change the crocheter's cognitive load?

8. Describe the difference between a beginner crocheter and an experienced crocheter in terms of generative model precision. How does the experienced crocheter's model differ in its predictions about stitch formation?

9. When you learn a new stitch (for example, the bobble stitch for the first time), describe your experience in Active Inference terms. What is unfamiliar, what prediction errors arise, and how does your model update?

10. How does reading a written pattern differ from watching a video tutorial in terms of the sensory states available to the crocheter? Which provides higher-precision information for a visual learner?

11. Describe muscle memory in crochet as a habituated prior. What happens when you switch from single crochet to double crochet after a long stretch of the same stitch?

12. How does the working edge of the fabric (the current row) connect past actions to future actions? In what sense does the structure of completed rows constrain what can be stitched next?

13. Consider crocheting in a noisy environment (a bustling cafe) versus a quiet room. How does environmental noise affect the precision of sensory states available to the crocheter?

14. What is the Active Inference interpretation of a crocheter who decides to modify a pattern mid-project — changing a color, adding a border, or adjusting the size? Is this a failure of the generative model or an update to it?

15. How does the concept of "reading your work" (examining the fabric to determine your position and progress) function as state estimation in Active Inference?
