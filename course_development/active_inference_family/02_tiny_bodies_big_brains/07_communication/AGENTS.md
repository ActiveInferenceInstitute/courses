# AGENTS: Module 07 — Communication

## Conventions
- **Perspective**: Developmental neuroscience
- **Topic**: Communication
- **Subtitle**: How Language Emerges
- **Course**: Tiny Bodies, Big Brains
- **Lab Type**: Development Tracker — The Language Milestone Tracker
- **Key Concepts**: Broca's area, Wernicke's area, phoneme discrimination, vocabulary explosion, grammar acquisition

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children exploring how communication develops
- No prior science background assumed

### Tone
- Warm, expressive, and celebratory of all forms of communication
- Present communication as much more than words — faces, gestures, sounds, touch
- Use examples from daily family life: a baby's cry, a toddler's first word, a child explaining their day
- Celebrate every communication milestone, from cooing to storytelling

### Active Inference Integration
- **Communication** is how agents align their generative models — sharing what they know and believe
- **Non-verbal communication** (faces, gestures) came before words and still carries most of the message
- **Language** is a powerful tool for sharing complex models — we can describe things that are not present
- **Turn-taking** in conversation mirrors the active inference loop: predict what they will say, listen, update, respond
- **Joint attention** (both looking at the same thing) is the foundation of shared understanding

### Activity Design
- Activities should explore multiple forms of communication (words, gestures, drawings, sounds)
- Include charades, drawing games, and sound-matching activities
- Use recording devices to capture and replay voices and sounds
- Younger children practice naming emotions and making faces; older children write stories or give mini-presentations
- Include activities where communication is intentionally limited (no talking, only gestures) to highlight non-verbal skills

- Link back to Module 06 (Learning): Language is one of the most important things the brain learns
- Link forward to Module 08 (Planning): Communication helps us plan and coordinate as a family
