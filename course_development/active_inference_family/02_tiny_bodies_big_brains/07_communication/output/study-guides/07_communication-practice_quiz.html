Practice Quiz: Faces and Feelings

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. Which is a way to communicate WITHOUT words? A) Smiling. B) Texting. C) Speaking.

  2. What are Mirror Neurons? A) Mirrors in your bathroom. B) Brain cells that copy what others do/feel. C) A robot.

  3. True or False: You should hide your feelings.

    • (False. Sharing feelings helps us connect).

  4. If your friend is crying, how might they feel? A) Happy. B) Sad or Hurt. C) Hungry.

  5. What does a "Surprise" face look like? A) Eyes wide, Mouth open. B) Eyes closed, sleeping. C) Grumpy face.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. Show me your "Excited" face.
  2. How do you help someone who is sad? (Hug? Listen?).
  3. Why is it hard to understand jokes in a text message? (No face/voice data!).
