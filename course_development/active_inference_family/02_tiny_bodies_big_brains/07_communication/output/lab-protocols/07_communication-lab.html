Lab: Emotion Masks

Goal

To think about how different feelings look.

What You Need

  • Paper plates.
  • Crayons/Markers.
  • Popsicle sticks (handles).

Steps

1. The Design

Pick an emotion for your mask.

  • Happy: Big smile, crinkly eyes.
  • Angry: Sharp eyebrows, frown.
  • Scared: Squiggly mouth, big eyes.

2. The Build

Draw the face on the plate. Tape the stick to the bottom.

3. The Show

Hold up the mask. Change your body to match!

  • If Happy mask -> Dance!
  • If Angry mask -> Stomp!

Discussion

  • Does your body want to match your face? (Yes, it's connected!).
  • How can you tell if someone is wearing a "mask" (faking it)? (The eyes usually tell the truth!).

What We Learned

Feelings are powerful signals. We can express them with art and movement.
