# Module 07: Communication — Faces and Feelings

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand that we **Communicate** even without words.
2. Learn how to read **Faces** (Happy, Sad, Angry, Scared).
3. Know that your feelings are signals to other people.

## Introduction: The Silent Language

Before babies can talk, they are masters of communication.

* **Cry**: "I need help!"
* **Smile**: "I like this!"
* **Rub Eyes**: "I am tired."
We all speak this silent language every day.
Your face is a billboard that tells people how you feel.

## Key Concepts

### 1. The Signal (Sending)

You are a radio tower.
You send signals with:

* YOUR FACE (Eyebrows, Mouth).
* YOUR BODY (Slumped shoulders, Jumping up and down).
* YOUR VOICE (Loud, Quiet, Whiny).

### 2. The Receiver (Listening)

To understand someone, you have to "tune in."
Look at their face.
Are they smiling? Are they frowning?
This is called **Empathy**. It means feeling what they feel.

### 3. Mirror Neurons (The Copycats)

Inside your brain, you have special cells called Mirror Neurons.
When you see someone smile -> Your brain practices smiling.
When you see someone cry -> Your brain feels sad too.
This is how we connect with each other.

## Activities

### Activity 1: Emotion Charades

Write emotions on cards (Happy, Sad, Angry, Surprised, Sleepy).
Pick a card.
Make the face (No talking!).
Can your partner guess it?
Try to do it with JUST your eyes!

### Activity 2: The Mirror Game

Stand face-to-face with a partner.
One person is the "Leader."
Move slowly (Move arm, tilt head, make a face).
The other person is the "Mirror." They must copy EXACTLY.
Switch roles.
How did it feel to be connected?

## Summary

Words are important, but feelings are the foundation. By reading faces and sharing feelings, we build a bridge from one heart to another.
