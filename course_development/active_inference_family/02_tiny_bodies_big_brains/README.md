## What This Course Is About

This course explores Active Inference through the lens of **developmental milestones** — the physical, cognitive, and social changes that transform your baby from a newborn into a walking, talking, thinking little person. Each module connects a core Active Inference concept to the developmental science behind what your baby is becoming.

Where Course 1 (Growing Together) asked "what do we do all day?", this course asks "what is happening inside my baby's brain and body as they grow?"

## The Core Insight

Development is not random. It follows a deep logic: the progressive refinement of your baby's **generative model** — their internal map of how the world works. Every milestone, from the first smile to the first step, represents a prediction that has been tested, refined, and confirmed. Your baby is building a brain by using it.
