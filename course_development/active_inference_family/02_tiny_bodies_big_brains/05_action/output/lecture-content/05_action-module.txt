Module 05: Action — Moving Mountains

Learning Objectives

Understand that Action is moving your body to change the world.
Learn about Gravity (The invisible magnet that pulls us down).
Know that learning to move takes a LOT of practice (Falling is okay!).

Introduction: The Astronaut

Babies are like astronauts on a new planet.
Gravity is heavy here!
Lifting a head is hard.
Rolling over is a mission.
Walking is like climbing a mountain.
But the baby keeps trying.
Why? Because they want to explore!

Key Concepts

Motor Control (Driving the Robot)

Your brain sends signals to your muscles.

 "Left foot up."
 "Right foot down."
 "Balance!"
At first, the signals are messy (Wobbly).
With practice, they become clear (Smooth).

Fine vs. Gross Motor Skills

 Gross Motor: Big movements (Running, Jumping, Dancing). Uses big muscles.
 Fine Motor: Tiny movements (Drawing, Buttoning a shirt, Picking up a cheerio). Uses tiny hand muscles.
Both are super important!

Gravity (The Opponent)

Gravity is always pulling you down.
To stand up, you have to fight gravity.
Building a block tower is a battle against gravity.
When things fall, gravity wins. When they stand, YOU win!

Activities

Activity 1: The Floor is Lava

Place pillows on the floor.
Jump from pillow to pillow.
Don't touch the floor!
You are using Gross Motor Skills and Balance to solve a problem.

Activity 2: The Cheerio Challenge

Put 10 Cheerios (or beads) on a plate.
Use only your thumb and pointer finger (Pincer Grasp).
Move them to a cup one by one.
Now try it with your other hand. Harder, right?
This is Fine Motor Practice.

Summary

Action is freedom. When we learn to control our bodies, we can conquer gravity, climb mountains, and build anything we can imagine.
