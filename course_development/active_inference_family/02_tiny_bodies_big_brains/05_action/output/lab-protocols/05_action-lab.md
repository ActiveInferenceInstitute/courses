# Lab: The Balance Beam

## Goal

To test your balance and focus.

## What You Need

* Masking tape (or a line on the sidewalk).
* A book (optional).

## Steps

### 1. The Line

Put a long piece of tape on the floor.
This is the "Beam." (It's safe because it's on the floor!).

### 2. The Walk

Walk heel-to-toe across the line.
Don't step off!
If you step off, you fell into the "Lava."

### 3. The Challenge

Try walking backward.
Try closing one eye.
Try balancing a book on your head while you walk.

## Discussion

* Which was hardest?
* Did you wave your arms to stay balanced? (That's your body adjusting center of gravity).
* Balance takes constant micro-adjustments by the brain.

## What We Learned

Moving beautifully requires a lot of behind-the-scenes work by the brain and body.
