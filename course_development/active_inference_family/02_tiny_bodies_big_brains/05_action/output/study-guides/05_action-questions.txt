Course — Module 05 — Think About It

Why do babies crawl before they walk? (It is safer and more stable).
How many times does a baby fall before learning to walk? (Thousands!).
Why is it hard to write with your left hand (if you are right-handed)? (The path in the brain isn't built yet).
Can you stand on one leg with your eyes closed? (Try it! It's hard without Visual Feedback).
Why do we have fingerprints? (To help us grip things).
How do birds fly against gravity? (Wings generate Lift).
What is your favorite sport? (Soccer? Gymnastics? Tag?).
Why do we stretch? (To keep muscles flexible).
If there was no gravity, what would happen? (We would float away!).
Can you wiggle your ears? (Some people can, some can't!).
