  1. What does your brain do? A) Nothing. B) Thinks, Remembers, and Controls the body. C) Glows in the dark.

  2. What is Focus? A) Looking at everything at once. B) Shinning your "Flashlight" on one thing. C) Sleeping.

  3. True or False: Your brain can grow stronger.

  4. Where do you keep your memories? A) In your pocket. B) In your Brain Library. C) Under the bed.
