Lab: The Magic Cup Game

Goal

To test your Focus and Object Permanence (Knowing things exist even when hidden).

What You Need

 3 cups (not see-through).
 1 small object (Coin, Grape, Lego).

Steps

The Hiding

Put the cups in a row.
Put the object under the Middle Cup.
Show the audience where it is. "See the grape?"

The Shuffle

Slide the cups around.
Switch the middle and left.
Switch the right and left.
Do it slowly at first.

The Guess

Ask: "Where is the grape?"
Point to a cup.
Lift it up.

Level Up

Do it faster!
See if your brain can track the object.

Discussion

 Did you lose track? Why? (Distraction? Too fast?).
 Your brain has to simulate the path of the hidden object.
This is called "Working Memory."

What We Learned

Our eyes only see the cups, but our brains "see" the grape moving underneath. That is Thinking!
