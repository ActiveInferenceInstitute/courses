# Syllabus: Tiny Bodies, Big Brains

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](./README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../README.md)

## Course Description

Tiny Bodies, Big Brains explores the developmental science behind your baby's milestones through the framework of Active Inference. Each module pairs a core concept with the neuroscience and psychology of how that capacity develops — from the first neural circuits to the emergence of language, planning, and self-awareness.

## Materials

No special equipment. Suggested: a notebook for tracking observations. Optional: a developmental milestone chart for reference.

## Assessment

Self-paced, self-directed. No grades. Each module includes a development tracker activity designed to deepen observation rather than test knowledge.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Describe the neuroscience behind major developmental milestones in accessible terms
2. Connect Active Inference concepts to the brain changes driving your baby's development
3. Distinguish between typical developmental variation and genuine concerns
4. Understand why milestones happen in a particular order — and why the timing varies
5. See your baby's development as the progressive refinement of a generative model
