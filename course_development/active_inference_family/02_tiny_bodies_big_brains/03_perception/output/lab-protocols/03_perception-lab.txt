Lab: The Mystery Bag

Goal

To use your sense of Touch without Sight.

What You Need

 A pillowcase or paper bag.
 Secret items (Pinecone, Cotton Ball, Car, Spoon).
 A blindfold (optional).

Steps

The Setup

Put the items in the bag. Don't let the "Guesser" see!

The Reach

The Guesser puts their hand in the bag.
Grab one object.
Don't pull it out yet!

The Perception

Describe it using only touch.

 "It feels rough."
 "It feels cold."
 "It has pointy edges."

The Prediction

"I predict it is a... Pinecone!"
Pull it out. Were you right?

Discussion

 Was it hard to guess?
 Your skin sent "Texture Data" to your brain.
Your brain matched it to a memory ("Pinecone Model").

What We Learned

We can "see" with our hands. Our brain is amazing at making pictures from touch!
