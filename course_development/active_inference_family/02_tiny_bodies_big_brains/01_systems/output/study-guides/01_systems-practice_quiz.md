# Practice Quiz: Your Amazing Body

## Part A: Reflect Together

1. **What is the "Engine" of your body?**
    A) Your feet.
    B) Your Heart and Lungs (Pump blood and air).
    C) Your hair.

2. **Why do we eat?**
    A) To get Energy (Fuel).
    B) Because we are bored.
    C) To make a mess.

3. **True or False**: Your body stays exactly the same every day.
    * (False. You are growing and changing!).

4. **What happens when you run fast?**
    A) You get colder.
    B) You breathe faster and heart beats harder (Response to Demand).
    C) You fall asleep.

5. **Which is a Good Input for your system?**
    A) Dirt.
    B) Water and Fruit.
    C) Gasoline.
