Course — Module 01 — Think About It

Why do you get hiccups? (Your diaphragm muscle is twitching!).
Where does food go after you swallow it? (The Stomach System).
Why do we need sleep? (To wash the brain and grow bones).
Can you tickle yourself? (Usually no. Why? Your brain predicts it!).
Why is blood red? (Oxygen).
How does a cut heal? (Tiny cells work like construction workers).
Why do you have eyebrows? (To keep sweat out of your eyes).
Do you have the same number of bones as a baby? (No! Babies have more; some fuse together).
Why do you shiver when cold? (Muscle friction makes heat).
If you were a robot, what fuel would you use? (Batteries? Solar? Pizza?).
