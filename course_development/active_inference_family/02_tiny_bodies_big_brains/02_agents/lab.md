# Lab: Sink or Float?

## Goal

To practice making predictions (Hypotheses) and testing them.

## What You Need

* A big bowl of water or the bathtub.
* Household items (Apple, Spoon, Lego, Sponge, Rock).
* Paper and Pencil.

## Steps

### 1. The Setup

Make a chart on the paper.
Columns: **Item**, **Guess (Sink/Float)**, **Result**, **Was I Right?**.

### 2. The Hypothesis

Pick up the Apple.
Feel how heavy it is.
Predict: Will it go down (Sink) or stay up (Float)?
Write "S" or "F".

### 3. The Test

Drop it in!
Sploosh.
What happened? (Apples float!).
Write the result.

### 4. Repeat

Test all the items.

## Discussion

* Were you surprised by any? (Sometimes heavy things like big ships float!).
* Why does the sponge float at first, then sink? (Absorption!).
* You just did Physics!

## What We Learned

We don't know the answer until we test it. Being a scientist is about being curious, not just being right.
