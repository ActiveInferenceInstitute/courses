## Overview

A warm, evidence-based curriculum that helps **parents and caregivers** understand their babies and small children through the lens of **Active Inference** — the scientific idea that every living creature, from the smallest newborn to the most experienced adult, navigates the world by making predictions and then acting to make those predictions come true.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **You don't need a science degree.** This curriculum translates cutting-edge neuroscience into everyday parenting wisdom. When your baby cries at a sudden noise, reaches for a toy just out of grasp, or lights up when you walk into the room — they are doing Active Inference. This curriculum helps you see it, understand it, and support it.

### Why This Matters for Your Family

Active Inference gives you a single, unifying lens for understanding:

- **Why babies cry**: They predicted something (your face, warmth, milk) and reality didn't match. The "surprise" is real, and it's uncomfortable.
- **Why toddlers repeat things endlessly**: They're building and testing a model of the world. Every "again!" is a scientific experiment.
- **Why co-regulation works**: When you stay calm, your child's brain borrows your predictions to calm its own. You are literally lending them your nervous system.
- **Why play matters so much**: Play is how children safely explore the gap between what they expect and what happens next.

## How to Use This Curriculum

### For Parents at Home

1. **Read the module** (lesson) — it takes about 10 minutes.
2. **Try the family activity** (lab) — these are designed for real life with real children.
3. **Reflect** using the study questions — deepen your understanding.
4. **Review** with the interactive dashboard — test yourself on key ideas.

### For Parent Groups or Family Educators

1. Assign one module per week.
2. Use the discussion questions in a group setting.
3. Have families report back on the family activity.
4. Use the cross-course map to connect ideas across courses.

### For Pediatric Professionals

This curriculum provides a research-backed framework for explaining child development to parents. The Active Inference lens is particularly powerful for explaining:

- Why routines matter (they reduce prediction error for children)
- Why co-regulation works (shared generative models)
- Why play is essential (safe exploration of prediction mismatches)
