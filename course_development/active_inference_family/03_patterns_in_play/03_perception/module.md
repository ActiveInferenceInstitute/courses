# Module 03: Perception — Reading the Room

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand that **Perception** means noticing what is happening in the game.
2. Learn about **Social Cues** (Is my friend happy or sad?).
3. Know that a good player gathers data with their Eyes and Ears.

## Introduction: The Detective

A game is full of clues.

* Where is the ball?
* Is your teammate shouting for a pass?
* Is your opponent tired?
To win the game, you have to be a Detective.
You have to **Read the Room**.

## Key Concepts

### 1. Social Signals (The Hidden Language)

People send signals all the time.

* **Smile + Laugh**: They are having fun.
* **Frown + Quiet**: They might be sad or bored.
* **Red Face + Clenched Fists**: They are ANGRY. (Red Alert!).
If you miss the signals, the game might stop being fun.

### 2. Tone of Voice (Data in Sound)

"Nice job!" can mean two things.

1. "Nice job!" (Happy voice) = They mean it.
2. "Niiice job..." (Sarcastic voice) = They think you messed up.
You have to listen to the *music* of the voice, not just the words.

### 3. Joint Attention (Looking Together)

When you point at a bird, your friend looks at the bird.
This is **Joint Attention**.
In a game, you look where the ball is going.
Great teams look at the same goal.

## Activities

### Activity 1: The Statues

Play "Red Light, Green Light."
But look at the LEADER's eyes.
If they are looking away -> Move!
If they are looking at you -> Freeze!
You are perceiving their Attention.

### Activity 2: Tone Twisters

Say the phrase "I like apples" in different ways:

* Happy.
* Angry.
* Sad.
* Scared.
Can your partner guess which one it is?

## Summary

Perception is more than seeing. It is understanding the feelings and intentions of the people around you.
