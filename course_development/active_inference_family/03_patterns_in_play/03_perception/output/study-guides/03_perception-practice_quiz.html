Practice Quiz: Reading the Room

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What does "Reading the Room" mean? A) Reading a book in a corner. B) Noticing how people are feeling and acting. C) Counting the chairs.

  2. If your friend is looking at their watch, what does that mean? A) They are hungry. B) They are bored or in a hurry. C) They like watches.

  3. True or False: You can say mean things in a nice voice and it's okay.

    • (False. The brain hears the mean words too).

  4. What is a "Social Cue"? A) A line of people. B) A signal like a smile or a frown. C) A pool stick.

  5. Why do we need to perceive others in a game? A) So we don't crash into them. B) To pass the ball to them. C) Both!

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. Show me a "Bored" face.
  2. How do you know when a game is over?
  3. Have you ever thought someone was mad but they weren't?
