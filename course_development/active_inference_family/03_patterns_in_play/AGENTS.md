# AGENTS: Course 3 — Patterns in Play

## Identity

- **Course**: Patterns in Play
- **Course Number**: 3 of 4
- **Perspective**: Learning through play — counting, sorting, building, imagining
- **Lab Type**: Play-Based Activity
- **Audience**: Parents and caregivers of children aged 1–6
- **Tone**: Warm, curious, observational. Treat play as serious cognitive work.

## Conventions

1. Every lesson must ground Active Inference concepts in **specific, observable play behavior** — not abstract theory.
2. Activities must use common household items or basic toys (blocks, dolls, crayons, cups). Never require specialized materials.
3. Age suggestions should be ranges (e.g., "2–4 years") and always note that "your child may be ready earlier or later."
4. Use the framing "what your child is really doing" to bridge from play behavior to Active Inference concepts.
5. Avoid the word "just" before "play" — play is never "just" anything.

## Module Topics

| Module | Topic | Play-Based Framing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Systems | Play spaces as micro-worlds with boundaries and rules |
| 02 | Agents | Dolls, figurines, and imaginary characters as agents |
| 03 | Perception | Surprise and prediction in peek-a-boo, jack-in-the-box |
| 04 | Cognition | Sorting, categorizing, and building mental models |
| 05 | Action | Construction play — stacking, building, assembly |
| 06 | Learning | Repetition, mastery, and the power of "again!" |
| 07 | Communication | Storytelling, pretend dialogue, narrative play |
| 08 | Planning | Multi-step play scenarios, treasure hunts, rule-making |
