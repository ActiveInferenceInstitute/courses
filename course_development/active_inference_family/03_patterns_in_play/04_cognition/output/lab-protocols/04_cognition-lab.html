Lab: Treasure Hunt

Goal

To create and follow a Plan (A Map).

What You Need

  • A "Treasure" (Toy, Snack, Sticker).
  • Paper and Crayons.
  • A Hider and a Finder.

Steps

1. The Hiding

The Hider hides the treasure. (Don't let the Finder see!).

2. The Map (The Code)

The Hider draws a map.

  • "Start at the Couch."
  • "Take 3 steps to the TV."
  • "Look under the Rug." This map is external cognition (Thinking on paper).

3. The Search

The Finder must decode the map. Follow the steps to find the goal.

Discussion

  • Was the map easy to read?
  • Did the Finder predict where it was?
  • Maps translate the world into a model.

What We Learned

Thinking is just making a map in your head.
