Practice Quiz: Teamwork

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What makes a team strong? A) Being the loudest. B) Working together (Cooperation). C) Having cool jerseys.

  2. Why do we pass the ball? A) Because it is hot. B) To move it to a better position to score. C) To get rid of it.

  3. True or False: You can communicate without talking.

    • (True. Hand signals, eye contact, body language).

  4. What happens if a rower stops rowing? A) The boat goes faster. B) The boat turns or slows down (Drag). C) Nothing.

  5. How do you build trust? A) Lying. B) Doing what you say you will do. C) Hiding.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. Have you ever been on a team? What was your job?
  2. What is the hardest part of working in a group?
  3. Design a secret handshake with your partner.
