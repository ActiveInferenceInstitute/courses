What is "The Weakest Link"? (The part of the system most likely to break).
Why do wolves hunt in packs? (To catch bigger prey).
What is a "Ball Hog"? (An agent who refuses to share).
How do bees tell each other where flowers are? (The Waggle Dance).
Why do we high-five? (Physical connection reinforces the bond).
Can a team win if they hate each other? (It is very hard).
What does "There is no 'I' in Team" mean? (The group goal > individual goal).
How do synchronized swimmers stay together underwater? (Rhythm and counting).
Why do Quarterbacks use code words? (Encrypted communication).
