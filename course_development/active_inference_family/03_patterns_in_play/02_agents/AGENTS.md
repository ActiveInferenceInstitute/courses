# AGENTS: Module 02 — Agents

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Learning through play — dolls, figurines, and imaginary agents
- **Topic**: Agents
- **Subtitle**: When Teddy Becomes Real
- **Course**: Patterns in Play
- **Lab Type**: Play-Based Activity — The Agent Spotter
- **Key Concepts**: agent, theory of mind, generative model of another agent, intentional stance, pretend play
- **Learning Goals**: Recognize pretend agency as cognitive practice, connect toy play to social cognition development

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children exploring pretend play and imagination
- No prior knowledge of Active Inference assumed

### Tone
- Imaginative, playful, and respectful of children's pretend worlds
- Take pretend play seriously as cognitive work — it is how children practice understanding other minds
- Use character language: "What does Teddy want? What is Teddy feeling?"
- Celebrate the richness of children's imaginative lives

### Active Inference Integration
- **Pretend agency** is when children assign goals and feelings to toys — practicing theory of mind
- **Theory of mind** is building a generative model of another agent's beliefs and goals
- **Intentional stance** means treating something as if it has intentions — children do this naturally with dolls and stuffed animals
- **Role-playing** lets children simulate being another agent, testing different policies and perspectives
- **Shared pretend play** requires aligning generative models — "In this game, Teddy is the doctor"

### Activity Design
- Activities should involve stuffed animals, dolls, figurines, or imagined characters
- Include "interviews" with toys to practice perspective-taking
- Use role-play scenarios from daily life (school, doctor, store) played out with toys
- Younger children give voices to toys; older children write stories or scripts for characters
- Include a discussion about how pretend play helps us understand real people

### Key Connections
- Link back to Module 01 (Systems): Players are agents within the game system
- Link forward to Module 03 (Perception): How do our characters perceive their world?
