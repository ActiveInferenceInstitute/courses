Course — Module 02 — Think About It

Why does a soccer team wear the same color? (Identity/Team Signal).
What does the Goalie do? (Protects the goal).
Why do we shake hands after a match? (Respect).
Is it fun to win every single time? (No, it gets boring).
Why do we need referees? (To be the Judge Agent).
Can you play catch by yourself? (No, you need a Partner Agent).
What is a "Sore Loser"? (Someone who forgets it's just a game).
Why do coaches exist? (To help specific agents improve).
Do robots play soccer? (Yes! RoboCup).
If you could be any player in a game, who would you be?
