Module 02: Agents — The Players

Learning Objectives

Understand that Players are Agents in a Game System.
Learn about Roles (Goalie, Seekers, Hiders).
Know that a good player respects the other players.

Introduction: The Cast of Characters

In a play, everyone has a part.
In a game, everyone has a Role.
If everyone tries to be the Goalie, the goal is empty!
Agents must know their job to make the system work.

Key Concepts

The Role (The Job)

Different games have different jobs.

 Tag: "It" vs. "Runner."
 Hide and Seek: "Seeker" vs. "Hider."
 Soccer: "Offense" vs. "Defense."
You have to switch your brain when you switch your role.

The Opponent (The Helper)

Is the other team your enemy?
NO! They are your helpers.
Without an opponent, there is no game.
Tennis against a wall is practice. Tennis against a friend is a GAME.
Respect your opponent because they make the fun possible.

Sportsmanship (The Agent's Code)

 Win without bragging.
 Lose without crying.
 Say "Good Game."
This is the Golden Rule of Agents.

Activities

Activity 1: Role Swap

Play Tag.
Freeze!
Now the Runners are "It" and "It" is a Runner.
How does the game change?
Is it harder to catch or to run?

Activity 2: The Silent Teammate

Build a Lego tower with a partner.
BUT... you cannot talk!
You have to watch what they are doing and help them.
(Agent Coordination).

Summary

A game is only as good as its players. By knowing your role and respecting others, you become a Master Agent.
