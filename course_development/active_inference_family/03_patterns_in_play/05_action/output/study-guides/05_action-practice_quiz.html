Practice Quiz: Taking Turns

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What is Impulse Control? A) A remote control for the TV. B) Stopping yourself from doing something you want to do. C) A joystick.

  2. Why do we take turns? A) To be fair. B) To prevent chaos. C) Both!

  3. True or False: Moving is harder than Waiting.

    • (False! Waiting (Inhibition) is often harder for the brain).

  4. Who goes first in a game? A) The oldest. B) The youngest. C) Whatever the rules say (Coin toss, Rock Paper Scissors).

  5. What happens if everyone talks at once? A) Communication failure. B) A great conversation. C) Silence.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. When is it hardest for you to wait? (Birthday? Christmas?).
  2. how do you decide who gets the front seat?
  3. Let's play "Don't Blink." (Inhibiting the blink reflex!).
