# Lab: Jenga (The Tower of Patience)

## Goal

To practice fine motor skills and turn-taking under pressure.

## What You Need

* A Jenga set (or block tower).

## Steps

### 1. The Build

Build the tower together.

### 2. The Move

Player 1 removes a block.
Player 2 MUST WAIT.
Even if the tower is wobbling!
Even if they want to help!

### 3. The Pressure

As the tower gets higher, the pressure goes up.
Your hand might shake.
Take a deep breath. (Calm the brain).

## Discussion

* Did you want to grab the tower when it started to fall? (Reflex).
* Was it hard to watch your partner make a "bad" move?
* Turn-taking is a contract: "I trust you with the tower."

## What We Learned

Games teach us to control our bodies and respect the rhythm of others.
