# Learning Pathways: Active Inference for Families

> Different ways to navigate this curriculum depending on your situation and needs.

---

## Pathway 1: The Full Journey (Recommended)

**For**: First-time parents, anyone who wants the complete picture.
**Duration**: ~32 weeks at 1 module per week.

Work through each course in order:

1. **Growing Together** (Weeks 1–8): Understand your family as a system.
2. **Tiny Bodies, Big Brains** (Weeks 9–16): Go inside your baby's developing brain and body.
3. **Patterns in Play** (Weeks 17–24): See learning happen through play.
4. **Screens, Toys & Tools** (Weeks 25–32): Navigate modern parenting decisions.
