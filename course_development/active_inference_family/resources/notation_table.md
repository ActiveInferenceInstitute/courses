# Notation Table: Active Inference for Families

> This curriculum uses minimal notation — almost everything is explained in plain language. These few simple symbols appear in diagrams and concept cards throughout the courses.

## Core Symbols

| Symbol | What It Means | Everyday Analogy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **s** | Sensory states — what comes IN from the world | What your baby sees, hears, touches, tastes, smells |
| **a** | Active states — what goes OUT to the world | What your baby does: reaches, cries, crawls, babbles |
| **μ** (mu) | Internal states — what's happening INSIDE | Your baby's mood, hunger level, mental model of the world |
| **→** | Causes or leads to | "This leads to that" |
| **⟲** | Feedback loop — something that circles back on itself | Baby cries → parent responds → baby calms → baby learns "crying works" |
| **↑** | Increases or goes up | Prediction error increases when something unexpected happens |
| **↓** | Decreases or goes down | Prediction error decreases when the baby figures something out |

## Diagram Conventions

| Element | How We Draw It | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Circle** | ○ | An agent (baby, parent, pet) |
| **Dashed border** | ⦿ | The Markov blanket — the boundary between inside and outside |
| **Solid arrow** | →  | Direct influence |
| **Dashed arrow** | ⇢ | Indirect influence or prediction |
| **Happy face** | 😊 | Low surprise — predictions match reality |
| **Surprised face** | 😲 | High surprise — prediction error |
