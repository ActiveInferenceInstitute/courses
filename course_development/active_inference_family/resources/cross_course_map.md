## How to Read This Map

**Vertically**: Follow one topic down a column to see how a single course builds from systems to planning.

**Horizontally**: Follow one topic across to see how the same concept looks through 4 different lenses:

- Course 1 (Growing Together) gives you the **family** perspective.
- Course 2 (Tiny Bodies, Big Brains) gives you the **developmental science** perspective.
- Course 3 (Patterns in Play) gives you the **play-based learning** perspective.
- Course 4 (Screens, Toys & Tools) gives you the **modern parenting** perspective.
