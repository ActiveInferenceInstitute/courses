# AGENTS: Module 02 — Agents

## Conventions
- **Perspective**: Daily family life
- **Topic**: Agents
- **Subtitle**: Your Baby Is a Tiny Scientist
- **Course**: Growing Together
- **Lab Type**: Family Observation — The Motivation Log
- **Key Concepts**: agent, policy, prior beliefs, preferences, goals, reflexes

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children learning together
- No prior knowledge of Active Inference assumed

### Tone
- Warm, empathetic, and curious
- Use language that validates every family member's experience ("your goals matter," "everyone sees things differently")
- Frame conflict as a natural result of different agents with different goals, not as anyone being "bad"
- Encourage perspective-taking with humor and playfulness

### Active Inference Integration
- **Agent** is introduced as "anyone who can act and has goals" — babies, children, parents, even pets
- **Goals** map to what Active Inference calls "preferred states" — the conditions each agent tries to bring about
- **Agency** is the power to make choices, growing as children develop more autonomy
- **Perspective** connects to the idea that each agent has its own generative model of the world
- **Prior beliefs** are the expectations each agent carries — "I expect dinner at 6" or "I expect a bedtime story"

### Activity Design
- Activities should encourage role-playing and perspective-switching
- Include opportunities for children to "be" another family member for a few minutes
- Use props like shoes, hats, or glasses to make perspective-switching tangible
- Encourage guessing games about what family members want or need
- Suitable for mixed ages: younger children act it out, older children discuss the "why"

### Key Connections
- Link back to Module 01 (Systems): Agents are the parts that make up the family system
- Link forward to Module 03 (Perception): Agents use their senses to understand the world
