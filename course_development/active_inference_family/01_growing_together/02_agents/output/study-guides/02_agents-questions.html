Growing Together — Module 02: Agents — Think About It

Part A: Family Discussion Questions

  1. What is your biggest goal for today? (Play? Eat? Rest?).
  2. What makes someone an "Agent"? (Hint: They can act and they have goals).
  3. Why do people in a family sometimes disagree? (Different goals bumping into each other!).
  4. What does "Agency" mean? (The power to make choices). How much agency do you have?
  5. If you were the parent for a day, what would you do differently?
  6. Can a pet be an agent? What is the dog's goal when it brings you a ball?
  7. Think of a time someone surprised you. What goal were they trying to reach?
  8. How does your way of seeing things differ from your sibling's or parent's?
  9. What is one choice you made today that changed something in the family?
  10. Why is it important to ask people what their goal is instead of just guessing?

Part B: Hands-On Activities and Reflection

  1. Play the "Why Detective" game: Watch a family member for five minutes. Write down what they are doing and guess their goal. Then ask them! Were you right?
  2. Draw an "Island" for each family member. On each island, draw or write three things that person loves or wants. Where do the islands overlap?
  3. Try wearing someone else's shoes for five minutes. Walk around the house as that person. What do you notice that you did not notice before?
  4. Make a "Goal Collage" by cutting out pictures or drawing things each family member wants to do this month. Hang it on the fridge.
  5. Have a "Perspective Swap" at dinner: Everyone says one nice thing from another person's point of view. ("If I were Dad, I would be proud of...").
  6. Write down three choices you made today. For each one, write what happened because of your choice. You have agency!
  7. Play a board game together. After the game, discuss: What was each player's goal? Did any goals conflict?
  8. Ask each family member: "What is the hardest choice you had to make today?" Listen carefully to understand their perspective.
  9. Together, create a "Family Agent Card" for each person — name, superpower, favorite goal, and one thing they need help with.
  10. Before bed tonight, tell a family member one goal you have for tomorrow. Ask them to help you reach it. Teamwork between agents!
