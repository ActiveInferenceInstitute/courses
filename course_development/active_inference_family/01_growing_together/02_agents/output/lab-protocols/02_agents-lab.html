Lab: Treasure Map of Wants

Goal

To visualize the different goals of each family member.

What You Need

  • Large paper.
  • Magazines or markers.
  • Glue.

Steps

1. The Islands

Draw 4 (or more) Islands on the paper. Label each island with a family member's name.

2. Fill the Islands (The Goals)

Cut out pictures or draw what each person wants/loves.

  • Mom's Island: Quiet time, Coffee, Clean house.
  • Kid's Island: Legos, Video Games, Pizza.
  • Dog's Island: Bones, Walks, Squirrels.

3. Build Bridges

How can we connect the islands?

  • "If I help Mom clean (Her goal), she lets me play Video Games (My goal)."
  • "If we take the Dog for a walk (His goal), we get exercise (Mom's goal)." Draw bridges between the wants.

Discussion

  • Are there any goals that are the same? (Everyone likes Pizza!).
  • Are there goals that are far apart? (Mom wants Quiet, I want Loud).
  • How can we respect each island?

What We Learned

We aren't all on the same island, but we can build bridges to help each other be happy.
