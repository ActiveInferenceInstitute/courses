# Practice Quiz: Family Agents

## Part A: Reflect Together

1. **What is an Agent?**
    A) A spy.
    B) Anyone who can act and has goals (You, Me, The Cat).
    C) A robot only.

2. **Why do people fight?**
    A) Because they are mean.
    B) Because their Goals act against each other (Collision!).
    C) Because they are bored.

3. **True or False**: Everyone sees the world exactly the same way.
    * (False. We all have different Perspectives).

4. **What is "Agency"?**
    A) A building.
    B) The power to make choices and take action.
    C) Being old.

5. **Who has the goal "Keep the family safe"?**
    A) Usually Parents.
    B) The Baby.
    C) The Mailman.

## Part B: Discussion Questions

1. Think of a time you were mad at a family member. What was *your* goal? What was *their* goal?
2. How can we help the different agents in our house work together?
3. If you could swap agency with anyone for a day, who would it be?
