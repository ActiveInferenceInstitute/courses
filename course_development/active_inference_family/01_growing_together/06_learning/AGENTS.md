# AGENTS: Module 06 — Learning

## Conventions
- **Perspective**: Daily family life
- **Topic**: Learning
- **Subtitle**: How Your Baby Learns From You
- **Course**: Growing Together
- **Lab Type**: Family Observation — The Teaching Moment Log
- **Key Concepts**: model updating, responsiveness, co-regulation, serve and return, narration, secure attachment

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children learning together
- No prior knowledge of Active Inference assumed

### Tone
- Warm, patient, and growth-oriented
- Normalize mistakes as essential to learning, not failures
- Use the word "yet" frequently ("You cannot do it... yet!")
- Celebrate progress over perfection — show that everyone is always learning, including parents

### Active Inference Integration
- **Learning** means updating your generative model when reality surprises you
- **Surprise** (prediction error) is the teacher — when something unexpected happens, the brain updates
- **Model updating** is introduced as "changing your mind" based on new information
- **Mistakes** are reframed as useful data — signals that say "update required"
- **Flexible models** (being open to new ideas) lead to better outcomes than rigid ones

### Activity Design
- Activities should involve teaching and learning between family members in both directions
- Include skill-sharing where children teach parents something
- Use before/after comparisons to make learning visible (old drawings, early attempts)
- Younger children focus on the pride of mastery; older children reflect on the process
- Create a safe space where mistakes are celebrated, not criticized

### Key Connections
- Link back to Module 05 (Action): We learn by doing and seeing what happens
- Link forward to Module 07 (Communication): Sharing what we learn requires communication
