Course — Module 06 — Think About It

  1. What is the hardest thing you ever learned? (Walking? Talking? Division?).
  2. Is it okay to cry when you thrive? (Frustration is part of learning).
  3. Do parents know everything? (No! They are learning too).
  4. Why do babies put everything in their mouths? (They are gathering data!).
  5. What was your biggest "Surprise" today?
  6. How does sleep help you learn? (It saves the updates).
  7. Can you learn from watching someone else? (Yes, that is social learning).
  8. What would happen if you never made a mistake? (You would never improve).
  9. Why do we go to school? (To build complex models of the world).
  10. What do you want to learn next? (Spanish? Karate? Cooking?).
