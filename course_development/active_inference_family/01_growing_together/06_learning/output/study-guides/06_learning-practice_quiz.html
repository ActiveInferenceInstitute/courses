Practice Quiz: Family Learning

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What is a "Mistake"? A) A disaster. B) Proof that you are bad. C) A chance to learn and update your model.

  2. What should you say when you don't know the answer? A) "I give up." B) "I don't know... YET." C) Lie.

  3. True or False: Once you are an adult, you stop learning.

    • (False. Adults learn new jobs, recipes, and technology all the time).

  4. How do we learn best? A) By doing it perfectly the first time. B) By trying, failing, and trying again (Practice). C) By sleeping all day.

  5. What is "Surprise"? A) A birthday party. B) When something happens that you didn't predict (New Data!). C) A scary monster.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. Tell the story of a funny mistake you made.
  2. What is something Mom or Dad is learning right now?
  3. Name 3 things you couldn't do when you were a baby.
