# AGENTS: Module 08 — Planning

## Conventions
- **Perspective**: Daily family life
- **Topic**: Planning
- **Subtitle**: How Your Family Prepares
- **Course**: Growing Together
- **Lab Type**: Family Observation — The Routine Architect
- **Key Concepts**: planning, routines, transitions, prediction error, anticipation, flexibility, temporal depth

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children learning together
- No prior knowledge of Active Inference assumed

### Tone
- Warm, organized, and forward-looking
- Present planning as exciting ("time travel!") rather than boring or restrictive
- Celebrate both structure (routines) and flexibility (changing plans when needed)
- Use visual and concrete language (maps, treasure, timelines)

### Active Inference Integration
- **Planning** is how the brain simulates future states and picks the best path to reach a goal
- **Temporal depth** is how far into the future an agent can "see" — children develop this gradually
- **Routines** are plans that have become habitual, reducing the need for active decision-making each time
- **Expected free energy** (simplified) is introduced as "picking the choice that leads to the best future"
- **Flexibility** means being able to update the plan when surprise happens — not everything goes as predicted

### Activity Design
- Activities should involve collaborative planning with visible outputs (posters, timelines, calendars)
- Include both short-term plans (weekend) and long-term goals (family project, vacation)
- Use drawing and writing to make plans concrete and shareable
- Younger children place stickers on schedules; older children reason about sequence and priorities
- Always include a discussion about what happens when plans change

### Key Connections
- Link back to Module 07 (Communication): Planning requires communicating about the future
- Link to course overview: Planning ties all eight modules together — systems, agents, perception, cognition, action, learning, and communication all feed into how we plan
