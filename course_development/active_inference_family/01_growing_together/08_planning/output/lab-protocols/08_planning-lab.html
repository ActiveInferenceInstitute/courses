Lab: The Giant Weekend Planner

Goal

To work together to plan a fun weekend.

What You Need

  • A giant piece of paper (or tape 4 sheets together).
  • Markers.

Steps

1. The Timeline

Draw a line across the paper. Mark "Saturday" and "Sunday". Mark "Morning", "Afternoon", "Night".

2. The Brainstorm (Cognition)

Ask everyone: "What is one thing you HAVE to do?" (Chores, Soccer). Write it in black. Ask everyone: "What is one thing you WANT to do?" (Park, Movie). Write it in colors.

3. The Puzzle

Fit the pieces onto the timeline.

  • "We can't go to the park at night."
  • "We have to clean before we play." Move things around until they fit.

Discussion

  • Is there enough time for everything?
  • Do we need to save something for next weekend?
  • Does everyone have something fun to do?

What We Learned

Planning isn't about controlling everything. It's about making space for the things that matter most.
