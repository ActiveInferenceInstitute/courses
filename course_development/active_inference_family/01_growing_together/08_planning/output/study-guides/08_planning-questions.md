# Course — Module 08 — Think About It

1. What would happen if we didn't have clocks? (Chaos!).
2. How do you know when it is your birthday? (The Calendar).
3. Why do we pack bags *before* we go on a trip? (Planning ahead saves stress).
4. Can you change a plan? (Yes! Flexibility is key. If it rains, we play inside).
5. What takes longer: Brushing teeth or watching a movie?
6. Do you have a "Routine"? (Something you do the same way every time).
7. Why is it hard to wait for Christmas? ( The future feels far away).
8. What do you want to be when you grow up? (That is a VERY long-term plan!).
9. Who is the best planner in your family?
10. If you could plan the "Perfect Day", what would you do?
