# AGENTS: Module 05 — Action

## Conventions
- **Perspective**: Daily family life
- **Topic**: Action
- **Subtitle**: What Your Baby Does
- **Course**: Growing Together
- **Lab Type**: Family Observation — The Movement Milestone Tracker
- **Key Concepts**: motor prediction, reaching, grasping, proprioception, motor milestones, practice

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children learning together
- No prior knowledge of Active Inference assumed

### Tone
- Energetic, encouraging, and hands-on
- Celebrate all kinds of action — chores, play, helping, creating
- Frame chores not as punishment but as contributions to the family system
- Use action words and active language ("build," "cook," "clean," "run," "create")

### Active Inference Integration
- **Action** is how agents change the world to match their predictions and goals
- **Habits** are actions that have become automatic — the brain no longer needs to plan them step by step
- **Play** is active hypothesis testing — children test the limits of their bodies and the rules of the world
- **Motor prediction** means the brain predicts how the body will move before it happens
- **Chores** are actions that reduce entropy (disorder) in the family system

### Activity Design
- Activities should be physical and involve movement
- Include both "work" activities (helpful chores) and "play" activities (games, challenges)
- Use timers, songs, or challenges to make action fun
- Younger children do simple physical tasks; older children take on more complex helping roles
- Include a creative element (making coupon books, inventing new games)

### Key Connections
- Link back to Module 04 (Cognition): We think before we act
- Link forward to Module 06 (Learning): Actions teach us what works and what does not
