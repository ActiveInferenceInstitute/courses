# Lab: Chore Coupons of Love

## Goal

To give the gift of Action (Service) to your family.

## What You Need

* Paper strips.
* Crayons.
* Stapler (optional).

## Steps

### 1. Brainstorm Actions

Think of things you can do to help.

* "One Free Hug."
* "I will set the table."
* "I will tell a joke."
* "I will clean my room (No asking!)."

### 2. Make the Coupons

Write the action on a paper strip.
Decorate it with hearts or stars.

### 3. Give the Gift

Give the coupon book to a parent or sibling.
Tell them: "You can use these whenever you need help!"

## Discussion

* How did it feel to give help?
* How did it feel when they used a coupon?
* Action is a way to say "I Love You."

## What We Learned

Small actions add up to a happy system. We have the power to make someone's day better by doing something kind.
