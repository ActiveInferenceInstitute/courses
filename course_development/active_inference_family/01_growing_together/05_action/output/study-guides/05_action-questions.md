# Course — Module 05 — Think About It

1. What is your favorite thing to DO? (Run? Draw? Build?).
2. Why do we have to clean up? (Entropy! Things get messy naturally).
3. What happens if nobody takes out the trash? (It smells, and attracts bugs).
4. Can work be fun? (Yes, if we do it together or make it a game).
5. What is a habit you do every morning?
6. How does playing Tag help your brain? (Teaches strategy and body control).
7. Do you have to think about walking? (No, it is a habit/reflex).
8. Why is it hard to stop playing video games? (Because they act on your brain's reward system).
9. How do you help your parents? (Small actions matter!).
10. If you could have a robot do one chore for you, what would it be?
