# Practice Quiz: Family Action

## Part A: Reflect Together

1. **What is a Chore?**
    A) A terrible punishment.
    B) An action that helps the family system run smoothly.
    C) A type of dance.

2. **Which is a Good Habit?**
    A) Leaving wet towels on the floor.
    B) Brushing teeth before bed.
    C) Screaming when you wake up.

3. **True or False**: Play is a waste of time.
    * (False. Play helps us learn, grow strong, and bond).

4. **If a task is boring, how can you fix it?**
    A) Don't do it.
    B) Make it a game or do it with music.
    C) Cry.

5. **Who should do the work in a family?**
    A) Only Mom.
    B) Only Dad.
    C) Everyone should pitch in (Teamwork).

## Part B: Discussion Questions

1. What is one chore you actually like doing? (Feeding the cat? Watering plants?).
2. How does it feel when your room is clean?
3. Let's make a "Action Plan" for Saturday. What shall we do?
