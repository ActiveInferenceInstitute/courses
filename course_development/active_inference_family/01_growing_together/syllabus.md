# Syllabus: Growing Together

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](./README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../README.md)

## Course Description

Growing Together introduces Active Inference through the everyday rhythms of family life. Each week explores one core concept through the lens of what families actually *do*: feeding, sleeping, soothing, playing, and talking with babies. No science background required — just curiosity about why your family works the way it does.

## Materials

No purchases required. All activities use everyday household items and observation.

## Assessment

This is a self-paced, self-directed course. There are no tests or grades. Engage at whatever depth feels right for your family.

- **Practice quizzes**: Self-check your understanding (no pressure)
- **Reflection questions**: Think and discuss with a partner or group
- **Family labs**: Observe and do — the real learning happens here
- **Dashboards**: Interactive concept cards for quick review

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Describe your family as a system with boundaries, internal states, and active maintenance
2. Recognize your baby as an agent — a being with goals, predictions, and preferences
3. See everyday moments (feeding, crying, sleeping) through the lens of Active Inference
4. Use the language of prediction, surprise, and model-updating to understand your child's behavior
5. Feel more confident that the ordinary things you do as a parent are scientifically profound
