## What This Course Is About

This course looks at Active Inference through the lens of **daily family life** — the feeding, sleeping, crying, soothing, and routine-building that fill your days as a parent. Every module connects a core Active Inference concept to something you already do, helping you see the remarkable science behind the ordinary moments of raising a child.

You don't need a science background. You don't need to read papers. You just need to be curious about why your baby does what they do — and why you respond the way you respond.

## The Core Insight

Your family is a living system. It has boundaries (who's in, who's out), internal states (moods, health, hunger), and a constant flow of sensing and acting that keeps everyone alive and (mostly) sane. Active Inference gives you a framework for understanding how this system works — from the level of a single neuron in your baby's brain to the level of your whole household navigating a Tuesday morning.

## How to Use This Course

1. **Read one module per week** — each takes 15–20 minutes
2. **Try the Family Lab** — a simple observation or activity you can do at home
3. **Reflect with the study questions** — alone or with a co-parent, friend, or group
4. **Check the dashboard** — interactive concept cards reinforce key ideas
5. **Cross-reference** — see how the same topic looks through different lenses in [Course 2](../02_tiny_bodies_big_brains/), [Course 3](../03_patterns_in_play/), and [Course 4](../04_screens_toys_tools/)
