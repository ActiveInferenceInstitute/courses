Course — Module 07 — Think About It

What is the hardest part about listening? (Waiting your turn!).
Why do people yell? (They feel they are not being heard).
Can you communicate with a baby who can't talk? (Yes! Smiles, pointing, crying).
How do you know if someone is really listening to you? (Eye contact, nodding).
What is a "Secret Code"? (Only your system understands it).
Why is texting harder than talking? (No face clues!).
What happens if you give the wrong instructions? (Confusion!).
Is silence a kind of communication? (Yes, it can mean peace or anger).
How does a dog tell you it wants to go out? (Barking, scratching door).
What is the most important word in your family? (Love? Please? Sorry?).
