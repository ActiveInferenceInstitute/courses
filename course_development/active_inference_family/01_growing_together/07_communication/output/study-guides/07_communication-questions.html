Course — Module 07 — Think About It

  1. What is the hardest part about listening? (Waiting your turn!).
  2. Why do people yell? (They feel they are not being heard).
  3. Can you communicate with a baby who can't talk? (Yes! Smiles, pointing, crying).
  4. How do you know if someone is really listening to you? (Eye contact, nodding).
  5. What is a "Secret Code"? (Only your system understands it).
  6. Why is texting harder than talking? (No face clues!).
  7. What happens if you give the wrong instructions? (Confusion!).
  8. Is silence a kind of communication? (Yes, it can mean peace or anger).
  9. How does a dog tell you it wants to go out? (Barking, scratching door).
  10. What is the most important word in your family? (Love? Please? Sorry?).
