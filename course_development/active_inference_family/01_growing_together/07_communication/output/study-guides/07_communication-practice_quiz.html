Practice Quiz: Family Talk

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What is the goal of Communication? A) To make noise. B) To share thoughts and feelings (Alignment). C) To win an argument.

  2. Which is a "Good Listener" action? A) Looking at your phone. B) Looking at the speaker's eyes. C) Humming loudly.

  3. True or False: You can only communicate with words.

    • (False. Hugs, high-fives, and smiles are messages too).

  4. What is "Noise"? A) Anything that blocks the message. B) Music only. C) Silence.

  5. If you are angry, what is the best way to communicate? A) Hit something. B) Say "I need a timeout" or "I feel mad." C) Break a toy.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. When is the best time to talk in our house? (Dinner? Bedtime?).
  2. How do we handle interruptions? ("Excuse me?").
  3. Let's practice saying "I feel..." instead of "You are...".
