Lab: The Mystery Box

Goal

To use your sense of Touch to perceive the world without eyes.

What You Need

  • A shoe box.
  • Scissors (cut a hole in the side).
  • Mystery Objects (Pinecone, Cotton Ball, Toy Car, Spoon).

Steps

1. The Setup

Cut a hole in the box big enough for a hand. Hide an object inside. Don't let the "Feeler" see!

2. The Perception

The Feeler puts their hand in. Describe what you feel:

  • "It is cold."
  • "It is smooth."
  • "It has wheels."

3. The Guess

Make a Hypothesis (Guess). "Is it a Toy Car?" Pull it out to check!

My Mystery Log

What We Learned

Our hands are amazing sensors. Even without eyes, our brain can build a picture of the world just by touching!
