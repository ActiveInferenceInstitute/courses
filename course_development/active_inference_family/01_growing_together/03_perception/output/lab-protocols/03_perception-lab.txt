Lab: The Mystery Box

Goal

To use your sense of Touch to perceive the world without eyes.

What You Need

 A shoe box.
 Scissors (cut a hole in the side).
 Mystery Objects (Pinecone, Cotton Ball, Toy Car, Spoon).

Steps

The Setup

Cut a hole in the box big enough for a hand.
Hide an object inside.
Don't let the "Feeler" see!

The Perception

The Feeler puts their hand in.
Describe what you feel:

 "It is cold."
 "It is smooth."
 "It has wheels."

The Guess

Make a Hypothesis (Guess).
"Is it a Toy Car?"
Pull it out to check!

My Mystery Log

| Object | Clues (What I felt) | Did I guess right? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Spoon | Cold, hard, long handle. | Yes! |
| Kiwi | Fuzzy, round, soft. | Thought it was a tennis ball! (Surprise). |

What We Learned

Our hands are amazing sensors. Even without eyes, our brain can build a picture of the world just by touching!
