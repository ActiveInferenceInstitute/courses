# AGENTS: Module 01 — Systems

## Conventions
- **Perspective**: Daily family life
- **Topic**: Systems
- **Subtitle**: Your Family Is a Living System
- **Course**: Growing Together
- **Lab Type**: Family Observation — The Family Map
- **Key Concepts**: system, Markov blanket, NESS, internal states, boundary, entropy, routines

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children learning together
- No prior knowledge of Active Inference assumed

### Tone
- Warm, encouraging, and playful
- Use everyday family language ("team," "connected," "working together")
- Avoid academic jargon; when introducing a concept, use a simple analogy first
- Celebrate the family unit as a natural example of a living system

### Active Inference Integration
- **System boundaries** map to the family home, the "inside" vs. "outside" world
- **Markov blanket** is introduced as the invisible bubble around the family that lets good things in and keeps the family safe
- **NESS (Non-Equilibrium Steady State)** is introduced as "balance" — the family staying healthy and happy by constantly adjusting
- **Entropy** is introduced as "messiness" or "chaos" — when routines break down, things get disorganized

### Activity Design
- Activities should involve the whole family sitting or standing together
- Use common household items (yarn, paper, markers, hangers)
- Include a physical, hands-on element (building, drawing, tossing)
- Allow children of different ages to participate at their own level
- Keep activities under 20 minutes for younger children, extendable for older ones
