Lecture Content

Module 01: Systems — The Family System

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand that a Family is a System made of people.
  2. Learn that systems have Parts (Parents, Kids, Pets) and Connections (Love, Rules, Talk).
  3. Know that if one part changes, the whole system changes.

Introduction: We Are A Team

A car is a system of wheels and engines. A forest is a system of trees and animals. A Family is a system too! You are not just one person. You are part of a team.

  • The Parts: Mom, Dad, You, Baby Brother, Dog.
  • The Connections: Who cooks dinner? Who plays games? Who gives hugs?

In a system, everyone matters. If one person is grumpy, the whole house feels grumpy. If one person is happy, the whole house shines!

Key Concepts

1. The Mobile (Balance)

Imagine a hanging mobile (like for a baby). If you pull one string, everything moves. A family is like that.

  • If Mom gets a new job -> The schedule changes.
  • If You get sick -> Dad stays home. We are all connected.

2. Boundaries (The House)

Every system has a boundary.

  • Inside: Your family, your rules, your safe space.
  • Outside: The world, school, neighbors. The boundary keeps the system safe but lets good things in (like friends and pizza!).

3. Energy (The Battery)

Systems need energy to work.

  • Cars need gas.
  • Families need Love, Food, and Sleep. If the "Family Battery" is low (everyone is tired), the system doesn't work well. We need to recharge together!

Activities

Activity 1: The String Web

Sit in a circle. Hold a ball of yarn. Toss it to someone and say "I love you because..." Keep tossing until everyone is holding the string. Now, one person pull the string. Does everyone feel it? We are connected!

Activity 2: Family Roles Map

Draw your family. Draw lines between people to show what they do for each other.

  • Mom -> Cooks for -> Me.
  • Me -> Feeds -> The Dog.
  • Dad -> Reads to -> Me. See how busy and helpful your system is!

Summary

A family is a living system. We are different parts working together to make a happy, healthy home. When we understand our system, we can take better care of each other.

References

  • The Family Book by Todd Parr
  • Thinking in Systems by Donella Meadows (For Parents)

Lab Protocol

Lab: Build a Family Mobile

Goal

To visualize how connected your family is by building a hanging mobile.

What You Need

  • A clothes hanger (or a stick).
  • String or yarn.
  • Paper and markers.
  • Scissors and tape.

Steps

1. Draw the Parts

Cut out shapes from paper. Draw a picture of each family member on a shape. Don't forget pets! You can also add "Values" like "Love", "Fun", "Kindness".

2. Connect the Strings

Tape a string to each picture. Tie the strings to the hanger at different lengths and places.

3. Find Balance

Hold the hanger up. Does it tip to one side? Move the strings until it balances perfectly. This shows that everyone has a place!

discussion

  • Touch one picture (make it swing).
  • Watch how all the other pictures move too.
  • This is how a system works. What you do affects everyone else!

What We Learned

We are balanced and connected. We move together!

Practice Quiz

Practice Quiz: The Family System

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. True or False: A family is just a bunch of people standing near each other.

    • (False. A family is a System because we are connected and affect each other).

  2. Which is an Example of Balance? A) Everyone shouting at once. B) Taking turns talking so everyone is heard. C) Eating only candy.

  3. What is a Family Boundary? A) A brick wall. B) The rules and love that keep us safe and define "Us". C) A fence.

  4. How do we recharge the Family Battery? A) By arguing. B) By ignoring each other. C) By spending time together (Game night, Movie night, Hugs).

  5. If one part of the system breaks (e.g., the car), does it affect the family? A) Yes, it might make us late or stressed. B) No, the car is not people.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. Name one "Job" you do that helps the family system. (e.g., Setting the table, being cheerful).
  2. What is your favorite family tradition? (This is a Repeating System Pattern!).
  3. How can we make our system stronger this week?
