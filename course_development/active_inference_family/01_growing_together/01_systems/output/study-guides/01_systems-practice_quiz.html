Practice Quiz: The Family System

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. True or False: A family is just a bunch of people standing near each other.

    • (False. A family is a System because we are connected and affect each other).

  2. Which is an Example of Balance? A) Everyone shouting at once. B) Taking turns talking so everyone is heard. C) Eating only candy.

  3. What is a Family Boundary? A) A brick wall. B) The rules and love that keep us safe and define "Us". C) A fence.

  4. How do we recharge the Family Battery? A) By arguing. B) By ignoring each other. C) By spending time together (Game night, Movie night, Hugs).

  5. If one part of the system breaks (e.g., the car), does it affect the family? A) Yes, it might make us late or stressed. B) No, the car is not people.

Part B: Discussion Questions

  1. Name one "Job" you do that helps the family system. (e.g., Setting the table, being cheerful).
  2. What is your favorite family tradition? (This is a Repeating System Pattern!).
  3. How can we make our system stronger this week?
