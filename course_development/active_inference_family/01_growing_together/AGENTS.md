# AGENTS: Course 1 — Growing Together

## Identity

- **Course**: Growing Together
- **Course Number**: 1 of 4
- **Perspective**: Daily family life — feeding, sleeping, soothing, routines
- **Lab Type**: Family Observation
- **Audience**: Parents of newborns through toddlers

## Content Conventions

- Every module connects one Active Inference concept to a daily parenting experience
- Tone is warm, reassuring, and practical — never clinical
- Examples draw from feeding, sleep, crying, transitions, and everyday moments
- Key terms appear in **bold** on first use and link to the [Glossary](../resources/glossary.md)

## Module–Topic Mapping

| Module | Topic | Daily Life Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Systems | Family as a system with boundaries and routines |
| 02 | Agents | Baby as an active agent with goals and drives |

| 08 | Planning | Routines, transitions, and anticipation |
