# Lab: The Family Crest

## Goal

To make a symbol that represents your Family Brain (Values).

## What You Need

* Paper or cardboard (Shield shape).
* Markers, glitter, stickers.

## Steps

### 1. The Shield

Cut out a shield shape.
Divide it into 4 parts.

### 2. The Values

In each part, draw something your family loves or believes in.

* Part 1: Favorite Activity (Camping?).
* Part 2: Favorite Food (Tacos?).
* Part 3: Make a Rule Symbol (Heart for Kindness).
* Part 4: Family Animal (Our Mascot).

### 3. The Motto

Write a sentence at the bottom.
"The Smiths: We Work Hard and Play Hard."
"The Garcias: Always Kind."

## Discussion

* Why did we pick these things?
* Does this shield show who we are?
* Hang it on the fridge!

## What We Learned

Our family has a unique identity. This "Model" helps us remember who we are and how to make choices.
