# AGENTS: Module 04 — Cognition

## Conventions
- **Perspective**: Daily family life
- **Topic**: Cognition
- **Subtitle**: How Your Baby Thinks Before Words
- **Course**: Growing Together
- **Lab Type**: Family Observation — The Expectation Spotter
- **Key Concepts**: generative model, object permanence, expectation violation, A-not-B error, temporal patterns

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children learning together
- No prior knowledge of Active Inference assumed

### Tone
- Warm, curious, and empowering
- Present thinking as something the whole family does together ("the Family Brain")
- Use relatable examples: choosing dinner, solving problems, following rules
- Celebrate children's ideas and reasoning, even when they arrive at unexpected conclusions

### Active Inference Integration
- **Generative model** is introduced as "our picture of how things work" — family values, rules, and expectations
- **Cognition** is the process of using our model to make sense of situations and decide what to do
- **Prediction** is how rules help us know what to expect ("If I knock, they will open the door")
- **Problem solving** follows the Active Inference loop: notice surprise, update the model, choose an action
- **Distributed cognition** means the family thinks together — voting, discussing, sharing ideas

### Activity Design
- Activities should involve thinking, deciding, and creating together
- Include rule-making and rule-testing activities
- Use voting or group decision-making to practice distributed cognition
- Younger children draw or build; older children discuss reasons and values
- Keep problem-solving activities low-stakes and fun (silly rules, family votes about movie night)

- Link back to Module 03 (Perception): Cognition uses what perception gathers
- Link forward to Module 05 (Action): Thinking leads to doing
