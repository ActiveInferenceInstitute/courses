Course — Module 05 — Think About It

Why do gamers lean when they steer in Mario Kart? (Body trying to help!).
Why does the controller vibrate? (Haptic feedback to alert the brain).
What is "Rage Quitting"? (When frustration > fun).
Can video games make you smarter? (Yes, they improve reaction time and problem solving).
Why are some games scary? (Immersion makes your brain think it's real).
How do pro gamers play so fast? (Muscle memory and prediction).
What is "Lag"? (A delay in the feedback loop).
Why is the A button usually green? (Green means Go).
Do you look at the controller when you play? (No, you know where the buttons are).
If you could control a real robot with a controller, would you?
