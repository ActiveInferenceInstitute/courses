# AGENTS: Module 02 — Agents

## Conventions
- **Perspective**: Technology and media in early childhood
- **Topic**: Agents
- **Subtitle**: Digital Agency vs Passivity
- **Course**: Screens, Toys & Tools
- **Lab Type**: Media Activity — The Engagement Analyzer
- **Key Concepts**: active vs passive engagement, lean-back/lean-forward, illusion of interactivity, agency spectrum

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children exploring the difference between active and passive technology use
- No prior knowledge of Active Inference or technology assumed

### Tone
- Thoughtful, empowering, and question-driven
- Help families distinguish between being a creator (active agent) and being a consumer (passive receiver)
- Use the metaphor of "driving vs. being a passenger" — both are fine, but you should know which one you are
- Avoid demonizing any specific technology; focus on the quality of engagement

### Active Inference Integration
- **Active engagement** is when the child is an agent — making choices, creating, experimenting
- **Passive engagement** is when the child is receiving without acting — watching, scrolling, absorbing
- **Agency spectrum** ranges from full agency (building with blocks, coding) to minimal agency (watching autoplay videos)
- **Illusion of interactivity** is when a screen feels interactive but actually offers no meaningful choices
- **Robots and AI** are artificial agents — they can act but do not have feelings or true understanding

### Activity Design
- Activities should compare active and passive engagement side by side
- Include a "lean forward vs. lean back" sorting exercise for different media activities
- Use observation logs to track when the child is creating vs. consuming
- Younger children sort activities into "I make" vs. "I watch" categories; older children rate activities on an agency spectrum
- Include building a simple "robot" from household items to explore what it means to be an agent

### Key Connections
- Link back to Module 01 (Systems): Technology is a system; now we ask who has agency within it
- Link forward to Module 03 (Perception): How do screens affect what we see and feel?
