Course — Module 02 — Think About It

Do robots dream? (Probably not... yet).
Can a robot feel pain? (No, but it can sense damage).
Why do we make robots look like humans? (So we like them more).
What is the difference between a toaster and a robot? (A robot can make decisions).
Can a robot verify truth? (Only if its data is true).
Will robots take all the jobs? (They will take the boring jobs!).
Can a robot write a poem? (Yes, AI can. But does it feel the poem?).
Why do robots need batteries? (Energy = Action).
If a robot breaks a vase, is it "guilty"? (No, it's just a machine).
