# Module 02: Agents — AI and Robots

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand that **Robots** are Agents made of metal and code.
2. Learn about **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** (Computer brains).
3. Know the difference between a Human (Needs sleep, has feelings) and a Robot (Needs electricity, has code).

## Introduction: The Robot Friend

Have you ever talked to Siri or Alexa?
Are they alive?
No. They are **Agents**.
They have "Eyes" (Cameras), "Ears" (Microphones), and a "Brain" (Chips).
They can help us, play music, and tell jokes.
But they don't feel happy or sad like you do.

## Key Concepts

### 1. The Sensor (Robot Senses)

Robots don't have skin. They have **Sensors**.

* Lidar (Laser vision).
* Heat Sensors (To find people).
* Touch Buttons.
Sensors give the robot data about the world.

### 2. The Code (Robot Rules)

You have a brain that learns.
A robot has **Code** that tells it what to do.

* "IF button is pressed, THEN say Hello."
* "IF battery is low, THEN go to sleep."
Robots love rules.

### 3. Human vs. Machine

* Humans: Eat food, dream, love, get tired.
* Machines: Eat electricity, calculate, never get tired.
We work best when we work TOGETHER (Cyborg Team!).

## Activities

### Activity 1: Robot Walk

Pretend to be a robot.
Robots move one joint at a time.

* "Rotate head."
* "Lift arm."
* "Extend leg."
Does it feel efficient? Or stiff?

### Activity 2: The Turing Test (Kid Version)

One person hides behind the couch.
The other person asks questions.
"Do you like ice cream?"
The person behind the couch tries to answer like a ROBOT.
Can you trick them?

## Summary

Robots are amazing helpers, but they are not people. The best future is one where Humans and Robots are friends.
