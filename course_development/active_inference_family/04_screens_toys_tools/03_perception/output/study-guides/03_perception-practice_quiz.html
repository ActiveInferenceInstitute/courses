Practice Quiz: Screens and Senses

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What is a Pixel? A) A tiny fairy. B) A dot of light on a screen. C) A pickle.

  2. How many colors make up a TV picture? A) 3 (Red, Green, Blue). B) 100. C) 1.

  3. True or False: A movie is just a lot of photos shown very fast.

    • (True. Persistence of Vision).

  4. Why do screens use bright colors? A) To save battery. B) To grab your attention. C) Because they like rainbows.

  5. Which sense does a screen NOT use? A) Sight. B) Sound. C) Smell.
