Module 03: Perception — Screens and Senses

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand that a Screen is made of millions of tiny lights (Pixels).
  2. Learn about Optical Illusions (Your brain can be tricked).
  3. Know that screens are designed to grab your Attention.

Introduction: The Magic Glass

Look at your tablet. It looks like a window into another world. But touch it. It's just glass. How does it show you a movie? It uses a trick called "Persistence of Vision." It flashes pictures so fast your brain thinks they are moving. It is a Magic Trick for your eyes.

Key Concepts

1. Pixels (The Dots)

If you look REALLY close at a TV (with a magnifying glass), you see dots. Red, Green, Blue. All the colors you see are made of these three lights. Millions of dots working together to make a picture.

2. Frame Rate (The Flipbook)

Movies aren't real life. They are 24 photos every second. Your brain smoothes them out. If the frame rate is slow (lag), the magic breaks.

3. Attention Capture

Why are cartoons so bright? Why are buttons red? Designers use colors and sounds to grab your Perception. They want you to look. You have to decide where to look.

Activities

Activity 1: The Flipbook

Take a stack of sticky notes. Draw a ball on the first page. Draw it slightly lower on the next. Flip through them fast. The ball moves! You just made a movie.

Activity 2: The RGB Test

Put a tiny drop of water on a phone screen (Be careful!). Look through the droplet like a magnifying glass. Can you see the teeny tiny Red, Green, and Blue lights? That is a Pixel.

Summary

Screens are cool, but they are just lights and tricks. Don't believe everything your eyes tell you!
