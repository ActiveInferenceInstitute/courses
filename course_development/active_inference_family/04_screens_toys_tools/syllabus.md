# Syllabus: Screens, Toys & Tools

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](./README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../README.md)

## Course Description

Screens, Toys & Tools applies Active Inference to the most pressing questions in modern parenting: How does technology shape my child's developing brain? Which toys promote genuine learning? How do I manage screen time without guilt or rigidity? Each module provides a framework for thinking — not a list of rules.

## Materials

No purchases required. Activities involve observing your existing media environment and toys.

## Assessment

Self-paced. No grades. Each module includes a practical activity designed to help you make informed decisions about media and toys in your specific family context.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Evaluate any toy, app, or media through the lens of "what model does this build?"
2. Distinguish between active engagement and passive consumption in your child's media use
3. Understand the research on screen time, educational media, and digital communication
4. Design a family media plan based on Active Inference principles rather than rigid rules
5. Feel confident in your technology decisions — and flexible enough to adjust them
