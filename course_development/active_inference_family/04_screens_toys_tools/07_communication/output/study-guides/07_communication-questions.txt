Course — Module 07 — Think About It

Why is it hard to tell if someone is joking in a text? (No tone of voice).
What is a "Troll"? (Someone who upsets people for fun).
Why do people use filters on photos? (To change their perception).
Is it rude to look at your phone while talking to someone? (Yes, "Phubbing").
What is "The Cloud" doing with your data? (Storing it forever).
How do YouTubers make money? (Ads and sponsors).
Can you be friends with someone you never met? (Yes, digitally).
Why do we use emojis? (To add emotion to text).
What should you do if you see bullying online? (Report it/Tell an adult).
