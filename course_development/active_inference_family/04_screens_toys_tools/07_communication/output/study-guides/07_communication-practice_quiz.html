Practice Quiz: Social Media and Sharing

Part A: Reflect Together

  1. What is a Digital Footprint? A) A dirty shoe. B) The trail of things you post online that stays forever. C) A robot foot.

  2. Why do people use Emojis? A) Because they can't spell. B) To show how they feel (Add emotion). C) To draw pictures.

  3. True or False: It is okay to be mean online because nobody knows it's you.

    • (False. Always be kind).

  4. What should you do if someone is mean to you online? A) Be mean back. B) Tell an adult and block them. C) Cry silently.

  5. Before you post, you should: A) Close your eyes. B) THINK (Is it True/Helpful/Inspiring/Necessary/Kind?). C) Eat a snack.
