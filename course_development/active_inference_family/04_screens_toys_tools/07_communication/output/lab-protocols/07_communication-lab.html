Lab: Emoji Charades

Goal

To understand how we communicate emotion without words.

What You Need

  • Your face.
  • Paper with drawing of emojis.

Steps

1. Draw

Draw 4 emojis on separate cards:

  • Happy 😀
  • Sad 😢
  • Angry 😠
  • Confused 😕

2. Act

Pick a card (don't show anyone). Make that face. Can your partner guess the emotion?

3. Context

Now, say "I love pizza" using the Angry face. Does it change the meaning? Yes! It sounds like sarcasm. Tone and Face change the meaning of words.

Discussion

  • Why is it hard to understand feelings in a text message?
  • Because we can't see the face!

What We Learned

Communication is more than just words. It is feelings too.
