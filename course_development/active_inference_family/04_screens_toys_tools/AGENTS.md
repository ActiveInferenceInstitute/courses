# AGENTS: Course 4 — Screens, Toys & Tools

## Identity

- **Course**: Screens, Toys & Tools
- **Course Number**: 4 of 4
- **Perspective**: Technology, media, and material culture in early childhood
- **Lab Type**: Media Audit / Design Challenge
- **Audience**: Parents navigating screens, toys, and technology

## Content Conventions

- Avoid simple good/bad judgments about technology — use Active Inference as a thinking framework
- Frame every toy and screen as "what kind of model does this build?"
- Include practical decision-making tools, not just theory
- Acknowledge the real pressures on modern parents (need for breaks, digital communication)

## Module–Topic Mapping

| Module | Topic | Technology Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Systems | Home as an informational ecosystem — what signals fill the space? |
| 02 | Agents | Active vs passive engagement with media and toys |
| 03 | Perception | How screen stimuli differ from real-world sensory input |
| 04 | Cognition | How media shapes attention, categorization, and thinking |
| 05 | Action | Physical vs digital play — what the body learns from each |
| 06 | Learning | Transfer of learning from media to real life |
| 07 | Communication | Video chat, audiobooks, and digital language exposure |
| 08 | Planning | Family media plans, routines, and healthy habits |
