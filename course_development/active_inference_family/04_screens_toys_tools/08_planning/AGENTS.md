# AGENTS: Module 08 — Planning

## Conventions
- **Perspective**: Technology and media in early childhood
- **Topic**: Planning
- **Subtitle**: Building a Family Media Plan
- **Course**: Screens, Toys & Tools
- **Lab Type**: Media Activity — The Media Plan Designer
- **Key Concepts**: decision framework, routines not rules, age-appropriate evolution, self-compassion

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children creating a shared media plan together
- No prior knowledge of Active Inference or technology assumed

### Tone
- Supportive, practical, and flexible
- Present media planning as an act of care, not restriction
- Use the metaphor of a "family media diet" — balanced, intentional, and revisable
- Include self-compassion: no family gets this perfect, and plans can be updated

### Active Inference Integration
- **Media planning** is the family choosing policies (rules and routines) that minimize expected free energy (stress, conflict, missed opportunities)
- **Routines not rules** means building habits that feel natural rather than imposing rigid restrictions
- **Age-appropriate evolution** means the plan updates as children grow — the model adapts to new data
- **Decision framework** gives families a consistent way to evaluate new media: "Does it meet our four pillars?"
- **Self-compassion** is recognizing that surprise and mistakes are part of the process — update the plan, do not feel guilty

### Activity Design
- Activities should involve the whole family designing a media plan together
- Include a "media menu" where the family decides what to include and what to limit
- Use poster-making, calendar-marking, and family voting to make the plan concrete
- Younger children choose their favorite approved activities; older children participate in setting boundaries
- Include a plan review date — the plan is a living document that gets updated

### Key Connections
- Link back to Module 07 (Communication): Planning requires family communication
- Link to course overview: The media plan ties together all the concepts — systems thinking, agency, perception awareness, cognitive impact, physical action, real learning, genuine communication, and thoughtful planning
