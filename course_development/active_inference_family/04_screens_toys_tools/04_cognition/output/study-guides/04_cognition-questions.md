# Course — Module 04 — Think About It

1. How does Google know the answer? (It reads the whole internet).
2. Is a computer smart or just fast? (Mostly fast).
3. Why do ads follow you around the internet? (Tracking cookies).
4. Can a computer write a book? (Yes, AI like ChatGPT).
5. What happens if you feed a computer bad information? (Garbage In, Garbage Out).
6. Does a computer have a favorite color? (No, no feelings).
7. How does GPS know where you are? (Satellites in space).
8. Why is math important for computers? (Computers speak logic/math).
9. Can a computer make a mistake? (Yes, bugs or bad data).
10. If you could ask a Super Computer one question, what would it be?
