# AGENTS: Module 04 — Cognition

## Conventions
- **Perspective**: Technology and media in early childhood
- **Topic**: Cognition
- **Subtitle**: How Media Shapes Thinking
- **Course**: Screens, Toys & Tools
- **Lab Type**: Media Activity — The Attention Tracker
- **Key Concepts**: attention training, toy complexity, scripted vs unscripted, categorization effects

## Content Guidelines

### Audience
- Families with children ages 3-12
- Parents and children exploring how technology affects attention and thinking
- No prior knowledge of Active Inference or technology assumed

### Tone
- Thoughtful, curious, and empowering
- Help families notice how different media affect attention spans and thinking
- Use the metaphor of "brain food" — some media nourishes thinking, some is like junk food
- Avoid guilt; focus on awareness and intentional choices

### Active Inference Integration
- **Attention** is how the brain selects what to include in its generative model — media competes for attention
- **Scripted vs. unscripted play** maps to constrained vs. flexible generative models — open-ended toys encourage richer thinking
- **Algorithms** are computer cognition — simple rules that produce complex results
- **Categorization** through media: apps that sort, label, and organize teach cognitive skills
- **Cognitive flexibility** is challenged when media provides all the answers — the brain does not have to think for itself

### Activity Design
- Activities should compare attention and thinking with different media types
- Include attention tracking experiments: time yourself focused on a book vs. a screen
- Use simple algorithm activities (follow step-by-step instructions to create something)
- Younger children sort toys by "makes me think" vs. "does the thinking for me"; older children analyze their favorite apps
- Include unplugged "search engine" activities using books and encyclopedias

### Key Connections
- Link back to Module 03 (Perception): What we perceive on screens shapes how we think
- Link forward to Module 05 (Action): How does digital play compare to physical play?
