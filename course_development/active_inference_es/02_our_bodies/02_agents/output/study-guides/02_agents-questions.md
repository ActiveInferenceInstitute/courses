# Course — Module 02 — Think About It

1. Who is the boss of your body? (Captain Brain!)
2. Can your hand move if your brain doesn't tell it to? (What about reflexes?)
3. What is a goal you have for today? (Eat lunch? Play a game? Learn something new?)
4. If Captain Brain falls asleep, what does the Body Ship do? (It rests too!)
5. Why do we need eyes (the spyglass)? Can we sail with our eyes closed?
6. Is a car an agent? Does it have a brain? (No, the driver is the brain!)
7. What happens if the Captain sees danger (like an iceberg)?
8. Can you be an agent if you cannot move? (Yes, you can still think and feel!)
9. Do animals have a Captain Brain too?
10. What is the best thing about being the Captain of your own ship?
11. Draw yourself as Captain Brain standing on top of your Body Ship. Label the parts: Eyes = Spyglass, Arms = Oars, Legs = Sails.
12. Play "Captain Says" (like Simon Says). Write down five commands the Captain gave. ("Captain says touch your toes!")
13. Stand on one side of the room. Put a toy on the other side. Your goal: reach the toy WITHOUT using your feet! How did you do it? (Crawl? Roll? Scoot?)
14. Tell a story about Captain Brain going on an adventure. What did the Captain see through the spyglass? What dangers did the Captain avoid?
15. Draw three different agents: a person, an animal, and a robot. What goals do they each have?
16. Imagine your Body Ship is sailing through a storm (it is windy and rainy outside). What does Captain Brain decide to do?
17. Act out being a robot agent. Move stiffly and say "Beep boop! My goal is to find the cookie!"
18. Draw a picture of your Body Ship with all the crew members labeled: Heart = Engine, Lungs = Air Tanks, Stomach = Fuel Tank, Brain = Captain.
19. Think about something you wanted to do today. What was the goal? What actions did your body take to reach it?
20. Write a journal entry as Captain Brain: "Today I sailed my Body Ship to school. I used my eyes to see the bus, my legs to walk, and my brain to remember my homework!"
