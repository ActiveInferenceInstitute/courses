Module 02: Agents — Captain Brain

Learning Objectives

Understand that YOU are an Agent (the Boss of your body).
Learn that your Body is like a ship, and your Brain is the Captain.
Know that Agents have Goals (things they want to do).

Introduction: Captain Brain and the Body Ship

Meet Captain Brain! She lives in the tall tower (your head).
She sails a wonderful ship called The Body.
The Body Ship sails on the big blue Ocean called The World.

Captain Brain has a map. She looks through her spyglass (Eyes).
"Land ho! I see a yummy apple!"
She yells to the crew: "Legs, walk! Arms, reach!"
The Body Ship moves. Captain Brain gets the apple.
She is a smart Agent!

Key Concepts

Who is the Agent?

The Agent is YOU.
Specifically, it's the part of you that thinks, chooses, and feels.
Your body is your tool to move in the world.

The Goal

Every Agent has a Goal.

 Captain Brain's Goal: Stay safe and find food (and have fun!).
 Your Goal: Learn, play, and grow.
Without a goal, the ship would just float in circles!

Action and Perception

 Perception (Spyglass): Seeing the world coming in.
 Action (Steering): Moving the body out into the world.
Captain Brain needs BOTH to sail safely.

Activities

Activity 1: Simon Says (The Captain Speaks)

One person is "Captain Brain." Everyone else is a "Body Part."

 "Captain says... touch your toes!" (The Body obeys)
 "Captain says... blink your eyes!"
 "Jump!" (Uh oh! Captain didn't say. Don't move!)
This shows how the brain sends orders to the body.

Activity 2: The Goal Game

Stand on one side of the room.
Place a toy on the other side.
Goal: Get the toy without using your feet!
Plan: Can you crawl? Roll? Hop?
Your brain finds a way to reach the goal!

Summary

You are a special Agent. Your Brain is the Captain, your Body is the Ship, and you sail through the World. Use your spyglass (senses) and steering wheel (actions) to reach your Goals!
