## Learning Goals

1. **Understand** that YOU are an agent -- the Captain of your Body Ship.
2. **Learn** that your Brain is the Captain who gives orders to the Body crew.
3. **See** how Perception (spyglass) and Action (steering) work together.
4. **Name** a goal you have and describe how your body helps you reach it.
5. **Play** a game where you use your body to reach a goal without using your feet!

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module introduces children as **agents** using the metaphor of Captain Brain sailing the Body Ship. The brain is the decision-maker, while the body is the vessel that carries out actions. In Active Inference, an agent is any system that maintains preferred states through perception and action. The "Simon Says" game (Captain gives orders) is an excellent way to demonstrate how the brain controls the body. Encourage children to think about what makes them different from objects like rocks or chairs.
