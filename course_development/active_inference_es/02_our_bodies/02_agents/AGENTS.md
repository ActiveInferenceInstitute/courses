# Station: Agents (Our Bodies)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Simple body science
- **Topics**: Agents
- **Lab Style**: Drawing & Coloring Lab
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Present the child as "Captain Brain" sailing "The Body Ship" through the Ocean of the World.
- Use the metaphor: Eyes = Spyglass, Legs = Sails, Arms = Oars, Brain = Captain.
- Emphasize that YOU are the agent -- the one who thinks, feels, and chooses. Your body is the tool.
- Differentiate between goals (what the Captain wants) and actions (how the Ship moves).

## Active Inference Integration

- An **Agent** in Active Inference is a system that maintains itself by acting on the world to fulfill its goals (preferred states).
- For K-5: "You are the Captain of your Body Ship. Your brain decides where to go, and your body follows orders!"
- The body-as-ship metaphor maps to: Perception = looking through the spyglass, Action = steering the ship.

## Lab Design Principles

- Labs should involve role-playing: one child as "Captain Brain" giving orders, others as "body parts."
- Materials: just the children themselves and some space to move.
- Include a goal-reaching challenge where the child must use their body creatively.

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "Who is the boss of your body?" "Can a car be an agent?"
- Questions 11-20: "Draw yourself as Captain Brain sailing the Body Ship." "Play Simon Says."
