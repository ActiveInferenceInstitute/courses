## Learning Goals

1. **Understand** that Planning means taking care of your future self.
2. **Learn** that Healthy Habits (sleep, good food, exercise) are your body's fuel plan.
3. **Discover** that small actions today lead to big results tomorrow.
4. **Create** a "Future Me" poster showing who you want to become.
5. **Practice** planning by designing a healthy lunchbox and a sleep schedule.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Planning** | Choosing actions today that make tomorrow better |
| **Habit** | Something you do every day that shapes your future |
| **Future You** | The person you will become based on the choices you make now |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module teaches **Planning** as caring for your future self through healthy habits. The key Active Inference idea is that planning involves evaluating future policies -- choosing actions today based on predicted outcomes tomorrow. The "Future Me" poster activity helps children visualize their goals and connect daily habits to long-term outcomes. The Strawberry Experiment (eat one now vs. plant for ten later) introduces delayed gratification. Discuss how every choice is a kind of plan.
