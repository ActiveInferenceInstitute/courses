# Station: Planning (Our Bodies)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Simple body science
- **Topics**: Planning
- **Lab Style**: Drawing & Coloring Lab
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Present Planning as "taking care of Future You" -- small actions today lead to big results tomorrow.
- Use the "Time Traveler" metaphor: you are traveling to Tomorrow and must plan for the trip.
- Teach Healthy Habits (sleep, food, exercise) as the brain's plan for keeping the Body Ship running.
- Include the Strawberry Experiment: eat one now vs. plant seeds for ten later (delayed gratification).

## Active Inference Integration

- **Planning** is selecting policies that minimize expected future free energy. Agents simulate future outcomes to choose the best action sequence.
- For K-5: "Smart Captains choose actions today that make Tomorrow better. Eating veggies and sleeping on time keeps the Body Ship strong!"

## Lab Design Principles

- Labs should involve creating a "Future Me" poster or a healthy habits chart.
- Materials: large paper, crayons, old magazines for cutting.
- Focus on visualizing the future self and connecting today's actions to future outcomes.

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "What happens if you never brush your teeth?" "Why do parents say eat your veggies?"
- Questions 11-20: "Create a Future Me poster." "Design a healthy lunchbox."
