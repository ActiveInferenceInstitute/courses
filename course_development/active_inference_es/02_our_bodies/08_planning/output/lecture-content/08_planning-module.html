Module 08: Planning — Healthy Habits

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand that Planning means taking care of the Future You.
  2. Learn about Healthy Habits (Sleep, Food, Exercise).
  3. Know that your Brain chooses actions today to reach a Goal tomorrow.

Introduction: The Time Traveler

Did you know you are a Time Traveler? You are traveling to Tomorrow! Captain Brain has to plan for the trip.

  • Goal: Arrive in Tomorrow feeling Happy and Strong.
  • Plan A: Eat only candy and stay up all night. (Result: Sick and Tired).
  • Plan B: Eat veggies and sleep on time. (Result: Strong and Energetic!).

Smart Captains choose Plan B!

Key Concepts

1. The Strawberry Experiment

Imagine a yummy strawberry. You want to eat it NOW (Short-term). But if you plant the seeds and wait (Planning), you will get 10 strawberries LATER (Long-term). Planning means waiting for the bigger reward.

2. Battery Power

Your Body Ship needs energy.

  • Sleep = Recharging the battery.
  • Food = Fuel for the engine. If you don't plan for sleep, your ship will stop moving!

3. Future You

Close your eyes. Imagine yourself as a grown-up. What do you want to be? An astronaut? A teacher? To be that person, you have to take care of your brain and body TODAY.

Activities

Activity 1: The Lunchbox Planner

Draw an empty lunchbox. Fill it with "Fuel Foods" (Healthy) and "Fun Foods" (Treats). Plan a lunch that gives you Super Energy. (Hint: Use colors! Red apples, Green carrots, Brown bread).

Activity 2: The Sleepy Chart

Make a chart for this week. Goal: Go to bed at the same time every night. Check off each day you do it. See how much better you feel on Friday!

Summary

Planning is the superpower of seeing the future. By choosing Healthy Habits today (eating well, sleeping, moving), Captain Brain ensures the Body Ship stays strong for all the adventures ahead!
