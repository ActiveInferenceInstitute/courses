Course — Module 08 — Think About It

  1. What would happen if you never brushed your teeth?
  2. Why do you have to go to school? (Planning for when you are a grown-up!)
  3. If you want to run a marathon, what do you have to do today? (Practice running!)
  4. Why does junk food taste good but make us feel bad?
  5. What time do you go to sleep? Is it easy to wake up?
  6. If you had a robot body, would you still need to sleep? (Yes, to charge!)
  7. Can you plan a surprise party? What steps do you need?
  8. Why do parents tell you to eat your veggies? (They are helping you plan!)
  9. What is your favorite healthy snack?
  10. Who do you want to be when you grow up? How will you get there?
