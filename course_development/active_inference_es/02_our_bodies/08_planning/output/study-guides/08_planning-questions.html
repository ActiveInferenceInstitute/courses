Course — Module 08 — Think About It

  1. What would happen if you never brushed your teeth? (Cavities! Ouch!)
  2. Why do you have to go to school? (You are planning for when you are a grown-up!)
  3. If you want to run a marathon, what do you have to do today? (Practice running!)
  4. Why does junk food taste good but sometimes make us feel bad later?
  5. What time do you go to sleep? Is it easy to wake up in the morning?
  6. If you had a robot body, would you still need to sleep? (Yes, to recharge the battery!)
  7. Can you plan a surprise party? What steps do you need to take?
  8. Why do parents tell you to eat your veggies? (They are helping you plan for a healthy future!)
  9. What is your favorite healthy snack?
  10. Who do you want to be when you grow up? How will you get there?
  11. Create a "Future Me" poster. Draw yourself as a grown-up in the middle. Around you, draw the habits that will get you there.
  12. Design a healthy lunchbox. Draw what you would pack. Include at least three different colors of food!
  13. Make a sleep chart for one week. Write down when you go to bed and when you wake up. Do you notice a difference in how you feel?
  14. Tell a story about two kids: one who plans and one who does not. What happens to each of them on a rainy day?
  15. Draw the Strawberry Experiment: one kid eats a strawberry now (1 strawberry). Another kid plants the seeds and waits (10 strawberries later!).
  16. Plan a perfect Saturday. Draw a schedule from wake-up to bedtime. Include fun, food, and rest!
  17. Imagine you are a bear getting ready for winter. What is your plan? (Eat a lot! Find a cave! Sleep!)
  18. Interview a family member: "What is one healthy habit you wish you started earlier?" Write down their answer.
  19. Draw your body as a car. Label the fuel (food), the recharging station (sleep), and the exercise track (play!).
  20. Write a letter to Future You (5 years from now). Tell them what habits you are starting today to help them be happy and strong.
