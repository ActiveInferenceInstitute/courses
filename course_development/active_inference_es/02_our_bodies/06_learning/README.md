## Learning Goals

1. **Understand** that learning means building new paths in your brain through practice.
2. **Discover** that mistakes are good -- they help your brain fix its map.
3. **Experience** how hard it is to learn something new (mirror drawing!).
4. **Learn** about Muscle Memory -- when practice makes actions automatic.
5. **See** that everyone struggles at first, and that is perfectly okay.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Learning** | Building new paths in your brain so you can do things better |
| **Practice** | Doing something again and again to make the brain path clear |
| **Muscle Memory** | When you have practiced so much that your body moves without thinking |
| **Mistake** | A signal that helps your brain learn -- it means "fix the path!" |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module explores **Learning** as the strengthening of neural pathways through practice. The Wobbly Bike analogy shows that new skills feel shaky at first but become smooth with repetition. The mirror drawing lab provides a visceral experience of learning -- children will struggle because the mirror reverses their predictions, forcing the brain to build new paths. Emphasize that frustration is a normal part of learning. In Active Inference, learning means updating the model so that future predictions are more accurate.
