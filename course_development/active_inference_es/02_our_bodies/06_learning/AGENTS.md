# Station: Learning (Our Bodies)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Simple body science
- **Topics**: Learning
- **Lab Style**: Drawing & Coloring Lab
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Use the "Wobbly Bike" story to show that learning takes practice: Day 1 = wobbly, Day 3 = smooth.
- Present learning as building a path through tall grass -- the more you walk it, the clearer it gets.
- Introduce "Muscle Memory" -- after lots of practice, your brain moves muscles automatically.
- Celebrate mistakes: "When you fall, your brain says 'Oops! Surprise!' and fixes the map."

## Active Inference Integration

- **Learning** means updating model parameters to reduce future prediction errors. Practice strengthens neural pathways.
- For K-5: "Practice makes a clear Path in your brain. At first the path is overgrown, but each time you walk it, it gets easier."

## Lab Design Principles

- Labs should involve mirror drawing, non-dominant hand writing, or other "brain-rewiring" activities.
- Materials: paper, pencil, mirror.
- Focus on the struggle of learning something new and the gradual improvement.

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "What were you bad at before but are good at now?" "Does practice make perfect?"
- Questions 11-20: "Try the mirror drawing challenge." "Draw your brain path before and after practice."
