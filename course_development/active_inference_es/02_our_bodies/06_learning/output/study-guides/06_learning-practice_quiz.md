# Practice Quiz: Practice Power

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What helps the Brain build a strong path?
    A) Eating candy
    B) Sleeping all day
    C) Practice (doing it again and again)
    D) Yelling

    **Answer: C!** Great job! Just like walking through tall grass makes a clear trail, practice helps your brain build strong paths.

2. When you first learn something (like bike riding), how does it feel?
    A) Easy
    B) Wobbly and hard
    C) Boring
    D) Cold

    **Answer: B!** That's right! The first time is always wobbly because your brain has not built the path yet. But it gets better!

3. What happens when you make a mistake?
    A) Your Brain learns something new
    B) You fail forever
    C) Your ears fall off
    D) Nothing

    **Answer: A!** Exactly! Your brain says "Oops! Surprise!" and uses that mistake to fix its map. Mistakes are teachers!

4. What is "Muscle Memory"?
    A) Muscles have tiny brains
    B) Doing something automatically without thinking hard
    C) Forgetting how to walk
    D) Strong arms

    **Answer: B!** Nice work! After lots of practice, your brain moves your muscles automatically, like brushing your teeth without thinking about it.

5. If you want to be good at soccer, what should you do?
    A) Watch TV
    B) Practice kicking the ball every day
    C) Buy new shoes
    D) Eat pizza

    **Answer: B!** You got it! Practice is the secret to getting better. Every kick helps your brain's prediction get sharper.

6. Why is writing with your "Other Hand" hard?
    A) That hand is broken
    B) Your Brain hasn't built the path yet
    C) The pencil is heavy
    D) Because it is Tuesday

    **Answer: B!** That's right! Your brain has a strong path for your usual hand but not the other one. You would need lots of practice to build a new path.

7. Does everyone learn at the same speed?
    A) Yes, exactly the same
    B) No, everyone is different
    C) Only robots learn fast
    D) Nobody learns

    **Answer: B!** Exactly! Some people learn some things fast and other things slow. That is totally normal and okay!

8. What is like walking through tall grass again and again?
    A) Eating lunch
    B) Practice, because it makes a clear path in your brain
    C) Taking a bath
    D) Watching clouds

    **Answer: B!** Good thinking! Just like a trail gets clearer when you walk it every day, your brain's path gets stronger with practice.

9. On Day 1 of riding a bike, you are wobbly. On Day 3, you zoom! What happened?
    A) The bike got easier to ride
    B) Your brain built a new map for bike riding
    C) The ground got smoother
    D) Your shoes changed

    **Answer: B!** That's right! Your brain learned from each wobbly try and built a better and better map until riding felt smooth.

10. Why should you never give up when learning something new?
    A) Because your teacher said so
    B) Because every try helps your brain build a stronger path
    C) Because giving up makes you invisible
    D) Because there is a test tomorrow

    **Answer: B!** Awesome! Every single try, even the wobbly ones, helps your brain get better. Keep going!
