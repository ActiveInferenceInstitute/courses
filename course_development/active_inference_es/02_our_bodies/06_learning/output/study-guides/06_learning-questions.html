Course — Module 06 — Think About It

  1. What is something you represent really good at now, but were bad at before?
  2. Why do babies have to learn to walk? (Why aren't they born running?)
  3. Does practice make perfect? Or does practice make better?
  4. How does your brain feel when you are learning something really hard? (Tired? Frustrated?)
  5. Can you learn to wiggle your ears?
  6. If you stop practicing piano, what happens? (The path grows grass again!)
  7. Do you have to think about walking anymore? Why?
  8. Can you learn from watching someone else? (Mirror neurons!)
  9. What is the hardest thing you ever learned?
  10. Is it okay to cry when you make a mistake? (Yes! Then try again.)
