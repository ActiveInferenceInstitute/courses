# Course AGENTS: Our Bodies

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course README](./README.md) | [Curriculum AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Identity

- **Course**: Our Bodies
- **Number**: 2
- **Perspective**: Simple body science
- **Lab Type**: Drawing & Coloring Lab
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime. No formulas. Drawings and songs.

## Content Guidelines

- All concepts must be connected to **the human body** -- the heart as a pump, the brain as a captain, skin as a blanket.
- Use the metaphor of "Captain Brain and the Body Ship" throughout the course.
- Keep all language concrete and connected to things children can feel in their own bodies (heartbeat, breathing, blinking).
- Include physical activities: check your pulse, feel your muscles, test your senses.
- Every module should make children marvel at how amazing their own body is.

## Active Inference Integration

- **System** = "Your body is a team" (Heart, Lungs, Stomach, Brain working together)
- **Agent** = "You are the Captain of your Body Ship" (Brain as the decision-maker)
- **Perception** = "Your Five Spies bring clues to Captain Brain" (Senses as scouts)
- **Cognition** = "Your brain is a Prediction Machine" (Guessing what will happen next)
- **Action** = "Captain Brain pulls the Nerve Strings to move the Muscle Movers" (Motor control)
- **Learning** = "Practice makes the brain paths smooth" (The Wobbly Bike analogy)
- **Communication** = "Face Talk and Body Talk" (Non-verbal signals)
- **Planning** = "Taking care of Future You" (Healthy habits, sleep, food)

## Lab Design Principles

- Labs should involve **body-based activities**: checking heartbeat, mirror games, taste tests, drawing body maps.
- Materials: paper, crayons, mirrors, household objects for sensory testing.
- Every lab should connect back to the child's own body experience.
- Include reflection logs and simple tables for recording observations.

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: Body-based understanding questions ("Why does your heart beat faster when you run?")
- Questions 11-20: Creative activities involving drawing, acting out, and imagining ("Draw yourself as Captain Brain sailing the Body Ship")
- Keep all language warm and encouraging.

## Conventions

All modules in this course must:

1. Use language appropriate for Grades K-5.
2. Frame all concepts through the lens of simple body science.
3. Include drawing & coloring lab activities.
4. Adhere to notation standards in [../resources/notation_table.md](../resources/notation_table.md).
5. Cross-reference the shared [../resources/glossary.md](../resources/glossary.md).
