## Learning Goals

1. **Discover** that your brain is a Prediction Machine always asking "What happens next?"
2. **Learn** that your brain keeps a Memory Map built from everything you have experienced.
3. **Understand** the difference between a Good Prediction (matches reality) and Surprise (does not match).
4. **Practice** making predictions with patterns and probability games.
5. **Celebrate** surprise as the brain's way of saying "Time to learn!"

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cognition** | The thinking your brain does -- predicting, guessing, figuring out |
| **Prediction** | Your brain's guess about what will happen next |
| **Memory Map** | All the things your brain remembers, used to make predictions |
| **Surprise** | When what happens does not match what your brain predicted |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module presents **Cognition** as the brain's prediction-generating process. The brain uses a stored "Memory Map" (generative model) to anticipate what will happen next. When predictions match reality, the agent feels safe. When they do not match, surprise occurs, which triggers learning. The Magic Bag activity (predicting which color block you will pull out) gives children a hands-on experience of prediction and surprise. Discuss how babies stare at everything because everything is new and surprising to them.
