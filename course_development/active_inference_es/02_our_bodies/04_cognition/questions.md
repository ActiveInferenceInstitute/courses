# Course — Module 04 — Think About It

1. If I drop an egg, what do you predict will happen?
2. What would happen if gravity turned off? (Big surprise!)
3. Why do babies stare at everything? (Everything is a surprise!)
4. Can you predict what you will eat for dinner?
5. How do you know the sun will come up tomorrow? (Because it always does!)
6. If you see dark clouds, do you predict sunshine or rain?
7. Why is it hard to catch a fly? (Because the fly predicts your hand!)
8. Do you have "Muscle Memory"? (Like riding a bike without thinking?)
9. What is a "Surprise Party"? Does your brain like it?
10. Is dreaming a kind of thinking?
