Module 04: Cognition — The Prediction Machine

Learning Objectives

Understand that your Brain is a Prediction Machine.
Learn that "Thinking" means guessing what will happen next.
Know the difference between a Good Prediction and a Surprise.

Introduction: The Prediction Machine

Your Brain is like a super-fast computer. It is always asking: "What happens next?"

 If I drop this ball... Prediction: It will fall down!
 If I touch this hot stove... Prediction: Ouch!
 If I see a cloud... Prediction: Maybe rain?

Captain Brain uses these predictions to keep the Body Ship safe.

Key Concepts

The Model (Memory Map)

Your brain remembers everything that happened before.
It builds a map called a Generative Model.
"Last time I ate a lemon, it was sour."
"Next time I see a lemon, I predict... SOUR!"

Matching (Good Prediction)

If the ball falls down, your brain is happy.
"Yay! I was right! The world makes sense."

Surprise (Oops!)

If the ball floats UP... Whoa! Surprise!
Your brain wakes up! "That's weird! I need to learn!"
Surprise helps us learn new things.

Activities

Activity 1: The Magic Bag

Put 3 blue blocks and 1 red block in a bag.
Predict: If I pull one out, what color will it be?
(Probably Blue!)
Pull one out.
If it is Red... Surprise!
Your brain has to update its guess.

Activity 2: Pattern Power

Clap-Clap-Stomp. Clap-Clap-Stomp. Clap-Clap-...?
What comes next? (Stomp!)
Your brain learned the pattern and predicted the future!

Summary

Thinking is really just Predicting. Your Brain uses memories to guess the future. When you guess right, you are safe. When you are surprised, you learn something new!
