Course — Module 04 — Think About It

  1. If you drop an egg on the floor, what do you predict will happen?
  2. What would happen if gravity turned off? (That would be a big surprise!)
  3. Why do babies stare at everything? (Because everything is new and surprising!)
  4. Can you predict what you will eat for dinner tonight?
  5. How do you know the sun will come up tomorrow? (Because it always has before!)
  6. If you see dark clouds, do you predict sunshine or rain?
  7. Why is it hard to catch a fly? (Because the fly predicts where your hand is going!)
  8. Do you have "Muscle Memory"? Can you ride a bike without thinking about it?
  9. What is a "Surprise Party"? Does your brain like surprises or not?
  10. Is dreaming a kind of thinking? What is your brain doing when you dream?
  11. Play the Magic Bag game: put 3 blue blocks and 1 red block in a bag. Before you pull one out, predict: what color? Try it 10 times and keep score!
  12. Draw your brain's Memory Map. Include things you know for sure: "Fire is hot," "Ice cream is cold," "Dogs say woof."
  13. Finish these patterns by drawing what comes next: (A) Circle, Square, Circle, Square, ___ (B) Red, Red, Blue, Red, Red, ___
  14. Tell a story about a time your brain predicted something correctly. How did you know what would happen?
  15. Tell a story about a time your brain was completely surprised. What did you learn?
  16. Draw the Prediction Machine inside your head. Show it working: "Input: dark clouds. Prediction: rain! Output: grab umbrella."
  17. Shake a wrapped present. Is it heavy? Does it rattle? Draw what you predict is inside. Then open it and see!
  18. Imagine you are a baby seeing snow for the first time. Everything is surprising! Draw what a baby's face looks like seeing snow.
  19. Play "What Comes Next?" with a friend using clap patterns. Did your friend predict correctly?
  20. Write a story about a brain that lives inside a robot. The brain has to learn a new Memory Map for a planet it has never visited!
