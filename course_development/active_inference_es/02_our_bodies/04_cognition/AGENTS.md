# Station: Cognition (Our Bodies)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Simple body science
- **Topics**: Cognition
- **Lab Style**: Drawing & Coloring Lab
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Present the brain as a "Prediction Machine" that is always asking "What happens next?"
- Use the concept of a "Generative Model" as a "Memory Map" -- the brain's stored knowledge about how the world works.
- Show the difference between a Good Prediction (the ball falls down) and a Surprise (the ball floats up).
- Include pattern activities (clap-clap-stomp) and probability games (pulling blocks from a bag).

## Active Inference Integration

- **Cognition** is the brain's generative model generating predictions about expected sensory states.
- Surprise (prediction error) is the signal that something unexpected happened and the model needs updating.
- For K-5: "Your brain remembers everything. It builds a Memory Map. When something matches the map, you feel safe. When something surprises you, your brain wakes up!"

## Lab Design Principles

- Labs should involve prediction and pattern activities: pulling colored blocks from bags, finishing sequences.
- Materials: bags with colored blocks, pattern cards, objects to shake and guess.

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "What happens if you drop an egg?" "Why do babies stare at everything?"
- Questions 11-20: "Play the Magic Bag game and record your predictions." "Draw your brain's Memory Map."
