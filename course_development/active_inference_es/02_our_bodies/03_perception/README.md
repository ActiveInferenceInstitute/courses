## Learning Goals

1. **Identify** the Five Spies (Senses): Sight, Sound, Smell, Taste, and Touch.
2. **Understand** that your senses bring clues to Captain Brain, who puts the puzzle together.
3. **Discover** that perception means guessing what is "out there" based on clues.
4. **Experience** an illusion where your senses trick your brain.
5. **Play** the Mystery Box game and practice guessing with just your sense of touch.

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module teaches **Perception** as the brain's active inference process -- not passive reception but active guessing. The Five Spies metaphor shows that the brain sits "in the dark" and relies on sensory messengers for information about the world. The Mystery Box lab is an excellent hands-on way to experience this: children must guess objects using only touch, then discover whether their prediction was correct. Discuss illusions to show that perception is always an educated guess.
