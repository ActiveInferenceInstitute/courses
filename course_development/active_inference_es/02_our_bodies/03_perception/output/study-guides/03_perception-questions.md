# Course — Module 03 — Think About It

1. Which sense is your favorite? Why?
2. If you couldn't see, which sense would help you walk? (Touch? Hearing?)
3. Why do dogs have such big noses? (Super smell spies!)
4. Can your eyes trick you? (Have you ever seen a shadow that looked like a monster?)
5. Why does food taste bland when you have a cold?
6. How do bats see in the dark? (Ears!)
7. What kind of clues does your skin give you? (Hot, cold, sharp, soft...)
8. Do you think plants can "see"? (They turn to the light!)
9. Why do we have two ears? (To know where sound is coming from!)
10. If you hear a loud "BOOM," what does your brain guess happened? (Thunder? Fireworks?)
