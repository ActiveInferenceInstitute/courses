Practice Quiz: The Five Spies

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. Which spy helps you know if an apple is red? A) Agent Ear B) Agent Eye C) Agent Nose D) Agent Elbow

    Answer: B! Great job! Agent Eye sees colors like red, blue, and green and reports back to Captain Brain.

  2. Which spy helps you know if music is playing? A) Agent Eye B) Agent Tongue C) Agent Ear D) Agent Knee

    Answer: C! That's right! Agent Ear picks up sounds and brings them to Captain Brain as clues.

  3. What clues does Agent Skin bring? A) Colors (Red, Blue) B) Sounds (Loud, Quiet) C) Feelings (Hot, Cold, Soft, Rough) D) Smells (Stinky, Sweet)

    Answer: C! Exactly! Agent Skin tells you if something is hot, cold, soft, rough, smooth, or prickly.

  4. If you smell smoke, what does your brain guess? A) "Someone is baking cookies!" B) "There might be a fire!" C) "It is raining!" D) "Time to sleep!"

    Answer: B! Good thinking! Your brain uses the smell clue to make a guess that keeps you safe.

  5. What happens if you hold your nose while eating? A) Your eyes close B) You can't taste the flavor very well C) You turn purple D) Nothing

    Answer: B! That's right! Your nose and tongue work as a team. Without the nose, food does not taste as strong!

  6. Why does Captain Brain need clues? A) Because she is locked in the dark tower (skull) B) To win a prize C) To make a sandwich D) Because she is bored

    Answer: A! Exactly! Captain Brain cannot see the world directly. She relies on her five spies to bring her information.

  7. Can our senses sometimes trick us (Optical Illusion)? A) No, never B) Yes, sometimes C) Only on Tuesdays D) Only if you are a robot

    Answer: B! That's right! Sometimes the spies bring tricky clues, and the brain makes a wrong guess. That is called an illusion!

  8. In the story, all five spies worked together to figure out it was an apple. Which spy reported "Yum!"? A) Agent Eye B) Agent Ear C) Agent Nose D) Agent Tongue

    Answer: D! Nice work! Agent Tongue was the last spy to check, and it tasted the apple and said "Yum!"

  9. A stick in water looks bent, but it is really straight. Why? A) The stick is broken B) The water makes a tricky clue for your eyes, so your brain guesses wrong C) Someone bent the stick D) Water hates sticks

    Answer: B! Exactly! The water bends the light, so Agent Eye sends a tricky clue. Your brain's guess is wrong, but it is not your fault!

  10. If you hear "Meow" and see a grey tail, what does Captain Brain guess? A) A dog B) A cat C) A fish D) A robot

    Answer: B! You got it! Captain Brain puts the sound clue and the sight clue together and solves the mystery: it is a cat!

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  1. Draw a face and circle the Sense Organs (Eyes, Ears, Nose, Mouth).
  2. What is the loudest sound you ever heard? What made it?
  3. Imagine you have a "Sixth Sense." What would it do? (See through walls? Hear ants?)
  4. Try the Mystery Box game: close your eyes and touch something. Can you guess what it is using only Agent Skin (touch)?
  5. Which of the five spies do you think is the most important? Why?
