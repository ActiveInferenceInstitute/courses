Course — Module 03 — Think About It

  1. Which sense is your favorite? Why?
  2. If you could not see, which sense would help you walk around? (Touch? Hearing?)
  3. Why do dogs have such big noses? (They are super smell spies!)
  4. Can your eyes trick you? (Have you ever seen a shadow that looked like a monster?)
  5. Why does food taste bland when you have a cold? (Your Nose Spy is not working!)
  6. How do bats "see" in the dark? (They use their ears!)
  7. What kind of clues does your skin give you? (Hot, cold, sharp, soft, bumpy...)
  8. Do you think plants can "see"? (Hint: They turn toward the light!)
  9. Why do we have two ears instead of one? (To know where sounds come from!)
  10. If you hear a loud "BOOM," what does your brain guess happened? (Thunder? Fireworks? A balloon?)
  11. Draw the Five Spies as little characters. Give each one a name and a special skill. (Agent Eyes has big binoculars!)
  12. Play the Mystery Box game. Put three objects in a bag. Reach in without looking. Write down your guesses and whether you were right.
  13. Close your eyes and ask someone to put three different foods near your nose. Can you guess what they are just by smelling?
  14. Tell a story about a time your senses tricked you. What did you think was there? What was it really?
  15. Draw Captain Brain in the dark tower, and the Five Spies running to deliver their reports. What clues are they bringing?
  16. Make a "Sense Map" of your kitchen. What can you See? Hear? Smell? Touch? Taste?
  17. Imagine you are a mole underground. Which senses would you use the most? (Touch and smell!) Draw your mole using those senses.
  18. Try the Taste Test: close your eyes and hold your nose. Eat a jellybean. Can you taste the flavor? Now let go of your nose. Whoa! Write about what happened.
  19. Draw an optical illusion. (Two lines that look different lengths but are the same!) Show it to a friend. Did their brain get tricked?
  20. Write a short adventure story about the Five Spies going on a mission to solve a mystery: "What is that strange sound in the attic?"
