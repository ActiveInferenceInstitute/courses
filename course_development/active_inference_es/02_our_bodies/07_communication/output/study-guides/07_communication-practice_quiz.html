Practice Quiz: Face Talk

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is Communication? A) Making noise B) Sending a message to another mind C) Sleeping D) Eating lunch

    Answer: B! Great job! Communication is sending a message from your brain to someone else's brain, with or without words.

  2. If I smile, what am I saying? A) "I am angry!" B) "I am happy or friendly!" C) "I am a fish!" D) "Go away!"

    Answer: B! That's right! A smile sends the message "I am happy" or "I want to be friends!" Your face is talking!

  3. How do we talk without words? A) Using our Face and Body (Gestures) B) Telepathy C) Magic D) We can't

    Answer: A! Exactly! You can wave, point, smile, frown, and shrug. Your body sends messages all the time, even when you are quiet.

  4. If a dog growls, what is the message? A) "Pet me!" B) "I am dangerous/scared, stay back!" C) "Hello!" D) "I want a car."

    Answer: B! Good thinking! A growl is a dog's way of communicating "I am not happy, please give me space."

  5. Why do we point at things? A) To make our finger longer B) To show someone else where to look (Shared Attention) C) To poke the air D) No reason

    Answer: B! You got it! Pointing shares your attention with someone else. You are saying "Look over there!" without using words.

  6. What does a "Red Light" communicate to a car? A) "Go fast!" B) "Stop!" C) "Turn left!" D) "Dance!"

    Answer: B! That's right! A red light sends a clear message to everyone: "Stop moving!" Communication does not need words.

  7. Can we communicate with animals? A) No, never B) Yes, using signals they understand (like "Sit" or whistles) C) Only if they speak English D) Only on Sundays

    Answer: B! Exactly! You can teach a dog to sit using a hand signal. That is communication between different kinds of agents!

  8. What does it mean when someone crosses their arms and frowns? A) They are very happy B) They are probably upset or annoyed C) They are sleepy D) They want to play tag

    Answer: B! Nice work! Even without saying a word, crossed arms and a frown send the message "I am not happy right now."

  9. When you play Charades and act out an elephant (using your arm as a trunk), what are you doing? A) Exercising B) Communicating without words by using your body C) Being silly for no reason D) Pretending to be a tree

    Answer: B! That's right! In Charades, your body IS the message. You are communicating an idea without any words at all.

  10. Why is listening an important part of communication? A) It is not important B) Because communication goes BOTH ways -- sending AND receiving C) Because it makes your ears bigger D) Only teachers need to listen

    Answer: B! Exactly! Communication is not just about talking. You also need to listen and look to receive the other person's message.

  2. If you couldn't talk, how would you ask for water? (Show the gesture).
  3. Why is it important to listen to others?
  4. Make three different faces in the mirror: happy, sad, and surprised. How does each one send a different message?
