Course — Module 07 — Think About It

  1. Can you say "No" without speaking? (Shake your head!)
  2. How do dogs say "I am happy"? (Wag their tail!)
  3. How do cats say "Stay away"? (Hiss! Arch their back!)
  4. If someone is crying, what does that signal tell your brain?
  5. Why do we wave hello? (To signal "I see you! I am friendly!")
  6. Can you tell if someone is lying? (Sometimes their face looks funny or they look away.)
  7. Do babies communicate? How? (Crying, cooing, pointing, reaching.)
  8. What happens if you send the wrong signal? (A misunderstanding!)
  9. Why do traffic lights use colors (Red, Green) instead of shouting?
  10. Is a hug a kind of communication? What message does it send?
  11. Play Silent Charades: act out five words without talking. Can your friend guess them all? Write down the results.
  12. Draw five faces showing five different emotions: Happy, Sad, Angry, Surprised, Scared. What is different about each face?
  13. Stand in front of a mirror. Try to make a face that says "I am thinking really hard." What do your eyebrows do? Your mouth?
  14. Watch a movie or show with the sound OFF for one minute. Can you tell what the characters are feeling just from their bodies and faces?
  15. Make up five new hand signals and teach them to a friend. (Example: Touching your chin = "I am hungry.")
  16. Imagine you are a dog. How would you communicate "I want to play"? (Bow down, wag tail!) Draw it.
  17. Tell a story about a baby who cannot talk yet but needs to tell their parent something important. How does the baby communicate?
  18. Draw a comic strip where two characters communicate only through faces and gestures -- no speech bubbles allowed!
  19. Try communicating a message to someone across the room using only your face and hands. Did they understand?
  20. Write about a time you could tell how someone was feeling just by looking at them. What signals did you notice?
