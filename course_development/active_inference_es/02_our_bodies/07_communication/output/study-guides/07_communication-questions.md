# Course — Module 07 — Think About It

1. Can you say "No" without speaking? (Shake head!)
2. How do dogs say "I am happy"? (Wag tail!)
3. How do cats say "stay away"? (Hiss!)
4. If someone is crying, what does that signal mean?
5. Why do we wave hello? (To show "I see you!")
6. Can you tell if someone is lying? (Sometimes their face looks funny.)
7. Do babies communicate? How? (Crying, cooing, pointing.)
8. What happens if you send the wrong signal? (A misunderstanding!)
9. Why do traffic lights use colors (Red/Green) instead of shouting?
10. Is a hug a kind of communication?
