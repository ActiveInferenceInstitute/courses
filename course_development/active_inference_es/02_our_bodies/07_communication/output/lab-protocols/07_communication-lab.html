Lab: The Silent Actor

Goal

Send messages using only your Face and Body -- no words allowed!

What You Need

  • Paper slips with words to act out
  • A timer (phone or clock)
  • Friends or family to play with

Steps

1. Write the Clues

Write down these words on paper slips (or make up your own!):

  • Monkey
  • Sleeping
  • Playing Soccer
  • Eating Spaghetti
  • Brushing Teeth
  • Swimming
  • Being Really Cold
  • A Happy Puppy

2. Pick and Act

Pick a paper slip from the pile. Set the timer for 1 minute. Now act it out using your body and face!

  • Use your hands to show actions.
  • Use your face to show feelings.
  • Move your whole body.
  • NO SOUNDS! Not even "Hmm" or "Shhh"!

3. The Guess

Can your friends guess the word before time runs out? If they guess it, everyone cheers! If they cannot guess it, show them and laugh together.

4. Switch Turns

Everyone takes a turn being the Silent Actor. Who is the best at sending body messages?
