## Learning Goals

1. **Discover** that we communicate without words through faces and bodies.
2. **Identify** non-verbal signals: smiles, frowns, waves, points, shrugs.
3. **Understand** that other people's brains are always "reading" your body signals.
4. **Practice** sending messages using only your face and body in a charades game.
5. **See** how communication helps agents understand each other and work together.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Communication** | Sending a message from one brain to another |
| **Face Talk** | Using your face (smiles, frowns) to send messages |
| **Body Talk** | Using your body (waving, pointing) to send messages |
| **Signal** | Something you do that sends a message to another person's brain |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module introduces **non-verbal communication** as a rich source of social information. Children learn that they are constantly sending signals through facial expressions, body posture, and gestures. In Active Inference, social inference involves predicting others' internal states from observable signals. The Silent Actor (charades) lab gives children hands-on experience of how much they can communicate without any words at all. Discuss how babies communicate before they learn to talk.
