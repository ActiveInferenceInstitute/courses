# Station: Communication (Our Bodies)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Simple body science
- **Topics**: Communication
- **Lab Style**: Drawing & Coloring Lab
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Teach that we communicate without words through "Face Talk" (smiles, frowns) and "Body Talk" (waving, pointing).
- Captain Brain wants to tell others how it feels -- it uses the Face Agent and Hand Agent to send signals.
- Show that humans are always sending signals, even when quiet (slumped shoulders = sad, big eyes = scared).
- Include charades as a communication activity.

## Active Inference Integration

- **Communication** allows agents to share predictions and coordinate actions. Non-verbal signals are a rich source of social inference.
- For K-5: "Your face and body are always talking! A smile says 'I am happy,' and a frown says 'Something is wrong.' Other people's brains read these signals."

## Lab Design Principles

- Labs should involve silent charades: acting out words, emotions, and animals without speaking.
- Materials: paper slips with words, a timer.
- Focus on how much information the body can communicate without any words.

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "Can you say 'No' without speaking?" "How do dogs show they are happy?"
- Questions 11-20: "Play silent charades." "Draw five faces showing five different emotions."
