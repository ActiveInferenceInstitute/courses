# Practice Quiz: Face Talk

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is Communication?
    A) Making noise
    B) Sending a message to another mind
    C) Sleeping
    D) Eating lunch

2. If I smile, what am I saying?
    A) "I am angry!"
    B) "I am happy or friendly!"
    C) "I am a fish!"
    D) "Go away!"

3. How do we talk without words?
    A) Using our Face and Body (Gestures)
    B) Telepathy
    C) Magic
    D) We can't

4. If a dog growls, what is the message?
    A) "Pet me!"
    B) "I am dangerous/scared, stay back!"
    C) "Hello!"
    D) "I want a car."

5. Why do we point at things?
    A) To make our finger longer
    B) To show someone else where to look (Shared Attention)
    C) to poke the air
    D) No reason

6. What does a "Red Light" communicate to a car?
    A) "Go fast!"
    B) "Stop!"
    C) "Turn left!"
    D) "Dance!"

7. Can we communicate with animals?
    A) No, never
    B) Yes, using signals they understand (like "Sit" or whistles)
    C) Only if they speak English
    D) Only on Sundays

2. If you couldn't talk, how would you ask for water? (Show the gesture).
3. Why is it important to listen to others?
