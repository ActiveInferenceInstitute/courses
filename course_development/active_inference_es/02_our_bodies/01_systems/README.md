## Learning Goals

1. **Discover** that your body is a system made of many parts working together.
2. **Meet** the Body Team: Heart (Pump), Lungs (Bellows), Stomach (Mixer), Brain (Captain).
3. **Feel** your own heartbeat and notice how it changes when you move.
4. **Understand** that your skin is the "blanket" that keeps your insides safe.
5. **See** how all the body parts need each other to keep you healthy and happy.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module introduces the **body as a system** -- a team of organs that depend on each other. The key Active Inference concept is the **Markov Blanket**, presented as the skin that separates internal states from external states. Have children feel their own heartbeat before and after jumping jacks to experience how the system responds to changes. Ask: "Why did your heart go faster?" This builds intuition for how systems maintain themselves (homeostasis).
