# Station: Systems (Our Bodies)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Simple body science
- **Topics**: Systems
- **Lab Style**: Drawing & Coloring Lab
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Introduce the body as "The Body Team" with Heart (Pump), Lungs (Bellows), Stomach (Mixer), and Brain (Captain).
- Use the "Markov Blanket" concept as Skin -- the blanket that separates inside from outside.
- Connect to real body experiences: feeling your heartbeat, breathing, eating.
- Use sound effects and physical activities to make lessons interactive.

## Active Inference Integration

- The body is a **System** with internal states (organs, blood) and external states (the world outside the skin).
- The **Skin** acts as the Markov Blanket -- the boundary that defines where "you" end and the "world" begins.
- For K-5: "Your body is the most amazing system of all! The Heart, Lungs, Stomach, and Brain all work together inside your Skin blanket."

## Lab Design Principles

- Labs should involve body observation: checking heartbeat before and after exercise, feeling breathing.
- Materials: just the child's own body, paper, crayons for drawing.
- Activities should be physically active and multi-sensory.

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "Why does your heart beat faster when you run?" "What job does your skin do?"
- Questions 11-20: "Draw your body system and label the parts." "Act out being a heartbeat."
