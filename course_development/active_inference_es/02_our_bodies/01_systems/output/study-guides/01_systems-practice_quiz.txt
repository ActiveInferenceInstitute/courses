Practice Quiz: The Body Team

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

What is a System?
    A) A messy room
    B) Many parts working together as one
    C) A single rock
    D) A cloud

    Answer: B! Great job! A system is when many parts team up to work as one, like the parts of your body!

Which part is the "Pump"?
    A) The Lungs
    B) The Brain
    C) The Heart
    D) The Feet

    Answer: C! That's right! Your heart pumps blood all around your body, like a little pump that never stops.

What does your Skin do?
    A) Holds your insides IN and keeps dirt OUT
    B) Tastes food
    C) Pumps blood
    D) Makes music

    Answer: A! Exactly! Your skin is like a wall that protects everything inside you from the outside world.

Why do Lungs breathe faster when you run?
    A) They are singing
    B) To bring more air to the working muscles
    C) To cool down your teeth
    D) Because they are bored

    Answer: B! Nice thinking! When you run, your muscles need more air, so your lungs work faster to keep up. Teamwork!

Who is the "Captain" of the Body Team?
    A) The Stomach
    B) The Big Toe
    C) The Brain
    D) The Elbow

    Answer: C! You got it! The brain is the captain that tells all the other parts what to do.

Is a bicycle a system?
    A) Yes, it has parts (wheels, chain) that work together
    B) No, it is not alive
    C) Only if it is red
    D) No

    Answer: A! That's right! A bicycle has wheels, pedals, and a chain that all work together. It does not need to be alive to be a system!

What happens if one part of a system breaks (like a flat tire)?
    A) The whole system has trouble working
    B) Nothing
    C) It goes faster
    D) It turns into a bird

    Answer: A! Exactly! If one part breaks, the whole team has a problem. That is why every part matters.

What does the Stomach do for the Body Team?
    A) It helps you see
    B) It turns food into energy
    C) It helps you hear music
    D) It makes you taller

    Answer: B! Good thinking! Your stomach is the mixer that turns yummy food into energy so you can run and play.

What happens when your Heart, Lungs, and Legs all work together while you run?
    A) They fight with each other
    B) They take turns sleeping
    C) They team up so you can run fast and strong
    D) They stop working

    Answer: C! Awesome! Your legs move, your lungs breathe faster, and your heart pumps harder. That is a system working at full speed!

Your skin is called a "Markov Blanket." What does that mean in kid words?
    A) It is a warm blanket for sleeping
    B) It is the boundary that shows where "you" end and the "world" begins
    C) It is a type of towel
    D) It is a math problem

    Answer: B! That's right! Your skin wraps around you and keeps your insides safe. It is the edge of the "you" system!
