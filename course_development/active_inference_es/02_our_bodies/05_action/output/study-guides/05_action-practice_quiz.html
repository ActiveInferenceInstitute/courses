Practice Quiz: Muscle Movers

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. Who pulls the strings in your body? A) The Wind B) Captain Brain C) The Cat D) The Shoes

    Answer: B! Great job! Captain Brain is the puppeteer that sends signals down to your muscles to make you move.

  2. What are the "Strings"? A) Hair B) Nerves C) Spaghetti D) Bones

    Answer: B! That's right! Nerves are like long wires that carry messages from your brain to your muscles.

  3. What are the "Movers"? A) Muscles B) Teeth C) Ears D) Eyebrows

    Answer: A! Exactly! Muscles pull on your bones to make you move, jump, dance, and play.

  4. Why do we move? A) To change the world (get food, play) B) To be annoying C) Because gravity pushes us D) No reason

    Answer: A! Nice work! We move to reach our goals, like getting food, playing with friends, or staying safe.

  5. If you close your eyes and touch your nose, how do you know where your hand is? A) Magic B) Proprioception (Body Sense) C) You guessed D) You looked in a mirror

    Answer: B! You got it! Proprioception is your body's secret sense that tells your brain where all your parts are, even with your eyes closed.

  6. Which is an ACTION? A) Seeing a ball B) Hearing a bird C) Kicking a soccer ball D) Smelling pizza

    Answer: C! That's right! Kicking is an action because you use your body to change something in the world. Seeing, hearing, and smelling are perception.

  7. What happens if the "strings" (nerves) get cut? A) You can't move that part B) You move faster C) You turn blue D) It tickles

    Answer: A! Exactly! If the nerve is cut, the message from the brain cannot reach the muscle, so that part cannot move.

  8. How fast does a signal travel from your brain to your finger? A) Very slowly, like a turtle B) Super fast, like a zip! C) It takes about one hour D) It does not move at all

    Answer: B! Wow! The signal zooms from your brain, down your neck, down your arm, to your finger in a flash. That is called a Motor Command!

  9. What is the difference between Perception and Action? A) There is no difference B) Perception brings information IN; Action sends movement OUT C) Perception is louder D) Action happens only at night

    Answer: B! Smart thinking! Perception is the world coming to your brain, and action is your brain reaching out to change the world.

  10. Why can you brush your teeth while thinking about something else? A) Your teeth brush themselves B) Your brain built a path for that action, so it happens almost automatically C) Someone else is brushing for you D) Toothbrushes are magic

    Answer: B! That's right! After lots of practice, your brain makes a clear path so some actions feel easy and automatic.

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  2. Try to walk without bending your knees. Is it hard? Why?
  3. Draw a stick figure running. Show where adding muscles would help!
  4. Close your eyes and touch your elbow with your other hand. How did you know where it was? (That is proprioception!)
  5. If you were a puppet and Captain Brain was the puppeteer, what dance would you want Captain Brain to make you do?
