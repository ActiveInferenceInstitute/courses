Course — Module 05 — Think About It

  1. How fast can you run? What makes you go faster?
  2. Can you wiggle your ears? Why is it hard? (Not many muscle strings there!)
  3. What happens to your muscles if you use them a lot? (They get stronger!)
  4. Why do babies wobble when they walk? (They are still learning to pull the strings!)
  5. If you touch something hot, you pull away FAST. Did you think about it? (Reflex!)
  6. Can you move things with your mind (without touching them)? (No, you need Action!)
  7. Why do we shiver when we are cold? (Muscles moving to make heat!)
  8. How do birds fly? (Big chest muscles!)
  9. What is your favorite way to move? (Dance? Swim? Bike?)
  10. If you sit still all day, how does your body feel?
