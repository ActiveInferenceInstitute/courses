## Learning Goals

1. **Understand** how your brain moves your body: Brain sends signals through Nerves to Muscles.
2. **Learn** the marionette metaphor: Brain = Puppeteer, Nerves = Strings, Muscles = Movers.
3. **Discover** proprioception -- your body's hidden sense of where your parts are.
4. **Experience** the speed of your brain-to-body signal in a mirror game.
5. **See** how action changes the world to make your predictions come true.

| Word | What It Means |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module explains **Action** through the marionette metaphor: the brain sends motor commands through nerves to muscles, which pull bones to create movement. The Mirror, Mirror lab challenges children to perceive and act simultaneously, building awareness of the perception-action loop. The proprioception activity (touch your nose with eyes closed) introduces the "hidden sixth sense" that tells us where our body parts are. In Active Inference, action is how the agent changes the world to match its predictions.
