# Station: Action (Our Bodies)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Simple body science
- **Topics**: Action
- **Lab Style**: Drawing & Coloring Lab
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Present the body as a marionette: Brain = Puppeteer, Nerves = Strings, Muscles = Movers.
- Teach that action is how the brain sends signals through nerves to move muscles, which pull bones.
- Include proprioception (body sense): "Close your eyes and touch your nose -- how did you know where your hand was?"
- Use the String Puller and Mirror activities to make the concept physical.

## Active Inference Integration

- **Action** in Active Inference changes the world to match the brain's predictions. Motor commands flow from the brain through nerves to muscles.
- For K-5: "Captain Brain pulls the Nerve Strings, the Muscle Movers pull the Bones, and YOU move!"
- Connect to Perception (feeling your body move) and proprioception (knowing where your body parts are).

## Lab Design Principles

- Labs should involve physical movement: mirror games, string-pulling activities, reflexes.
- Materials: a partner, string and a toy car (optional).
- Focus on the speed and coordination of the brain-nerve-muscle system.

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "How fast can you run?" "What happens if nerves get hurt?"
- Questions 11-20: "Play Mirror, Mirror with a friend." "Draw the path of a signal from brain to finger."
