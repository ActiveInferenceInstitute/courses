# Lab: The Telephone Game

## Goal

See how hard it is to pass a message perfectly!

## What You Need

* A group of friends or family (at least 3 people)
* A secret sentence

## Steps

### 1. The Secret Message

One person (The Starter) thinks of a silly sentence.
Example: "The purple monkey eats pizza on the moon."

### 2. The Whisper

The Starter whispers the message into the ear of the next person.
**Rule**: You can only whisper it ONCE! No repeating!

### 3. Pass it On

The second person whispers what they *heard* to the third person.
And so on... until the last person.

### 4. The Reveal

The last person says the message out loud.
Was it right? Or did it change?
"The purple monkey eats pizza" might become "The turtle monkey heats feet."
