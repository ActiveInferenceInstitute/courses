# Course — Module 07 — Think About It

1. If Andy didn't leave a trail, what would have happened to the strawberry?
2. How do bees tell each other where flowers are? (Do you know about the waggle dance?)
3. Why do we need to talk? Can't we just guess what people want?
4. Can you communicate without making a sound? How?
5. If you speak English and your friend speaks Spanish, is it hard to share maps?
6. What does a red traffic light communicate to a driver?
7. Do dogs communicate? How? (Barking? Wagging tails?)
8. Why is drawings sometimes better than talking?
9. If you are sad, how do you communicate that to your mom?
10. What is the most important thing you ever told someone?
