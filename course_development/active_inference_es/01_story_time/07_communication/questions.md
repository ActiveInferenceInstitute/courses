# Course — Module 07 — Think About It

1. If Andy the Ant did not leave a scent trail, what would have happened to the strawberry?
2. How do bees tell each other where flowers are? (Do you know about the waggle dance?)
3. Why do we need to talk? Can we just guess what people want?
4. Can you communicate without making a sound? How? (Hint: waving, pointing, drawing!)
5. If you speak English and your friend speaks Spanish, is it hard to share your maps? Why?
6. What does a red traffic light communicate to a driver?
7. Do dogs communicate? How? (Barking? Wagging tails? Whining?)
8. Why is drawing sometimes better than talking for sending a message?
9. If you are sad, how do you communicate that to someone who cares about you?
10. What is the most important thing you have ever told someone?
11. Play "Telephone" with your family. Whisper a message to one person, they whisper it to the next. Did it change by the end? Draw what happened.
12. Sit back-to-back with a friend. Describe a simple picture (a snowman, a house) without showing it. Have your friend draw what you describe. How close was it?
13. Make up a secret code with a friend. (Example: pulling your ear means "Let's go outside!") Test it at school or at home.
14. Draw a picture that tells a story without any words. Can your friend understand it?
15. Imagine you are an ant. Draw the scent trail you would leave to tell your friends where the best picnic food is.
16. How many ways can you say "Hello" without using the word "Hello"? (Wave, smile, nod, high-five...) Draw each one.
17. Tell a story about two animals who need to work together but speak different "languages." How do they figure it out?
18. Draw a map of your house for someone who has never been there. This is communication through drawing!
19. Act out a feeling (happy, scared, excited) without talking. Can your friend guess the feeling?
20. Write a short message using only pictures (no words). Give it to a friend and see if they can read your picture-message.
