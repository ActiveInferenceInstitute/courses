# Practice Quiz: Communication

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is Communication?
    A) Eating lunch alone
    B) Sleeping
    C) Sharing information with others
    D) Being invisible

    **Answer: C!** Great job! Communication is how we send a message from our brain to someone else's brain.

2. How did Andy the Ant ask for help?
    A) He yelled "HELP!"
    B) He used a special smell trail
    C) He sent a text message
    D) He danced

    **Answer: B!** That's right! Andy left a scent trail that told the other ants "Yummy food this way!"

3. Why do we communicate?
    A) To confuse people
    B) To share our maps and work together
    C) To make noise
    D) To hide

    **Answer: B!** Exactly! When we share what we know, we help each other and can do bigger things together.

4. Which one is a way to communicate?
    A) Pointing a finger
    B) Writing a letter
    C) Talking
    D) All of the above!

    **Answer: D!** You got it! Pointing, writing, and talking are ALL ways to send messages to other people.

5. If you tell your friend "Watch out for the puddle!", what are you doing?
    A) Updating their map so they don't get wet
    B) Being mean
    C) Singing a song
    D) Predicting rain

    **Answer: A!** Nice work! You are sharing what you know so your friend can avoid the puddle. That is communication helping the team!

6. Can animals communicate?
    A) Yes, many ways (barks, smells, dances)
    B) No, only humans talk
    C) Only cats
    D) Only on Tuesdays

    **Answer: A!** That's right! Dogs bark, bees dance, and ants use smells. Animals have many cool ways to share information.

7. What happens when we work together (like the ants)?
    A) We get weaker
    B) We can do big things we couldn't do alone
    C) We get hungry
    D) We get lost

    **Answer: B!** Exactly! Andy could not carry the strawberry alone, but ten ants working together could carry it easily.

8. What is the strawberry too big for Andy to do alone?
    A) Eat
    B) Carry
    C) See
    D) Count

    **Answer: B!** That's right! The strawberry was too big for one little ant to carry, so Andy needed to communicate with his friends for help.

9. When you point at something, what are you doing?
    A) Exercising your finger
    B) Showing someone else where to look
    C) Being rude
    D) Nothing at all

    **Answer: B!** Good thinking! Pointing is a way of communicating without words. You are sharing your map with someone else.

10. What makes Andy and his ant friends a "Super Agent"?
    A) They wear capes
    B) They communicate and work together as a team
    C) They are really big
    D) They can fly

    **Answer: B!** Awesome! When agents communicate and help each other, they become much stronger than any one agent alone.
