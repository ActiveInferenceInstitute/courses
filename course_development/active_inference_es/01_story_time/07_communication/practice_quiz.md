# Practice Quiz: Communication

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is Communication?
    A) Eating lunch alone
    B) Sleeping
    C) Sharing information with others
    D) Being invisible

2. How did Andy the Ant ask for help?
    A) He yelled "HELP!"
    B) He used a special smell trail
    C) He sent a text message
    D) He danced

3. Why do we communicate?
    A) To confuse people
    B) To share our maps and work together
    C) To make noise
    D) To hide

4. Which one is a way to communicate?
    A) Pointing a finger
    B) Writing a letter
    C) Talking
    D) All of the above!

5. If you tell your friend "Watch out for the puddle!", what are you doing?
    A) Updating their map so they don't get wet
    B) Being mean
    C) Singing a song
    D) Predicting rain

6. Can animals communicate?
    A) Yes, many ways (barks, smells, dances)
    B) No, only humans talk
    C) Only cats
    D) Only on Tuesdays

7. What happens when we work together (like the ants)?
    A) We get weaker
    B) We can do big things we couldn't do alone
    C) We get hungry
    D) We get lost

## Part B: Tell Us What You Think

1. If you couldn't talk for a whole day, how would you ask for a glass of water? (Show me!)
2. Draw a picture of two friends talking. What are they saying?
3. Why is it important to listen when someone is communicating to you?
