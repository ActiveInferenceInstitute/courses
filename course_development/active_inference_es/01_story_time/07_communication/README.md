## Learning Goals

1. **Understand** that Communication means sending a message from one brain to another.
2. **Name** different ways we communicate: talking, pointing, drawing, writing.
3. **Discover** that animals communicate too -- ants use smells, bees dance!
4. **Experience** communication by playing a game where you describe something to a friend.
5. **See** how communication helps agents work together as a team.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Communication** | Sending a message from one brain to another |
| **Message** | The idea or information you want to share |
| **Signal** | The thing you use to send the message (words, pictures, smells) |
| **Team** | Agents working together by sharing messages |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module introduces **Communication** as the sharing of internal models between agents. The story of Andy the Ant shows how sharing information (the location of a strawberry) allows agents to coordinate and achieve goals they could not achieve alone. In Active Inference, communication aligns the generative models of different agents so they can act together. Encourage children to notice all the different ways they communicate every day -- not just words, but gestures, facial expressions, drawings, and even the way they arrange things.
