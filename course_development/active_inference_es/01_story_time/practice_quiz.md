# Practice Quiz: Story Time Review

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is a System?
    A) A single thing all by itself
    B) A group of parts that work together
    C) A pile of random stuff
    D) A nap

    **Answer: B!** Great job! A system is a team of parts working together to do something special, like the Forest Team!

2. What makes Squeaky the Squirrel an Agent?
    A) He has a fluffy tail
    B) He can sense, think, and make choices
    C) He likes acorns
    D) He lives in a tree

    **Answer: B!** That's right! An agent is something that senses the world and makes choices to reach a goal.

3. How did Oliver the Owl use Perception?
    A) He used his ears, eyes, and nose to figure out what was happening
    B) He read a book about it
    C) He asked a friend
    D) He stayed asleep

    **Answer: A!** Exactly! Oliver heard a snap, saw a shadow, and smelled blueberries. His senses brought him clues!

4. What does Cognition mean?
    A) Running really fast
    B) Thinking and making predictions
    C) Eating a sandwich
    D) Being invisible

    **Answer: B!** Nice work! Cognition is what your brain does when it thinks about what your senses are telling you and guesses what will happen next.

5. How did Benny the Beaver use Action?
    A) He wished for a pond
    B) He chomped trees and built a dam to change the river
    C) He took a nap by the water
    D) He sang a song to the river

    **Answer: B!** You got it! Benny did not just think about a pond. He took action and built one!

6. What did Beep-Boop learn from dropping the bowling ball?
    A) That all balls are the same
    B) That some balls are heavy and you should check first
    C) That bowling is his favorite sport
    D) That robots cannot lift things

    **Answer: B!** That's right! Beep-Boop was surprised and updated his map. That is learning!

7. How did Andy the Ant communicate with his friends?
    A) He sent a text message
    B) He left a scent trail that said "Yummy food this way!"
    C) He yelled really loud
    D) He wrote a letter

    **Answer: B!** Exactly! Ants use special smells to share information with each other. What a cool way to communicate!

8. Why did Nutty the Squirrel gather nuts instead of playing?
    A) He does not like playing
    B) He planned ahead so he would not be hungry in winter
    C) Someone told him to
    D) He wanted to count them

    **Answer: B!** Smart! Nutty used planning to think about the future and chose the path that kept him safe and warm.

9. Which story character showed that small actions can make big changes?
    A) Oliver the Owl
    B) Benny the Beaver
    C) Andy the Ant
    D) Sally

    **Answer: B!** That's right! Benny showed that many small actions (chomp, splash, push) added up to a whole dam that changed the river.

10. What do ALL the story characters have in common?
    A) They are all squirrels
    B) They all use their brains to sense, think, act, and learn
    C) They all live in the forest
    D) They all like nuts

    **Answer: B!** Wonderful! Whether it is an owl, a beaver, an ant, or a robot, they all sense the world, think about it, take action, and learn from what happens.
