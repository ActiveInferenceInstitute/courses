# Practice Quiz: Learning

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What helps us learn the most?
    A) Being perfect
    B) Making a mistake and being surprised
    C) Sleeping
    D) Eating candy

    **Answer: B!** Great job! When we are surprised, it means our brain got something wrong and now it can fix its map. That is learning!

2. What did Beep-Boop predict about the bowling ball?
    A) It was heavy
    B) It was light
    C) It was cheese
    D) It was hot

    **Answer: B!** That's right! Beep-Boop thought all balls were light, so he predicted the bowling ball would be light too.

3. What really happened (Surprise)?
    A) It was heavy!
    B) It floated away
    C) It tickled him
    D) It disappeared

    **Answer: A!** Exactly! The bowling ball was HEAVY and Beep-Boop fell over. What a surprise!

4. When you learn, what do you update?
    A) Your shoes
    B) The map in your head
    C) The weather
    D) Your friend

    **Answer: B!** Nice work! Your brain has a map of how the world works, and learning means fixing that map when something surprises you.

5. If you try to ride a bike and fall off, what should you do?
    A) Cry forever
    B) Never try again
    C) Learn from the mistake and try again
    D) Sell the bike

    **Answer: C!** You got it! Falling is just your brain learning what NOT to do. Try again and you will get better.

6. Is Surprise good for learning?
    A) Yes, it tells us our prediction was wrong
    B) No, surprise is scary
    C) Only on birthdays
    D) Surprise doesn't matter

    **Answer: A!** That's right! Surprise is like your brain's alarm clock saying "Wake up! There is something new to learn!"

7. Can robots learn?
    A) No
    B) Yes, if they can update their maps
    C) Only if they have legs
    D) Only if they eat batteries

    **Answer: B!** Smart thinking! Robots like Beep-Boop can learn by fixing the rules in their computer brains, just like we fix our maps.

8. What did Beep-Boop write on his new map after his mistake?
    A) "All balls are light"
    B) "Some balls are heavy. Check first!"
    C) "I do not like balls"
    D) "Bowling is scary"

    **Answer: B!** That's right! Beep-Boop updated his map so he would not make the same mistake again. That is learning!

9. Why does practicing sports or reading help you get better?
    A) Because your shoes get faster
    B) Because practice makes your brain's predictions better
    C) Because the book gets lighter
    D) Because the ball gets softer

    **Answer: B!** Exactly! Every time you practice, your brain's map gets a little better, and your predictions improve.

10. If you thought a box was empty but it was full of heavy books, how would you feel?
    A) Bored
    B) Surprised!
    C) Sleepy
    D) Invisible

    **Answer: B!** You would be surprised because your prediction was wrong. And now your brain knows: check before you lift!
