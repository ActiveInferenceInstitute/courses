Course — Module 06 — Think About It

  1. What is the last thing you learned how to do? (Tie shoes? Ride a bike? Read a word?)
  2. Why did Beep-Boop the Robot fall over when he tried to throw the bowling ball?
  3. Is it okay to make mistakes? Why?
  4. If you never made a mistake, would you ever learn anything new?
  5. What does your face look like when you are Surprised? (Show me!)
  6. How do babies learn to walk? (Do they fall down? Yes, a lot!)
  7. Can you learn to juggle by just watching? Or do you need to try and fail?
  8. What is "Surprise"? (Is it when something happens that you did not predict?)
  9. If you touch a stove and it is cold, are you surprised? Why?
  10. Who is the best learner you know? What makes them good at learning?
  11. Draw Beep-Boop the Robot before and after learning. Before: falling over with the bowling ball. After: checking if the ball is heavy first!
  12. Think of something you are really good at now but were bad at first. Draw three pictures: (1) First try, (2) Practicing, (3) Got it!
  13. Tell a story about a time you were surprised. What did you learn from it?
  14. Make a "Mistake Museum." Draw three of your biggest mistakes and what you learned from each one.
  15. Play the Heavy Box Trick from the story. Find two containers -- put something heavy in one and something light in the other. Watch your friend's face when they lift the heavy one!
  16. Draw your brain's map before and after learning something new. (Example: Before: "All dogs are big." After: "Some dogs are tiny!")
  17. Imagine you are a baby bird learning to fly. Tell the story of your first flight. Did you fall? Did you try again?
  18. Act out being Beep-Boop. Pretend to lift something you thought was light but it is really heavy! Show the surprise on your face.
  19. Draw a comic strip showing: (1) You predict something, (2) You are surprised, (3) You update your brain's map.
  20. Write a letter to Beep-Boop the Robot giving him advice about making mistakes. Tell him why mistakes are actually great!
