# Station: Learning (Story Time)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Narrative & imagination
- **Topics**: Learning
- **Lab Style**: Storytime Activity
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Introduce Learning through **The Little Robot's Mistake** -- Beep-Boop tries to throw a bowling ball and falls over because his map was wrong.
- Emphasize that Learning = "fixing the map in your head" when you are surprised.
- Make mistakes feel positive and exciting: "Surprise is the teacher!"
- Show that practice is just doing the same thing over and over until the brain's map gets better.

## Active Inference Integration

- **Learning** in Active Inference is the process of updating the generative model (the brain's map) to reduce prediction error. When predictions fail (surprise), the model parameters are adjusted.
- For K-5: "When something surprises you, your brain says 'Oops! My map was wrong!' and fixes it. That is Learning!"
- Connect to Cognition (the map that needs updating) and Surprise (the signal that triggers learning).

## Lab Design Principles

- Learning labs should involve **surprise and discovery** -- heavy boxes that look light, drawing from memory.
- Materials: two identical boxes (one heavy, one light), toys, paper, pencils.
- Children should experience the moment of surprise when their prediction is wrong.
- Reflection should ask: "What surprised you? How did you fix your guess?"

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "What is the last thing you learned?" "Why did Beep-Boop fall over?"
- Questions 11-20: "Draw yourself learning something new." "Tell a story about a mistake that taught you something."
- Celebrate mistakes as learning opportunities.
