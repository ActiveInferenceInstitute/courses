## Learning Goals

1. **Understand** that Learning means fixing the map in your head when you are surprised.
2. **Discover** that mistakes and surprises are the best teachers.
3. **See** how practice makes your brain's predictions better and faster.
4. **Experience** surprise by trying an activity where your prediction is wrong.
5. **Celebrate** mistakes as chances to learn something new!

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Learning** | Fixing the map in your head so you can guess better next time |
| **Surprise** | When something happens that you did not expect -- your brain says "Oops!" |
| **Mistake** | Getting something wrong -- but it helps your brain learn! |
| **Practice** | Doing something again and again so your brain gets better at it |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module introduces **Learning** as the process of updating the brain's internal map when predictions fail. The story of Beep-Boop the Robot shows that surprise (prediction error) is the signal that drives learning. When Beep-Boop tries to throw a bowling ball like a regular ball, he falls over -- and then updates his map: "Some balls are heavy!" In Active Inference, learning means adjusting model parameters to reduce future prediction errors. Help children see mistakes as positive: every "Oops!" is the brain getting smarter.
