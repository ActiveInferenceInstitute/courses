Practice Quiz: Agents

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is an Agent? A) Something that sits still forever B) Something that makes choices to reach a goal C) A rock D) The wind

    Answer: B! Great job! An agent can feel the world and make choices about what to do next.

  2. What was Squeaky's big choice? A) To fly or swim B) To eat the nut or hide it C) To paint a picture D) To sing a song

    Answer: B! That's right! Squeaky had to decide: eat the nut now, or save it for winter.

  3. Which one is an Agent? A) A playful puppy B) A brick C) A spoon D) A puddle

    Answer: A! You got it! A puppy can sense things and make choices, like running to get a treat.

  4. A Goal is: A) Something you want to happen B) Something you hate C) A funny hat D) A scary monster

    Answer: A! Nice work! A goal is what you are trying to do, like finding food or getting to the park.

  5. If your goal is "Clean Room," what is a good Action? A) Throw toys on the floor B) Pick up toys and put them in the box C) Eat a cookie D) Take a nap

    Answer: B! Exactly! Picking up toys is the action that helps you reach the "Clean Room" goal.

  6. Can a robot be an agent? A) No, never B) Yes, if it can sense things and make choices C) Only if it has green eyes D) Only if it eats nuts

    Answer: B! That's right! A robot that can sense and choose is an agent, just like you.

  7. Why did Squeaky hide the nut? A) He didn't like it B) He wanted to save it for winter safety C) He lost it D) He was full

    Answer: B! Smart thinking! Squeaky chose to save the nut so he would have food when winter came.

  8. What makes a rock different from an agent? A) A rock is round B) A rock cannot make choices C) A rock is heavier than a squirrel D) A rock lives outside

    Answer: B! Exactly! If you roll a rock down a hill, it cannot choose to stop. Agents choose, rocks do not.

  9. What did Squeaky use to dig the hole for his nut? A) A shovel from the store B) His paws C) A magic wand D) A spoon

    Answer: B! That's right! Squeaky used his paws to dig. His body helped him take action to reach his goal.

  10. Which of these is an agent with a goal? A) A soccer ball sitting on the grass B) A chair in the classroom C) A bird building a nest D) A crayon in a box

    Answer: C! Wonderful! A bird has the goal of making a cozy home, and it acts by gathering sticks and twigs.
