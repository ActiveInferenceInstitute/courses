# Practice Quiz: Agents

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is an Agent?
    A) Something that sits still forever
    B) Something that makes choices to reach a goal
    C) A rock
    D) The wind

2. What was Squeaky's big choice?
    A) To fly or swim
    B) To eat the nut or hide it
    C) To paint a picture
    D) To sing a song

3. Which one is an Agent?
    A) A playful puppy
    B) A brick
    C) A spoon
    D) A puddle

4. A Goal is:
    A) Something you want to happen
    B) Something you hate
    C) A funny hat
    D) A scary monster

5. If your goal is "Clean Room," what is a good Action?
    A) Throw toys on the floor
    B) Pick up toys and put them in the box
    C) Eat a cookie
    D) Take a nap

6. Can a robot be an agent?
    A) No, never
    B) Yes, if it can sense things and make choices
    C) Only if it has green eyes
    D) Only if it eats nuts

7. Why did Squeaky hide the nut?
    A) He didn't like it
    B) He wanted to save it for winter safety
    C) He lost it
    D) He was full

## Part B: Tell Us What You Think

1. Imagine you are a Superhero Agent. What is your goal?
2. What action would you take to save the day?
3. Draw a picture of an agent and a non-agent (object).
