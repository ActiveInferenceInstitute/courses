Lecture Content

Module 02: Agents — Squeaky's Big Choice

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand what an Agent is (something that makes choices).
  2. Learn that agents have Goals (what they want).
  3. See how agents use Actions to reach their goals.

Introduction: The Big Green Nut

Squeaky the Squirrel found a giant, green nut. It was the biggest nut he had ever seen!

His tummy rumbled. Grrrrr.

"Oh boy!" said Squeaky. "I have a Goal. I want my tummy to stop rumbling."

Squeaky had to make a Choice.

  1. He could Eat the nut right now.
  2. He could Hide the nut for winter.

If he ate it now, his tummy would be full, but he might be hungry later. If he hid it, he would be hungry now, but safe for winter.

Squeaky thought hard. He looked at the sky. It was getting cold. He decided to Hide the nut. He used his paws to dig a hole. Scritch, scratch, pat, pat.

Squeaky is an Agent because he felt something (hunger), thought about it, and made a choice!

Key Concepts

1. What is an Agent?

An Agent is something that can feel the world and make choices.

  • You are an agent.
  • Squeaky is an agent.
  • A Robot can be an agent.

A rock is not an agent. If you roll a rock down a hill, it can't choose to stop. It just rolls!

2. Goals (What We Want)

Agents have Goals. A goal is something you want to happen.

  • Squeaky's Goal: Stay full and safe.
  • Your Goal: Have fun, learn, eat tasty snacks.
  • Soccer Player's Goal: Kick the ball into the net.

3. Actions (What We Do)

To reach a goal, agents take Actions.

  • Goal: Get to the other side of the playground.
  • Action: Run! Or Skip! Or Walk!

Squeaky's Goal was "Winter Safety," so his Action was "Dig a Hole."

Activities

Activity 1: Agent or Object?

I will say a word. If it's an Agent (makes choices), jump up! If it's just an Object (no choices), crouch down like a rock.

  • A Cat! (Jump!)
  • A Pillow! (Crouch!)
  • A Teacher! (Jump!)
  • A Shoe! (Crouch!)

Activity 2: The Goal Game

Imagine you are a hungry bear. What is your goal? (Find honey!) What action do you take? (Sniff for bees!)

Summary

An Agent is something that acts to reach a Goal. Unlike a rock or a shoe, an agent senses the world and chooses what to do next. You are a very special agent because you can choose to do kind and amazing things!

References

  • Don't Let the Pigeon Drive the Bus! by Mo Willems (A story about a pigeon with a goal!)

Lab Protocol

Lab: The Maze Game

Goal

Help a paper agent find its way to a yummy treat at the end of a maze!

What You Need

  • Paper (big piece is best!)
  • Pencils or markers
  • A small toy, button, or coin (this is your Agent)
  • Crayons to decorate

Steps

1. Draw a Maze

Draw a simple maze on your paper.

  • Draw a big Start circle on one side. Write "START" inside it.
  • Draw a Goal star on the other side. Draw something yummy there (a cookie, a strawberry, a pizza slice!).
  • Draw walls (thick lines) that the agent cannot walk through.
  • Make sure there is at least one path from Start to Goal!

2. Name Your Agent

Give your toy a name and a goal. (Example: "This is Nibbles the Mouse. Her goal is to reach the cheese!")

3. Be the Agent

Put your toy on the Start circle. You are now the Agent's brain! Move your toy slowly along the paths.

  • "Oh no! A wall!" -> Think: "Should I turn left or right?"
  • "The path is clear!" -> Action: Move forward!
  • "A dead end!" -> Surprise! Turn around and try a different way.

4. Reach the Goal

When your toy reaches the star, cheer! "Nibbles made it to the cheese!" Count how many turns you had to make.

5. Try Again

Can you find a shorter path? Move your agent again and see if there is a faster way!

Practice Quiz

Practice Quiz: Agents

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is an Agent? A) Something that sits still forever B) Something that makes choices to reach a goal C) A rock D) The wind

    Answer: B! Great job! An agent can feel the world and make choices about what to do next.

  2. What was Squeaky's big choice? A) To fly or swim B) To eat the nut or hide it C) To paint a picture D) To sing a song

    Answer: B! That's right! Squeaky had to decide: eat the nut now, or save it for winter.

  3. Which one is an Agent? A) A playful puppy B) A brick C) A spoon D) A puddle

    Answer: A! You got it! A puppy can sense things and make choices, like running to get a treat.

  4. A Goal is: A) Something you want to happen B) Something you hate C) A funny hat D) A scary monster

    Answer: A! Nice work! A goal is what you are trying to do, like finding food or getting to the park.

  5. If your goal is "Clean Room," what is a good Action? A) Throw toys on the floor B) Pick up toys and put them in the box C) Eat a cookie D) Take a nap

    Answer: B! Exactly! Picking up toys is the action that helps you reach the "Clean Room" goal.

  6. Can a robot be an agent? A) No, never B) Yes, if it can sense things and make choices C) Only if it has green eyes D) Only if it eats nuts

    Answer: B! That's right! A robot that can sense and choose is an agent, just like you.

  7. Why did Squeaky hide the nut? A) He didn't like it B) He wanted to save it for winter safety C) He lost it D) He was full

    Answer: B! Smart thinking! Squeaky chose to save the nut so he would have food when winter came.

  8. What makes a rock different from an agent? A) A rock is round B) A rock cannot make choices C) A rock is heavier than a squirrel D) A rock lives outside

    Answer: B! Exactly! If you roll a rock down a hill, it cannot choose to stop. Agents choose, rocks do not.

  9. What did Squeaky use to dig the hole for his nut? A) A shovel from the store B) His paws C) A magic wand D) A spoon

    Answer: B! That's right! Squeaky used his paws to dig. His body helped him take action to reach his goal.

  10. Which of these is an agent with a goal? A) A soccer ball sitting on the grass B) A chair in the classroom C) A bird building a nest D) A crayon in a box

    Answer: C! Wonderful! A bird has the goal of making a cozy home, and it acts by gathering sticks and twigs.

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  1. Imagine you are a Superhero Agent. What is your goal?
  2. What action would you take to save the day?
  3. Draw a picture of an agent and a non-agent (object).
  4. Think about your pet (or an animal you know). What is one goal it has? What action does it take to reach that goal?
  5. If Squeaky had eaten the nut right away instead of hiding it, what do you think would have happened in winter?
