# Station: Agents (Story Time)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Narrative & imagination
- **Topics**: Agents
- **Lab Style**: Storytime Activity
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Introduce Agents through **Squeaky's Big Choice** -- a squirrel who finds a nut and must decide what to do.
- Emphasize that an Agent is "something that makes choices," unlike a rock or a shoe.
- Use the "Agent or Object?" jumping game as a recurring activity pattern.
- Goals should be presented as "what you want" -- simple desires like food, warmth, fun.

## Active Inference Integration

- An **Agent** is a system that minimizes surprise by acting on the world. In Active Inference, agents have goals (preferred states) and take actions to reach them.
- For K-5: "An Agent is something that feels the world and makes choices. You are an Agent! A rock is not."
- Connect to Perception (how agents sense) and Action (how agents do things).

## Lab Design Principles

- The Agents lab should involve **maze navigation** -- a paper maze where children guide a toy "agent" to a goal.
- Materials: paper, pencils, a small toy or button.
- Children experience decision-making: "Which way should I go? Left or right?"
- Reflection should ask: "Did your agent reach its goal? Did it have to change its plan?"

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "Is a cat an agent? Why?" "What is Squeaky's goal?"
- Questions 11-20: "Draw your own agent character." "Act out being an agent with a goal."
- Focus on the difference between things that choose and things that don't.
