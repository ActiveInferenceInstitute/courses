Lab: The Mystery Box

Goal

Use your sense of Touch to guess what is inside a box differently than your eyes!

What You Need

  • A box (shoe box or tissue box)
  • A towel or blanket to cover it
  • Secret objects (spoon, pinecone, cotton ball, rock, toy car)

Steps

1. Hide the Objects

Ask a friend or teacher to put one secret object in the box and cover it. No peeking!

2. Reach and Feel

Put your hand inside. Don't look! Use your fingers.

  • Is it hard or soft?
  • Is it bumpy or smooth?
  • Is it cold or warm?

3. Make a Guess

Tell your brain what you feel. "It feels round... and smooth... and heavy. I guess it is an Apple!"

4. Check

Pull it out. Were you right? Did your senses tell you the truth?
