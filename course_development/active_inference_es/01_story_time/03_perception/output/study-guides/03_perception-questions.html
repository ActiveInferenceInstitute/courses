Course — Module 03 — Think About It

  1. What is your favorite smell?
  2. What sound wakes you up in the morning?
  3. If you were an bat, which sense would you use the most? (Hearing!)
  4. Can you taste a color?
  5. Why do we need to feel pain? (Is it a warning?)
  6. Close your eyes. Can you still tell where you are? How?
  7. What does "loud" feel like?
  8. Do clouds make a sound?
  9. If you see a tail wagging behind a bush, what does your brain guess is there?
  10. What is the softest thing you have ever touched?
