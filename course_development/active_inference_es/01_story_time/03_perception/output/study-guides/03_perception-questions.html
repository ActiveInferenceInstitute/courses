Course — Module 03 — Think About It

  1. What is your favorite smell? Why do you like it?
  2. What sound wakes you up in the morning?
  3. If you were a bat, which sense would you use the most? (Hint: Hearing!)
  4. Can you taste a color? Why or why not?
  5. Why do we need to feel pain? (Is it like a warning alarm?)
  6. Close your eyes. Can you still tell where you are? How do you know?
  7. What does "loud" feel like? Does your whole body notice it?
  8. If you see a tail wagging behind a bush, what does your brain guess is there?
  9. What is the softest thing you have ever touched?
  10. How did Oliver the Owl figure out it was a bear and not a monster?
  11. Draw a picture of your favorite food. Now draw all the senses you use to enjoy it: What does it look like? Smell like? Taste like? Sound like when you bite it? Feel like in your hands?
  12. Play "I Spy" with a friend. Write down three things you spied and the clues you gave.
  13. Close your eyes and have someone make three sounds. Draw a picture of what you think made each sound.
  14. Tell a story about a time your eyes tricked you. Maybe a shadow looked like something scary!
  15. Draw Oliver the Owl using all his senses in the dark forest. Label each sense he is using.
  16. Make a "Sense Chart" for your bedroom. What can you See? Hear? Smell? Touch? (Probably nothing to taste!)
  17. Imagine you are a deep-sea fish. It is very dark! Which senses would you use the most? Draw your fish using those senses.
  18. Act out being Oliver the Owl. Close your eyes, listen for sounds, sniff the air. What do you notice?
  19. Draw two pictures: "What I thought it was" and "What it really was." (Example: I thought the shadow was a monster, but it was just my backpack!)
  20. Write a short poem about your five senses. Start with: "My eyes can see, my ears can hear..."
