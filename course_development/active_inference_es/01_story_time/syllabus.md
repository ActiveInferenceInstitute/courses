# Syllabus: Story Time

> **Active Inference for Elementary School** | **Audience**: Grades K-5

## Course Description

Story Time explores Active Inference through narrative & imagination. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn.

## Schedule

| Week | Module | Topic | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | [Systems](./01_systems/module.md) | Your World is Full of Systems! | Storytime Activity |
| Week 2 | [Agents](./02_agents/module.md) | What Makes You... You? | Storytime Activity |
| Week 3 | [Perception](./03_perception/module.md) | The Surprise Monster | Storytime Activity |
| Week 4 | [Cognition](./04_cognition/module.md) | Your Brain's Best Guess | Storytime Activity |
