# Lab: Action Charades

## Goal

Use Action to show a word without speaking!

## What You Need

* Paper slips with action words written on them (Run, Sleep, Eat, Brush Teeth, Fly)
* A bowl to hold the slips
* Friends or family to guess

## Steps

### 1. Pick a Secret Action

Reach into the bowl and pick one paper slip. Don't show anyone!
(Example: "Brush Teeth")

### 2. Plan Your Action

Think: "How can I show this using only my body? I need to move my hand up and down near my mouth."

### 3. Take Action

Do the movement! No talking! Use your whole body to show the word.
Make your movements big and clear so your friends can see what you are doing.

### 4. Friend's Perception

Your friend watches (Perception) and guesses (Cognition).
"Are you eating corn?" -> "No."
"Are you brushing your teeth?" -> "YES!"

### 5. Switch Turns

Now your friend picks a slip and acts it out. Can you guess what they are doing?
Keep playing until all the slips are used up!
