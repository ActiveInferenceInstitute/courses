Course — Module 05 — Think About It

  1. What is your favorite action? (Running? Drawing? Sleeping? Dancing?)
  2. If Benny the Beaver just thought about a pond but never built a dam, would the river change?
  3. How does action change the world? Can you give an example?
  4. Can you change how you feel by taking an action? (Like hugging a teddy bear when you are sad?)
  5. What action do you take when you are thirsty?
  6. Is "listening" an action? Why or why not?
  7. How is Action different from Perception? (Hint: one is going OUT, one is coming IN.)
  8. If you want to be a fast runner, what actions do you need to take?
  9. Can a plant take action? (Do sunflowers move toward the sun?)
  10. What is the smallest action you can do? (Blink! Wiggle a finger!)
  11. Draw a before-and-after picture. Before: a messy room. After: you cleaned it! The action was cleaning.
  12. Act out three actions without talking. Can your friend guess all three?
  13. Draw Benny the Beaver building his dam. Show the river before (fast and loud) and after (a quiet pond).
  14. Imagine you have magic hands that can build anything. What would you build? Draw it and explain the actions you would take.
  15. Make a list of five actions you did today. Next to each one, write what changed because of it.
  16. Tell a story about a caterpillar who takes action to become a butterfly. What steps does it take?
  17. Play "Simon Says" with your family. Write down three commands you gave as "Simon."
  18. Draw a superhero whose power is ACTION. What can they do? How do they change the world?
  19. Imagine you are Benny the Beaver. But instead of a dam, you want to build a bridge. What actions do you take? Draw your plan.
  20. Write a short adventure story where the hero has to take three different actions to save the day. What were the actions?
