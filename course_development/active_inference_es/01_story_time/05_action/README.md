## Learning Goals

1. **Understand** that Action means doing something to change the world (or yourself).
2. **See** the difference between thinking about something and actually doing it.
3. **Discover** that actions can make your predictions come true (like Benny building a dam!).
4. **Practice** using your body to communicate through Action Charades.
5. **Notice** how every action changes something -- even a tiny action like blinking!

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Doing something to change the world or yourself |
| **Change** | When something becomes different because of an action |
| **Prediction** | What you think will happen -- action can make it come true! |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module introduces **Action** as the way agents change the world to match their predictions. The story of Benny the Beaver shows that just thinking about a quiet pond does not create one -- you have to take action and build a dam! In Active Inference, action is one of the two main ways to reduce surprise: you can either update your beliefs (perception) or change the world (action). Encourage children to notice their own actions throughout the day and ask: "What did I just change?"
