# Lab: Story Time Adventure Book

## Goal

Create your very own story about a character who uses all the Active Inference ideas we learned: Systems, Agents, Perception, Cognition, Action, Learning, Communication, and Planning!

## What You Need

* 4 sheets of paper (folded in half to make a little book)
* Crayons, markers, or colored pencils
* Stapler or tape to hold the book together
* Your imagination!

## Steps

### 1. Pick Your Hero

Think of a character for your story. It could be an animal, a robot, a kid, or something totally made up!
(Example: "Sunny the Sunflower" or "Zip the Space Dog")

### 2. Give Your Hero a Goal

What does your character want?
"Sunny wants to find water because she is thirsty."
"Zip wants to find his way home to his space station."

### 3. Draw Each Page

On each page of your book, draw your hero doing one of these things:

* **Page 1**: Your hero in their System (where they live, who is around them)
* **Page 2**: Your hero notices something with their senses (Perception)
* **Page 3**: Your hero thinks about what to do (Cognition)
* **Page 4**: Your hero does something (Action)
* **Page 5**: Something surprising happens! Your hero learns (Learning)
* **Page 6**: Your hero tells a friend what happened (Communication)
* **Page 7**: Your hero makes a plan for next time (Planning)
* **Page 8**: The happy ending!

### 4. Share Your Story

Read your book out loud to a friend or family member. Use funny voices!
