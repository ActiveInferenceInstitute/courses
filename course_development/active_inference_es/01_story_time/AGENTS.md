# Course AGENTS: Story Time

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course README](./README.md) | [Curriculum AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Identity

- **Course**: Story Time
- **Number**: 1
- **Perspective**: Narrative & imagination
- **Lab Type**: Storytime Activity
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime. No formulas. Drawings and songs.

## Content Guidelines

- All concepts must be introduced through **stories with named characters** (e.g., Squeaky the Squirrel, Oliver the Owl, Benny the Beaver).
- Use dialogue, sound effects (*Snap! Crunch!*), and sensory language to bring ideas to life.
- Keep sentences short (8-12 words on average). Avoid jargon.
- Use repetition and call-and-response patterns to reinforce key ideas.
- Every module should feel like a bedtime story that also teaches something real about how brains work.

## Active Inference Integration

- **System** = "A team that works together" (Forest Team, Body Team)
- **Agent** = "Something that makes choices" (Squeaky choosing to hide the nut)
- **Perception** = "Noticing with your senses" (Oliver hearing a sound)
- **Cognition** = "Thinking and guessing" (Sally predicting rain)
- **Action** = "Doing something to change the world" (Benny building a dam)
- **Learning** = "Fixing your brain's map when surprised" (Beep-Boop and the bowling ball)
- **Communication** = "Sharing your map with others" (Andy the Ant's scent trail)
- **Planning** = "Visiting the future in your head" (Nutty gathering nuts for winter)

## Lab Design Principles

- Labs should be playful and require only household materials (paper, crayons, small toys).
- Every lab must include a creative or imaginative element (drawing, storytelling, acting).
- Instructions should use conversational language: "Now it's your turn!" rather than "Complete the following task."
- Include a reflection log with simple questions and fill-in areas.

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: Understanding questions phrased in simple, wonder-filled language ("Can you find...?" "What would happen if...?")
- Questions 11-20: Creative activity questions (draw, imagine, tell a story, act out)
- Avoid the word "analyze." Use "think about," "imagine," or "what do you notice?"

## Conventions

All modules in this course must:

1. Use language appropriate for Grades K-5.
2. Frame all concepts through the lens of narrative & imagination.
3. Include storytime activity activities.
4. Adhere to notation standards in [../resources/notation_table.md](../resources/notation_table.md).
5. Cross-reference the shared [../resources/glossary.md](../resources/glossary.md).
