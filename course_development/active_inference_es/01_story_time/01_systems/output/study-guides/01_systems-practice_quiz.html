Practice Quiz: Systems

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is a System? A) A pile of rocks B) A group of parts that work together C) A single leaf D) Nothing

    Answer: B! Great job! A system is when parts team up to do something none of them could do alone.

  2. In the story, who was part of the Forest Team? A) A robot B) An alien C) An Oak Tree and a Squirrel D) A car

    Answer: C! That's right! The Oak Tree, Squeaky the Squirrel, the Soil, and the Rain all work together in the Forest Team.

  3. What happens if you take a wheel off a bicycle? A) It goes faster B) It stops working as a system C) It turns into a skateboard D) It flies away

    Answer: B! You got it! Without all its parts, a bicycle system breaks down.

  4. Which one is a System? A) A cloud of dust B) Your body C) A broken toy D) A single grain of sand

    Answer: B! Wonderful! Your body has a heart, lungs, brain, and more all working together as a team.

  5. What keeps a system's parts inside? A) The Edge (boundary) B) The wind C) Magic D) Sticky tape

    Answer: A! Nice work! The edge, or boundary, holds the system together. Your skin is your body's edge!

  6. Why do parts work together? A) To help the system do something special B) To make noise C) To sleep D) To hide

    Answer: A! Exactly! When parts work together, the system can do amazing things it could never do with just one part.

  7. Is a family a system? A) Yes, because they help each other B) No, because they are people C) Only on Tuesdays D) Only if they are robots

    Answer: A! You bet! A family is a wonderful system where everyone helps each other out.

  8. What did the Rain do for the Forest Team? A) It made everything muddy and sad B) It gave the trees water to drink C) It washed away all the squirrels D) It painted the sky purple

    Answer: B! Great thinking! Rain gives water to the trees so they can grow tall and strong.

  9. What happens when bees visit flowers in the forest? A) The bees take a nap on the petals B) The flowers get mad C) The bees help spread pollen so new flowers can grow D) The flowers fly away

    Answer: C! Awesome! Bees and flowers help each other -- that is a system working together.

  10. Which of these is NOT a part of a School System? A) Teachers B) Students C) Books D) A whale

    Answer: D! Ha, a whale does not belong in a school system (unless it is a school of fish!). Teachers, students, and books all work together.
