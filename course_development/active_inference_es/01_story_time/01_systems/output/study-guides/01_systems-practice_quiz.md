## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is a System?
    A) A pile of rocks
    B) A group of parts that work together
    C) A single leaf
    D) Nothing

2. In the story, who was part of the Forest Team?
    A) A robot
    B) An alien
    C) An Oak Tree and a Squirrel
    D) A car

3. What happens if you take a wheel off a bicycle?
    A) It goes faster
    B) It stops working as a system
    C) It turns into a skateboard
    D) It flies away

4. Which one is a System?
    A) A cloud of dust
    B) Your body
    C) A broken toy
    D) A single grain of sand

5. What keeps a system's parts inside?
    A) The Edge (boundary)
    B) The wind
    C) Magic
    D) Sticky tape

6. Why do parts work together?
    A) To help the system do something special
    B) To make noise
    C) To sleep
    D) To hide

7. Is a family a system?
    A) Yes, because they help each other
    B) No, because they are people
    C) Only on Tuesdays
    D) Only if they are robots
