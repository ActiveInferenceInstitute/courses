## Learning Goals

1. **Discover** that a system is a group of things that work together like a team.
2. **Spot** systems all around you -- in your kitchen, your school, and even outside!
3. **Draw** a system and show its parts and its edge (boundary).
4. **Explain** how the parts of a system help each other.
5. **Wonder** what happens when a part of a system is missing.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **System** | A group of parts that work together to do something special |
| **Parts** | The pieces inside a system (like wheels on a bike) |
| **Edge (Boundary)** | The line between inside the system and outside the system |
| **Team** | People or things working together -- just like a system! |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module introduces the concept of a **System** using the story of a forest where trees, squirrels, and soil all work together. The key Active Inference idea here is that systems have parts that depend on each other, and a boundary that separates inside from outside. Encourage children to look for systems everywhere -- a bicycle, a family, a classroom. Ask them: "What happens if we take away one part?" This builds intuition for how systems maintain themselves.
