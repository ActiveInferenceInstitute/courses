Lecture Content

Module 08: Planning — The Squirrel's Winter Plan

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand what Planning is (thinking about the future).
  2. Learn how to choose the best path to reach your goal.
  3. See how predicting helps us avoid bad surprises (like being hungry in winter!).

Introduction: The Squirrel's Winter Plan

Nutty the Squirrel looked at the leaves falling. He knew winter was coming. Goal: Have a full tummy all winter. Surprise to Avoid: Being hungry!

Nutty had two choices:

  1. Play all day: Fun now, but hungry later.
  2. Gather nuts: Hard work now, but happy later.

Nutty put on his Thinking Cap. He imagined the future. "If I play, I will be cold and hungry. That is a bad surprise!" "If I work, I will be warm and full. That is a good plan!"

So, Nutty gathered nuts. When winter came, he was cozy and full. Good planning, Nutty!

Key Concepts

1. What is Planning?

Planning is using your map to visit the future before you go there.

  • You don't have to experience the bad surprise (hunger) to learn.
  • You can imagine it and choose a different path!

2. Choosing Paths

Imagine a maze. You can go Left or Right.

  • Left Path: Leads to a tiger. (Scary!)
  • Right Path: Leads to a strawberry. (Yummy!)
  • Planning: Choosing the Right Path because you predict it makes you happier.

3. Deep Thinking

Planning is like a superpower. It lets you see what might happen.

  • "If I don't brush my teeth -> Cavities!" (I will brush.)
  • "If I share my toys -> More friends!" (I will share.)

Activities

Activity 1: The Weekend Plan

Draw a picture of what you want to do this weekend. Now, draw how you will make it happen. (Want to go to the park? Plan: Ask Mom, put on shoes, bring ball.)

Activity 2: The Maze Game

Draw a maze on paper. Use your finger to trace the paths without drawing. Find the dead ends in your head first. That is planning!

Summary

Planning is thinking ahead. We use our maps to imagine the future and choose the actions that keep us safe and happy. Because Nutty planned, he had a great winter. You can be a planner too!

References

  • The Ant and the Grasshopper (An Aesop Fable about planning vs. playing.)
  • If You Give a Mouse a Cookie by Laura Numeroff (A funny chain of events!)

Lab Protocol

Lab: The Planning Maze

Goal

Use your brain to plan a path before you move!

What You Need

  • Paper and markers
  • Small toys (like a LEGO figure or a coin)

Steps

1. Build the Maze

Draw a simple maze on a piece of paper. Or build one on the floor using books and pillows! Put a "Treasure" (like a cookie) at the end.

2. The Robot Mode

Pretend to be a robot that CANNOT PLAN. Walk into every wall. "Bonk!" Is this faster or slower? (Slower! And it hurts!)

3. The Planner Mode

Now, stand at the start. Don't move your feet. Use your eyes and brain to find the path. "Go straight, turn left, then turn right..."

4. Execute

Walk the path you planned. Did you hit any walls? No! Planning saves time and bumps!

Practice Quiz

Practice Quiz: Planning

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is Planning? A) Thinking about the future and choosing a path B) Sleeping C) Eating a nut right now D) Forgetting everything

    Answer: A! Great job! Planning is when you use your brain to think ahead and choose the best thing to do.

  2. Why did Nutty gather nuts? A) He was bored B) To avoid the bad surprise of being hungry in winter C) To throw them D) To build a castle

    Answer: B! That's right! Nutty planned ahead so he would have food when winter came. Smart squirrel!

  3. If you see dark clouds (Perception), what is a good Plan? A) Wear a swimsuit B) Bring an umbrella C) Eat ice cream D) Sleep outside

    Answer: B! Nice thinking! Your brain predicts rain, so your plan is to bring an umbrella and stay dry.

  4. What helps us plan? A) Our "Thinking Cap" (imagination) B) A magic wand C) A rock D) Nothing

    Answer: A! Exactly! Your imagination lets you visit the future in your head before you actually go there.

  5. Is it better to plan or just guess? A) Just guess randomly B) Plan! It helps us reach our goals. C) Guessing is faster D) Planning is for robots only

    Answer: B! You got it! Planning helps us pick the path that gets us where we want to go.

  6. When you play a maze, why do you look ahead? A) To see the pretty lines B) To avoid dead ends (bad paths) C) To count the walls D) To trick the paper

    Answer: B! Smart! Looking ahead in a maze is planning. You imagine the path before you draw it.

  7. Can we plan for things far away in the future? A) No, only 1 second ahead B) Yes, like planning for next summer C) Only if you are a wizard D) Time isn't real

    Answer: B! That's right! We can plan for tomorrow, next week, or even next year. Planning is a superpower!

  8. What did Nutty imagine when he put on his Thinking Cap? A) Flying to the moon B) What would happen if he played all day vs. gathered nuts C) Eating pizza for dinner D) Swimming in the river

    Answer: B! Good memory! Nutty imagined two futures: one where he was hungry and cold, and one where he was warm and full.

  9. In the maze example, what is the "Right Path"? A) The path with a scary tiger B) The path that leads to a yummy strawberry C) The path that goes in a circle forever D) The path that is painted red

    Answer: B! Exactly! Planning means choosing the path that leads to something good and avoiding the path with a tiger.

  10. What is the difference between an ant that plays all summer and an ant that plans for winter? A) The planner is boring B) The planner will be warm and full when winter comes C) They are exactly the same D) The player is smarter

    Answer: B! That's right! Just like Nutty, the ant that plans ahead has a much better winter. Planning pays off!

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  1. What is your plan for tomorrow?
  2. If you wanted to build a LEGO tower, what would you need to plan first? (Finding the bricks?)
  3. Draw Nutty the Squirrel happy in his warm winter home.
  4. Think about getting ready for school in the morning. What are three steps in your plan? (Wake up, get dressed, eat breakfast?)
  5. If Nutty's plan did not work (maybe another animal took his nuts!), what should he do? Give up or make a new plan?
