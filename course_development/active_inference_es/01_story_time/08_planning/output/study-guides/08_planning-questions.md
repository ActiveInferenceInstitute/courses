# Course — Module 08 — Think About It

1. What would have happened if Nutty the Squirrel just played all day and never gathered nuts?
2. Do you plan what to wear in the morning? How do you decide?
3. Why is it better to think before you act?
4. Can you plan for something that happens next year? (Like a birthday party?)
5. What is a "bad surprise" you want to avoid? (Getting wet in rain? Being late for school?)
6. How does bringing an umbrella show that you are planning for rain?
7. Do squirrels have calendars? How do they know winter is coming? (Hint: Perception!)
8. If you want to be an astronaut when you grow up, what do you need to plan to do now?
9. Can you change your plan if something surprises you? Give an example.
10. Who is the best planner in your family? What makes them good at planning?
11. Draw a treasure map with two paths. One path has a dragon (bad surprise!). One path has gold (good goal!). Which path would you plan to take?
12. Plan a birthday party! Make a list of everything you need to do: (1) Send invitations, (2) Get a cake, (3) Buy decorations... What else?
13. Imagine you are Nutty the Squirrel. Draw your winter plan: where will you hide your nuts? How many do you need?
14. Draw a "Plan vs. No Plan" picture. Show what happens to a kid who plans for a rainy day (brings an umbrella) vs. a kid who does not plan (gets soaked!).
15. Tell a story about an ant who has to plan how to carry a giant cookie back to the ant hill. What is the ant's plan?
16. Plan your dream day. Draw a schedule showing what you would do from morning to night.
17. Play the Maze Game again (from Module 2). But this time, trace the path with your finger FIRST before moving your toy. That is planning!
18. Imagine you are going on a camping trip. What do you need to pack? Make a list. (Hint: Think about what might happen -- rain? cold? hungry?)
19. Draw two futures for yourself: one where you practiced reading every day, and one where you never practiced. Which future do you want?
20. Write a story about Nutty the Squirrel's friend who did NOT plan for winter. What happened? Did Nutty help?
