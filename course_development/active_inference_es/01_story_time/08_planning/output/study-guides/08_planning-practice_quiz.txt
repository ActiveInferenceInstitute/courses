Practice Quiz: Planning

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

What is Planning?
    A) Thinking about the future and choosing a path
    B) Sleeping
    C) Eating a nut right now
    D) Forgetting everything

    Answer: A! Great job! Planning is when you use your brain to think ahead and choose the best thing to do.

Why did Nutty gather nuts?
    A) He was bored
    B) To avoid the bad surprise of being hungry in winter
    C) To throw them
    D) To build a castle

    Answer: B! That's right! Nutty planned ahead so he would have food when winter came. Smart squirrel!

If you see dark clouds (Perception), what is a good Plan?
    A) Wear a swimsuit
    B) Bring an umbrella
    C) Eat ice cream
    D) Sleep outside

    Answer: B! Nice thinking! Your brain predicts rain, so your plan is to bring an umbrella and stay dry.

What helps us plan?
    A) Our "Thinking Cap" (imagination)
    B) A magic wand
    C) A rock
    D) Nothing

    Answer: A! Exactly! Your imagination lets you visit the future in your head before you actually go there.

Is it better to plan or just guess?
    A) Just guess randomly
    B) Plan! It helps us reach our goals.
    C) Guessing is faster
    D) Planning is for robots only

    Answer: B! You got it! Planning helps us pick the path that gets us where we want to go.

When you play a maze, why do you look ahead?
    A) To see the pretty lines
    B) To avoid dead ends (bad paths)
    C) To count the walls
    D) To trick the paper

    Answer: B! Smart! Looking ahead in a maze is planning. You imagine the path before you draw it.

Can we plan for things far away in the future?
    A) No, only 1 second ahead
    B) Yes, like planning for next summer
    C) Only if you are a wizard
    D) Time isn't real

    Answer: B! That's right! We can plan for tomorrow, next week, or even next year. Planning is a superpower!

What did Nutty imagine when he put on his Thinking Cap?
    A) Flying to the moon
    B) What would happen if he played all day vs. gathered nuts
    C) Eating pizza for dinner
    D) Swimming in the river

    Answer: B! Good memory! Nutty imagined two futures: one where he was hungry and cold, and one where he was warm and full.

In the maze example, what is the "Right Path"?
    A) The path with a scary tiger
    B) The path that leads to a yummy strawberry
    C) The path that goes in a circle forever
    D) The path that is painted red

    Answer: B! Exactly! Planning means choosing the path that leads to something good and avoiding the path with a tiger.

What is the difference between an ant that plays all summer and an ant that plans for winter?
    A) The planner is boring
    B) The planner will be warm and full when winter comes
    C) They are exactly the same
    D) The player is smarter

    Answer: B! That's right! Just like Nutty, the ant that plans ahead has a much better winter. Planning pays off!
