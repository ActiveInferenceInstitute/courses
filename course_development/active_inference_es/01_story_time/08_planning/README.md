## Learning Goals

1. **Understand** that Planning means thinking about the future before you act.
2. **Learn** to imagine different paths and choose the best one.
3. **See** how planning helps us avoid bad surprises (like Nutty avoiding winter hunger!).
4. **Practice** drawing a plan and following it step by step.
5. **Discover** that you can change your plan if something unexpected happens.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Planning** | Using your brain's map to visit the future before you go there |
| **Path** | One of the choices you can make -- each path leads somewhere different |
| **Goal** | Where you want to end up at the end of your plan |
| **Surprise** | Something you did not expect -- good planning helps you avoid bad surprises |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module introduces **Planning** as the ability to simulate future outcomes before acting. The story of Nutty the Squirrel shows how imagining the future (hungry in winter vs. cozy and full) helps an agent choose the right actions today. In Active Inference, planning involves evaluating different policies (action sequences) by predicting their outcomes using the generative model, then selecting the policy that minimizes expected free energy. For children, this translates to: "Think about what will happen if you choose Path A or Path B, then pick the one that makes you happiest."
