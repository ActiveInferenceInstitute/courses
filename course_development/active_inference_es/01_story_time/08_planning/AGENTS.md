# Station: Planning (Story Time)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Narrative & imagination
- **Topics**: Planning
- **Lab Style**: Storytime Activity
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Introduce Planning through **The Squirrel's Winter Plan** -- Nutty chooses between playing all day and gathering nuts for winter.
- Present Planning as "visiting the future in your head before you go there."
- Show that planning means imagining different paths and choosing the one with the best outcome.
- Emphasize that planning helps us avoid bad surprises (like being hungry in winter!).

## Active Inference Integration

- **Planning** in Active Inference is the evaluation of future action sequences by simulating expected outcomes using the generative model. The agent selects the policy (plan) that minimizes expected free energy.
- For K-5: "Planning is using your brain's map to visit the future before you go there. You imagine what will happen if you choose Path A or Path B, and then you pick the best one!"
- Connect to Cognition (imagining the future) and Action (carrying out the plan).

## Lab Design Principles

- Planning labs should involve **drawing plans and treasure maps** -- activities where children plan a path before following it.
- Materials: paper, crayons, tape for floor mazes.
- Children should practice imagining outcomes before acting.
- Reflection should ask: "What was your plan? Did it work? Would you change it next time?"

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "What would happen if Nutty just played all day?" "Do you plan what to wear?"
- Questions 11-20: "Draw a treasure map with two paths." "Plan a birthday party and list all the steps."
- Focus on the power and fun of thinking ahead.
