## Learning Goals

1. **Understand** that Cognition means thinking and guessing inside your head.
2. **Discover** that your brain is a Prediction Machine that always asks "What happens next?"
3. **Learn** that your brain keeps a map of the world and uses it to make guesses.
4. **Practice** making predictions and checking if they come true.
5. **Celebrate** being surprised -- because surprise means you get to learn something new!

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cognition** | A fancy word for thinking -- what happens inside your head |
| **Prediction** | A guess about what will happen next |
| **Map (Model)** | The picture of the world your brain keeps inside, built from everything you have learned |
| **Surprise** | When something happens that you did not predict |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module introduces **Cognition** as thinking and predicting. The story of Sally and her Thinking Cap shows how the brain uses past experience (grey clouds = rain) to predict the future. The key Active Inference concept here is the **generative model** -- the brain's internal map of how the world works. When predictions match reality, we feel comfortable. When they don't match, we experience surprise, which drives learning. Encourage children to notice their own predictions throughout the day: "What did you think would happen? Were you right?"
