Course — Module 06 — Think About It

Does a robot go to High School? (No, it learns in a lab).
Can a robot learn to play piano? (Yes, by practicing!).
Do robots forget things? (Not usually, unless their hard drive breaks).
Why does it take so long to teach a robot? (It needs thousands of examples).
Can a robot learn from another robot? (Yes! They can share data).
If a robot makes a mistake, is it embarrassed? (No, simple robotics have no feelings).
How do self-driving cars learn? (By driving millions of miles on virtual roads).
Can you teach a robot to lie? (If you show it that lying is the "Right" answer, yes. That's why we must be careful!).
Is the internet a robot teacher? (Robots scan the internet to learn).
What is better: A robot that knows everything, or a robot that can learn new things?
