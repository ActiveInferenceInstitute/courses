Module 06: Learning — Smart Robots

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand that some robots can Learn.
  2. Learn about Training (Showing examples).
  3. Know that robots learn from Mistakes.

Introduction: The Robot Student

Can a robot go to school? Sort of! Old robots only followed rules ("Turn Left"). If the map changed, they got stuck. New robots use Machine Learning. They practice!

  • Try 1: Crash. (Bad!)
  • Try 2: Crash. (Bad!)
  • Try 3: Turn early. (Good!) The robot remembers what worked.

Key Concepts

1. Training Data (Examples)

How do you teach a robot what a cat looks like? You show it 1,000 pictures of cats. And 1,000 pictures of dogs. The robot finds the pattern (Cats have pointy ears). This is called Training.

2. Trial and Error

Robots learn by trying. A walking robot falls down 100 times. But every time it falls, its brain changes slightly. "Don't do that again." Eventually, it walks perfectly!

3. Updates

Your brain grows when you learn. A robot's code gets an Update. It gets smarter over time.

Activities

Activity 1: Hot or Cold?

Hide a "Treasure" (Toy). Your friend is the Robot. They move around. You give Feeedback:

  • "Colder..." (Wrong way).
  • "Warmer..." (Getting closer).
  • "HOT!" (Success). The robot uses feedback to find the goal!

Activity 2: Cat vs. Dog Sorter

Draw 3 Cats and 3 Dogs on cards. Mix them up. Ask a friend to sort them. Ask: "How did you know it was a cat?" (Pointy ears? Whiskers?) You are explaining your Training Data.

Summary

Learning makes robots smart. Instead of just following rules, they can figure things out by practicing and looking at examples.
