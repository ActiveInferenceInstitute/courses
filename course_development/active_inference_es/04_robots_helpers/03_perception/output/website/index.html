Module 03: Perception — Robot Senses

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand that Sensors are how robots feel the world.
  2. Compare Human Senses (Eyes, Ears) to Robot Sensors (Cameras, Mics).
  3. Learn that robots need good data to make good decisions.

Introduction: The Robot Senses

How do you know if a wall is in front of you? You see it with your eyes. How does a robot know? It uses a Sensor.

  • Camera: Sees light and color.
  • Microphone: Hears sounds.
  • Bumper: Feels a crash.
  • Thermometer: Feels hot and cold.

Without sensors, a robot is blind and deaf!

Key Concepts

1. The Camera (Robot Eye)

A camera takes pictures really fast. The robot looks at the pixels. "I see a red blob. That is a ball." "I see a green blob. That is a tree."

2. The Microphone (Robot Ear)

Robots can listen to commands. When you say "Hey Siri" or "Alexa," a machine is listening! It turns sound waves into code.

3. Invisible Senses

Robots have superpowers! They can sense things humans can't.

  • Lidar: Seeing with lasers!
  • Infrared: Seeing heat (Night Vision).
  • Magnetism: Knowing which way is North.

Activities

Activity 1: The Blind Robot

One person is the Robot (Blindfolded). One person is the Sensor (The Guide). The Guide must whisper what they see to help the Robot walk safely. "Wall ahead. Stop." This shows how much the Robot trusts the Sensor!

Activity 2: Sensor Scavenger Hunt

Look around your house or school. Find 3 Machines with sensors.

  1. Smoke Alarm: Smells smoke!
  2. Automatic Door: Sees you coming!
  3. Thermostat: Feels the heat!

Summary

Perception is how robots understand the world. They use sensors to gather data, just like we use our five senses. Some robots can even sense things we can't!

References

Lab Protocol

Lab: Sensor Scavenger Hunt

Goal

To find hidden sensors in the real world.

What You Need

  • A Sensor Log (Paper).
  • A Pencil.
  • Your Detective Eyes.

Steps

1. The Search

Walk around your house, school, or street. Look for machines that "know" things.

2. The Log

Write down what you find.

Object What does it Sense? How do you know?
Automatic Door Motion (Movement) It opens when I walk near it.
Street Light Light (Day/Night) It turns on when it gets dark.
iPad Screen Touch (Finger) It swipes when I touch it.
Car Bumper Distance (Beep!) It beeps when backing up.

What We Learned

We live in a world full of robots and sensors! They are watching, listening, and feeling all the time to help us.

Practice Quiz

Practice Quiz: Robot Senses

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is a Sensor? A) A robot's shoe B) A part that gathers information (Sees, Hears, Feels) C) A battery D) A sandwich

  2. Which sensor matches the Human Eye? A) Microphone B) Camera C) Bumper D) Wheel

  3. Which sensor matches the Human Ear? A) Microphone B) Camera C) Thermometer D) Lidar

  4. How does a robot know it bumped into a wall? A) It guesses B) A Touch Sensor (Bumper) is pressed C) It cries D) It falls asleep

  5. Can robots see things humans can't? A) No, they are just like us B) Yes! Infrared (Heat) and Ultraviolet C) Only on Tuesdays D) Maybe

  6. What does a Smoke Alarm sense? A) Music B) Smoke particles C) Cats D) Rain

  7. If you cover a robot's camera, what happens? A) It is blind B) It sees better C) It flies D) Nothing

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  1. What superpower sensor would you want? (Night vision? Zoom?)
  2. Draw a robot with 10 eyes. Why does it need so many?
  3. Find a sensor in your room right now. (Hint: Helps change TV channels).
