Practice Quiz: Robot Senses

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is a Sensor? A) A robot's shoe B) A part that gathers information (Sees, Hears, Feels) C) A battery D) A sandwich

  2. Which sensor matches the Human Eye? A) Microphone B) Camera C) Bumper D) Wheel

  3. Which sensor matches the Human Ear? A) Microphone B) Camera C) Thermometer D) Lidar

  4. How does a robot know it bumped into a wall? A) It guesses B) A Touch Sensor (Bumper) is pressed C) It cries D) It falls asleep

  5. Can robots see things humans can't? A) No, they are just like us B) Yes! Infrared (Heat) and Ultraviolet C) Only on Tuesdays D) Maybe

  6. What does a Smoke Alarm sense? A) Music B) Smoke particles C) Cats D) Rain

  7. If you cover a robot's camera, what happens? A) It is blind B) It sees better C) It flies D) Nothing
