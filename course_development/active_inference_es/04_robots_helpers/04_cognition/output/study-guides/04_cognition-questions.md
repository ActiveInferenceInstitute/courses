# Course — Module 04 — Think About It

1. Are you smarter than a calculator? (You are! It can only add, you can dream).
2. Can a robot tell a joke? (Only if you program the joke into it).
3. Why do video games have "Glitches"? (Bad code/logic errors).
4. If a robot plays Chess, will it always win? (Usually, because it calculates moves fast).
5. What happens if you give a robot the wrong instructions? (It makes a mess).
6. Can a robot learn to be nice? (If "Nice" means saying "Please," yes).
7. Do you use algorithms? (Yes, getting dressed implies an order!).
8. Is your brain a computer? (Sort of, but much more squishy and complex).
9. Can robots begin to think like humans someday? (People are trying to make AI do this).
10. What is a "Bug" in code? (A mistake that breaks the program).
