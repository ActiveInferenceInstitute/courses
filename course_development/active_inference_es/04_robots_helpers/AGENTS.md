## Identity

- **Course**: Robots & Helpers
- **Number**: 4
- **Perspective**: Simple machines & digital helpers
- **Lab Type**: Build & Play
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime. No formulas. Drawings and songs.

## Conventions

All modules in this course must:

1. Use language appropriate for Grades K-5.
2. Frame all concepts through the lens of simple machines & digital helpers.
3. Include build & play activities.
4. Adhere to notation standards in [../resources/notation_table.md](../resources/notation_table.md).
5. Cross-reference the shared [../resources/glossary.md](../resources/glossary.md).
