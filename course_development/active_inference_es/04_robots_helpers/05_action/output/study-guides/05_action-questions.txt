Course — Module 05 — Think About It

Why do most robots have wheels instead of legs? (Easier to make and balance).
Can a robot fly? (Yes, Drones!).
How strong is a robot? (Some can lift cars!).
Be a robot: Try to pick up a coin with chopsticks. Is it hard? (That is how a robot feels!).
Why do robot motors make a "Whirrr" sound? (Spinning gears).
Can a robot swim? (Yes, underwater drones).
Do robots get tired? (Only when the battery dies).
Which is faster: A human or a car robot?
Can a robot tie shoelaces? (It's very hard for them!).
If a robot is stuck in mud, what can it do? (Use sensors to ask for help).
