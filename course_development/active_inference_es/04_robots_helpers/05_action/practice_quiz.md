# Practice Quiz: Robot Movers

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What part makes a robot move?
    A) The Paint
    B) The Actuator (Motor)
    C) The Sticker
    D) The Box

2. If you are building a robot to race on a flat track, what should it have?
    A) Square wheels
    B) Round Wheels (Fast!)
    C) Heavy legs
    D) No wheels

3. If you are building a robot to climb a mountain, what should it have?
    A) Tiny wheels
    B) Legs (To step over rocks)
    C) Skis
    D) A boat

4. What is a Gripper?
    A) A robot hand
    B) A robot foot
    C) A robot hat
    D) A robot nose

5. What do robots require to move?
    A) Pizza
    B) Power (Electricity/Battery)
    C) Sleep
    D) Water

6. What sound does a motor usually make?
    A) "Moooo"
    B) "Whirrrr" or "Zzzzzt"
    C) "Quack"
    D) "Kaboom"

7. Can robots be stronger than humans?
    A) Yes (Industrial robots lift huge things)
    B) No, humans are strongest
    C) Only if they eat spinach
    D) Never

## Part B: Tell Us What You Think

1. Design a robot hand. Does it have fingers? Or is it a magnet?
2. Would you rather have robot legs (Run fast) or robot arms (Lift heavy)?
3. Draw a Drone flying. Propellers are actuators too!
