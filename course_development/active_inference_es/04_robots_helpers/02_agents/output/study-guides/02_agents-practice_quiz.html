Practice Quiz: The Robot Job

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What is the difference between a Broom and a Robot Vacuum? A) The Broom is colorful B) The Broom is a Tool (You do the work), the Robot is an Agent (It does the work) C) The Robot eats dust D) Nothing

  2. What is a Goal? A) Ideally, a soccer net B) What the robot is trying to achieve (Clean room, Find rock) C) A battery D) A sandwich

  3. If a robot hits a wall, what should it do? A) Cry B) Keep hitting the wall forever C) Turn around (Use its code) D) Sleep

  4. Can a robot do anything it wants? A) Yes, they are free! B) No, it must follow its Programming (Rules) C) Only on weekends D) Maybe

  5. What is the "If-Then" rule? A) If Hungry, Then Eat B) If Wall, Then Turn C) If Button Pressed, Then Start D) All of the above!

  6. Are you an Agent? A) Yes! You have goals and can move. B) No, I am a tool. C) I am a plant. D) I don't know.

  7. What is the most important robot rule? A) Dance B) Be Safe (Don't hurt humans) C) Be Fast D) Be Shiny
