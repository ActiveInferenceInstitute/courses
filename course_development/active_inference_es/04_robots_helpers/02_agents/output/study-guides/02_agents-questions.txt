Course — Module 02 — Think About It

If a robot has no battery, is it still an agent? (It's a sleeping agent!).
Why don't we have robots that do everything yet? (It's hard to program them!).
Is a dog a robot? (No, it's alive. But it is an Agent!).
Can a robot change its mind? (Only if its code says so).
What if a robot makes a mistake? Who is responsible? (The human who coded it!).
Would you want a robot teacher?
Can a robot be an artist? (Some can draw, but do they feel it?).
Do robots have names? (We give them names, like Curiosity Rover).
Is a self-driving car a robot? (Yes, a very big one).
What is the most important rule for a robot? (Be Safe).
