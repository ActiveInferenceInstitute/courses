# Lab: Build a Junkyard Bot

## Goal

To build a model Robot System using recycled materials ("Junk").

## What You Need

* Empty boxes (cereal, shoe, tissues).
* Toilet paper rolls (Actuators/Arms).
* Bottle caps (Sensors/Eyes).
* Glue or Tape.
* Markers.

## Steps

### 1. The Design (Planning)

Think about what your robot does.

* Is it a Chef Bot? (Needs hands).
* Is it a Car Bot? (Needs wheels).

### 2. The Build (Systems)

* **Body**: Use a big box.
* **Head**: Use a smaller box.
* **Sensors**: Glue bottle caps for eyes.
* **Actuators**: Glue rolls for arms or legs.

### 3. The Details

Draw buttons for the **Computer Brain**.
Draw a **Battery Pack** on the back.
