# Course — Module 01 — Think About It

1. Is a toaster a robot? (It has sensors and heaters, but it doesn't think much!)
2. What is the difference between a Robot and a Remote Control Car? (Robots can think for themselves).
3. Do robots sleep? (They charge batteries/power off).
4. Can a robot feel happy? (No, they just run code).
5. Why do robots have wires? (To send electricity and signals).
6. If a robot loses a wheel, is the system broken?
7. What kind of robot would you build? (A cleaner? A chef?)
8. Are smart phones robots? (They are computers with sensors!)
9. Why are robots made of metal and plastic?
10. Can a robot break?
