Course — Module 08 — Think About It

Do you have a map in your head right now (of your house)? (Yes!).
How does a vacuum robot know where it has cleaned? (It makes a map as it goes).
Why is the shortest path the best? (Saves time and energy).
Can a robot get lost? (Yes, if its sensors break or the map is wrong).
What do you do if your plan fails? (Re-plan! Don't give up).
How does Google Maps know the way? (It acts like a giant robot brain planning paths for cars).
Is a maze hard for a robot? (Not if it follows the "Right Hand Rule" - keep your hand on the right wall).
Can a robot plan for tomorrow? (Only if programmed to schedule tasks).
If you move the furniture, does the robot know? (It will bump into it, then update the map).
Is Chess a game of planning? (Yes, thinking many moves ahead).
