# Lab: The Robot Maze

## Goal

To navigate a robot through a maze using a plan.

## What You Need

* Tape (Masking tape).
* A "Robot" (Friend).
* A Blindfold.

## Steps

### 1. Build the Maze

Use tape on the floor to make a path.
Put obstacles (Chairs) in the way.
Mark an "X" for the Goal.

### 2. The Plan (Command List)

Look at the maze.
Write down the steps:

* Forward 3 steps.
* Turn Left.
* Forward 5 steps.
* Stop.

### 3. The Run

Blindfold the Robot.
Read your plan.
Does the robot hit a wall?
If yes, your plan had a bug! Fix it and try again.

## My Maze Log

## What We Learned

Planning isn't just about knowing where to go. It's about knowing exactly HOW to get there, step by step, without hitting anything.
