# Course — Module 08 — Think About It

1. Do you have a map in your head right now (of your house)? (Yes!).
2. How does a vacuum robot know where it has cleaned? (It makes a map as it goes).
3. Why is the shortest path the best? (Saves time and energy).
4. Can a robot get lost? (Yes, if its sensors break or the map is wrong).
5. What do you do if your plan fails? (Re-plan! Don't give up).
6. How does Google Maps know the way? (It acts like a giant robot brain planning paths for cars).
7. Is a maze hard for a robot? (Not if it follows the "Right Hand Rule" - keep your hand on the right wall).
8. Can a robot plan for tomorrow? (Only if programmed to schedule tasks).
9. If you move the furniture, does the robot know? (It will bump into it, then update the map).
10. Is Chess a game of planning? (Yes, thinking many moves ahead).
