Lab: Guess How Many?

Goal

To practice Estimation (guessing close numbers) and compare it to Counting.

What You Need

 A clear jar or cup.
 Small items (Beans, Buttons, or Cereal).
 Paper and pencil.

Steps

The Setup

Ask a friend to put a handful of beans in the jar.
Don't count them yet!

The Estimate

Look at the jar.
Think about it.
Write down your Estimate: "I think there are ____ beans."

The Count

Pour them out.
Count them exactly (1, 2, 3...).
Write down the Real Number.

The Difference

How close were you?

 Real Number - Estimate = Difference.
If you guessed 20 and there were 22, the difference is 2. You are a Super Estimator!

What We Learned

Estimation is faster than counting, but less exact. It's a great tool for making quick decisions!
