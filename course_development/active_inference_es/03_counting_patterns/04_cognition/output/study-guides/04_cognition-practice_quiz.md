# Practice Quiz: The Guessing Game

## Part A: Pick the Best Answer

1. What is Estimation?
    A) Counting every single piece
    B) A smart guess (Prediction)
    C) Sleeping
    D) Eating

2. If you see a full jar of cookies, what is a BAD estimate?
    A) "About 20"
    B) "About 30"
    C) "One million!" (Too big)
    D) "Maybe 25"

3. Which takes longer?
    A) Estimation (Fast)
    B) Exact Counting (1, 2, 3...)
    C) Blinking
    D) Sneezing

4. If you flip a coin, what are the chances it is Heads?
    A) 100% (Always)
    B) 0% (Never)
    C) 50/50 (Half chances)
    D) It turns into a bird

5. Why do we Estimate?
    A) Because sometimes we don't need the exact number
    B) To be lazy
    C) Because numbers are scary
    D) To trick people

6. Which group has MORE? (Estimate!)
    A) A bag of 5 apples
    B) A bag of 100 grapes
    C) A bag of 1 watermelon
    D) A bag of air

7. Can you predict the future perfectly?
    A) Yes, I am magic
    B) No, we can only make a Best Guess
    C) Only on Tuesdays
    D) If I squint
