Lab: Agent Counter

Goal

To use your Agent skills to count everything in your "Environment" (Room).

What You Need

  • A notebook ("Detective Log").
  • A pencil.

Steps

1. The Mission

You are an Agent Detective. You need to know HOW MANY things are in your base.

2. The Hunt

Find and count these items:

  • Lights: ____
  • Doors: ____
  • Shoes: ____
  • Pillows: ____

3. The Challenge

Find something there is only ONE of. (Maybe a bed? A TV?) Find something there are MORE THAN 10 of. (Books? Legos?)

My Detective Log

Item Name Count
Example: Cats 2
Shoes
Windows

What We Learned

Counting turns the world into numbers. This helps us describe our environment exactly!
