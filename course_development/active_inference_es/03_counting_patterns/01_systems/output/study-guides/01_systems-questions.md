# Course -- Module 01 -- Think About It

1. What is your favorite number? Why?
2. What would happen if the number 3 disappeared? (We couldn't have triangles!)
3. Why do we need numbers? (To count toys, money, age...)
4. Can you count to infinity? (Nobody can! It goes on forever!)
5. Is Zero a number? (Yes! It means "None" or "Empty.")
6. How many fingers do you have? Is it a "Set" of fingers?
7. Do animals count? (Some can count a little bit!)
8. Why are phone numbers so long? (So everyone can have a different one.)
9. If I give you 2 cookies and then 2 more, how many do you have?
10. Can you count backward? Is it harder?
11. Draw the "Kingdom of Numbers." Give each number (1 through 10) its own castle. Decorate them!
12. Make a number line on the floor with tape or chalk. Jump on each number and say it out loud. Can you do it backward?
13. Sort a pile of objects (buttons, toys, crayons) into Sets. How many sets did you make? How many in each set?
14. Write the numbers 1 to 20. Circle your favorite. Draw a picture of that many things (if your favorite is 7, draw 7 stars).
15. What if numbers went 1, 3, 7, Banana? Draw what that silly kingdom would look like. Why would it be confusing?
16. Find 3 Systems in your house (a clock, a bookshelf, a remote control). Draw each one and label the parts.
17. Teach a younger child to count to 10. What was hardest for them? Write about it.
18. Make up a song about the numbers 1 to 5. Each number gets one line of the song.
19. Count everything in your backpack. Make a chart: Item Name and How Many.
20. Imagine you are King One or Queen Two. Write a short story about a day in the Kingdom of Numbers.
