Course -- Module 01 -- Think About It

What is your favorite number? Why?
What would happen if the number 3 disappeared? (We couldn't have triangles!)
Why do we need numbers? (To count toys, money, age...)
Can you count to infinity? (Nobody can! It goes on forever!)
Is Zero a number? (Yes! It means "None" or "Empty.")
How many fingers do you have? Is it a "Set" of fingers?
Do animals count? (Some can count a little bit!)
Why are phone numbers so long? (So everyone can have a different one.)
If I give you 2 cookies and then 2 more, how many do you have?
Can you count backward? Is it harder?
Draw the "Kingdom of Numbers." Give each number (1 through 10) its own castle. Decorate them!
Make a number line on the floor with tape or chalk. Jump on each number and say it out loud. Can you do it backward?
Sort a pile of objects (buttons, toys, crayons) into Sets. How many sets did you make? How many in each set?
Write the numbers 1 to 20. Circle your favorite. Draw a picture of that many things (if your favorite is 7, draw 7 stars).
What if numbers went 1, 3, 7, Banana? Draw what that silly kingdom would look like. Why would it be confusing?
Find 3 Systems in your house (a clock, a bookshelf, a remote control). Draw each one and label the parts.
Teach a younger child to count to 10. What was hardest for them? Write about it.
Make up a song about the numbers 1 to 5. Each number gets one line of the song.
Count everything in your backpack. Make a chart: Item Name and How Many.
Imagine you are King One or Queen Two. Write a short story about a day in the Kingdom of Numbers.
