# Course AGENTS: Counting & Patterns

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course README](./README.md) | [Curriculum AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Identity

- **Course**: Counting & Patterns
- **Number**: 3
- **Perspective**: Shapes, sorting, counting
- **Lab Type**: Hands-On Puzzle
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime. No formulas. Drawings and songs.

## Conventions

All modules in this course must:

1. Use language appropriate for Grades K-5.
2. Frame all concepts through the lens of shapes, sorting, counting.
3. Include hands-on puzzle activities.
4. Adhere to notation standards in [../resources/notation_table.md](../resources/notation_table.md).
5. Cross-reference the shared [../resources/glossary.md](../resources/glossary.md).
