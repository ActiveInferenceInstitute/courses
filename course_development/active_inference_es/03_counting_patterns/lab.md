# Lab: The Number Detective Notebook

## Goal

Create your own Number Detective Notebook -- a collection of counting mysteries, patterns, and estimation challenges from all eight stations!

## What You Need

* A blank notebook or stapled pages (at least 8 pages)
* Crayons, colored pencils, or markers
* A pencil
* Small objects to count (beans, buttons, coins)

## Steps

### 1. Make the Cover

Decorate the front of your notebook. Write: "NUMBER DETECTIVE NOTEBOOK" and your name. Draw Detective Brain on the cover -- a brain with a magnifying glass!

### 2. Station Pages

For each of the 8 stations, make one page:

| Page | Station | What to Write or Draw |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Systems | Draw the Kingdom of Numbers -- castles for 1, 2, 3... in order |
| 2 | Agents | Count 5 things in your room and write the numbers |
| 3 | Perception | Draw a pattern (like Red, Blue, Red, Blue) and leave one blank for a friend to guess |
| 4 | Cognition | Draw a jar of beans -- write your estimate, then the real count |
| 5 | Action | Count your jumps or steps and write the number |
| 6 | Learning | Write a math fact you know by heart now but had to learn |
| 7 | Communication | Invent a secret symbol for the numbers 1, 2, and 3 |
| 8 | Planning | Draw something you are saving up for -- how many days or tokens until you get it? |

### 3. The Detective Badge

On the last page, draw a big badge that says "Official Number Detective." Color it gold!

### 4. Share

Show your notebook to a friend or family member. Can they solve your pattern on Page 3? Can they decode your symbols on Page 7?
