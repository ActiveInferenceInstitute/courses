# Course — Module 05 — Think About It

1. How many times can you jump in 1 minute?
2. Can you pat your head 3 times and rub your tummy 3 times... at the same time?
3. Why do dancers count ("5, 6, 7, 8")?
4. If you take 10 big steps and 10 small steps, do you go the same distance?
5. Can you make a triangle with your fingers?
6. How many steps is it from your bed to your door?
7. If you spin around 5 times, do you get dizzy?
8. Why do sports have scores? (To count the number of goals!)
9. Can you clap a rhythm pattern? (Clap, Clap, Pause).
10. Is blinking an action? How many times do you blink in a minute?
