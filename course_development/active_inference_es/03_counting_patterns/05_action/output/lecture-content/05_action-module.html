Module 05: Action — Moving Math

Learning Objectives

  1. Understand that you can Do Math with your body.
  2. Learn about Counting Actions (Jumps, Claps, Steps).
  3. Know that moving correctly is an Action Goal.

Introduction: The Jumping Beans

Math isn't just for paper. It is for Moving!

  • Captain Brain says: "Start Engines!"
  • Captain Brain says: "Jump 3 times!"
  • Body Agent goes: Jump, Jump, Jump.

If you only jumped 2 times, you didn't reach the Goal. You have to Count while you Move!

Key Concepts

1. Counting Moves

Walking is just counting steps.

  • "Take 5 steps forward."
  • "Take 2 steps back." Your brain has to plan the number and your body has to do it.

2. Fast and Slow

Numbers can tell us about Speed too.

  • Fast Action: Clap 10 times in 10 seconds.
  • Slow Action: Clap 1 time in 10 seconds. The Number of actions changes, but the Time stays the same!

3. Geometry (Shapes)

You can make shapes with your body.

  • Stretch wide like a Star.
  • Curl up like a Ball (Circle).
  • Stand straight like a Pencil (Line). You are a Shape Shifter!

Activities

Activity 1: The Robot Walk

Pretend you are a robot. Commander says: "2 Steps Forward. 1 Step Left. 3 Jumps." Can you follow the program exactly? If you miscount, your robot crashes!

Activity 2: Number Hopscotch

Draw a hopscotch grid (1-10) with chalk. Throw a stone on a number (e.g., 7). Jump to that number. Count out loud as you jump: "1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7!"

Summary

Action brings numbers to life. We use counting to control our bodies (dancing, running, jumping). When Brain and Body count together, we can move with precision!
