Course — Module 06 — Think About It

  1. What is something you learned this year that was hard at first?
  2. Why do we have to memorize "Times Tables"?
  3. If you get a math problem wrong, should you be sad? (No! You learned something!)
  4. Can you learn in your sleep? (Your brain works while you sleep!)
  5. How do we remember phone numbers? (Patterns and chunks).
  6. Is it easier to learn a song or a list of random numbers? Why?
  7. Can a computer learn?
  8. Do you have to practice to be good at video games?
  9. Why do teachers give homework? (To help you Practice!)
  10. If you stop practicing, do you forget?
