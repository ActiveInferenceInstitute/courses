# Course -- Module 03 -- Think About It

1. Look at your shirt. Does it have a pattern?
2. What comes next: Day, Night, Day, Night, ___?
3. Why do police cars have sirens that go "Wee-Woo-Wee-Woo"? (A sound pattern!)
4. Can you find a pattern in the tiles on the floor?
5. What if the traffic lights went: Red, Green, Purple, Banana? (Confusing! No pattern!)
6. How do you know when to clap in a song?
7. Do flowers have patterns? (Look at the petals!)
8. Can you count by 10s? (10, 20, 30...). Is that a pattern?
9. What is a pattern you do every morning? (Wake up, Brush teeth, Get dressed).
10. Is your heartbeat a pattern?
11. Make a bead pattern (or use crayons to draw one): AB, AB, AB. Now make a harder one: AAB, AAB, AAB. Draw both and label the rule.
12. Create a rhythm pattern using claps and stomps (like Clap, Clap, Stomp, Clap, Clap, Stomp). Teach it to a friend. Could they follow along?
13. Go on a Pattern Hunt around your home. Find 5 patterns (on clothes, floors, walls, dishes). Draw each one and write what the rule is.
14. Make a fruit kebab with a pattern (Grape, Berry, Grape, Berry). Take a photo or draw it. Does the taste have a pattern too?
15. Draw a pattern and leave the last 3 spaces blank. Give it to a friend. Can they finish it? Were they right?
16. Find a number pattern: write 2, 4, 6, 8, ___. Now make your own number pattern and quiz a family member.
17. Listen to your favorite song. Can you find a part that repeats? Draw the pattern you hear (maybe using shapes for different sounds).
18. Break a pattern on purpose! Make a nice pattern (ABAB) and then change one piece. How does it feel? Draw the "broken" version next to the correct one.
19. Create a dance with exactly 4 moves that repeat. Perform it 3 times in a row. Film it or describe the moves in writing.
20. Imagine you are a Pattern Detective. Write a short case file: "The Case of the Missing Pattern." What pattern was broken? How did you solve it?
