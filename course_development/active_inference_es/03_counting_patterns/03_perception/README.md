## Learning Goals

1. **Understand** that Perception means noticing patterns in the world.
2. **Learn** about sequences -- things that repeat in a rule (Red, Blue, Red, Blue...).
3. **Discover** that your brain loves to predict "What Comes Next?"
4. **Practice** finding visual, number, and rhythm patterns.
5. **Experience** the surprise that happens when a pattern breaks.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pattern** | Something that repeats following a rule |
| **Sequence** | Things in a line that follow an order |
| **Predict** | Using the pattern to guess what comes next |
| **Surprise** | The feeling when the pattern breaks and something unexpected happens |

## Parent/Teacher Notes

This module introduces **Perception** as pattern detection. Children learn that their brains are always looking for rules in what they see, hear, and feel. The Pattern Detective metaphor frames perception as an active investigation: "What is the rule?" Once a pattern is found, the brain can predict what comes next. When a pattern breaks, the child experiences surprise -- a direct felt sense of prediction error. The fruit kebab and dance activities make pattern recognition physical and fun. In Active Inference, perception is the process of inferring the hidden causes (the rule) behind sensory observations.
