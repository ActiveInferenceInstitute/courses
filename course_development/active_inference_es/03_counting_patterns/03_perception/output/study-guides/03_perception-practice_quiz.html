Practice Quiz: The Pattern Detective

Part A: Pick the Best Answer

  1. What comes next? Square, Triangle, Square, Triangle, ____? A) Circle B) Square C) Dog D) Pizza

  2. What is a Pattern? A) Something that repeats with a rule B) A messy room C) Invisible ink D) A broken glass

  3. Which is a Number Pattern? A) 1, 2, C, 4 B) 5, 5, 5, 5 C) 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 D) Blue, Red, Green

  4. Why does our Brain like patterns? A) They help us Predict what comes next B) They are scary C) They taste good D) Because they are loud

  5. Finish the rhythm: Clap, Clap, Snap. Clap, Clap, ____. A) Jump B) Snap C) Sleep D) Meow

  6. Where can you find patterns? A) Only in math class B) Everywhere! (Clothes, music, nature) C) Only on TV D) Nowhere

  7. If the sun comes up every morning, is that a pattern? A) Yes (Day/Night cycle) B) No, it's magic C) Maybe D) Only in summer

Part B: Tell Us What You Think

  1. Draw a pattern using Shapes (Circle, Square, etc.).
  2. Write a number pattern (like 2, 4, 6...).
  3. Look at the floor or ceiling. Do you see a pattern? Draw it.
