Course — Module 03 — Think About It

Look at your shirt. Does it have a pattern?
What comes next: Day, Night, Day, Night, ___?
Why do police cars have sirens that go "Wee-Woo-Wee-Woo"? (A sound pattern!)
Can you find a pattern in the tiles on the floor?
What if the traffic lights went: Red, Green, Purple, Banana? (Confusing! No pattern!)
How do you know when to clap in a song?
Do flowers have patterns? (Look at the petals!)
Can you count by 10s? (10, 20, 30...). Is that a pattern?
What is a pattern you do every morning? (Wake up, Brush teeth, Get dressed).
Is your heartbeat a pattern?
