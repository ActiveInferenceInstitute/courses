# Station: Perception (Counting & Patterns)

> **Quick Navigation**: [Module README](./README.md) | [Course AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Conventions

- **Perspective**: Shapes, sorting, counting
- **Topics**: Perception
- **Lab Style**: Hands-On Puzzle
- **Audience**: Grades K-5
- **Tone**: Wonder, play, storytime

## Content Guidelines

- Perception is about noticing patterns: stripes on a zebra, tiles on a floor, numbers in a sequence.
- Detective Brain "sees the rule" -- Red, Blue, Red, Blue, so the next one must be Red!
- Cover visual patterns (stripes, shapes), number patterns (1, 2, 3... or 2, 4, 6...), and rhythm patterns (Clap, Clap, Stomp).
- Your brain PREDICTS what comes next. When it is right, everything feels smooth. When it is wrong, SURPRISE!

## Active Inference Integration

- **Perception** is pattern detection -- the brain extracts regularities from sensory input to generate predictions.
- For K-5: "Your eyes collect the clues, and your brain finds the rule. Once you know the rule, you can predict what comes next!"

## Lab Design Principles

- Labs should involve making patterns (bead sequences, fruit kebabs), breaking patterns (to create surprise), and asking friends to guess the next item.
- Materials: colored beads, Lego bricks, markers, fruit.
- Focus on the cycle: see the pattern, predict the next, check if you were right.

## Question Design Standards

- Questions 1-10: "What comes next: Day, Night, Day, Night, ___?" "Can you find a pattern in the tiles on the floor?"
- Questions 11-20: "Make your own pattern and quiz a friend." "Create a rhythm pattern with claps and stomps."
