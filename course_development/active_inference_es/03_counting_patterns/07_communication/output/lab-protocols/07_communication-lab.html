Lab: Symbol Factory

Goal

To Create your own number system (Symbols) and use them to communicate.

What You Need

  • Paper and markers.
  • A partner.

Steps

1. The Design

Invent new symbols for numbers 1, 2, and 3.

  • Example:
  • 1 = $\star$ (Star)
  • 2 = $\Delta$ (Triangle)
  • 3 = $\square$ (Box)

2. The Message

Write a math problem using YOUR symbols.

  • $\star + \Delta = ?$

3. The Decode

Give it to your partner. Can they solve it? They have to use the "Key" (The list you made in Step 1). Answer: $\star (1) + \Delta (2) = \square (3)$!

What We Learned

Symbols only work if everyone agrees on what they mean. That is why we all learn the same numbers in school!
