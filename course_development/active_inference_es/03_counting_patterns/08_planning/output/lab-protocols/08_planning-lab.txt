Lab: The Token Shop

Goal

To practice Earning and Spending to reach a Goal.

What You Need

 10 items (Paper clips, beans) to be "Tokens".
 3 Toys or Treats (Price them: 2, 5, and 10 tokens).

Steps

The Menu

Set up your store.

 Small Treat: 2 Tokens.
 Medium Toy: 5 Tokens.
 Big Prize: 10 Tokens.

The Bank

You start with 0 Tokens.
How do you get them?
Action Menu:

 Do 10 Jumping Jacks = 1 Token.
 Clean up toys = 2 Tokens.
 sing a song = 1 Token.

The Choice

Start working!
Earn your tokens.
When you have 2, do you spend them? Or save for the Big Prize?

My Savings Log

| Action Done | Tokens Earned | Total in Bank |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Jumping Jacks | 1 | 1 |
| Clean Up | 2 | 3 |

What We Learned

You have to Work (Action) to earn numbers. You have to Plan (Save) to get the big things you want!
