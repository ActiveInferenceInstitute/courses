## Overview

Welcome to **Active Inference for Elementary School**! This curriculum teaches the big ideas behind Active Inference — how our brains predict, learn, and explore — through stories, play, body activities, and hands-on fun.

Every lesson is designed for young learners. No equations, no jargon — just wonder, exploration, and discovery.

## Core Topics

Each course covers the same 8 topics in the same order — the Active Inference "spine":

| # | Topic | 01 Stories | 02 Bodies | 03 Patterns | 04 Robots |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Systems** | Story worlds | Body systems | Sets & groups | Robot parts |
| 2 | **Agents** | Characters | Me as explorer | Sorting agent | Robot friend |
| 3 | **Perception** | Noticing clues | 5 senses | Finding differences | Robot senses |
| 4 | **Cognition** | Guessing what's next | Thinking & feeling | Comparing patterns | Robot thinking |
| 5 | **Action** | What characters do | Moving & doing | Arranging & building | Robot moving |
| 6 | **Learning** | Learning from stories | Practice makes better | Getting better at patterns | Teaching robots |
| 7 | **Communication** | Telling & sharing | Talking & listening | Describing patterns | Robot talking |
| 8 | **Planning** | What happens next? | Getting ready | Planning ahead | Robot plans |

## Learning Pathway

```
Start Here
    ↓
[01 Story Time] ──→ Stories that make big ideas feel real
    ↓
[02 Our Bodies] ──→ Feeling and sensing the ideas in our own bodies
    ↓
[03 Counting & Patterns] ──→ Seeing the patterns with numbers and sorting
    ↓
[04 Robots & Helpers] ──→ Building things that predict and explore
```

**Recommended path**: Work through in order. Each course adds a new way of understanding the same big ideas. Teachers and parents can mix modules across courses for variety.

## Design Principles

1. **Wonder first**: Every lesson starts with a question or surprise
2. **Play-based**: Learning happens through stories, movement, and building
3. **No jargon**: All concepts expressed in everyday child-friendly language
4. **Multi-sensory**: Activities engage sight, touch, sound, and movement
5. **Spiral learning**: The same 8 topics return in each course at a new angle
