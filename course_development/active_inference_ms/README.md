## Overview

Welcome to **Active Inference for Middle School**! This curriculum introduces the big ideas behind how brains predict, learn, and make decisions — using real-life skills, body science, math detective work, and creative coding.

Content is pitched for curious, energetic middle schoolers. Lessons balance conceptual depth with hands-on activities and real-world connections.

## Core Topics

Each course covers the same 8 topics in the same order — the Active Inference "spine":

| # | Topic | 01 Life Skills | 02 Body Science | 03 Math Detectives | 04 Code & Create |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Systems** | Social systems | Body systems | Number systems | Digital systems |
| 2 | **Agents** | You as decision-maker | Your brain as agent | Data agent | Code agent |
| 3 | **Perception** | Reading social cues | Senses & signals | Observing data | Input & sensors |
| 4 | **Cognition** | Thinking it through | Brain processing | Analyzing patterns | Logic & algorithms |
| 5 | **Action** | Making choices | Movement & reflexes | Testing hypotheses | Output & actions |

| 7 | **Communication** | Expressing & persuading | Nerve signals | Sharing findings | APIs & messages |
| 8 | **Planning** | Setting goals | Health planning | Strategy | Program planning |

## Learning Pathway

```
Start Here
    ↓
[01 Real Life Skills] ──→ See Active Inference in your own decisions
    ↓
[02 Body Science] ──→ Discover the science inside your body
    ↓
[03 Math Detectives] ──→ Use data and logic to test predictions
    ↓
[04 Code & Create] ──→ Build your own prediction machines
```

**Recommended path**: Work through in order. Courses 01–02 build intuition; Courses 03–04 add formal skills. Teachers can interleave modules across courses for variety.

## Design Principles

1. **Real-world first**: Every lesson connects to something middle schoolers care about
2. **Active learning**: Labs and challenges are hands-on, not passive reading
3. **Scaffolded complexity**: Concepts build from intuitive to more formal across courses
4. **Engaging tone**: Energetic, respectful, and occasionally fun — never condescending
5. **Cross-curricular**: Connects to science, math, ELA, and technology standards
