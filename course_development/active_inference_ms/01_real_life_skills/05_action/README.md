# Module 05: Action

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Actions Speak Louder

Part of **Real Life Skills**.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. **Distinguish** between exploration (trying something new) and exploitation (using what you know)
2. **Explain** how actions reduce uncertainty about the world
3. **Define** a policy as a planned sequence of actions
4. **Evaluate** when to explore vs. exploit in real-life scenarios
5. **Design** an action sequence to achieve a personal goal
6. **Connect** action to Active Inference -- acting to make the world match your predictions

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Simple Definition |
|------|------------------|
| **Action** | Something an agent does to change the world or gather information |
| **Exploration** | Trying something new to learn more about the world |
| **Exploitation** | Using what you already know to get a good result |
| **Uncertainty** | Not knowing what will happen next |
| **Policy** | A planned sequence of actions to reach a goal |
| **Risk** | The chance that an action will not turn out as expected |
| **Reward** | A positive outcome from an action |

## Why This Matters

Should you try out for a new sport or stick with the one you are good at? Should you raise your hand when you are not sure of the answer? Should you try a new route to class? Every day you face choices between the safe-and-known and the risky-but-potentially-better. Understanding how action works in Active Inference helps you:
- Know when taking a risk is actually the smart move
- Plan multi-step strategies instead of just reacting
- Recognize that sometimes the best action is one that gives you information
- See yourself as an active participant shaping your world, not a passive observer
