# Lab: Actions Speak Louder -- The Explore vs. Exploit Challenge

## Objective

> **Learning Goal:** Investigate how you choose between exploring new options and sticking with what works, and discover how actions reduce uncertainty.

This is a **group challenge** designed for middle school students (Grades 6-8).

---

## Materials Needed

- 4 paper bags or cups (labeled A, B, C, D)
- Colored tokens or candies (different values in each bag)
- Score sheet and pencil
- Lab journal or notebook

---

## Part 1: The Mystery Bag Game (15 min)

Your teacher has set up 4 bags. Each bag contains tokens worth different point values, but you do not know which bag is best. You get 10 turns total. Each turn, you pick ONE bag and draw a token.

**The dilemma:** Do you keep picking from a bag that gave good results (exploit), or try a new bag to see if it is better (explore)?

## Part 2: Action as Uncertainty Reduction (10 min)

In Active Inference, actions do not just get you rewards -- they also **reduce uncertainty**. Sometimes the best action is one that gives you information, even if it does not pay off right away.

Think about these real-life scenarios. For each one, decide: is the best action to explore (get information) or exploit (use what you already know)?

| Scenario | Explore or Exploit? | Why? |
