# Lab Overview: Real Life Skills

## Course Lab Theme: Group Challenges

> All labs in the Real Life Skills course use a **Group Challenge** format, designed for teams of 2-4 middle school students working together.

---

## Lab Sequence

| Module | Lab Title | Core Activity | Active Inference Concept |
|--------|-----------|---------------|------------------------|
| 01 Systems | Systems Everywhere: Mapping Your World | Collaborative system mapping with poster paper | Boundaries and feedback loops |
| 02 Agents | Agent Profile: What Makes You Tick? | Agent profile cards and sorting activity | Sensing, modeling, acting |
| 03 Perception | Perception Detectives: When Your Brain Tricks You | Optical illusions and social prediction scenarios | Prediction error |
| 04 Cognition | Thinking Fast and Slow: Your Brain's Two Speeds | Cognitive puzzles and belief updating game | Fast vs. slow inference |
| 05 Action | Actions Speak Louder: Explore vs. Exploit | Mystery bag game with scoring | Exploration and exploitation |
| 06 Learning | Level Up: Tracking How You Learn | Real-time skill tracking and strategy audit | Model updating |
| 07 Communication | Connected: Reading Minds and Sending Signals | Describe-and-draw and text interpretation | Shared prediction |
| 08 Planning | Game Plan: Mapping Your Future Moves | Decision trees with dice-roll uncertainty | Future simulation |

---

## Common Materials

All labs in this course require only basic classroom materials:

- Paper (poster-size and regular)
- Colored markers and pens
- Sticky notes
- Pencils
- Timer or stopwatch
- Dice (for planning lab)
- Small objects for touch test (perception lab)
- Bags or cups (for action lab)

---

## Design Principles

1. **No technology required** -- all activities work with paper and classroom materials
2. **Collaborative** -- every lab involves partner or group work
3. **Time-boxed** -- each lab fits within a 40-minute class period
4. **Scaffolded** -- early labs (Systems, Agents) are simpler; later labs (Communication, Planning) build on earlier concepts
5. **Reflective** -- every lab ends with a reflection table connecting the activity to Active Inference
6. **Interactive** -- all labs include {fill:textarea} prompts for written responses
