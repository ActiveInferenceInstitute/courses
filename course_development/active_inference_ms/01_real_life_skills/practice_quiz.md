# Practice Quiz: Real Life Skills — Section Review

## Part A: Multiple Choice

1. In Active Inference, your brain is constantly trying to:
A) Memorize everything it sees
B) Predict what will happen next and reduce surprise
C) Avoid thinking about the future
D) Only focus on things that are fun

**Answer: B** — Active inference says your brain is a prediction machine that works to minimize the gap between expectations and reality.

2. A "system" in your everyday life could be:
A) Only a computer
B) Any group of connected parts working together, like your school or friend group
C) Something that never changes
D) Only things found in nature

**Answer: B** — Systems are everywhere: friend groups, families, schools, and daily routines all have connected parts.

3. What makes YOU an agent?
A) You follow instructions without thinking
B) You have goals, make predictions, and take actions
C) You never make mistakes
D) You only react to what other people do

**Answer: B** — Agents sense their environment, have goals, predict outcomes, and act to shape their world.

4. When you hear a song and your brain fills in the next lyric before it plays, that is an example of:
A) Random guessing
B) Your brain making a prediction based on past experience
C) Your ears being broken
D) A computer algorithm

**Answer: B** — Your brain uses its model of the song to predict what comes next.

5. Which of these is an example of a "prediction error" in daily life?
A) You expect your friend to text back quickly, but they don't reply for hours
B) You eat breakfast and it tastes exactly how you expected
C) You walk to school the same way every day
D) You breathe without thinking about it

**Answer: A** — A prediction error is the mismatch between what you expected and what actually happened.

6. Why do habits feel automatic?
A) Your brain has given up
B) Your brain has built a strong mental model from repeated experience, so it uses less energy
C) Habits aren't real
D) Your brain stops making predictions during habits

**Answer: B** — Repeated experience strengthens predictions so much that the action feels effortless.

7. When you change your study strategy after getting a bad grade, you are:
A) Giving up
B) Updating your mental model based on new evidence
C) Proving that Active Inference doesn't work
D) Making a random choice

**Answer: B** — The bad grade was a prediction error that drove you to update your approach.

8. The **spotlight effect**, where you feel like everyone is watching you, is really:
A) Proof that everyone notices you
B) A prediction error — your brain overestimates how much attention others pay to you
C) A sign you should stay home
D) Something only shy people experience

**Answer: B** — Your brain predicts you're the center of attention because you're focused on yourself, but others are focused on themselves too.

9. Communication fails most often because:
A) People use the wrong words
B) The sender's meaning and the receiver's prediction don't match, especially in text
C) Nobody tries to communicate
D) Words have only one meaning

**Answer: B** — Different mental models decode the same message differently, causing prediction gaps.

10. The best way to handle uncertainty about a new situation is to:
A) Avoid the situation completely
B) Take small, low-risk actions to gather data and reduce uncertainty
C) Make one huge dramatic move
D) Wait until all uncertainty disappears

**Answer: B** — Micro-experiments give you data, update your models, and build confidence for bigger steps.

2. Describe a time when you and a friend saw the same situation differently. Using the idea that everyone is an agent with their own mental model, explain why you might have disagreed.

3. Give an example from your life where you learned something new by doing something (taking an action) rather than just thinking about it. What prediction did you test, and how did your mental model change?

4. Pick two concepts from this section (systems, agents, perception, cognition, action, learning, communication, planning) and explain how they connect. Use a real-life example.
