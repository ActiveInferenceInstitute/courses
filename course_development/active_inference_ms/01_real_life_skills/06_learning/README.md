> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Level Up: How We Learn

Part of **Real Life Skills**.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. **Explain** learning as the process of updating your brain's internal model
2. **Demonstrate** a learning curve by tracking your own skill improvement
3. **Compare** your mental model of a task before and after practice
4. **Evaluate** different study strategies based on how much prediction error they generate
5. **Design** a study plan that uses Active Inference principles
6. **Describe** why mistakes are essential for learning

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Simple Definition |
|------|------------------|
| **Learning** | Updating your brain's model so future predictions improve |
| **Model updating** | Changing what your brain expects based on new experience |
| **Prediction error** | The gap between what you expected and what happened |
| **Practice** | Repeated attempts that generate prediction errors and drive updating |
| **Learning curve** | A graph showing how your performance improves over attempts |
| **Strategy** | A specific approach to learning or solving a problem |
| **Consolidation** | When your brain strengthens new learning over time (especially during sleep) |

## Why This Matters

You spend a huge chunk of your life learning -- in school, in sports, in hobbies, in friendships. But do you actually know HOW your brain learns? Most people think learning is about putting information IN, but Active Inference says learning is about updating your brain's predictions. This explains:
- Why practice tests work better than re-reading
- Why making mistakes is actually a good thing
- Why cramming the night before does not stick
- Why teaching someone else helps you learn faster

Understanding learning helps you learn better. That is basically a superpower.
