## Content Guidelines

### Audience Awareness
Middle schoolers are actively learning how to learn. They are often told to "study harder" without understanding what effective studying looks like. This module connects directly to their academic lives by framing learning through Active Inference. Use gaming metaphors (leveling up, XP, skill trees) alongside real study strategy comparisons. Students care deeply about being good at things -- use this motivation.

### Active Inference Integration
- **Learning as Model Updating**: In Active Inference, learning means changing the parameters of your generative model so future predictions are more accurate. For middle schoolers, say "learning is when your brain gets better at predicting."
- **Prediction Error Drives Learning**: You learn most when your prediction is wrong and you have to update. This explains why re-reading (low prediction error) is less effective than practice testing (high prediction error).
- **Repetition and Consolidation**: Repeated exposure strengthens model parameters. Connect this to spaced practice and why cramming works poorly.

### Lab Design Principles
- Real-time skill tracking makes learning visible
- Before/after comparison shows model updating
- Strategy audit connects to students' actual study habits
- Study plan design applies concepts immediately
- All activities completable in 30-40 minutes

Learning, model updating, prediction error, practice, strategy, repetition, improvement
