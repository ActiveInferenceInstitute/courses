## What Makes an Agent Tick?

Part of **Real Life Skills**.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. **Distinguish** between agents (things that sense, model, and act) and passive objects
2. **Identify** the three core abilities every agent has: sensing, modeling, and acting
3. **Describe** what an internal model is and why agents need one
4. **Explain** why different agents can react differently to the same situation
5. **Design** a simple agent by specifying its sensors, goals, and actions
6. **Relate** the concept of agency to your own everyday decision-making

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Simple Definition |
|------|------------------|
| **Agent** | Something that senses the world, builds expectations, and takes actions |
| **Object** | Something that does not sense, predict, or act on its own |
| **Sensor** | Anything an agent uses to gather information from the world |
| **Internal model** | An agent's mental picture of how the world works |
| **Goal** | A preferred state that an agent tries to reach or maintain |
| **Surprise** | What happens when reality does not match an agent's prediction |
| **Action** | What an agent does to change the world or itself |

## Why This Matters

You are an agent. Every time you check your phone, decide what to wear, choose who to sit with at lunch, or figure out how to respond to a text, you are sensing the world, comparing it to your expectations, and taking action. Understanding what makes you an agent helps you recognize why you make the choices you make, understand why other people see things differently, and start seeing the world through the lens of Active Inference.
