Lab: Game Plan -- Mapping Your Future Moves

Objective

Learning Goal: Practice planning as imagining future scenarios, weighing risks and rewards, and building decision trees to navigate uncertainty.

This is a group challenge designed for middle school students (Grades 6-8).

Materials Needed

  • Large paper or whiteboard
  • Colored markers
  • Sticky notes
  • Dice (one per group, for randomness)
  • Lab journal or notebook

Part 1: Decision Tree Challenge (15 min)

A decision tree maps out possible futures. Each branch is a choice, and each leaf is an outcome.

Scenario: It is Friday afternoon. You have a big project due Monday, a friend invited you to hang out Saturday, and there is a sports tryout Sunday morning.

Build a decision tree on your poster paper. Start with Friday evening and map out all your possible choices and outcomes through the weekend.

Use sticky notes for each decision point and draw arrows for each possible choice.

Part 3: Planning Under Uncertainty (10 min)

Real planning is hard because the future is uncertain. Let's add randomness.

Dice roll challenge: For each plan from Part 2, roll a die. If you roll 1-2, something goes wrong (unexpected obstacle). If you roll 3-4, things go as expected. If you roll 5-6, something unexpectedly good happens.
