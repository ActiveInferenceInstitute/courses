# Module 08: Planning

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Game Plan: Thinking Ahead

Part of **Real Life Skills**.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. **Build** a decision tree that maps possible choices and their outcomes
2. **Evaluate** plans by scoring their risk, reward, and uncertainty
3. **Explain** planning as imagining possible futures and selecting the best path
4. **Demonstrate** how uncertainty makes planning harder using randomness
5. **Design** a robust plan with a backup strategy for a real-life goal
6. **Connect** planning to Active Inference -- choosing actions that minimize expected surprise

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Simple Definition |
|------|------------------|
| **Planning** | Imagining possible futures and choosing the best sequence of actions |
| **Decision tree** | A diagram that maps out choices and their possible outcomes |
| **Risk** | The chance that something will go wrong |
| **Reward** | The positive outcome you hope to achieve |
| **Scenario** | One possible version of how the future could unfold |
| **Robust plan** | A plan that works well even when unexpected things happen |
| **Backup plan** | An alternative strategy if your first plan fails |

## Why This Matters

Every time you think about the weekend, figure out how to study for multiple tests, or decide whether to try out for a team, you are planning. But most people plan by just imagining ONE future. Active Inference teaches that good planning means imagining MULTIPLE futures, thinking about what could go wrong, and picking the path that gives you the best balance of reward and information. This helps you:
- Stop being caught off guard when things do not go as planned
- Make better decisions about how to spend your time
- Build flexible strategies instead of rigid ones
- Feel more confident about the future because you have thought it through
