# Active Inference for Middle School — Pedagogical Overview

## Vision

This curriculum makes Active Inference accessible to Grades 6-8 through four complementary lenses. Each course covers the same 8 foundational topics but from a different disciplinary perspective.

## Tone & Style

Curious, meme-aware, relatable. Light math (fractions, percentages). Scratch/block coding.
