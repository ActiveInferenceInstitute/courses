# Lab: What Do the Numbers Really Tell Us? -- Data Investigation

## Objective

> **Learning Goal:** Use data collection and analysis to explore the concept of observing data through probability and pattern-finding.

This is a **guided worksheet** designed for middle school students (Grades 6-8).

---

## Materials Needed

- Pencil and worksheet
- Calculator (optional)
- Graph paper or blank paper
- Dice, coins, or random number generator (optional)
- Lab journal or notebook
