# Unit 03: Math Detectives — Cracking the Code of Patterns and Predictions

## Learning Objectives

1. See how **pattern recognition and probability** are tools your brain already uses, and learn to sharpen them with math.
2. Apply **active inference thinking** to data analysis, statistics, and mathematical reasoning in ways that feel relevant and useful.
3. Develop your inner detective by using **evidence, prediction, and updating** to solve problems and make sense of uncertain information.

## Introduction

Here's a question: How does your phone's autocomplete know what word you're about to type? How does Netflix figure out what show you'll want to watch next? How do weather forecasters predict whether it'll rain on Saturday?

The answer to all of these is the same: **predictions based on patterns**. And that's exactly what your brain does every second of every day. You're already a pattern-recognition machine — you just haven't learned the math behind it yet.

In this unit, you'll become a Math Detective. You'll learn how to spot patterns in data, understand probability in a way that actually makes sense, and use the same prediction-and-update process that powers everything from recommendation algorithms to scientific discoveries. The best part? Once you see the world through this lens, you can't unsee it. Math stops being abstract and starts being a superpower.

## What You'll Explore in This Unit

### Module 1: Systems — The Hidden Structure in Data
Data doesn't exist in isolation. Every dataset is part of a system with inputs, outputs, and connections. You'll learn to see the bigger picture behind the numbers.

### Module 2: Agents — The Detective's Mindset
A good detective (and a good mathematician) doesn't just collect facts — they form hypotheses, test them, and update their thinking. You'll develop this active, questioning approach to math.

### Module 3: Perception — Spotting Patterns (and Avoiding Traps)
Your brain is wired to see patterns, but sometimes it sees patterns that aren't really there. You'll learn the difference between real signals and misleading noise in data.

### Module 4: Cognition — Building Mental Models with Math
Math gives you tools to build precise models of how things work. You'll learn how equations and graphs are just formalized versions of the predictions your brain already makes.

### Module 5: Action — Testing Your Predictions
Science and math are about doing, not just thinking. You'll design experiments, collect data, and see how your predictions hold up against reality.

### Module 6: Learning — Updating What You Know
When the data doesn't match your prediction, that's not a mistake — that's information. You'll learn Bayesian thinking: how to change your mind in proportion to the evidence.

### Module 7: Communication — Making Data Tell Its Story
Numbers don't speak for themselves. You'll learn to create graphs, charts, and explanations that communicate what the data actually means.

### Module 8: Planning — Using Predictions to Make Decisions
From game strategy to study planning, you'll learn to use mathematical predictions to make better decisions about what to do next.

## Summary

Math isn't about memorizing formulas — it's about understanding the patterns and predictions that shape everything around you. In this unit, you'll combine your natural pattern-recognition abilities with mathematical tools to become a sharper thinker, a better problem-solver, and someone who can see through the noise to find what's really going on. Time to investigate.
