## Applications

In Middle School, we see Planning manifest in:
*   **Specific Example 1**: Before solving a multi-step word problem, good math students plan their approach: "First I will figure out the rate, then multiply by time, then subtract the discount." That mental roadmap is your brain simulating future actions and predicting which sequence of steps will lead to the correct answer with the least wasted effort -- exactly what planning means in active inference.
*   **Specific Example 2**: Budgeting your allowance is mathematical planning in action. If you get twenty dollars a week and want to save for a sixty-dollar video game, your brain runs the model: "If I save ten dollars per week, I will have enough in six weeks, but if I also want snacks on Fridays, I need to adjust." You are simulating multiple future timelines and picking the plan whose predicted outcome best matches your goals.
