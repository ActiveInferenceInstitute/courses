# Syllabus: Math Detectives

> **Active Inference for Middle School** | **Audience**: Grades 6-8

## Course Description

Math Detectives explores Active Inference through probability as mystery-solving. Over 8 modules, students will build a comprehensive understanding of how living systems perceive, think, act, and learn.
