## Content Guidelines

### Course Theme
Math Detectives explores Active Inference through the lens of probability, data analysis, and pattern-finding. Students learn to collect data, find patterns, make predictions, and update those predictions based on new evidence. This course adds mathematical rigor to the intuitive concepts from Courses 1-2, framing every module as a mystery to be solved with data.

### Audience Awareness
Students are 11-14 years old and developing mathematical reasoning skills. Many students are anxious about math -- frame all activities as detective work and mystery-solving rather than "math problems." Use accessible math (fractions, percentages, simple graphs, averages) rather than algebra or advanced statistics. The goal is to build confidence with quantitative reasoning.

### Active Inference Integration
- Frame data collection as perception (gathering evidence)
- Frame pattern analysis as cognition (updating the model)
- Frame prediction as the core output of inference
- Frame prediction errors as clues that help you improve
- Use the predict-observe-update loop throughout every module

### Lab Design Principles
- All labs are Guided Worksheets with structured data tables
- Activities should be completable in 30-40 minutes
- Use simple materials (pencil, paper, calculator, dice)
- Every lab involves collecting real data and making predictions
- Labs connect mathematical findings to Active Inference concepts
