# Course AGENTS: Math Detectives

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course README](./README.md) | [Curriculum AGENTS](../AGENTS.md)

## Identity

- **Course**: Math Detectives
- **Number**: 3
- **Perspective**: Probability as mystery-solving
- **Lab Type**: Guided Worksheet
- **Audience**: Grades 6-8
- **Tone**: Curious, meme-aware, relatable. Light math (fractions, percentages). Scratch/block coding.

## Conventions

All modules in this course must:

1. Use language appropriate for Grades 6-8.
2. Frame all concepts through the lens of probability as mystery-solving.
3. Include guided worksheet activities.
4. Adhere to notation standards in [../resources/notation_table.md](../resources/notation_table.md).
5. Cross-reference the shared [../resources/glossary.md](../resources/glossary.md).
