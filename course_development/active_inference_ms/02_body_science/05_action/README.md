# Module 05: Action

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Movement and Reflexes: Your Body in Action

Part of **Body Science**.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. **Distinguish** between reflexes and voluntary movements
2. **Test** reflexes and explain why they bypass conscious thought
3. **Track** motor skill improvement over repeated practice attempts
4. **Explain** how motor prediction errors help the brain correct future movements
5. **Describe** action as the body making its predictions come true
6. **Connect** physical action to Active Inference's framework

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Simple Definition |
|------|------------------|
| **Reflex** | An automatic movement that happens without conscious control |
| **Voluntary movement** | A movement you choose to make with conscious thought |
| **Motor command** | A signal from the brain telling specific muscles to move |
| **Motor learning** | Getting better at physical skills through practice |
| **Accuracy** | How close your action result is to what you intended |
| **Prediction fulfillment** | Acting to make the world match your brain's prediction |

## Prerequisites

## Why This Matters

Every step you take, every ball you throw, every word you write involves your brain sending precise commands to your muscles. Your motor system gets better with practice because it learns from prediction errors -- the gap between where you aimed and where the ball actually went. Understanding this helps you improve at sports, music, art, and any physical skill.
