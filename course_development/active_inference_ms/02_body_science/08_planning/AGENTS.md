## Content Guidelines

### Audience Awareness
Middle schoolers often have irregular sleep schedules, inconsistent eating habits, and are beginning to take more responsibility for their own health. This module connects Active Inference planning to personal health in a way that feels empowering rather than preachy. Use circadian rhythm tracking as a self-discovery tool rather than a lecture about "good habits."

### Active Inference Integration
- **Biological Planning**: The body plans ahead through immune memory, circadian rhythms, and metabolic anticipation. These are biological implementations of the same planning principles covered in Course 1.
- **Circadian Rhythms as Temporal Predictions**: The body predicts what time of day it is and prepares accordingly. Jet lag and irregular schedules create prediction errors.
- **Health as Prediction Accuracy**: Being healthy means your body's predictions about its own state are accurate. Illness, poor sleep, and stress all increase prediction error.

### Lab Design Principles
- Biological planning examples for conceptual understanding
- Circadian rhythm self-tracking for personal relevance
- Health plan design for practical application
- All activities completable in 30-40 minutes

Circadian rhythm, immune memory, anticipation, health planning, regularity, biological clock
