# Module 08: Planning

> **Quick Navigation**: [Course Home](../README.md) | [Curriculum Home](../../README.md)

## Health Planning: Your Body's Prediction Machine

Part of **Body Science**.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. **Identify** examples of biological planning (immune memory, circadian rhythms, metabolic anticipation)
2. **Track** your personal circadian rhythm and identify peak alertness times
3. **Explain** how the body uses prediction to prepare for future events
4. **Design** a health plan based on how your body makes and uses predictions
5. **Describe** why regular schedules help the body's prediction systems
6. **Connect** biological planning to Active Inference's framework

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Simple Definition |
|------|------------------|
| **Circadian rhythm** | Your body's internal 24-hour clock |
| **Immune memory** | Your immune system's ability to remember past infections |
| **Anticipation** | The body preparing for something before it happens |
| **Biological clock** | Internal timing mechanisms that regulate body functions |
| **Regularity** | Keeping a consistent schedule that matches your body's predictions |
| **Jet lag** | What happens when your body's clock disagrees with the actual time |

## Why This Matters

Your body is planning ahead right now -- preparing for sleep tonight, maintaining immune defenses against infections you had years ago, and anticipating when your next meal is coming. When you work WITH your body's predictions (regular sleep schedule, consistent meals, appropriate exercise), everything runs more smoothly. When you fight against them (pulling all-nighters, skipping meals, jet lag), you feel terrible. Understanding this helps you make health decisions that actually work.
