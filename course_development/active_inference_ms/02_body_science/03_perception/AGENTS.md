- **Tone**: Curious, meme-aware, relatable

## Content Guidelines

### Audience Awareness
Middle schoolers are intrigued by sensory tricks and "weird body facts." Use temperature illusions, smell adaptation, and afterimages as hooks. Students may not realize they have more than five senses -- proprioception and vestibular sense are revelations for most. Connect sensory experiences to everyday situations (motion sickness, food tasting different when sick).

### Active Inference Integration
- **Senses as Prediction Checkers**: Each sense provides data that the brain compares against its predictions. Sensory adaptation happens when the prediction matches the input so well that the brain stops paying attention.
- **Sensory Integration**: The brain combines multiple sensory channels to reduce uncertainty. When channels conflict (motion sickness), prediction error spikes.
- **Internal Senses**: Interoception (sensing internal body states) is a key part of Active Inference. Help students understand that the brain models the body's internal state too.

### Lab Design Principles
- Multiple quick sensory experiments for variety and engagement
- Temperature illusion, smell adaptation, and afterimage experiments
- Sensory integration discussion for deeper understanding
- All experiments use simple, safe materials
- All activities completable in 30-40 minutes

### Key Vocabulary for This Module
Sense, sensor, adaptation, integration, prediction, interoception, proprioception
