- **Tone**: Curious, meme-aware, relatable

## Content Guidelines

### Audience Awareness
Middle schoolers are fascinated by the brain and how it controls everything. They are impressed by reaction time experiments and sensory tricks. Frame the brain as the "agent that runs everything" while helping students understand that agency involves prediction, not just reaction. Use concrete experiments (ruler drop, sensory deprivation) to make brain-as-agent tangible.

### Active Inference Integration
- **Brain as Active Inference Agent**: The brain receives sensory data, maintains a generative model of the body and environment, and sends motor commands. This is the biological instantiation of an Active Inference agent.
- **Reflexes as Habitual Policies**: Reflexes are pre-compiled action policies that bypass deliberate inference. They are fast because they skip the "thinking" step.
- **Prediction and Reaction Time**: Faster reactions occur when the brain can predict what is coming. Prediction reduces the processing needed for action selection.

### Lab Design Principles
- Reaction time experiment for measurable, engaging data collection
- Brain agent profile for conceptual mapping
- Reflex vs. decision sorting for understanding hierarchical control
- Sensory deprivation for experiential learning
- All activities completable in 30-40 minutes

### Key Vocabulary for This Module
