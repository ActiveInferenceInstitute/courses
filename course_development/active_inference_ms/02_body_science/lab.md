# Lab Overview: Body Science

## Course Lab Theme: Investigation Labs

> All labs in the Body Science course use an **Investigation Lab** format, featuring hands-on experiments with real data collection about the human body.

---

## Lab Sequence

| Module | Lab Title | Core Activity | Active Inference Concept |
|--------|-----------|---------------|------------------------|
| 01 Systems | Body Systems: Your Internal Network | Heart rate recovery and system mapping | Homeostasis as prediction |
| 02 Agents | Your Brain as Agent: The Command Center | Reaction time tests and brain profiling | Brain as Active Inference agent |
| 03 Perception | Senses and Signals | Temperature illusions and smell adaptation | Sensory prediction error |
| 04 Cognition | Brain Processing: How Your Neurons Think | Stroop Effect and neural telephone game | Hierarchical prediction |
| 05 Action | Movement and Reflexes | Reflex testing and motor skill tracking | Action as prediction fulfillment |
| 06 Learning | Brain Plasticity | Mirror drawing and sleep/learning surveys | Neuroplasticity as model updating |
| 07 Communication | Nerve Signals | Signal speed race and system comparison | Internal prediction maintenance |
| 08 Planning | Health Planning | Circadian rhythm tracking and health plan design | Biological anticipation |

---

## Common Materials

All labs in this course use safe, simple materials:

- Paper and pencils
- Timer or stopwatch
- Ruler (for reaction time test)
- Blindfold or eye mask
- Small rubber ball
- Ice cube or cold pack
- Scented cotton balls
- Mirror (for mirror drawing)

---

## Design Principles

1. **Safe and simple** -- all experiments use classroom-safe materials
2. **Data-driven** -- every lab involves measuring and recording real data
3. **Body-focused** -- students investigate their own body's responses
4. **Connected to health** -- labs relate to personal health and well-being
5. **Reflective** -- every lab ends with a reflection connecting to Active Inference
6. **Collaborative** -- all labs involve partner or group work
