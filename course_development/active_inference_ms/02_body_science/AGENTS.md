## Content Guidelines

### Course Theme
Body Science explores Active Inference through the lens of the human body. Students discover that their body is the most sophisticated prediction machine they will ever encounter -- from the immune system anticipating infections to the nervous system predicting sensory input. This course bridges the personal relevance of Course 1 (Real Life Skills) with the scientific rigor that builds toward Courses 3-4.

### Audience Awareness
Students are 11-14 years old and experiencing significant physical changes (puberty, growth spurts). They are curious about their bodies but may be self-conscious. Frame body content as empowering and scientific rather than personal. Use concrete experiments (reaction time, heart rate, sensory tests) to make abstract biological concepts tangible. Avoid content that could make students uncomfortable about their bodies.

### Active Inference Integration
- Frame each body system as a prediction-and-correction system
- Use homeostasis as the central metaphor for free energy minimization
- Connect sensory processing to the prediction-error framework
- Show how neuroplasticity is the biological basis of model updating
- Relate body communication (nervous + endocrine) to Active Inference message passing

### Lab Design Principles
- All labs are Investigation Labs with hands-on experiments
- Activities should be completable in 30-40 minutes
- Use safe, simple experiments (heart rate, reaction time, sensory tests)
- Every lab includes data collection tables and {fill:textarea} prompts
- Labs connect biological observations to Active Inference concepts
