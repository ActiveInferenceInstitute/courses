## Content Guidelines

### Audience Awareness
Middle schoolers are often told that their brains are "still developing," but they rarely get to see evidence of this. Mirror drawing provides a real-time demonstration of neuroplasticity. Sleep and learning surveys connect to their own experience and can motivate healthier sleep habits. Neuroplasticity case studies (taxi drivers, musicians) are concrete and memorable.

### Active Inference Integration
- **Neuroplasticity as Model Parameter Updating**: When the brain rewires itself, it is changing the parameters of its generative model to make better future predictions. This is the biological basis of Active Inference learning.
- **Sleep as Model Consolidation**: During sleep, the brain replays and consolidates new learning, strengthening updated predictions and pruning unhelpful ones.
- **Practice as Prediction Error Training**: Repeated practice generates prediction errors that drive synaptic changes.

### Lab Design Principles
- Mirror drawing for real-time neuroplasticity experience
- Case studies for understanding long-term brain changes
- Sleep survey for personal data collection and health connection
- All activities completable in 30-40 minutes
